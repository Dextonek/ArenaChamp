The Tower: a new game mode

A third mode beside duels and the raid. The raid stays exactly as it is. Nothing here is built yet.

A run

Floor 1→boon→Floor 2→boon→…→Floor 10: guardian→Floor 11→…

Each floor is one short room, 30 to 90 seconds. Clear it and the stairs open. Your health does not refill between floors. You have three lives. How high can you climb?

Six kinds of floor, so no two feel alike

What makes it its own game

  • Health carries over. In the raid you are full at every fight. Here every hit you take follows you upstairs.
  • Short rooms, many kinds. A dodging floor, then a horde, then a duel: the hero you picked is good at some and bad at others.
  • Boons, not raid upgrades. After each floor choose one of three: heal, a new power, or a relic that changes a spell. The tower gets its own set.
  • It gets stranger as you climb. From floor 11 floors mix two kinds (a duel inside a gauntlet), and the rooms get twists.
  • One number to brag about: your highest floor, on a board.
