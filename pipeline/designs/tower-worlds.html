Agreed 13 November · floors 1 to 5 being built

The Tower: floors as worlds

Your idea of 13 November, worked out, with the content you asked to add: each floor a small world with its own law, people, quests, roaming enemies, a rival climber, a ruler, and a gatekeeper at the gate up.

Climb a tower where every floor is a small world. Arrive at a camp, take quests, fight what roams, beat the floor's boss, then win a duel against the gatekeeper to go up. What you reached is kept, and you grow as you climb.

One floor, start to finish

Arrive at the camp→ Take the quest→ Cross the zone, fight packs→ A trial door, if you dare→ The floor's boss→ Duel the gatekeeper→ The floor above

About 20 minutes. Alone, or later with two friends.

The Drowned Harbour, grown from an arena into a zone about four times as wide. A sketch of the layout, not the art.

The parts of a floor

Arrive

The camp

Safe ground. The floor's keeper gives the main quest, a second figure gives side quests. The gate up is in sight, sealed.

We have it: heroes of the roster stand in as the people.

Explore

The zone

One map, four or five places joined by paths, in one look: harbour, glade, canyon, cavern, battlefield, frost, caldera, sky ruins, temple.

New: a map this wide. The looks and the camera exist.

Fight

What roams

Three kinds of small enemy a floor, in packs of three to five, plus one elite. They guard their places. Cleared stays cleared.

Partly new: the Tower's keepers walk and strike already; packs that wait, wake and call are new.

Follow

Quests

A main quest in three steps that leads you across the zone to the boss's door, and two side quests that pay boons.

New: a small quest system and a quest list on the screen.

Dare

A trial door

One a floor: a door into a single short room (cross the fire, hold out, break the crystal) for a relic.

We have it: these are the Tower's rooms of today.

Obey

The floor's law

One rule a floor that changes how you move through it: the tide comes in, the wood moves its paths, a storm crosses, the dark hides hunters, a war picks sides.

Partly there: the arenas' own events (the harbour's wave, the roots, the dark).

Hunt

A named enemy

One a floor walks the zone on its own road. It is strong, it is optional, and its bounty is a relic.

New.

Meet

The rival

Another climber, a hero you know. A word on the road, then a race or a short duel. Beat them and they pay a boon and stand beside you against the ruler.

Mostly there: the duel against the CPU, and allies that fight for you.

Find

Secrets and waystones

Three hidden caches off the paths and a lore stone with the floor's story. Waystones you touch let you step back from the camp, and you wake there after a fall.

New, and small.

Trade

Shards and the trader

Enemies, caches and quests drop shards, the floor's coin. The trader in the camp sells a heal, a boon, a life back, or a hint to a cache.

New, and small.

Win

The floor's boss

In its lair at the far end, with phases. Every tenth floor a Raid boss for a party: the Sun Warden, Ash and Ember, the Heart of Noon.

We have it to start: the Tower's guardian. Bosses of each floor's own come later.

Ascend

The gatekeeper

A duel, one against one, against a hero of the roster with its real spells, played by the computer. It gets better floor by floor. Win and the floor above is yours.

We have it: the duel against the CPU, all 40 heroes.

Growing as you climb

On a floor

Quests and the trial pay boons: a heal, a power, or a relic that changes a spell. They last for this climb. The Tower's 17 boons are ready.

For good

Each gatekeeper you beat opens the next floor on your account, and lets you keep one boon for ever as a sigil. Ten floors, ten sigils. They count in the Tower only, never in a duel against a player.

Falling, and mastering a floor

Three lives a floor

A fall takes one and puts you at the last waystone with half health. What you cleared stays cleared, quests keep their progress. With none left you begin the floor again from its camp.

Four stars a floor

Cleared · every quest done · no life lost · under the floor's time. Stars show on the floor's tile, and your best time goes on the week's board.

The five floors

Each with its own law, people, enemies, quest, rival, ruler and gatekeeper.

1

The Drowned Harbour

The tide. Every 80 seconds the sea comes in for 15: low ground floods, slows and hurts. Quays and decks stay dry.

People
Gus keeps the harbour (main quest). Anka, washed ashore (side quests).
Roams
Drowned dockers that strike, net casters that shoot and root, slow armoured brine crabs. Elite: the Harbourmaster's Mate. Named: Old Barnacle.
Quest
Clear the fish market · light the lighthouse · bring the captain's key from the wreck.
Rival
Zephyr: a race to the lighthouse.
Ruler
The Drowned Captain. Anchor sweeps and a cannon volley from the wreck. At half he calls the tide early and fights in the water, where only the deck is safe.
Gatekeeper
Marin, the tide. The sea comes in once during the duel.
2

The Walking Wood

The wood walks. Every two minutes roots close one path and open another. The map changes under you.

People
Bo the shepherd (main quest). Mortis, digging for something (side quests).
Roams
Bark hounds in fast packs, thorn sprites that shoot, slow heavy moss giants. Elite: the Stag-Crowned. Named: the Hollow Oak.
Quest
Free three bound wisps · follow them to the heart · cut the three roots that feed it.
Rival
Nyx: a duel in the thicket.
Ruler
The Elder Root. A heart tree that does not move. Cut its feeding roots, which grow back, while it throws thorns and raises walls of roots.
Gatekeeper
Fenna, the grove. Roots rise as cover in the middle of the duel.
3

The Storm Canyon

The storm. A wall of sand crosses the canyon every 90 seconds. Inside it you see a few steps and take harm, unless you stand behind a rock.

People
Pip the courier (main quest). Kaia, watching the storm (side quests).
Roams
Raiders that strike, slingers that shoot, dust wraiths that only come in the storm. Elite: the Raider Chief. Named: Dune Mother.
Quest
Carry water to three wells · shelter the caravan through a storm · break the raiders' gate.
Rival
Vesper: a duel on the bridge.
Ruler
The Scarab Colossus. Burrows and bursts up under you. In its last phase it calls the storm, and only its own shell gives shelter.
Gatekeeper
Sefu, the sand. A storm crosses the ring once.
4

The Lightless Deep

The dark. Only crystals give light. Beyond it you see nothing, and the lurkers hunt. A shard you carry lights a few steps.

People
Mabel the delver (main quest). Isolde, who lost her lantern (side quests).
Roams
Shard mites in swarms, lurkers that wait in the dark and leap, slow crystal golems that throw a bolt back. Elite: the Lantern Thief. Named: the Pale One.
Quest
Carry a great shard to three dark crystals and wake them. Each one lights a hall.
Rival
Tempo: a race against the dark.
Ruler
The Prism Mother. Beams turn between her crystals. Break a crystal to make a dark gap to stand in. In the last phase the light goes out.
Gatekeeper
Opaline, the prism. Half the ring is dark.
5

The Field of Banners

The war. Two ghost armies fight for ever. Take a banner in the camp: its soldiers leave you be, the others hunt you.

People
Cantor the war singer (main quest). Sir Whiskers, a deserter (side quests).
Roams
Footmen that strike, bowmen that shoot, knights that charge, all of the other banner. Elite: the Banner Bearer. Named: the Deserter Knight.
Quest
Take three standards · hold the hill for a minute · break the siege line.
Rival
Brannoc: a duel between the lines.
Ruler
The Last Marshal. He fights with his guard, waves of both banners. Fell his standard and the guard kneels. In the last phase he is alone, on a horse of smoke.
Gatekeeper
Oblivion, the void. Arrows fall on a line now and then.

Beyond floor 5 the gate opens on a landing that says the climb goes on. Floors 6 to 10 are the next work, the tenth with the Sun Warden for a group.

What the first version will and will not be

  • You play alone, with no account needed, as the Tower's first version was. Friends on a floor come later.
  • The zones are drawn in code from the arenas' own pieces and colours: a first pass for you to direct, not finished art.
  • A ruler is a giant with its own attacks and phases, wearing a hero's body for now. Bodies of their own come with you.
  • The small enemies are simple figures drawn in code, like the Tower's keepers today.
  • Your climb is kept in this browser, not yet on your account.

How the game fits together

  • The Tower is the road. It is what you do when you open the game.
  • Duels are how a gate is won. The same duel you can also play against people.
  • The Raid's bosses rule every tenth floor, where you need a party.
  • The Tower of today stays as Trials: the quick weekly mode, and the rooms behind the trial doors. Nothing built is thrown away.
