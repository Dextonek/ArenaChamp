# New hero ideas (2026-11-04)

The user asked for a pillar hero and a mirror hero, and for more suggestions that are "unique and good to play or funny". Each idea below
does one thing no current hero does, and each follows the [kits-v5](kits-v5.md) rules: one clear question, a weakness any hero can use, and
everything visible to both players. None comes from a League champion. Numbers are first guesses, and nothing here is built yet.

**A new hero costs more than a kit change:** each needs a model too. The cost line counts the engine and spell work only.

## The two you asked for

### Bram and Gerald, the Pillar Mason (bruiser, melee)
Bram built a pillar, carved a grumpy face on it, named it Gerald, and takes it everywhere. Gerald is not happy about this.

- **Question:** do I keep Gerald as cover, or throw him at you?
- **Weakness:** without Gerald he is a plain fighter, and Gerald can be broken. Walk away from the pillar and Bram has to choose between you and him.
- **Funny:** Gerald's carved face changes: bored, alarmed when shoved, furious when he hits you. Bram shouts "SORRY GERALD" when he breaks.

| Key | Spell | What it does | Answer |
|---|---|---|---|
| P | Best Friends | There is one Gerald, a real pillar that blocks walking, sight and bolts for both players, with 220 HP. Within 3 m of him, Bram takes 15% less damage | Fight away from Gerald, or break him |
| Q | Raise Gerald | Gerald bursts up at a spot (9 m, 0.7 s warning): 50 and a knock-up within 1.5 m. An old Gerald crumbles first | Step off the circle: 1.5 m takes 0.63 s with reaction |
| W | The Shove | With Gerald within 3 m, Bram shoves him 8 m in a straight line at 8 m/s. He hits you for 60 and carries you along. Pinned against a wall: stunned 0.9 s | The line shows first. Step sideways, and stay away from walls behind you |
| E | A Good Lean | Bram dashes to Gerald from up to 8 m and hugs him for a 60 shield | Break Gerald first, or wait where Bram must land |
| R | TIMBER! | Gerald topples along a 10 m line. A long shadow shows it for 1 s, then 150. He breaks, leaving rubble that slows | Leave the shadow. Bram has no pillar afterwards, so that is your window |

Second decision: Gerald is his cover, his weapon and his escape, and using one spends the others. Cost: medium. It needs a pillar that moves, on top of the obstacles the engine already has.

### Silvane, the Vain (marksman, ranged attack)
She fights with hand mirrors, because nothing else is worthy of her face. They bounce round the arena and come back to her.

- **Question:** do I chase the mirror coming back, or stay where the shot is good?
- **Weakness:** open ground gives her no bounces, and she has no real damage up close.
- **Funny:** a mirror she catches makes her stop and pose ("GORGEOUS"). Breaking her standing mirror gives you Seven Years' Bad Luck, which lasts 7 seconds.
- **Against Opaline:** Opaline places one exact shot off a wall. Silvane fills the room and runs to catch what returns. Opaline is geometry; Silvane is chaos.

| Key | Spell | What it does | Answer |
|---|---|---|---|
| P | Fairest of All | Each bounce adds 25% damage. A mirror that comes back to her can be caught by walking into it: the spell comes back at once and she moves 20% faster for 2 s | Hit her while she walks into the catch, which is a place you can predict |
| Q | Hand Mirror | A spinning mirror (10 m, 3 bounces): 40. The first bounce is drawn for both players | Sidestep. Stay out of corners, where mirrors stack up |
| W | Vanity Stand | A full-length mirror at a spot for 6 s, 60 HP. It blocks bolts and her mirrors bounce off it at any angle. Whoever breaks it gets Bad Luck: 15% less damage for 7 s | Move so it gives her no angle, or walk round it and take the risk |
| E | Through the Glass | She steps into her Stand and out the other side, up to 9 m. With no Stand, only a 3 m step | Stand near the mirror: you know where she comes out |
| R | Hall of Mirrors | She spins and throws 8 mirrors in a circle: 30 each, up to 2 bounces, and each can hit only once. They are slow, and there are gaps | Find a gap, and get near her: mirrors fired from the middle spread apart |

Second decision: every thrown mirror is a choice between holding position and walking to catch it. Cost: small, because the bank shots are already being built for Opaline.

## What I would build first

1. **Bram and Gerald and Silvane**, because you asked for them, and Silvane is cheap once the bank shots exist.
2. **Professor Puff**, the funniest for the least work.
3. **Sir Whiskers** or **Mr. Ledger**, because each makes the opponent change how they play with very little engine work.
