# Style notes from the four Roblox reference videos

- **Impact frames are the signature.** Both John Kler effects stop time for 0.1-0.2 s at the hit with full-screen value inversions: black screen + white (or blue) silhouettes and focus lines, then the inverse (white or flat cyan + black silhouettes), each 2-3 frames at 60 fps. Rule: every finisher gets a 2-3 step full-screen, fully opaque impact frame that draws the targets as flat silhouettes, then heavy shake starts on the first normal frame.
- **Implode before you explode.** The big hits are preceded by a 0.2-0.4 s pull-in: the black hole shrinks to a point, the cage lines pull to the centre over a tightening ground spiral. Rule: the release must be preceded by a visible contraction toward the impact point.
- **Darken the world first.** The lightning video grades the whole scene dark blue before the chain starts; the emote video sits on a night backdrop with a vignette; the orb video uses a stormy dark sky. Rule: big spells dim and tint the scene (FinishPass grade) for their duration so additive layers glow without blowing out, and restore it at the end.
- **Black is a colour.** Pure black sphere cores, black spike shards, black hand silhouettes, black slash strokes over bright orbs, black smoke balls in the aftermath. Rule: every effect has at least one solid, normal-blended black or near-black shape on or in front of its brightest layer.
- **Hard edges over soft glow.** Bursts are spiky stars, stretched diamond shards, crescent slashes, crisp thin rings and painted brush strokes with alpha-clipped edges. Soft glow is only a thin halo. Rule: the main shapes use hard alpha (alphaTest or step masks); soft glows are secondary and small.
- **Anime graphic vocabulary.** Radial focus/speed lines on impacts and pull-ins, four-point star glints, hollow circle pops, crescent wings and slashes. Rule: add 1-2 flat 2D graphic elements (focus lines, star glints, crescents) to each effect to give it the illustrated Roblox look.
- **One dominant hue plus a complementary accent, with a white core.** Violet with orange candles; teal with red motes; pink orb with blue arcs; coral swirl with a cyan core; magenta burst with white-blue lightning. Rule: pick one main hue, one small complementary accent, white for the hottest 5%, and black for contrast. Push fire toward pink/magenta when it sits in a purple scene.
- **Huge scale jumps and screen-filling beats.** A tiny seed orb becomes a screen-sized ring in 2 frames; a 10 m disc collapses to a point. Rule: the key beat should be 3-5x bigger than the thing that caused it and briefly fill most of the screen.
- **Readable order of events.** The chain lightning hops caster -> front row -> sides -> back row at about 0.15 s per hop; things pulled into the black hole spiral visibly. Rule: when an effect touches many targets, sequence it in a clear spatial order instead of hitting all at once, then pay it off with a simultaneous hit.
- **Sustain with flicker, not with more light.** Long holds stay alive through lightning that redraws every 2-3 frames, random 2-3 frame arcs and spark spits at nodes, not by adding glow. Rule: holds use flickering thin arcs and small spark bursts; total brightness stays flat.
- **Bright -> dark -> clean exit.** After the bright burst the effects switch to dark smoke balls, black flakes and falling rocks, and the frame is clean about 1-1.5 s after the hit. Rule: aftermath layers are dark and normal-blended, and nothing lingers past ~1.5 s except a fading ground mark.
- **Loops need a beat and a hero shape.** The emote and orb auras each have a solid hero prop or silhouette (pumpkin, moon sigil, grave hands, crowned sphere) and a periodic beat (charge -> wing sweep -> flame burst every ~2 s, slash strokes every ~0.25 s). Rule: a looping aura gets one iconic shape plus a 1-3 s rhythmic beat; a reflective or dark floor doubles the read.
