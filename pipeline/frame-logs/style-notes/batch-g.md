# Style notes from batch G (new, not in VFX_PRINCIPLES §6–§7)

- **Cut to a void set for the charge, cut back to the world for the result.** Finisher cutscenes stage the charge in a
  separate black or starry space (orb in black, dragon in front of a moon) with no ground, then hard-cut back to the
  arena for the hit. Leaving the environment and returning to it is itself an impact. (Hiyuki orb, Castorice wing nova)
- **Cutscene shot grammar: close -> wide -> ground-level -> reveal (held).** Lead-in shots are 0.15–0.3 s each and
  alternate size (detail on the weapon, wide for context, camera at 0.3 m looking up for scale); only the reveal or the
  charge is held 1–1.6 s with a slow 10–15% push-in. Stillness on the held shot is the anticipation.
- **Whip-pan smears and 1-frame graphic cards as transitions.** Between angles, use a 0.3–0.6 s full-screen diagonal
  speed smear (with themed particles like butterflies flying through it) or single frames of flat colour shapes / a
  flat-colour cut-in panel (Hiyuki's cyan blade card). The transition is a VFX layer, not an editing gap.
- **Escalate a long channel in colour stages.** A beam/breath that lasts several seconds changes its impact look in
  2–3 stages (blue splash -> magenta starburst -> gold explosion), each switch marked by one inverted/posterised frame,
  ideally with an off-palette accent (acid green). The hit keeps growing instead of just lasting.
- **Exit through a hold: whiteout or hard cut.** Cinematic finishers do not show their clean-up: they ramp into a held
  white screen with a grey vignette, or hard-cut to a calm wide gameplay shot. Gameplay moves instead exit with a grey,
  normal-blended dust vortex that keeps spinning after the colour has drained (colour leaves first, motion stays).
- **A dead beat between two hits.** A 0.1–0.15 s hold with no VFX at all (character frozen in a crouch) between the
  wind-up burst and the real hit makes the second beat land much harder. Repeat the world tint on both beats.
- **Turn the axis at the impact.** A horizontal charge and a horizontal shot that end in a vertical pillar (Fuga) read
  as a new event. Give the release one perfect clean shape (a thin golden ring as the "bow") inside the noisy charge,
  right before the shot.
- **Draw the telegraph with a moving point.** A spark that runs around and draws the rune circle (then a cold white
  four-point glint with a long horizontal flare) pulls the eye to the spot before the warm ignition. Cold cue, warm
  payoff.
- **Show where an attack comes from 0.3 s before it arrives.** Blade rain first tears a crack/shard rift in the sky,
  then 6 parallel dark blades arrive 1 frame apart and hang 1 s as scars in the air. Dark blade bodies, hot thin edges.
- **Themed and digital particles give identity.** Butterflies/petals instead of sparks for a flower-themed summoner,
  black glitch cubes and red damage-digit sprites for a "corrupted" slash, and a tiny graphic full stop at the end of a
  combo (a rising dotted line with a black diamond tip).
