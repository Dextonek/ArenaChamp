## Technique notes
- **Monochrome + prism.** Everything is white, grey and black; the only colour is a thin rainbow (RGB-split) fringe on bright shards and at the screen edges (pink left, teal right). That is chromatic aberration used as the "colour" of the move. Build: a screen pass that offsets R and B outward by a few px proportional to distance from centre, scaled by a per-beat intensity curve (0 → 1 for 0.3 s at each hit).
- **Anti-flash.** Before the two biggest whiteouts there is one frame that is darker than the base (7.00 and 14.10, the second is a literal soft black sphere over the caster). Dark → white reads stronger than base → white.
- **Glass bubble.** After each big hit a rim-only sphere (thin white outline, crack lines, transparent inside) holds 0.2-0.3 s with prism flakes inside. Fresnel-only shell, additive.
- **Ring stacks.** 2-3 thin crisp rings stacked vertically, rising and widening at different speeds, then collapsing inward. Very cheap and gives height.
- **Grass of wisps.** A ring of 20-40 tall thin vertical wisp ribbons (alpha-eroded strips) rising, swaying, holding ~0.8 s. Reads as a pillar made of strands, not a solid beam.
- **Ink tendrils for charge.** The charge is a dark smoky tendril (normal-blended, white rim) curling upward: dark charge, white release.
- **Flat flare plane.** A wide horizontal ellipse of white (like an anamorphic flare) floats at chest height on each release.
- **Dust curls finish every beat.** Grey normal-blended dust hooks on the ground are the last 0.5 s of each beat; no coloured residue.
- What reads well: strict value structure (black / grey / white), colour only as a 2-3 px fringe, every hit framed by a dark beat before it and grey dust after it.
