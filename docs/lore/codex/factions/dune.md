# The Dune Throne

*The oldest court on the Dial: the sun is the king's father, and the Noon Seat is his.* · [Factions](README.md) ·
[The Sands](../realms/sands.md) · [Dune folk](../peoples/dune.md)

## What it is

The kings of the Sands always called the sun their father, and the Sands say it fell into their dunes on purpose. The Dune Throne is the
oldest court on the Dial: a king, his embalmer, a veiled priestess of the tombs, the judge who weighs the hearts of the dead, an archer of
the mirage and the captain of the lion at the gate. They want the sun's seat for their king.

## Ranks

- **The King**, who holds the crook: [Sefu](../heroes/sefu.md), since year 1. The crown is cursed and passes; the crook does not.
- **The Five Offices**, older than any king now living:
  - **the Embalmer**, who keeps the dead kings whole: [Vitriol](../heroes/vitriol.md);
  - **the Weigher**, the judge of the dead: [Nebet](../heroes/nebet.md);
  - **the Veiled**, the priestesses of the tombs, who guard the dead with their eyes: [Lithea](../heroes/lithea.md), the last;
  - **the Mirage**, the archers of the Glass Flats: [Sahar](../heroes/sahar.md);
  - **the Gate**, the captain of the lion: [Ramet](../heroes/ramet.md), of the Ninefold.
- **The Houses**, the noble families of the river towns, each with a house name said only at court.
- **The Guard**, in bronze scales and lion hoods.

## Rites and symbols

- **The sigil:** a crook across a sun half sunk in a dune. Ochre, sandstone, turquoise and verdigris.
- **The Homecoming** at midsummer, with fires on the dunes.
- **The Weighing:** a king's heart against a feather; Nebet's feather is, they say, one of Anka's.
- **The Crook:** the king holds it in every judgement; a judged enemy "cannot run far".
- **The Count of Days:** since the curse, the court keeps a count of the days the crown has stayed on one head.

## Secrets

- **The crown.** Sefu wears the cursed crown still, and has outlasted everyone it passed to; nobody knows why, and he believes it is
  because he is the sun's true heir. Vitriol knows it is because of a draught Sefu takes every night. It is shortening his life.
- **Asenet's gate.** Ramet opened the gate for Asenet on the night she was driven out. Sefu has never asked him why. The court suspects;
  nobody says.
- **The storms.** Some of the Sands' storms are called. Not by the Throne, the Throne says.
- **The heart in the Valley.** One heart in the Valley of Lids was weighed and found heavier than the feather, and the king it belonged
  to was buried anyway. Nebet knows whose.

## What it wants from the Tower

The Noon Seat for its king: Sefu as the new sun, if the climb can be won.

## How it works against the others

With old patience and new allies. The Throne allies with the Court of Dusk (who would rather a king in the Seat than a sun); trades with
the Underforge on the Brass Road; challenges the Noonsworn every midsummer; sends its champions to the Ring for the right to climb; and
hunts Asenet, quietly.

## Ties

Friends of the Court of Dusk (of convenience) and the Underforge; rivals of the Noonsworn and the Windward.
