# The Underforge

*The builders of the Deep: the fallen sun is the best fire the Dial ever had, and it fell to them.* · [Factions](README.md) ·
[The Deep](../realms/deep.md) · [Kilnborn](../peoples/kiln.md) · [Mole-folk](../peoples/mole.md)

## What it is

The Underforge believe the fallen sun is the best fire the Dial ever had, and that it fell to them: its heat rises through the Ember
Caldera, its light lies in their crystals, and they were the first to see it come. They are smiths, inventors, masons, miners, a herald
of black glass, a golem-maker and a sapper, and they build. They forge the Truce's iron and trade with the Dune Throne.

## Ranks

- **The Hall of Marks**, the masters of the great forge, the masons, the miners and the engineers, each house under its maker's mark.
- **The Master of the Forge**: [Brannoc](../heroes/brannoc.md).
- **The Herald of the Fire**, who speaks for the Caldera: [Itzel](../heroes/itzel.md).
- **The Masters**, each the head of a craft: masonry ([Bram](../heroes/bram.md)), engines ([Ada](../heroes/ada.md)), golems
  ([Hesk](../heroes/hesk.md)), sapping ([Tova](../heroes/tova.md)).
- **The Old Mothers** of the mole-folk, who sit in the Hall when the seams are concerned, and the miners of the Lamp Road
  ([Mabel](../heroes/mabel.md)).
- **The Journeymen and the Apprentices**, who earn their mark by making one great piece.

## Rites and symbols

- **The sigil:** an anvil under a lantern. Forge orange, copper, limestone and lantern gold.
- **The Marking:** a maker cuts their mark on their first great piece, and is a master.
- **The Kindling** at midsummer: every furnace fed, the day the fire came up.
- **The Bellows-Oath:** sworn with a hand on a working bellows: "I will tend the fire until its owner comes."

## Secrets

- **The drill.** The Underforge is drilling a shaft from the Caldera toward the Hall of Noon under the Tower, to reach the Heart from
  below. The Truce does not know. It is two-thirds done.
- **The fire is fading too.** The Caldera burns a little lower every year, as the light pales. The Hall of Marks has measured it and told
  nobody, because it would mean the Noonsworn are right that something must be done.
- **Hesk's first golem.** It walked into the black water when Kohaku stole its word. It did not drown. It is still walking, somewhere
  under the Deep, with no word on its brow.

## What it wants from the Tower

The Heart of Noon left below and its fire brought up through the rock: a sun underground, lighting the Deep and warming the Dial. If the
Heart cannot be reached, the Ember of Aubade, sunk in the great forge and fed until it burns like a sun.

## How it works against the others

With iron and contracts. The Underforge forges the Truce's iron and the Gaol's chains and is paid for both; trades with the Throne on
the Brass Road; quarrels with the Noonsworn, who want every drop of the Deep's light carried up into the sky (Brannoc fights Orren for
the Ember whenever the Ring allows); and fights the Moot over the Wood's edge, where its mines and axes go on eating.

## Ties

Friends of the Truce and the Dune Throne; rivals of the Noonsworn; enemies of the Greenwood Moot.
