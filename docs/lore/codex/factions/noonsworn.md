# The Noonsworn

*The sworn of the dawn: the sun can be hung again, and must be.* · [Factions](README.md) · [Aubade](../realms/aubade.md) ·
[Aubade folk](../peoples/aubade.md)

## What it is

The Noonsworn believe the sun can be hung again over the Tower, and that it is their duty to do it. They keep the Oath of the Dawn,
the last verse of the Hymn of Noon, the bells that call the morning, and the Ember of Aubade, a piece of the old sun's light kept in a
lantern since its last noon. They are warm, brave and certain, and certainty makes them poor at listening.

## Ranks

- **The Elders of the Bell**, nine old paladins and ringers who advise the Council of Bells and decide the order's course.
- **The Paladins**, sworn knights of the White Barracks, in white and gold plate with the wings of plates, about four hundred;
  [Auriel](../heroes/auriel.md) is the most famous.
- **The Bearer**, one at a time, sworn to carry the Ember: [Orren](../heroes/orren.md) since year 90.
- **The Ringers**, who keep the hundred bell towers.
- **The Choir**, the Hymn's keepers since the Fall: [Cantor](../heroes/cantor.md) teaches it, and it has never again been a hundred.
- **The Sworn**, everyone who has taken the oath and wears the dawn line, from Lowlight fishermen to bell-founders.
- **The Friends of the Dawn**, honorary members of other peoples: [Anka](../heroes/anka.md), the sun's promise; [Opaline](../heroes/opaline.md),
  who breaks light out of the Deep; [Campana](../heroes/campana.md), the bell-founder.

## Rites and symbols

- **The sigil:** a sun disc whose crack is mended in gold. Dawn gold, ivory, ember and hymn rose.
- **The Oath of the Dawn:** sworn at first light, the dawn line tattooed at the first bell. "Every night ends. I will help it end."
- **The Ringing:** the hundred bells every morning; the hundredth rung short.
- **The Lily:** a lily laid on each of the choir's empty chairs at midsummer.
- **The Burst:** the paladins' shields are made to burst outward when broken, as the dawn does.

## Secrets

- **The paling.** The Hourkeepers told the Elders in year 60 that the sun had been paling for three hundred years before the Fall. The
  Elders decided the Noonsworn should not be told.
- **The aging.** The Elders know the Hourkeepers' theory that the Noon-touched may age all at once if the sun is hung again. They have
  decided it is a price worth paying. They have not asked the Noon-touched.
- **The Ember.** Nobody knows what the Ember of Aubade will do in the Noon Seat. The Elders have chosen to believe.
- **The missing verse.** The Hymn of Noon had a last verse that only the choir's master knew. He did not come down. Cantor does not
  know it. Mum might.

## What it wants from the Tower

A champion of the Noonsworn to win the climb and carry the Ember of Aubade to the Noon Seat, where it will be hung, and the sun will
rise whole.

## How it works against the others

Openly. The Noonsworn fight the Court of Dusk on sight and in the Ring, and patrol the night roads to protect travellers from it; they
call the Dune Throne's claim heresy and challenge its champions every midsummer; they argue with the Underforge over the Deep's light,
and Opaline breaks it out of the crystals for them; and they court the Hourkeepers, whose measurements they need and whose caution they
cannot stand.

## Ties

Friends of the Truce and the Hourkeepers; rivals of the Dune Throne and the Underforge; enemies of the Court of Dusk.
