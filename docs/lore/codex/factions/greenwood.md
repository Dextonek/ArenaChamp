# The Greenwood Moot

*The council of the Walking Wood: the Tower drinks the Dial, and its roots must be cut.* · [Factions](README.md) ·
[The Walking Wood](../realms/wood.md) · [Wood folk](../peoples/wood.md) · [Briarkin](../peoples/briar.md)

## What it is

The Walking Wood began to walk on the day of the Fall, as if to look for the sun, and the Moot is the people who live in it and around
it: wardens, woodcutters, herders, hunters, hive-keepers, a moth priestess and two changeling children. They believe the Tower drinks
the Dial, and that every floor it fills is a realm it has taken. They argue among themselves about almost everything, and agree about
the Tower.

## Ranks

The Moot has as few ranks as it can manage.
- **The Speakers**, one from each village and each Briarkin court, who meet under the oldest tree in the Antler Glades.
- **The Grove Warden**, who keeps the Wood from trampling the Dial and holds its first gate in the Climb: [Fenna](../heroes/fenna.md).
- **The Huntmaster**, who leads the Hunt of the Moot: [Arden](../heroes/arden.md).
- **The Sworn**, every Wood folk who has sworn the Moot's oath and grown horns.
- **The Courts**, the Briarkin, who sit in the Moot when they feel like it: [Sirix](../heroes/sirix.md) for the Hive, [Vela](../heroes/vela.md)
  for the Moth, [Pim and Pom](../heroes/pim-and-pom.md) for nobody in particular.
- **The hill speakers**, the shepherds of the high pastures: [Bo](../heroes/bo.md) speaks for the hills, slowly.
- **The edge**: the woodcutters and charcoal burners of the villages, who keep the Wood from walking over them: [Hank](../heroes/hank.md).

## Rites and symbols

- **The sigil:** a leaf with a spiral vein, crowned with antlers. Leaf green, bark cream, wax amber and pine.
- **The Moot's oath:** "I keep the Wood, and the Wood keeps me." Sworn at the oldest tree with a hand on its bark. Horns follow.
- **The Debt of Trees:** a felled tree is paid for with a planted one; the Moot keeps the count.
- **The Horn-Call:** the Moot is summoned by horns blown from glade to glade, and the Wood stands still while it meets.
- **The Shedding:** the sworn shed their antlers in spring and plant the tips.

## Secrets

- **The swapped children.** Since the first winter after the Fall some Briarkin children are born two for one. The courts left the
  first pair, Pim and Pom, on Grandma Ironknit's doorstep because they were afraid. The Moot has counted forty pairs since. It has
  told no one outside the Wood.
- **The horns.** The Moot does not know why the oath grows horns, and some of the Speakers fear that the Wood is not keeping its
  people but taking them, slowly.
- **The roots.** The Moot's champions have found, in the Climb's Wood, a root that goes up into the Tower's wall. Hank cut a piece of
  it and brought it home. It is still alive in a pot in Fenna's garden.

## What it wants from the Tower

The Tower's roots cut, and the Wood left to walk where it likes, as long as it does not walk over the villages.

## How it works against the others

Slowly, and by blocking. The Moot closes the Wood's paths to the Underforge's timber buyers, leaves the Court's buried dead to the
Hunt, and sends its champions to the Ring to fight about the Wood's edge. It allies with the Windward (rain for the Wood, timber for the
ships) and lets the Hourkeepers' surveyors through on the Frostfall road in return for maps of where the Wood has walked.

## Ties

Friends of the Windward; enemies of the Court of Dusk (whose dead are buried in the Wood's soil) and of the Underforge (whose axes and
mines eat the Wood's edge).
