# The Court of Dusk

*The nobility of the long evening: the dusk is the Dial's true age, and the Noon Seat must stay empty.* · [Factions](README.md) ·
[Gloaming](../realms/gloaming.md) · [Gloaming folk](../peoples/gloaming.md)

## What it is

When the sun fell its light left Gloaming first and came back last, and in that long twilight the Court of Dusk came into its own. The
Court believes the dusk is the Dial's true age and that the night has as good a claim as the day. Its people are not monsters, or not
all of them: they keep the night roads safe, bury the dead and shelter whatever the daylight hunts. But a new sun would end them, and
they will do a great deal to keep the Noon Seat empty. They keep the letter of the Truce and very little of its spirit.

## Ranks

- **The Countess**, head of the Court: [Vesper](../heroes/vesper.md), since its founding.
- **The Houses**, the noble families of Gloaming, each with a seat at the Court and a debt to the Countess: the Countess's house, the
  Mirror house ([Silvane](../heroes/silvane.md)), the house of Bells, and nine others.
- **The Lord Below**, by the Ossuary Pact: the lord of the Long Dead, [Mortis](../heroes/mortis.md), an ally not a subject.
- **The Knife**, the Countess's guard and the Court's assassin: [Nyx](../heroes/nyx.md), first of the Cast.
- **The Harlequins**, the Court's players and spies, masked: [Mum](../heroes/mum.md) is their first.
- **The Wardens of the Night Roads**, the Court's soldiers, with violet lanterns.
- **The Tolerated**: those the Court shelters without trusting, [Kohaku](../heroes/kohaku.md) of the Fox court, and the watcher on the
  eaves, [Corbel](../heroes/corbel.md).

## Rites and symbols

- **The sigil:** a crescent moon held in a hand mirror. Crimson, violet, mirror silver and bone.
- **The Long Evening** at midsummer, and the Naming every night before the dark mirror.
- **The Masque of Houses** at midwinter, where debts are traded and nobody's face is their own.
- **The Mirror Oath:** a member of the Court swears by looking into Silvane's first mirror; a lie told before it is seen.

## Secrets

- **The debt.** The Court's long nights were bought on credit from the Tally. The interest has been paid for a hundred years; the
  principal never. This year Mr. Ledger has written about it.
- **The sleepers.** The two bell-ringers who never woke on the night road in year 86 were not killed by wolves. The Court knows who did
  it, and it was not Vesper.
- **The other plan.** If the Noon Seat cannot be kept empty, the Court would rather a king sat in it than a sun: hence the alliance with
  the Throne. If that fails, the Court has a third plan, which only Vesper and Silvane know, and which involves Silvane's first mirror
  and the sun's last light inside it.
- **The Cast.** The Court keeps the Cast because nobody else would; it does not know what they are, and is a little afraid of them.

## What it wants from the Tower

The Noon Seat kept empty: no new sun to burn them, and their roads, their dead and their secrets kept. And to see what is in the Seat
before anyone else.

## How it works against the others

Quietly. The Court spies (through mirrors and harlequins), borrows (from the Tally), allies (with the Throne), and duels only when it is
sure; it keeps the Noonsworn's caravans watched on the night roads and Orren's Ember in its sights; it buries its overflow dead in the
Wood's soil, which the Moot hates, and lets Mortis raise them; and it sends champions to the Ring who keep every rule to the letter,
which drives the Arbiter to distraction.

## Ties

Friends, of convenience, of the Dune Throne; rivals of the Truce; enemies of the Noonsworn, the Greenwood Moot and the Hourkeepers.
