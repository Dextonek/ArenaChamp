# The factions of the Dial

*Eight factions and the loners: who wants what from the sun, and how they work against each other.* · [Codex](../README.md)

A faction is not a realm or a people. It is a belief about the sun and an organisation that acts on it; most draw on one realm and
one or two peoples, and every one of them sends champions to the Ring.

## How they stand

- **Friends:** the Truce with the Hourkeepers, the Windward, the Noonsworn and the Underforge; the Noonsworn with the Hourkeepers; the
  Court with the Throne (of convenience); the Moot with the Windward; the Underforge with the Throne.
- **Rivals:** the Truce and the Court; the Noonsworn with the Throne and with the Underforge; the Windward and the Throne.
- **Enemies:** the Noonsworn and the Court; the Court and the Moot; the Court and the Hourkeepers; the Moot and the Underforge.

The great question that divides them is the Noon Seat: hang the sun again (the Noonsworn, the Hourkeepers), crown a king in it (the
Throne), keep it empty (the Court), keep the sun below (the Underforge), cut the Tower down (the Moot), or whatever works (the
Windward). The Truce holds them all to the Ring.

## The hundredth summer

This year every faction has made its move: the Noonsworn have their Sunbearer; the Court has climbed; the Throne has three new
champions; the Underforge is drilling toward the Hall of Noon from below; the Moot is following the Wood east; the Windward have
hired out half their champions; the Hourkeepers have sent Thessa to the Tear. The Truce has doubled the Arbiter's watch.
