# The loners

*Five heroes who belong to no faction, each for a reason of their own.* · [Factions](README.md)

Five heroes belong to no faction. Oblivion wants no world at all. Sir Whiskers has served too many courts to join another. The Great
Zambini has joined all of them, in writing. Ostrel carries every faction's dead, and so belongs to none. Asenet, the Uncrowned, was
driven out of the Sands and is welcome nowhere else.

## Oblivion, the Unmaker

[Oblivion](../heroes/oblivion.md) came through the Tear on the day of the Fall. It is the only one of the Unmade that has walked the Dial,
and it wants nothing any faction can give: it wants the Dial unmade, hour by hour, door by door. It holds the War's first gate in the Climb, the one thing on the Dial it has truly unmade. Every faction is its enemy, and three heroes (Wick, Tempo and Thessa) have sworn to send it back.

## Sir Whiskers, the Lynx Duelist

[Sir Whiskers](../heroes/sir-whiskers.md) is a knight of the Ninefold, and the Ninefold swear to courts and causes and move on. He has
served many courts and belongs to none now; he duels for whoever is most interesting, and lands on his feet.

## The Great Zambini, the Illusionist

[The Great Zambini](../heroes/the-great-zambini.md) has sworn loyalty to every court on the Dial, in writing, more than once. He is a
Briarkin of no court, and a Briarkin keeps every promise to the letter: the letters say "for the length of the performance".

## Ostrel, the Ferryman

[Ostrel](../heroes/ostrel.md) ferries the dead of every faction to rest, and so belongs to none. He is one of the Long Dead, and the Long
Dead keep their purposes absolutely: his is to carry, not to take sides.

## Asenet, the Uncrowned

[Asenet](../heroes/asenet.md) was queen of the Sands when the sun fell. Driven out by her brother, she cursed the crown and has walked the
Dial since, welcome nowhere. She slipped Kessa's chain in the spring of year 99.

## What the loners want from the Tower

Nothing in common. Oblivion wants the Tower unmade with everything else. Sir Whiskers wants to see what is at the top. Zambini wants an
audience at the Noon Seat. Ostrel wants to know where the dead of the floors go. Asenet wants the crown to stop.
