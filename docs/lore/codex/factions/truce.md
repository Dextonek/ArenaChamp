# The Truce of the Ring

*The keepers of the Ring: every quarrel settled one against one, and no army on the Dial.* · [Factions](README.md) ·
[The Tower and the Ring](../realms/tower.md)

## What it is

The Truce is the oath the realms swore at the Tower's foot in year 2, and the people who keep it. It serves the rules, not a side.
It believes a quarrel settled in the Ring is a war that was not fought, and it is the reason the Dial still has realms instead of
one long battlefield. Every realm needs it, and none of them quite trusts it.

## Ranks

- **The Arbiter** calls the rules in the Ring and judges every foul. One at a time; [Referee Whistler](../heroes/referee-whistler.md)
  since year 2.
- **The Officers of the Ring**, each with a charge: the Tithe-Keeper (the Tally; [Mr. Ledger](../heroes/mr-ledger.md)), the Doorwarden
  (the Hundred Doors; [Wick](../heroes/wick.md)), the Feastmistress (the Truce Table; [Madame Cleaver](../heroes/madame-cleaver.md)),
  the Mountain (the first ring; [Big Yoko](../heroes/big-yoko.md)), the Saltwarden (the edge; [Morwen](../heroes/morwen.md)), the
  Chainwarden (the Gaol of Oaths; [Kessa](../heroes/kessa.md)), and the First Courier ([Pip](../heroes/pip.md)).
- **The Wardens**, who ride the Field, keep order at the Ringside and escort champions on the roads; about three hundred, from every
  people.
- **The couriers**, who carry challenges by hand.
- **The champions**, who are not members: any hero who fights in the Ring is under the Truce while they fight and while they eat.

## Rites and symbols

- **The sigil:** a ring with the Tower's shadow standing across it. Black, ivory, oxblood and gold, colours no realm claims.
- **The Reading:** at every midsummer the five clauses are read aloud in the Ring by the Arbiter, and every officer answers "kept".
- **The Whistle:** a duel begins and ends with the Arbiter's silver horn; the sound that both armies of the Banners still stop for.
- **The Table:** every duel ends at the Truce Table, winner and loser side by side. Whoever eats there may not strike in anger
  outside the Ring.
- **The Tally:** every duel and every debt it settles, written in the great red book chained to Mr. Ledger's wrist.
- **The Salt:** the Ring's edge salted every dawn.

## Secrets

- **The blank page.** The duel in year 90 that opened the Tower's gate is not in the Tally. The page is blank. Mr. Ledger says he wrote
  it. Whistler says he called it. Nobody remembers the champion. The Truce has told no one outside its officers.
- **The Doors were already there.** The Hundred Doors under the stands were found in year 3, already hung and locked. The Truce keeps
  them as if it built them. Wick knows where most of them go.
- **The Gaol is one short.** Asenet's escape in year 99 is public; what is not is that she left by a door that was not on Kessa's map.
- **The Tally holds every realm.** Every realm on the Dial owes the Tally something. If the Truce called every debt at once, it would
  own the Dial. It never has.

## What it wants from the Tower

Every quarrel in the Ring and no army on the Dial; the Tower's gate opened only to a champion who earned it. If anyone reaches the
Noon Seat, the Truce's great promise is kept. Its officers know that after that the Truce may no longer be needed, and do not agree on
what to do about it.

## How it works against the others

It does not take sides; it takes notes. It watches the Court of Dusk's champions twice; it holds the Court's debt and has started to
ask about the principal; it keeps Morwen's salt between Mortis's dead and the crowd; it pays the Underforge for iron and the Windward
for ships; and it keeps the Hourkeepers' observatory out of every war in return for a copy of every record. Its weapon is that every
faction needs it more than it needs any faction.

## Ties

Friends of the Hourkeepers (who keep its records), the Windward (whose ships carry its champions), the Noonsworn (who believe in oaths)
and the Underforge (who forged its iron); rivals of the Court of Dusk.
