# The Windward

*The readers of the sky and sailors of the sea: mend the sky, by whoever can, and keep the roads open.* · [Factions](README.md) ·
[The Drowned Coast](../realms/coast.md) · [Coast folk](../peoples/coast.md)

## What it is

The Windward believe in what works. Since the Fall the weather has no master, the sea stands higher and the winds keep no seasons, and
someone has to read them and sail anyway. They are sailors, dancers, storm callers, augurs, aeronauts and kite fliers, and the keeper of
a sea temple, and they are the most practical people on the Dial. They will back any faction that can mend the sky.

## Ranks

The Windward is a guild more than an order.
- **The Council of Captains** at Saltreach, where the Windward speak loudest.
- **The Augur**, who reads the sky from the Weather Spire: [Barry Cloudsley](../heroes/barry-cloudsley.md).
- **The Storm-Callers**, the Spires' order of weather-workers: [Kaia](../heroes/kaia.md) is their best and least obedient.
- **The Tide Dancers** of the sea temple: [Marin](../heroes/marin.md).
- **The Spire-born**, the wind-walkers: [Zephyr](../heroes/zephyr.md).
- **The Fliers**: aeronauts and kite-fliers who carry messages between the Spires and map the sky: [Professor Puff](../heroes/professor-puff.md),
  [Lian](../heroes/lian.md).
- **The Keeper** of the sea temple, who is not a priest: [Gus](../heroes/gus.md).

## Rites and symbols

- **The sigil:** a wave curling up into a cloud. Sea blue, storm blue, cloud and wind mint.
- **The Forecast:** called from the Weather Spire every eight seconds, as long as any forecast now holds; every Windward ship carries a
  copy, which is out of date by the time it is read.
- **The Tide Line:** a thin blue tattoo round the forearm for each great flood lived through.
- **The Whistle:** the Windward whistle to call the wind, never indoors.
- **Kite Day**, when the whole coast flies.

## Secrets

- **The Spires.** The Windward have found, in the Spires, rooms with doors that open onto the sky, and machinery nobody understands. They
  have told the Hourkeepers, who asked them to keep it quiet.
- **The weather can be bought.** Kaia's storm-callers have sold rain to the Wood and storms to the Throne's enemies. The Council pretends
  not to know.
- **The Sands' storms.** Barry Cloudsley believes the storms that come off the Sands are not natural, and that someone in Tamerit is
  calling them. He cannot prove it.

## What it wants from the Tower

The sky mended, by whoever can do it, and the roads of the sea and the wind kept open. The Windward have no plan for the Noon Seat; they
have a list of what each faction would do with it, and odds.

## How it works against the others

By being needed. The Windward carry the Truce's champions and the Noonsworn's bells, the Underforge's iron and the Wood's timber, and can
stop carrying any of them. They quarrel with the Dune Throne over the Sands' storms and the Sands' king, who would like the sea to belong
to someone; they hire their champions out; and they back whoever is winning.

## Ties

Friends of the Truce and the Greenwood Moot; rivals of the Dune Throne.
