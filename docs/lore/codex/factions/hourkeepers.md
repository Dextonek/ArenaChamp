# The Hourkeepers

*The keepers of time and record: nothing can be mended that is not first understood.* · [Factions](README.md) ·
[Frostfall](../realms/frostfall.md) · [Northerners](../peoples/north.md)

## What it is

The Hourkeepers believe nothing can be mended that is not first understood, and that the Dial's real wound is time: since the Tower's
shadow stopped, the hours slip. They are keepers of clocks, calligraphers, star readers, a fate-spinner, a warden of the cold, a dancer of
the northern light and a seamstress of the sky. They lean toward the Noonsworn, because the shadow only falls when a sun hangs above the
Tower, but they want to know why it fell before anyone hangs it again.

## Ranks

- **The Council of the Lens**, seven Hourkeepers and two from Hearthmere, who govern Frostfall.
- **The Keepers**, each with a charge: the great clock ([Tempo](../heroes/tempo.md)), the library's script ([Shiori](../heroes/shiori.md)),
  the northern sky ([Lyra](../heroes/lyra.md)), the threads of lives ([Grandma Ironknit](../heroes/grandma-ironknit.md)), the cold that
  keeps the library ([Isolde](../heroes/isolde.md)), the northern light ([Saga](../heroes/saga.md)), and the Tear ([Thessa](../heroes/thessa.md)).
- **The Readers**, scholars of the observatory and the library, several hundred.
- **The Surveyors**, who walk the Dial measuring the slip, the Wood's walking and the Tear.
- **The Correspondents**, Hourkeepers in every court, who send everything back to Frostfall.

## Rites and symbols

- **The sigil:** an hourglass set in a four-point star. Dial chartreuse, ink blue, night violet and chart parchment.
- **The Slip-sheet**, published every month: the measure of how far the hours have slipped.
- **The Longest Night:** one true thing written down.
- **The Lens:** every new Reader looks once through the Great Lens at the Nail, and writes what they see.

## Secrets

- **The Noon Folio.** The Hourkeepers measured the sun paling for three hundred years before the Fall and told nobody. They told the
  Noonsworn's Elders in year 60.
- **The aging.** They believe that if the hours run true again the Noon-touched may age all at once. They have told the Noonsworn's
  Elders and Auriel, and nobody else.
- **The floors are records.** The Hourkeepers believe the Climb's floors are not realms the Tower took but records it kept. They are not
  sure. They have a room at the observatory for the evidence.
- **Ironknit.** Grandma Ironknit is a Briarkin of the Spindle court. The Hourkeepers have always known.

## What it wants from the Tower

The hours running true again: a sun over the Tower, the Tear sewn shut, and a record of everything kept until then. First, to know why
it fell.

## How it works against the others

By knowing. The Hourkeepers keep the Truce's records and get its protection; tell the Noonsworn what they need to know and no more; oppose
the Court of Dusk, which would keep the hour stopped at dusk for ever; and send Thessa to sew the Tear while Tempo and Wick hunt the thing
that came through it.

## Ties

Friends of the Truce and the Noonsworn; enemies of the Court of Dusk.
