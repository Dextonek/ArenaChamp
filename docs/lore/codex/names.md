# Names

*Every new name in the codex, checked against the game and against other games and stories.* · [Codex](README.md)

`python pipeline/tools/lore-codex.py --check` reads the first table: every name in it must be listed once and must not already be a name in the game
(a hero, a title, a spell or passive, a faction, a place, a person or ruler of the Climb). It also searches every codex page and LORE.md for a list of
well-known names from other games, books, films and shows (`FRANCHISE` in `pipeline/tools/lore-page.py`). **That search is a list, not a proof**: it
holds the names of the big franchises a player is likely to know. The coined words below were also looked up by hand where noted. Every name here is a
first pick, for the user to change.

**Confirmed by the user (2026-11-16):** the Story book's names: the Aumbry, Senmere, Iselm, Linnet, the Market of Noon, the Dawnless,
The Long Noon.

## Kept from the game, and meetings on purpose

Names the game already had, which the codex uses as they are: the Dial, the Tower, the Gnomon, the Noonfall, the Ring, the Truce of the Ring, the
Tear, the Heart of Noon, Ash and Ember, the Sun Warden, the Sun Court, the Noon Seat, the Hall of Noon, the Cinder Pit, the Hundred Doors, the Truce
Table, the Tally-house, the Gaol of Oaths, Tamerit, the Spires, the Ember Caldera, the Antler Glades, the Night Temple, the Mirror Hall, the Brass
Road, the Lamp Road, the Glass Flats, the Prism Stair, the Tide (the Coast folk's faith, and the tide of the Climb's Sea floors), the Hymn of Noon, the Ember of Aubade, the Noon-touched, the obol, every hero, title, spell and Climb name.

The check also knows the twenty new heroes' spell names (from `pipeline/designs/kit-*.md`). One meets a festival here on purpose: Nebet's
ultimate, The Weighing, is named for the rite of the Sands it comes from.

Meetings on purpose, which the check allows: Cantor and the Blind Cantor; Ostrel the Ferryman and the Deep's Ferryman; Itzel the Obsidian Herald and the
War's Herald; the Scarab Colossus and the Scarab King (mother and son);
the Last Marshal (a Climb ruler) and the azure's commander in the War of the Banners, who are the same man.

## Looked up

Searched on the web for a clash with a well-known game, book or film (2026-11-15): Briarkin, Kilnborn, Fallborn, Harrowmark, Sallowmouth, the Ninefold,
the Underbough. None is a franchise's name. Harrowmark turns up only as a mercenary company in one small fan-made setting online (and is close to
Harrowhark, a character in a novel series: a different word); "under-bough" is an old English word for a low branch. Ordinary English words (the Cast,
the Restless, the Measure) and plain compounds (Mole-folk, Coast folk) were not searched.

Searched on the web for the Story book's names (2026-11-15): the Aumbry, Senmere, Iselm, the Dawnless, The Long Noon. None is a game's,
book's or show's name (the nearest: *The Long High Noon*, a Western novel). Linnet is an ordinary English word, a bird; a minor character in one
fantasy series and one small game carry it. Nerys was the first pick for Iselm's daughter and was dropped: it is a famous character's name.
