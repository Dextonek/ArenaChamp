# The Field of Banners

*The old battlefield at the eighth hour, where the war over the sun was fought and lost by everyone.* · [Realms](README.md) ·
[The Ninefold](../peoples/ninefold.md) · [The Climb: the War](../climb/war.md)

## The land

Between the southern hills and the Walking Wood, west of the Tower, lies a wide, low country of rolling fields, small rivers and
willow. Before the Fall it was **Harrowmark**, the kingdom of the middle: rich, green and proud, with orchards, a castle at Harrow
and a road that every traveller to the Tower took. Its crimson kings grew fat on the tolls.

In the first years after the Fall the war over the sun's pieces was fought here, crimson against azure, until the fields were mud,
the orchards stumps, the castle a shell and the king who began it buried in the field where he fell. The kingdom did not survive the
war. The land is still there, and it has come back green, but it belongs to no realm now. The Truce holds it, and keeps it empty.

## Places

- **The Fallen Battlefield.** The Ninth Field, where the last battle was fought in year 2: a wide shallow valley of grass over mud,
  with rusted iron still coming up in every ploughing. The Truce's arena is here. On misty mornings people see the banners.
- **The Barrow of Harrow.** A long mound beside the Banner Road where King Orlan is buried with his household guard, under a crimson
  stone the Truce does not let anyone move.
- **Harrow.** The ruined castle and the town round it, half abandoned; the last of Harrowmark's people live in what is left, farm the
  edges and sell bread and beer to travellers on the Banner Road.
- **The Keep of Nine.** On a ridge above the Field's northern edge, the old keep of the Ninefold knights: grey towers, a long hall
  with nine hundred hooks for swords and eighty of them in use when the Fall came, eleven now. [Sir Whiskers](../heroes/sir-whiskers.md)
  is of this keep.
- **The old camps.** The orchard of Harrow, where both armies wintered in the Winter of Mud and [Madame Cleaver](../heroes/madame-cleaver.md)
  fed them from one fire. A white linen flag still hangs from a dead apple tree there.
- **The Banner Road.** From the Wood's edge across the Field to the Tower: the road the armies marched, now the road the Wood folk drive
  sheep and timber to the Ring.

## Rulers and law

No one. The Field Rider, a rider to the Truce sworn the same day, keeps the Field as common ground: no realm may hold it, no army may camp on it, and the Truce's
wardens ride it twice a year. Harrow governs itself by a town meeting under the castle gate; the Keep of Nine governs itself by its
oath. Disputes go to the Ring, which is close.

## Faith and festivals

Every faith on the Dial fought here and none stayed. What the people of the Field keep is **the Laying**: on the anniversary of the
Ninth Field, everyone in Harrow walks the battlefield and lays a stone wherever the plough has turned up bone. The Ninefold hold the
**Count** at their keep on the same night, calling the names of every knight who spent a life on the Field.

## Food and trade

Bread, beer, apples from the replanted orchards, mutton from the Wood's drovers. The Field sells little but passage, horses and
the scrap iron that comes up in the furrows, which the Underforge buys by weight.

## Old wounds

All of them. The War of the Banners began here and ended here; every realm left dead in this mud. Aubade lost a third of its
paladins; the Keep of Nine lost sixty knights in a day; Harrowmark lost itself. The Last Marshal, who led the azure, was never found.
And in the Climb, the War is still being fought: the fifth chapter holds the Field as it was in the last days, the two armies still
fighting, because nobody inside has told them it is over ([the War](../climb/war.md)).

## Its stake in the Tower

None, and it is tired of being asked. Harrow would like the Tower to stop drawing armies past its door. The Keep of Nine sends knights
to the Ring for whoever is most interesting.
