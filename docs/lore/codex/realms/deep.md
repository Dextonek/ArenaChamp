# The Deep

*The halls under the southern hills, at the sixth and seventh hours, lit by crystals and by the fallen sun's fire.* ·
[Realms](README.md) · [Kilnborn](../peoples/kiln.md) · [Mole-folk](../peoples/mole.md) · [The Underforge](../factions/underforge.md) ·
[The Climb: the Deep](../climb/deep.md)

## The land

Above ground, the southern hills are a rolling country of grey stone, gorse and goat farms, with the river Sefet rising among them.
Below them is the Deep: nine centuries of halls, galleries, shafts and seams, dug by the mole-folk first and the hill people after,
going down further than anyone has measured. Before the Fall it was dark except for lamps. On the day of the Fall the sun's light ran
down through the rock into the crystals of the halls (the Shining), and since then the Deep has been lit from within: violet, rose
and amber crystals glow in the walls, brightest near the Tower side and fading toward the far halls.

The sun's fire came up too. A cold crater in the hills filled with fire from below and became the **Ember Caldera**, and round it
the hill people built the great forge and became the Kilnborn.

## Places

- **The Ember Caldera.** A crater half a mile across, its floor a lake of slow fire that cools at the edges into black glass. Heat
  shimmers over it; the hills round it are warm all winter. The Truce's arena of the Ember Caldera is on a shelf of black glass above
  the fire. [Itzel](../heroes/itzel.md) speaks for the fire.
- **The great forge.** Built into the Caldera's rim, a hall of iron and stone with nine furnaces fed by the fire below, where
  [Brannoc](../heroes/brannoc.md) works. The Ring's iron and the Gaol's chains were made here.
- **The great lift.** A shaft from the hill tops down to the forge halls, with a lift of iron cages worked by water and counterweights,
  rebuilt by [Ada](../heroes/ada.md), whose workshop hangs above it.
- **The masons' halls.** Galleries of carved limestone where the Underforge's masons work, [Bram](../heroes/bram.md) among them, with
  Gerald. Every pillar is carved with the mark of its maker.
- **The Prism Stair.** A stair of three thousand steps cut down through a vein of clear crystal, where the light breaks into colours
  as you descend. At its top, a glass-cutter's house where [Opaline](../heroes/opaline.md) lives.
- **The Crystal Cavern.** A hall of great crystals the size of trees, the brightest place in the Deep. The Truce's arena is here.
- **The Lamp Road.** The mole-folk's great tunnel from the Deep's mouth down to the crystal halls, which they keep open.
  [Mabel](../heroes/mabel.md) keeps it.
- **The deepest seams.** Where the mole-folk's warrens are: round rooms, warm rock, tunnels exactly as wide as a mole-folk's shoulders.
- **The black water.** A lake under the Deep where the light does not reach, which nobody fishes and the far-hall people pole across.
  [Hesk](../heroes/hesk.md)'s first golem walked into it.
- **The far halls.** The galleries furthest from the Tower, where the light never came and the Caldera's fire never reached: still
  dark, lit by candles and fireflies, home of Kilnborn who kept their old colour. The Climb's fourth chapter holds them as they were.

## Rulers and law

The Kilnborn are governed by the **Hall of Marks**, the masters of the great forge, the masons, the miners and the engineers, each
house's head sitting under its maker's mark; the mole-folk by their **Old Mothers**, each ruling a warren. The two peoples meet when
something concerns both and settle disputes by the oldest seam-song or the best-made argument. The law is a builder's law: what is
made belongs to its maker, a wall that falls is its builder's fault, a seam belongs to whoever opened it, and no one cuts a crystal
another has claimed.

## Faith and festivals

The Anvil of the Kilnborn and the Dark of the mole-folk ([Kilnborn](../peoples/kiln.md), [Mole-folk](../peoples/mole.md)). Festivals:
- **The Kindling**, on midsummer: the Kilnborn celebrate the day the fire came up, with every furnace fed and a night of beer in the
  forge halls. The mole-folk stay home; to them it is the day the dark was ruined.
- **The Marking**, when a Kilnborn finishes their first great piece and cuts their mark on it.
- **The Quiet**, the mole-folk's day of silence, when nobody digs and everyone listens to the rock.

## Food and trade

Mushrooms, root bread, goat, cave cheese, cave fish, grubs (the mole-folk), and a thick dark beer. The Deep sells iron, copper, brass,
tools, weapons, clockwork, crystals, black glass, powder and golems; it buys food from the Sands and the Field, timber from the Wood
(at the Wood's expense, the Wood says), and glass and gold from the Sands on the Brass Road.

## Old wounds

- **The Wood's edge.** The Underforge's mines and axes eat the Wood's edge, and the Wood's roots break into the Deep's halls. In year
  47 they nearly went to war.
- **The light.** The Noonsworn want every drop of the Deep's crystal light carried up into the sky; the miners say it belongs to those
  who dug down to it. Opaline breaks it out; Mabel objects with her claws.
- **The Shining.** To the mole-folk, the light was a loss. Their old dark is gone, and their young want the light for themselves.

## Its stake in the Tower

The Underforge wants the Heart of Noon left below and its fire brought up through the rock: a sun underground, lighting the Deep
and warming the Dial. It forges the Truce's iron, trades with the Throne and quarrels with the Noonsworn, and its champions climb to
reach the Heart before anyone moves it.
