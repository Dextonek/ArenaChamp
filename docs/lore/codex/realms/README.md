# The realms of the Dial

*Nine realms round a Tower, laid out like the hours on a sundial.* · [Codex](../README.md)

<!-- map -->

The map is the Dial seen from above: the realms sit at their hours round the Tower, each in its ruling faction's colour, and the
dark line is the Tower's shadow, stopped at the hour of the Fall. North is at the top, where the pole star stands over Frostfall.

## The nine

| Realm | Hour | Who lives there | Who rules | What it wants from the Tower |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [The Tower and the Ring](tower.md) | the centre | every people, under the Truce | the Truce of the Ring | every quarrel settled in the Ring, and the gate opened only to a champion who earned it |
| [Frostfall](frostfall.md) | 12 | Northerners; Briarkin on the road south | the Hourkeepers' Council of the Lens | to know why the sun fell, then a sun over the Tower and the Tear sewn shut |
| [Aubade](aubade.md) | 1 | Aubade folk | the Council of Bells (the Noonsworn in all but name) | the sun hung again, by the Ember of Aubade |
| [The Sands](sands.md) | 3 to 4 | Dune folk; Ninefold lions at the gate | the Dune Throne | the Noon Seat for their king |
| [The Drowned Coast](coast.md) | 5 | Coast folk | the harbour captains and the Windward | the sky mended, by whoever can |
| [The Deep](deep.md) | 6 to 7 | Kilnborn, mole-folk | the Underforge's Hall of Marks and the Old Mothers | the Heart left below, its fire brought up through the rock |
| [The Field of Banners](banners.md) | 8 | the last of Harrowmark; the Ninefold at their keep | nobody, under the Truce | nothing, and it is tired of being asked |
| [The Walking Wood](wood.md) | 9 to 10 | Wood folk, Briarkin | the Greenwood Moot | the Tower's roots cut |
| [Gloaming](gloaming.md) | 11 | Gloaming folk, the Long Dead, the Cast | the Court of Dusk | the Noon Seat kept empty |

## Roads between them

- **The Dawn Road**, Aubade to the Tower: the oldest paved road, white stone, a bell tower every ten miles.
- **The Brass Road**, the Deep to the Sands and on to the Tower: caravans every month since year 12, guarded by both.
- **The Frostfall road**, Frostfall south past the Wood's edge to the Ring: the Waymeet inn where it crosses the Dawn Road; Grandma
  Ironknit's cottage; the Moot's tollhouses, which move.
- **The night roads**, out of Gloaming east and south: patrolled by the Court's wardens, safe at night and not by day.
- **The Banner Road**, across the Field from the Wood to the Tower, through the old battlefield and past the Barrow of Harrow.
- **The sea lanes**, round the Dial by the Outer Sea, from the Coast to every realm that has a shore.
- **The Lamp Road**, the mole-folk's great tunnel from the Deep's mouth down to the crystal halls.

## The arenas

The Truce blesses fields in the realms where duels may be fought as in the Ring. They are the game's arenas: the Drowned Harbour
(the Coast), the Verdant Glade (the Wood), the Sandstone Canyon (the Sands), the Crystal Cavern (the Deep), the Fallen Battlefield
(the Field), Frostfall Lake (Frostfall), the Night Temple (Gloaming), the Sky Ruins (the Spires) and the Ember Caldera (the Deep).
