# The Drowned Coast

*The south-eastern shore at the fifth hour: the Drowned Harbour, the Lantern Coast, and the Spires standing in the sea.* ·
[Realms](README.md) · [Coast folk](../peoples/coast.md) · [The Windward](../factions/windward.md) · [The Climb: the Sea](../climb/sea.md)

## The land

The coast runs south-west from the mouth of the Sefet to the southern hills: a long shore of coves, headlands, salt marsh and orange
terraces, with a lighthouse on every point. Off it, a mile out to sea, stand **the Spires**: nine towers of a pale stone that is not
stone, rising straight from the water to heights that make sailors dizzy, broken at the tops, older than any record. Nobody built
them, as far as the Coast folk know. The Coast folk have lived in them since before they wrote things down.

On the day of the Fall the sun's heat lifted the sea, and the great port of **Sallowmouth** went under in an afternoon. The sea never
went back out. What was Sallowmouth is now the **Drowned Harbour**: a harbour city with water to its first-floor windows, its streets
canals, its cathedral of the sea a temple with its floor always wet. The new harbour towns stand on higher ground behind it.

## Places

- **The Drowned Harbour.** The old port, half under water: rooftops, bell towers and masts standing out of a green bay. Boats go
  through its streets. The Truce's arena is on its old fish-market square, which stands just above the high tide. Under it, cut into
  the rock below the old harbour wall, is the Gaol of Oaths.
- **The sea temple.** The great temple of the harbour, which [Marin](../heroes/marin.md) danced dry on the day of the Fall: a hall of
  pale pillars and sea-glass windows, its floor always wet, kept by [Gus](../heroes/gus.md). The Coast folk's nearest thing to a
  church, which Gus denies.
- **The Gaol of Oaths.** The Truce's prison for oath-breakers, in caverns under the drowned harbour wall that flood at the spring
  tides. Its chains were forged in the Ember Caldera. [Kessa](../heroes/kessa.md) is its warden. One prisoner has left without her
  leave.
- **Saltreach.** The largest of the new harbour towns, on a ridge behind the Drowned Harbour: rope walks, boatyards, a fish market, the
  Windward's council house. Gus was born here.
- **Tern's Landing.** A fishing town south along the coast, where [Morwen](../heroes/morwen.md) grew up in a cottage the sea later took.
- **The Lantern Coast.** The headlands south of Saltreach, a lighthouse on each, lit every night since the Night of Lanterns in year 23.
- **The Spires.** Nine towers in the sea. The **Weather Spire** (the highest) holds the weather post where [Barry
  Cloudsley](../heroes/barry-cloudsley.md) calls the forecast; the **Storm Spire** holds the storm-callers' tower where
  [Kaia](../heroes/kaia.md) trained; the **Kite Spire** the kite-post where [Lian](../heroes/lian.md) sends messages; and
  [Professor Puff](../heroes/professor-puff.md) moors his basket to whichever has room. The Sky Ruins, the Truce's arena, are on the
  broken top of the fourth.

## Rulers and law

The Coast has no king. Each town is ruled by its **harbour captains**, elected by its boat crews every spring; the towns meet as
the **Council of Captains** at Saltreach when something concerns them all, and the Windward ([faction](../factions/windward.md))
speaks there more loudly than anyone. The law is the law of the sea: what floats is shared, what sinks is lost, a rescue is owed, a
wreck belongs to whoever reaches it first. Quarrels are settled at sea, or by the Truce.

## Faith and festivals

The Tide ([Coast folk](../peoples/coast.md)). Festivals:
- **The Drowning**, on midsummer: the Coast folk row out over Sallowmouth at noon and drop flowers on the water for the drowned.
- **The Lights**, on the anniversary of the Night of Lanterns: every lamp on the coast lit at once.
- **Kite Day**, in the windy month: the whole coast flies kites from the Spires; Lian's dragon leads.
- **The Floor-Washing**, every new moon at the sea temple, which Gus insists is not a festival.

## Food and trade

Fish soup with saffron, grilled sardines, mussels, seaweed bread, salt cod, oranges, and a fierce orange spirit. The Coast sells
fish, salt, rope, sails, boats, ship time, weather forecasts and glass floats; it buys almost everything else, and carries it for
everyone.

## Old wounds

- **Sallowmouth.** Half the city drowned in an afternoon. Every family has a name on the Drowning's list, and the drowned are still
  there, in a way: on the first floors of the Climb, the drowned dockers are the Coast's own dead ([the Sea](../climb/sea.md)).
- **The masterless weather.** Since the Fall the storms come when they like, and the Coast loses ships every year. The Coast blames
  the Sands, whose storms come off the dunes.
- **The Gaol.** The Coast folk never wanted a prison under their harbour and still resent it; the Truce pays rent.

## Its stake in the Tower

The Windward want the sky mended, by whoever can do it, and the roads of the sea and wind kept open. The Coast would back any
faction that could give it back its seasons. Its champions climb for the faction that pays them, and for the view.
