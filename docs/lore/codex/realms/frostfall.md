# Frostfall

*The white north at the twelfth hour, under the pole star.* · [Realms](README.md) · [Northerners](../peoples/north.md) ·
[The Hourkeepers](../factions/hourkeepers.md)

## The land

Frostfall is the top of the Dial: a high, cold land of moors, birch, black rock and ice, round a great lake that is frozen nine months
of the year. Above it the **Nail**, the pole star, stands still while the other stars turn round it. The light comes last to the north
and leaves it first in winter; in midwinter Frostfall has four hours of thin day, and in midsummer it has almost no night. Since the
Fall the winters have grown longer, and the Hourkeepers can say by exactly how much.

To the east the moors run down to the ice coast and the Outer Sea, where seals and whales come and the Northerners fish through the
ice. To the south the land softens into reindeer pastures and pine, then the Frostfall road runs down past the Walking Wood toward
the Ring. To the west are the night roads to Gloaming.

## Places

- **Frostfall Lake.** A round lake two miles across, so clear that on a still day you can see the bottom twenty fathoms down. It
  freezes from the edges inward in autumn and the ice is as clear as the water. The Truce's arena of Frostfall Lake is on its
  ice. The lake-born families live in shore villages round it and keep the ice.
- **The observatory.** On a spur of black rock at the lake's north shore: the stone ring of the first star-watchers, now the heart of
  a great round building of black stone and brass, with a dome that opens to the sky and the **Great Lens** that measured the sun's
  paling for three hundred years. The Hourkeepers' great clock, kept by [Tempo](../heroes/tempo.md), stands in its central hall.
- **The frozen library.** Under the lake's ice at its western end, a library built in caverns that the cold keeps dry and still: the
  Rime Library, where nothing written can fade. Its shelves run for miles. The Noon Folio is here, and the record of every year since
  the Long Count began. [Isolde](../heroes/isolde.md) guards it; [Shiori](../heroes/shiori.md) writes in it.
- **Hearthmere.** The town at the lake's south end: timber houses with turf roofs, a market under a long roof, the tutors' college,
  the lens grinders' street. Most Northerners who are not Hourkeepers live here.
- **The aurora terraces.** Stone terraces on the east shore where [Saga](../heroes/saga.md) and the dancers of the northern light
  dance on the nights the sky is lit, and the Hourkeepers read the hour from the curtains when the stars are hidden.
- **The reindeer moors.** The great pastures south of the lake, home to the herding families and the Wardens of the Winter.
- **The Waymeet.** Far south, where the Frostfall road crosses the Dawn Road: the oldest inn on the Dial, kept by the same Northern
  family for six hundred years.

## Rulers and law

Frostfall is governed by the **Council of the Lens**: seven Hourkeepers chosen by the observatory and the library, and two from
Hearthmere chosen by the town, who meet in the dome under the open sky. Its law is written, numbered and cross-referenced, and every
judgement must say which earlier judgement it follows. Disputes are settled by finding out who is right, and the finding is entered
in the library. Crime is rare; argument is common and enjoyed.

## Faith and festivals

The faith is the Measure ([Northerners](../peoples/north.md)). Festivals:
- **The Longest Night**, at midwinter: every Northerner writes down one true thing learned that year, and the pages are bound into
  the year's book in the library.
- **The Nail's Turning**, at the spring equinox: the Hourkeepers publish the year's Slip-sheet and the whole town checks its clocks.
- **The Lake Opening**, when the ice breaks: the lake-born race boats across the first open water.
- **The Lights**, on the first aurora of winter: Saga dances, and everyone stays up.

## Food and trade

Fish from under the ice, reindeer, barley bread, cloudberries, smoked salmon, butter, and the black salt tea. Frostfall sells
lenses, paper, ink, clocks, wool, and its scholars' time; it buys grain from the Field and Aubade, wax and honey from the Wood,
brass from the Deep and wine, reluctantly, from Gloaming.

## Old wounds

- **The Long Dusk** of year 0 reached Frostfall too: a winter that did not end until the next summer, in which the herds starved.
- **Gloaming.** The Court of Dusk would keep the hour stopped at dusk, and the Hourkeepers will not allow it. The night roads between
  them are cold in every sense.
- **Brannoc.** In year 40 the smith of the Underforge followed Isolde north and set up his forge on the lake's shore; the ice
  cracked, the lake-born were furious, and Isolde froze his forge solid to send him home. The shore still has a black scar where it
  stood.

## Its stake in the Tower

Frostfall wants the hours to run true again, which means a sun over the Tower; but it wants to know why the sun fell before anyone
hangs it, and it wants the Tear sewn shut. Its champions climb to measure. The observatory has a room set aside for what they bring
back.
