# The Sands

*The desert of the Dune Throne, at the third and fourth hours, where the sand is still warm.* · [Realms](README.md) ·
[Dune folk](../peoples/dune.md) · [The Dune Throne](../factions/dune.md) · [The Climb: the Sands](../climb/sands.md)

## The land

The Sands fill the south-east of the Dial between Aubade's cliffs and the southern hills: a great desert of red and ochre dunes,
rock plateaus, dry canyons and one river. The river **Sefet** comes down from the southern hills and runs east to the sea through a
green ribbon of fields and palms a mile wide, and on it stands every town of the Sands that matters. North of the river lie the
**Glass Flats**, where the sun's heat came down on the day of the Fall and the sand turned to a plain of glass that still rings
underfoot; south of it the **Storm Canyon**, a maze of red rock where the sandstorms of the Dial are born.

The Sands say the sun fell into their dunes, and dig a hand's depth into any dune at midnight and the sand is warm. The Hourkeepers
have measured it and agree it is warm, and decline to say why.

## Places

- **Tamerit.** The city of the Throne, on both banks of the Sefet where the Brass Road crosses it: white and ochre walls, palm courts,
  a market of a thousand awnings, and at its heart the **Throne Hall**, a hall of sandstone pillars round the Dune Throne itself, a
  seat of gold and turquoise on a step of glass from the Flats. [Sefu](../heroes/sefu.md) rules from it.
- **The gate of Tamerit.** The great gate in the city's north wall, guarded by a lion of sandstone and gold the size of a house, that
  wakes when its rider sits on its back. Its rider is [Ramet](../heroes/ramet.md), captain of the gate, a lion of the Ninefold.
- **The Valley of Lids.** West of Tamerit, a dry valley of tombs cut into the cliffs, where six centuries of kings lie with their
  hearts weighed. The veiled priestesses of the tombs guarded it with their eyes; [Lithea](../heroes/lithea.md) is the last of them.
  [Nebet](../heroes/nebet.md) holds the scale in its Hall of Weighing.
- **The embalmers' house.** In Tamerit's old quarter, a windowless house of white plaster that smells of resin, where the Throne's
  dead are kept whole. [Vitriol](../heroes/vitriol.md) is its master.
- **The Glass Flats.** A plain of green-black glass forty miles across, crazed with cracks, hot by day and singing in the wind at
  night. Mirages stand on it all day. [Sahar](../heroes/sahar.md) was born on its edge. Zephyr and Ramet race across it every year.
- **The Storm Canyon.** The red maze south of the river, where the storms rise and the caravan towns huddle in its walls. The Truce's
  arena of the Sandstone Canyon is here. So is the Climb's third chapter, as the Tower holds it.
- **The oases of the Brass Road.** A line of wells from the Deep to Tamerit: Sefet's Spring, the Three Palms, Copper Well.

## Rulers and law

The **Dune Throne** rules: the king, his court and the old offices of the Sands (the embalmer, the judge of the dead, the captain of
the gate, the priestesses of the tombs, the archers of the mirage). The king's word is law, and the law is old and written on the
Throne Hall's pillars. Under the king the river towns govern themselves through their councils of houses, and the caravans through
their masters. Since the curse on the crown the court has grown watchful: a king who wears the crown too long falls, and every
courtier counts the days.

## Faith and festivals

The faith of the Father ([Dune folk](../peoples/dune.md)): the sun came home to the Sands and will rise again in a king. Festivals:
- **The Homecoming**, on midsummer: the Dune folk do not mourn the Fall; they celebrate the day the Father came home, with fires on
  the dunes all night. The other realms find this tasteless.
- **The Weighing**, when a king or a great noble dies: the heart is weighed against a feather in the Valley of Lids.
- **The Night of Wells**, in the hottest week: every family draws water for a stranger.

## Food and trade

Flatbread, dates, lentils, goat, river fish, pomegranates, honey cakes, and the sour barley drink. The Sands sell glass (and
especially the lightning glass of the Flats), linen, turquoise, gold, perfume, embalming oils and the services of their archers.
They buy iron and tools from the Deep on the Brass Road, timber from the Coast, and grain when the river is low.

## Old wounds

- **Asenet.** The Sands blamed their queen for the Fall because she ruled that year. In year 1 her brother took the throne, and on the
  night she was driven out she cursed the crown: it will never stay on one head. It has passed from head to head ever since, and
  those who wore it too long have fallen. [Asenet](../heroes/asenet.md) is loose again.
- **The crimson banner.** The Throne joined King Orlan of Harrowmark in the first year of the War of the Banners, for a share of the
  sun's pieces, and withdrew when Sefu took the crown. Aubade has not forgotten.
- **The storms.** The Sands' storms come off the dunes and out to sea, and the Coast folk blame the Sands for them.

## Its stake in the Tower

The Throne wants the Noon Seat for its king: Sefu as the new sun, if the climb can be won. It sends its champions to the Ring for
the right to climb, allies with the Court of Dusk (better a king in the Seat than a sun, the Court says), and trades with the
Underforge for the iron to do it.
