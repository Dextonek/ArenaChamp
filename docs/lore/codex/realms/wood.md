# The Walking Wood

*The great western forest at the ninth and tenth hours, which began to walk on the day of the Fall.* · [Realms](README.md) ·
[Wood folk](../peoples/wood.md) · [Briarkin](../peoples/briar.md) · [The Greenwood Moot](../factions/greenwood.md) ·
[The Climb: the Wood](../climb/wood.md)

## The land

The Walking Wood covers the west of the Dial: oak, beech and pine, hollow hive-oaks the size of towers, glades of antlered trees,
mossy springs, standing stones, and above it to the south-west the high pastures where the forest meets the hills. Before the Fall it
stood still, and was called the Still Wood. On the day of the Fall it pulled up its roots and began to walk east, toward the Tower,
as if it had felt the sun go and set off to find it.

It walks slowly: a grove may move a hundred yards in a month, a stand of pines a mile in a year. It walks at night, mostly. The paths
inside it change, the villages at its edge are built on skids, and a farmer may wake to find an oak grove in the barley. In a hundred
years the Wood's eastern edge has moved about thirty miles toward the Tower. It is moving faster this year.

## Places

- **The Antler Glades.** The heart of the forest, a ring of glades among trees whose branches grow like antlers, where the **Moot**
  meets under the oldest tree. The Truce's arena of the Verdant Glade is the Moot's outer glade.
- **The hive-trees.** Hollow oaks the size of towers, full of amber wax and wasps, in the south of the Wood; the Briarkin's Hive court
  lives in them, and [Sirix](../heroes/sirix.md) is their warden.
- **The high pastures.** The hill grazing above the Wood's south-western edge, where the shepherds' summer towns are and
  [Bo](../heroes/bo.md) herds.
- **The charcoal clearings.** Clearings where the burners make charcoal for Aubade's foundry and the Deep's forges, and pay for every
  tree with a planting.
- **The Wood's edge.** The ring of villages on skids that follow the forest east: Hankshold (named, to his embarrassment, for
  [Hank](../heroes/hank.md)), Third Glade, Wayside. They have no champion of their own; Hank fights for them.
- **The Underbough.** The deep glades and hollow hills of the Briarkin, where the folk's paths do not go, and which are never in the
  same place two years running.
- **Grandma Ironknit's cottage.** On the Frostfall road at the Wood's north-eastern edge: a stone cottage with a spinning wheel in the
  window, where the changeling twins were left.
- **The night meadows.** Open meadows in the north of the Wood where the Moth court flies at dusk and [Vela](../heroes/vela.md) lays
  the Wood to sleep, with paper lanterns in the trees.

## Rulers and law

The **Greenwood Moot** ([faction](../factions/greenwood.md)) rules by meeting: every village sends a speaker, the Briarkin courts
send whoever they like, and the Moot decides by talking until nobody objects. The **Hunt of the Moot**, led by
[Arden](../heroes/arden.md), keeps the paths. The law is the forest's: a felled tree is paid for with a planted one, a hive is not
robbed in the first frost, a stag is not hunted in the year it sheds, the folk keep to the paths and the fae to the glades (the old
treaty, which no longer quite works because the glades move).

## Faith and festivals

The Root of the Wood folk and the Telling of the Briarkin ([Wood folk](../peoples/wood.md), [Briarkin](../peoples/briar.md)).
Festivals:
- **The First Step**, on midsummer: the anniversary of the day the Wood began to walk. The villages move house that night, all of
  them, to wherever the forest has gone.
- **The Shedding**, in early spring: the sworn shed their antlers (Hank saws his) and plant the tips.
- **The Hive-Wake**, when the wasps first fly: honey cakes left at the hive-trees.
- **Hollowtide**, at the first frost: food left out for the Briarkin, and nobody whistles after dark.

## Food and trade

Bread, cheese, honey, mushrooms, venison, mutton, nuts, hard cider, and the Briarkin's flower wine, which is not to be drunk by anyone
who wants to remember the evening. The Wood sells timber, charcoal, wax, honey, amber, wool, furs and mutton; it buys salt, iron
tools (reluctantly), cloth and paper.

## Old wounds

- **The Underforge.** Its mines and axes eat the Wood's edge, and the Wood's roots break into its halls. Year 47 was nearly a war.
- **The Court of Dusk.** The Court buries the dead it cannot fit under Gloaming in the Wood's soil, and Mortis raises them. The Moot
  hates it.
- **The hive-tree.** Professor Puff's gas floated one out of the Wood in year 55, by accident, and the swarm has not forgiven him.
- **The swapped children.** Since the first winter after the Fall some Briarkin children are born two for one. The courts are afraid,
  and the Moot does not know what to do about it.

## Its stake in the Tower

The Moot believes the Tower drinks the Dial and that every floor it fills is a realm it has taken; it wants the Tower's roots cut, and
the Wood left to walk where it likes, as long as it does not walk over the villages. Its champions climb to find the roots.
