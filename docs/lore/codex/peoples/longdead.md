# The Long Dead

*Not living. The bone-lords: the dead of the Dial whose will outlasted their flesh.* · [Peoples](README.md) ·
[Gloaming](../realms/gloaming.md) · [The Court of Dusk](../factions/dusk.md)

## Origin

Most of the dead lie down. A few do not. When someone dies with a purpose unfinished, and the purpose is strong enough, and they
are buried where the dark is deep and the bone is kept, they sometimes **get up**. The Gloaming folk have known this for as long as
they have buried their dead under the castle-city, and about 450 years before the Fall they made it a pact: the ossuaries under
Gloaming were sealed with their first lord inside, and the dead who got up there were given halls, honour and work in return for
keeping the city's dead quiet. Those who get up are **the Long Dead**. They are not many: a few hundred on the whole Dial.

## Homeland

The ossuaries under [Gloaming](../realms/gloaming.md): halls of bone older than the Court of Dusk, stacked skull on skull, lit by
teal soulfire. Long Dead who have taken up other work live elsewhere: one keeps a ledger under the Ring, one poles a boat on rivers
of light.

## Body

A Long Dead looks like what they were, with the flesh **drawn back**. The newest are almost living: skin gone the colour and
texture of old vellum, tight on the bone, sunken at the cheeks and eyes, the knuckles, the jaw and the brow showing bone through
it. The oldest are bare bone, a skull with a jaw that still moves, and wear hoods and masks out of courtesy. All have **eyes of
cold fire** in the sockets, teal in the ossuaries, ember-red in those who keep debts, pale blue in those who ferry. They do not
breathe, do not age, and are very still when they are not moving.

## In the figure

On the pack's human body, thin.
- **Can be:** vellum-grey skin drawn tight, bone showing at the brow, cheek, jaw and knuckles (a face and hand texture, with small
  built bone pieces); a skull face under a hood (Mortis now); hands of bone; glowing eyes; robes, ledgers, staffs, poles, coins.
- **Cannot be:** gore or rot (the Long Dead are dry and clean; the art bible's "never gritty" holds); a skeleton with no body
  (the figure must keep the pack's human outline under robes).
- **Reads as:** a thin dark figure with a pale bony face and two points of cold light. Beside the Gloaming folk, who also have
  lights in the face, the Long Dead are bonier and their light is in sockets, not in eyes.

## Lifespan and the Fall

They do not end unless their purpose does, or someone breaks every bone. A Long Dead whose purpose is finished lies down, gladly
or not. The Fall touched them as restlessness. Since the sun fell, the dead of the whole Dial have been uneasy, and many more have
tried to get up than ever before: the drowned of the Coast, the soldiers of the Banners. Most of these are not Long Dead. They
are **the Restless**: dead who got up without a will of their own, and walk because something keeps them walking. The Long Dead
despise them, pity them, and (in [Mortis](../heroes/mortis.md)'s case) employ them.

## Culture

A people of memory and contract. Every Long Dead knows exactly why they got up, and keeps a **Reason**, written on a strip of
linen inside the ribs, which they will show only to another of the Long Dead. They keep their promises absolutely, because a
broken promise would end them. They are courteous, patient, dry in their humour, and very hard to hurry. They do not eat; they
like to be offered food anyway. Their crafts are bone, carving, archive and law: half the contracts on the Dial are drawn up by
someone who has been dead for two hundred years.

## Faith

The Long Dead keep the **Unfinished**. They believe every life is a sentence, and that most end mid-word; the Long Dead are the
ones who insisted on finishing theirs. They honour the Vigil of the Gloaming folk and are honoured in it. They have a quiet
quarrel with [Ostrel](../heroes/ostrel.md), the ferryman, who is one of them and holds that the kindest end of any sentence is a
full stop.

## Names

They keep the names they died with, and add the year: Mortis (an ossuary name, chosen; his living name is on a stone he does not
visit), Ledger, Ostrel. They are addressed as "the Long" by the living, which they find funny.

## How they fight

With bone and patience. The Long Dead raise walls and spires of bone, call the Restless, and do not tire or bleed. They are
slow, they burn, and salt troubles them: a line of salt is a line most of them cannot cross, which is why the Truce hired
[Morwen](../heroes/morwen.md).

## Heroes and Climb people

- **In the game:** [Mortis](../heroes/mortis.md), and [Mr. Ledger](../heroes/mr-ledger.md), whose figure moves from an old man to

- **New:** [Ostrel](../heroes/ostrel.md).
- **On the Climb:** none; the Climb's walking dead (the drowned dockers, the ashen soldiers, the Drowned Admiral) are the Restless,
  not the Long Dead ([bestiary](../climb/bestiary.md)).

## Ties to other peoples

Bound to the Gloaming folk; respected and feared by the Dune folk, whose embalmers keep their kings whole for a different reason;
avoided by the Briarkin; hated by [Anka](../heroes/anka.md), who will not stay buried.
