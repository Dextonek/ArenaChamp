# The Fallborn

*Not born. The beings the Noonfall made or let in: the Embers, the Cast, and the Unmade.* · [Peoples](README.md) ·
[The Tower](../realms/tower.md)

The Fallborn are not a people in the way the others are. They have no homeland, no children and no common tongue. What they share
is that **none of them existed before the Fall**. The Hourkeepers group them because they must be written down somewhere, and the
Fallborn have accepted the name, because it is true. There are three kinds, and they could hardly be less alike.

## The Embers

**Origin.** When the sun cracked and fell, sparks of it scattered over the Dial. Most went out. A few did not, and became
something. The Embers are those sparks, grown into beings with a will. The greatest are **Ash and Ember**, the sun's two last
embers, who burn in the Cinder Pit beneath the Tower. The first to walk was [Anka](../heroes/anka.md): a spark that fell into
the sea off the Drowned Coast and walked out of the surf the next morning as a woman. Others are smaller: the Hourkeepers count
nine Embers on the Dial, most of them no larger than a candle-flame that thinks.

**Body.** An Ember takes a shape from the first thing it sees, and Anka's first sight was a dancer on the shore. Embers look
like people of flame and gold: skin warm and bright, glowing faintly at its edges (the line of the jaw, the fingers, the hair's
roots); hair like flame, dark red to gold; eyes like coals. They are warm to the touch, hot when angry, and they burn when they
die, and rise from what they leave on the ground.

<!-- team -->**In the figure.** On the pack's human body: warm skin with an emissive rim at the edges, flame-coloured hair, feathers and fans
as costume. **Cannot be:** a body made of fire (the figure keeps a solid skin; the fire is in the spells), true wings.<!-- /team -->

**Life and fire.** An Ember does not age: each fire makes it new, about twenty in a human's eyes. It can be put out only by
cold, water held long enough, or by being kept from its ashes. It remembers every life.

## The Cast

**Origin.** At the moment the sun fell, its last light threw every shadow on the Dial long and hard across the ground. A few of
those shadows did not come back when the light changed. They peeled off their walls and walked. These are **the Cast**. The
first and best known is [Nyx](../heroes/nyx.md), thrown across the wall of the Night Temple in Gloaming. The Court of Dusk took
the Cast in, since nobody else would, and most of them serve it.

**Body.** A Cast looks like the shadow of a person standing up: a slim human figure of matte, light-drinking black, with a thin
hard edge of violet where light catches it, and no face at all; a pale slit where eyes would be, mint or white. They cast no
shadow of their own. They fade when they are left alone, and come back sharper when someone looks for them.

<!-- team -->**In the figure.** On the pack's human body, as Nyx is: matte black, a violet edge, a hood, no face. **Cannot be:** flat (the
figure must keep the body's volume), a smoke without legs.<!-- /team -->

**Life.** They do not age. They are hurt by full light and by being forgotten, and nobody knows what kills them. The Hourkeepers
count perhaps forty.

## The Unmade

**Origin.** When the sun fell, it tore the sky above the Tower: the Tear, black with a burning pink edge. Through it, on the day of
the Fall, came something the Dial has no word for. The Hourkeepers call it **the Unmaker**; the codex uses its name in the Ring,
[Oblivion](../heroes/oblivion.md). It is the only one of its kind that has walked the Dial. Smaller things come through the Tear
at times, rifts that drift and pull and close, and the Hourkeepers call all of them the Unmade.

**Body.** It wears a shape, not a body: a towering knight in black plate, cracked with glowing pink rifts, a horned helm with one
burning slit, a ragged cloak whose hem breaks into floating shards. What is inside the plate, if anything, nobody has seen.

<!-- team -->**In the figure.** On the pack's human body, as Oblivion is. **Cannot be:** shapeless, or a creature with more limbs; the
pack's clips need the knight.<!-- /team -->

**Life.** Not born, not ageing; whatever it touches stops being able to end. Wick, Tempo and Thessa have sworn to send it back.

## Culture, faith, names

The Fallborn share none. Anka keeps the dances of the shore and the faith of the Noonsworn, who call her the sun's promise. The
Cast keep the Court's manners and the Vigil, and take names given them by whoever first named them. The Unmaker keeps nothing, and
its name is the Ring's. The only thing the Fallborn have in common is a question every one of them is asked, and none can answer:
what were you, before?

## How they fight

The Embers burn and rise; the Cast fade and strike from the fade; the Unmade pull, and what is pulled in does not always come
back.

## Ties to other peoples

The Embers are loved by the Aubade folk and feared by the mole-folk and the Long Dead. The Cast belong to the Gloaming folk. The
Unmade has no ties, and every people on the Dial has a reason to want it gone.
