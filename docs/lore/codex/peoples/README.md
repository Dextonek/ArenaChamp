# The peoples of the Dial

Twelve peoples live on the Dial. <!-- team -->The user, 2026-11-14, of the heroes: "they seem all the same, like human". <!-- /team -->So the Dial is not
only human: **four peoples are human**, each with its own build, dress, skin, hair and marks; **three are human peoples the
Fall changed** (eyes that shine, ash-grey skin, horns); and **five are not human at all**. Back to the [codex](../README.md).

Every hero belongs to exactly one people (the lore check holds to it). On the game's Play card the people stands between the
faction and the home: *Noonsworn · Aubade folk · from Aubade*.

## The one style, and what a people can be in it

All heroes are built the one way (`pipeline/designs/style-v4/README.md`): the pack's realistic human body on the pack's human
skeleton, moving only with the pack's clips. So a people is shown by what can be built on that body:
- **can:** skin colour and texture (ash-grey, vellum, frost-pale, bark-brown, a dapple), eyes (one colour, eyeshine, cold fire),
  marks on the skin (tattoos, seams, star lines), horns and antlers, ears, an animal's head on the human head (a cat's, a mole's),
  bone showing at the face and hands, wings or chitin carried as a cloak, a stone skin, hair, dress and props;
- **cannot:** a body of another shape (four legs, digitigrade legs, a tail that moves, a body with no legs), real flight, anything
  cartoon-goofy (`docs/ART_BIBLE.md` §1), and gore.

The pack has one face per sex, so faces alone never tell a people apart. Skin, eyes, horns, heads and marks do.

## The twelve

| People | Kind | Home | In one line |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Aubade folk](aubade.md) | human | Aubade | Tall, golden-brown, long hair bound with gold thread; the dawn line on the sworn. They rang the sun up, and lost their choir when it fell |
| [Dune folk](dune.md) | human | The Sands | Lean, dark-skinned, kohl to the temples, layered linen. The oldest court; they call the sun the Father |
| [Gloaming folk](gloaming.md) | changed | Gloaming | Pale, blue-grey, eyes that shine back light. A century of dusk made them a people of the night |
| [Northerners](north.md) | human | Frostfall | Broad, dark-skinned, white-braided, star lines on jaw and hands; the lake-born are frost-pale. They measure everything |
| [Coast folk](coast.md) | human | The Drowned Coast | Wiry, sea-tanned, salt-bleached, tide lines on the forearms. They read the weather and sail anyway |
| [Kilnborn](kiln.md) | changed | The Deep | Short, broad, ash-grey skin, ember seams at the forge. The sun's heat came up through their rock |
| [Wood folk](wood.md) | changed | The Walking Wood | Ruddy, moss-eyed; the sworn grow horns. They follow a forest that walks |
| [Mole-folk](mole.md) | other | The deepest seams | A mole's head, velvet fur, gilded claws. The Deep's first people, who call themselves the Loam |
| [The Ninefold](ninefold.md) | other | The Keep of Nine | A great cat's head, lynx or lion; nine lives. Knights of every court, fewer every year |
| [Briarkin](briar.md) | other | The Underbough | Pointed ears, eyes of one colour, a court's sign. The fae of the Wood, who fade when forgotten |
| [The Long Dead](longdead.md) | other | The ossuaries | Vellum skin on bone, eyes of cold fire. The dead whose purpose would not lie down |
| [The Fallborn](fallborn.md) | other | Where the Fall made them | Three kinds: the Embers (sparks of the sun), the Cast (shadows that walked), the Unmade (what came through the Tear) |

## Where the peoples meet

The Ring is the one place every people meets on equal terms, under the Truce. Elsewhere they keep to their realms, with these
crossings: the Brass Road (Kilnborn, mole-folk, Dune folk), the Frostfall road (Northerners, Briarkin, Wood folk), the night roads
(Gloaming folk, the Cast, the Long Dead), the sea (Coast folk and everyone who can pay), and the Field of Banners, where all of them
once fought and the Ninefold still keep their old keep.
