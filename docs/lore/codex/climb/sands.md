# The Sands (floors 21 to 30)

*The Climb's third chapter: the Storm Canyon in the great storm of the year after the Fall.* · [The Climb](README.md) ·
[The Sands](../realms/sands.md) · [Dune folk](../peoples/dune.md)

## The land

The twenty-first floor opens in a red canyon under a sky the colour of rust. It is the Storm Canyon in the great storm of year 1, the
storm the Sands still call the Throne-Wind because it blew on the night Sefu took the crown: storms cross the canyon on a line, dust
wraiths walk in the haze, the sand is marked where something burrows, and a caravan that never reached Tamerit is still trying to.
The chapter's floors are the same desert in its other corners: the oasis, the watch fort, the fallen obelisk, the tombs, the ruined
temple, the travellers' court, the wagon line, the raiders' camp, the sun shrine.

## Its people

All of them Dune folk, who say the storm caught them:
- **The Star-Reader**, the keeper of all ten floors, reads the storms in the stars, which she learned at Frostfall as a girl; she can
  see the stars through the dust when nobody else can.
- **The Storm-Counter**, the camp's quest giver, counts the storms: how many have crossed, how many are to come. The number never
  gets smaller.
- **The Beacon-Keeper**, out in the land, keeps the watch fires burning on the canyon rim so the caravan can find its way.
- **The Silk-Merchant** (odd floors), the last caravan's master, sells its silks and wants it brought home.
- **The Glass-Seller** (even floors) sells lightning glass from the Flats, the glass a storm leaves where it strikes the sand.

## Its rulers and enemies

The **Scarab Colossus**, a giantess in the beetle's shell, rules the twenty-first floor from under the sand, and burrows; above her her son the Scarab King, then the Dune
Colossus, the Brass Sphinx, the Glass Serpent, the Mirage Lord, the Sand Tyrant, the Red Jackal and the Sun-Eater ([rulers](rulers.md)).
Their packs are caravan raiders and slingers, Dune folk caught in the storm, who rob what passes; and the dust
wraiths; and the great ones, the Dune Mother, the Raider Chief, the Storm Count and the Glass Widow ([bestiary](bestiary.md)).

## The gate and the crown

[Sefu](../heroes/sefu.md), the Dune Sovereign, holds the chapter's first gate: it is his storm, the one that blew on the night he took the
crown, and in the Tower he still sits in judgement at its end. At the thirtieth floor, the crown, waits **the Heart of Noon**: the fallen sun's heart itself, or what the Tower holds of it. The gates of the floors above are held by other champions' echoes, of any realm, drawn to the Tower's gates from the Ring.

## What climbers say

The Dune Throne's champions say the Sands' chapter proves the sun came home to them: its crown is the Heart. The Noonsworn say it
proves the Heart is in the Tower and must be carried up. [Vesper](../heroes/vesper.md) fought a duel on the twenty-first floor and
says the storm reminded her of home.
