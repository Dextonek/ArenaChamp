# The rulers of the Climb

*The worst thing each realm had, floor by floor, and the crowns that are the sun's own.* · [The Climb](README.md) ·
[Bestiary](bestiary.md)

Each floor of the Climb has a ruler who must fall before its gate opens. The handmade first floor of each chapter has its own; the
generated floors have theirs; the tenth floor of each chapter is a crown, a piece of the fallen sun. Their stories, as the floors'
people tell them.

## The Sea

- **The Drowned Admiral (floors 1 and 2).** Admiral of Sallowmouth's fleet, who on the morning of the Fall ordered every ship to stay
  at anchor to ride out the strange tide, and went down with all of them. His flagship's wreck lies across the harbour mouth; he
  fights with its anchor and its cannon, and calls the tide. He still believes he gave the right order. (The floor's oldest code calls
  him the Drowned Captain.)
- **The Salt King (3).** The master of the salt pans north of the harbour, who sold salt by weight and cheated every buyer by a
  fingertip. The sea took his pans and made him its own: crusted white, he weighs climbers now.
- **Mother of Wrecks (4).** A wreckers' matriarch who lit false lights on the headlands to bring ships onto the rocks. The water came
  in the morning after her best night. She lights them still.
- **The Tide Judge (5).** The harbour's magistrate, who judged the drowned debtors on the quay steps at low tide, and set the tide as
  their sentence. Now the tide is his.
- **The Pearl Tyrant (6).** A pearl merchant who owned every diver on the coast and the debts of their families. He drowned holding
  the strongbox. The Pale Diver serves him still.
- **The Bell Below (7).** The harbour's old bell, which hung in the sea temple's tower before Campana cast the new one, cracked and
  thrown into the sea. It rings underwater, and something answers.
- **The Lantern Leviathan (8).** A great fish of the deep sea that followed the lamps into the harbour on the morning the water rose,
  with a lantern of its own growing from its head.
- **The Gull Queen (9).** The keeper of the harbour's gulls, who fed them from her hand for forty years, and whom they would not leave
  when she drowned. They have grown large.
- **The Sun Warden (10, the crown).** The keeper of the sun's post at the Tower's top, who kept it for longer than any record, received
  the choir every noon for four hundred years, and did not come down when the sun fell. He wears gold and white and a great dial, and
  his dial still strikes. Whether he is a man, a Long Dead, a Fallborn or something the Tower made, nobody knows. He meets climbers as
  if they were late.

## The Wood

- **The Elder Root (11).** The heart of the Walking Wood as the Tower holds it: a trunk too wide to see round, roots that arch, a
  glowing core, three roots that feed it. The Moot say it is one of the Tower's own roots, come up through the forest to drink.
- **The Moss Warden (12).** The first warden of the Moot, who swore its oath before it had a name and grew horns of moss; in the Tower
  he guards the Wood from everyone, including the folk.
- **The Thorn Mother (13).** A Briarkin of the old courts who wove thorn hedges round the Underbough to keep the folk out, and in the
  Tower weaves them round everything.
- **The Antler King (14).** A great stag-crowned lord of the Wood, the Stag-Crowned's elder, who leads the bark hounds in the hunt.
- **The Hollow Elder (15).** The oldest of the walking trees, hollowed by lightning, with a light inside it that is not a fire.
- **The Briar Witch (16).** A Wood folk herbwoman who sold curses at the Wood's edge and was taken by her own briars in the first year.
- **The Lantern Root (17).** A root that grew a lantern of amber to lure the lost into the Wood's dark, and feeds on what comes.
- **The Grey Oak (18).** A dead oak that would not fall, grey as ash, and walks anyway; the Moot's oath-tree before the Fall.
- **The Mire Heart (19).** A bog at the Wood's lowest point that woke with the Wood, swallowing what walks across it.
- **Ash and Ember (20, the crown).** The fallen sun's two last embers, which fell into the Cinder Pit and burn there: two shapes of fire
  who watch each other. The greatest of the [Embers](../peoples/fallborn.md). In the Tower they are met in the Wood, which fears fire
  above everything.

## The Sands

- **The Scarab Colossus (21).** A beetle the size of a house that the great storm uncovered from under the Storm Canyon, which burrows,
  marks the sand and calls the storm. The Dune folk say it rolled the sun across the sky once. It is the mother of the Scarab King.
- **The Scarab King (22).** Its son, smaller, quicker and gilded, worshipped by the raiders as a god.
- **The Dune Colossus (23).** A giant of sand and stone that walks with the dunes, made, the tomb paintings say, by the first king to
  guard the Brass Road.
- **The Brass Sphinx (24).** A lion of brass with a woman's face, from the gate of a city the Sands buried long ago, who asks a riddle
  nobody has answered and fights everyone who tries.
- **The Glass Serpent (25).** A serpent of lightning glass from the Flats, born the day the sun's heat struck the sand.
- **The Mirage Lord (26).** A prince of the Throne who walked onto the Glass Flats to be alone and became a mirage of himself.
  [Sahar](../heroes/sahar.md) has heard of him from his grandmother.
- **The Sand Tyrant (27).** A caravan master of the old days who sold water by the cup in a drought, and drowned in sand.
- **The Red Jackal (28).** The Raider Chief's master, a bandit lord of the Storm Canyon, who rode for the crimson banner and took his
  share.
- **The Sun-Eater (29).** A priest of the Father who tried to swallow a piece of the fallen sun to become it, and burns from inside.
- **The Heart of Noon (30, the crown).** The fallen sun's heart, or what the Tower holds of it: a sphere of white fire in a hall of black
  glass, whose light is the day. The Hourkeepers will not call it Fallborn, because it was there first.

## The Deep

- **The Prism Mother (31).** A crystal the size of a hall that woke when the others went out, and turns its beams in the dark to find
  them. She is what the Deep's light becomes with nowhere to go.
- **The Hollow Queen (32).** An Old Mother of the mole-folk who dug too deep alone, and came back hollow.
- **The Lamp Warden (33).** The keeper of the far halls' lamps, who swore they would never go out, and went out with them.
- **The Glass Abbot (34).** The head of a small order of glass-cutters in the far halls, who cut his own eyes into crystal to see in
  the dark.
- **Mother of Echoes (35).** A voice in the galleries that answer every step twice, which answers a third time.
- **The Seam King (36).** A Kilnborn mine-lord who owned the violet seam and worked his miners in the dark until the seam went out.
- **The Blind Cantor (37).** One of the hundred: a bass of the choir who sang the Hymn on the day of the Fall and was blinded by the
  sun's cracking, and did not come down the Tower, but came out somewhere else. [Cantor](../heroes/cantor.md) does not know he is here.
- **The Gem Tyrant (38).** A crystal-merchant who hoarded the Deep's light and was buried by it.
- **The Drowned Lantern (39).** A lamp that fell into the black water and kept burning under it, and now walks out of it.
- **The Sun Warden (40, the crown).** The keeper of the sun's post, met a second time; he remembers the first.

## The War

- **The Last Marshal (41).** The knight of Harrowmark who refused King Orlan and led the Azure League; never found after the Ninth
  Field. In the Tower he guards both banners: the guard marches at his call, the line burns, and at the end he fights alone on a horse
  of smoke.
- **The Iron Marshal (42).** The crimson's marshal, Orlan's cousin, who held the siege line through the Winter of Mud.
- **The Siege Lord (43).** An Underforge engineer who built the crimson's siege engines for pay, and stayed to see them fire.
- **The Ashen General (44).** The general of the ashen soldiers, a commander who died in the first battle and has led the dead since.
- **The Banner Knight (45).** The crimson's standard-bearer, who swore the banner would never touch the ground, and has held it up for
  a hundred years.
- **The Crow King (46).** A Gloaming lord who rode to the crimson banner with his house guard and his crows, for a share of the sun.
- **The Black Herald (47).** The crimson's herald, who carried King Orlan's last orders and has been carrying them since.
- **The War Drummer (48).** A drummer boy of the azure whose drum still beats the advance, and the dead get up when they hear it.
- **The Grey Captain (49).** An azure captain who deserted on the night before the Ninth Field and has regretted it on every floor.
- **Ash and Ember (50, the crown).** The fallen sun's two last embers again, at the top of the Climb, under the Noon Seat.
