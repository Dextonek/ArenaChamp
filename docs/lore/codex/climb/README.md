# The Climb

*Fifty floors inside the Tower, in five chapters, each a realm of the Dial as the Tower holds it.* · [Codex](../README.md) ·
[The Tower and the Ring](../realms/tower.md) · [The Tower and the Sun](../tower-and-sun.md)

## The gate opens

For ninety years after the Truce the Tower's gate stayed shut. In the ninetieth summer it opened for a champion of the Ring, and
the Tally's page for that duel has been blank ever since. Inside was a harbour drowned on a morning a century ago. Since then the
gate opens to every champion of the Ring who has earned it, and the Climb has grown floor by floor: fifty floors now, in five
chapters of ten.

## What the Climb is (a proposal)

*The Tower's own story has not been chosen ([The Tower and the Sun](../tower-and-sun.md)). This is the proposal the Climb was
written with, "a Tower that collects worlds", for the user to keep or change. The chapters below describe the floors as places, and
fit any answer.*

Each chapter holds a realm of the Dial as it was at one hour: **the Sea** as the Drowned Harbour on the morning the water came,
**the Wood** walking, **the Sands** and their storm, **the Deep** without light, and **the War** on the Field of Banners, where the
two armies still fight because nobody inside has told them it is over. The people of the floors were there at that hour, and they
still keep their posts. Each floor's ruler is the worst thing its realm had.

In each chapter one floor (the first) is made by hand; eight floors are its own parts rearranged and stronger; and the tenth is a
**crown**, where a piece of the fallen sun waits: the Sun Warden, who kept the sun's post; Ash and Ember, its two last sparks; the
Heart of Noon, its heart. The crowns are the sun's own, and the Raid's bosses.

## Who you meet

- **The keeper** of a chapter is the same person on all ten floors, the thread through it: they know the Tower better than anyone
  and do not know how they got there.
- **The quest givers**, one at the camp and one out in the land, have something they need done that they could not do in a hundred
  years.
- **The traders** take turns floor by floor, and will take obols, which nobody in the Tower should know about.
- **The rival**, a hero of the Ring who climbed ahead and wants the right to go higher, waits on some floors for a duel.
- **The gatekeeper**, a hero of the chapter's realm, holds the gate up at the end of the chapter's first floor. Four of them are
  heroes of that realm. At the War it is Oblivion, because that endless war is the one thing on the Dial it has truly unmade.
- **The ruler** of each floor, the worst thing its realm had ([rulers](rulers.md)), and its enemies ([bestiary](bestiary.md)).

## Above the fiftieth floor

**The Noon Seat**, where the sun rested at noon. It is empty. Whoever reaches it decides what hangs there. No champion has.

## What the factions say about the Climb

- **The Noonsworn** climb to carry the Ember to the Seat; Auriel and Orren have gone up as far as the Sea's crown.
- **The Court of Dusk** climbs to see the Seat first; Vesper has reached the Wood's crown.
- **The Greenwood Moot** climbs to find the Tower's roots, and believes the floors are realms the Tower has eaten.
- **The Underforge** climbs to reach the Heart before anyone moves it.
- **The Dune Throne** climbs for its king.
- **The Hourkeepers** climb to measure, and say the floors are records, not realms.
- **The Windward** climb for whoever pays, and for the view.
- **The Truce** keeps the gate, and counts who goes up and who comes down.
