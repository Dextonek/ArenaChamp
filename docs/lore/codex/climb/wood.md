# The Wood (floors 11 to 20)

*The Climb's second chapter: the Walking Wood in its first year of walking.* · [The Climb](README.md) ·
[The Walking Wood](../realms/wood.md) · [Wood folk](../peoples/wood.md) · [Briarkin](../peoples/briar.md)

## The land

The eleventh floor opens in a forest that is moving. The Walking Wood, as it was in the first year after the Fall, when it had only
just pulled up its roots: groves shift in the night, paths close behind you, and the ground tilts as the trees lean east. Wisps,
pale blue lights, drift through the trees, bound to the forest. At the Wood's heart stands the **Elder Root**, the oak the Tower keeps in an old man's shape, crowned with its roots, its roots arching from his hem, a glowing core in his staff, and three roots that feed it. The chapter's floors are the same forest in other moods:
the old oak, the mossy spring, the standing stones, the charcoal clearing, the bark den, the stag glade, the giants' glade, the
wayside shrine, the thicket.

## Its people

All of them Wood folk, and none of them sworn to the Moot, so none of them horned:
- **The Moss-Abbess**, the keeper of all ten floors, blows a great curled horn that keeps the Wood asleep long enough to cross it.
  The horn is an instrument, not her own: she never swore. She helped [Vela](../heroes/vela.md) lay the Wood to sleep, outside, and
  does not know Vela is still doing it alone.
- **The Path-Lighter**, the camp's quest giver, lights the spore lamps that mark the paths, and the paths move.
- **The Kiln-Mother**, out in the land, tends the charcoal fires, and pays for every tree with a planting even here.
- **The Amber-Seller** (odd floors) sells the Wood's amber, some of it with things inside that are still alive.
- **The Charm-Pedlar** (even floors) carries the Briarkin's charms on a frame over his head, and wears them against the Briarkin.

And on every floor the chapter's common folk walk at their work, and do not stop for climbers:
- **The Woodcutter**, a Wood man who never swore, so no horns, carries a bundle of sticks and his axe. He cuts only what has
  fallen: in this Wood a standing tree may walk off with the axe still in it.

## Its rulers and enemies

The **Elder Root** rules the eleventh floor; above it the Moss Warden, the Thorn Mother, the Antler King, the Hollow Elder, the Briar
Witch, the Lantern Root, the Grey Oak and the Mire Heart ([rulers](rulers.md)). Their packs are bark hounds, thorn sprites (Briarkin
who faded inside the Tower and half came back), moss giants and feeding roots, and their great ones: the Stag-Crowned, the Hollow Oak,
Mossjaw, Old Rootfoot, Grandmother Thorn, the Pale Stag, the Bark Widow and the Hollow Hunter ([bestiary](bestiary.md)).

## The gate and the crown

[Fenna](../heroes/fenna.md), the Grove Warden, holds the chapter's first gate: in the Tower as outside, she is the one who keeps the Wood
from trampling what it walks over, and roots rise as cover where she stands. At the twentieth floor, the crown, **Ash and Ember** burn:
the fallen sun's two last embers, and the Wood is afraid of them. The gates of the floors above are held by other champions' echoes, of any realm, drawn to the Tower's gates from the Ring.

## What climbers say

The Moot's champions climb the Wood looking for the Tower's roots, and say the Elder Root is one of them. The Hourkeepers say it is a
tree. [Hank](../heroes/hank.md) climbed it once with his axe, and came back without saying what he cut.
