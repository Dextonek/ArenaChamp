# The Deep (floors 31 to 40)

*The Climb's fourth chapter: the far halls of the Deep on the night their crystals went out.* · [The Climb](README.md) ·
[The Deep](../realms/deep.md) · [Kilnborn](../peoples/kiln.md) · [Mole-folk](../peoples/mole.md)

## The land

The thirty-first floor opens in the dark. It is the far halls of the Deep, where the sun's light never reached and the Caldera's fire
never came, on a night when even their few crystals went out: only crystals give light, and beyond their glow you see nothing, and
the lurkers hunt there. A great shard carried in the hand lights a few steps more; a crystal woken lights its hall for good. Three
great crystals in the first floor went out, and with them the way. The chapter's floors are the same dark in other galleries: the
first, second and third halls, the miners' camp, the ore yard, the flooded vault, the seam of violet crystal that went out one night,
galleries that answer every step twice, a cave beside water that has never seen the sun.

## Its people

Kilnborn of the far halls, who kept their old colour because the fire never reached them:
- **The Echo-Hermit**, the keeper of all ten floors, finds the way by echoes, a skill he learned from a mole-folk Old Mother when he
  was young; he clicks his tongue and listens, and walks where the sound comes back.
- **The Candle-Crowned**, the camp's quest giver, keeps the last candles burning in a crown on her head, and will not let them go out.
- **The Ferryman**, out in the land, poles the black water and brings the living back across it. He is not
  [Ostrel](../heroes/ostrel.md), who ferries the dead, and the two do not speak.
- **The Crystal-Seller** (odd floors) sells cut light, one shard at a time.
- **The Firefly-Seller** (even floors) sells living light in jars on a yoke, the only light in the Deep that is not a crystal.

## Its rulers and enemies

The **Prism Mother** rules the thirty-first floor, beams turning in the dark; above her the Hollow Queen, the Lamp Warden, the Glass
Abbot, Mother of Echoes, the Seam King, the Blind Cantor, the Gem Tyrant and the Drowned Lantern ([rulers](rulers.md)). Their packs
are shard mites, which come in swarms; crystal golems, slow and armoured; and lurkers, which wait in the dark and leap
([bestiary](bestiary.md)).

## The gate and the crown

[Opaline](../heroes/opaline.md), the Lightbreaker, holds the chapter's first gate: she breaks light out of crystal, and in the Tower she is
the only one who can make the far halls bright; the roof gives way where she fights. At the fortieth floor, the crown, **the Sun
Warden** waits again: the second time a climber meets him, and he remembers the first. The gates of the floors above are held by other champions' echoes, of any realm, drawn to the Tower's gates from the Ring.

## What climbers say

The Underforge's champions say the Deep's chapter is a warning: this is what the Deep would be without the fire. The mole-folk say
it is what the Deep was, and that it was not so bad. Everyone agrees the lurkers are worse than the dark.
