# The War (floors 41 to 50)

*The Climb's fifth chapter: the Field of Banners in the last days of the war, still being fought.* · [The Climb](README.md) ·
[The Field of Banners](../realms/banners.md)

## The land

The forty-first floor opens on a battlefield. It is the Field of Banners in the last days of the War, in the spring of year 2: the
siege line, the burned farm, the field hospital, the old fort, the ruined keep, the watchtower, the signal fire, the siege works.
Crimson and azure footmen, bowmen and knights fight across it, and ashen soldiers, the dead of both banners, fight beside them.
Arrows fall on a line. Nobody inside has told them the war is over, and they would not believe it: in the Tower, the Ninth Field has
not been fought yet. The chapter's floors are the same war, rearranged and harder each time.

## Its people

Harrowmark's children, the last people of the Field, and one Ninefold knight:
- **The Herald**, the keeper of all ten floors, speaks for both banners, and has been sent to arrange a truce on every floor; nobody
  has listened yet. (Not to be confused with [Itzel](../heroes/itzel.md), the Obsidian Herald of the Underforge.)
- **The Turncloak**, the camp's quest giver, fought for both banners, one after the other, and spent eight lives doing it. He is a
  lynx knight of the [Ninefold](../peoples/ninefold.md) on his last life, and keeps his helm on so nobody sees it.
- **The White Sister**, out in the land, mends the wounded of both sides in the field hospital. She was born in Aubade, in Lowlight.
- **The Camp-Mother** (odd floors) feeds both armies from one fire. She learned it, she says, from a cook called Cleaver.
- **The Quartermaster** (even floors) keeps the stores, and sells them, to whoever pays.

And on every floor the chapter's common folk walk at their work, and do not stop for climbers:
- **The Gleaner**, a Harrowmark woman, gleans the spent arrows from the furrows and sells them back to both banners. It is the
  only harvest the Field still gives, she says, and the only one that comes up every morning.

## Its rulers and enemies

The **Last Marshal** rules the forty-first floor: the knight who led the azure and was never found, guarding both banners now, alone
at the end on a horse of smoke. Above him the Iron Marshal, the Siege Lord, the Ashen General, the Banner Knight, the Crow King, the
Black Herald, the War Drummer and the Grey Captain ([rulers](rulers.md)). Their packs are the footmen, bowmen and knights of the
crimson and the azure, the ashen soldiers, and the Ash Captain ([bestiary](bestiary.md)).

## The gate and the crown

[Oblivion](../heroes/oblivion.md), the Unmaker, holds the chapter's first gate, because this endless war is the one thing on the Dial it has
truly unmade: nothing it touches can end. At the fiftieth floor, the crown, **Ash and Ember** burn again, the last sparks of the sun,
at the top of the Climb; and above them, the Noon Seat. The gates of the floors above are held by other champions' echoes, of any realm, drawn to the Tower's gates from the Ring.

## What climbers say

[Madame Cleaver](../heroes/madame-cleaver.md) climbed to the forty-first floor, found the Camp-Mother at her cauldron, and came down
without a word. [Sir Whiskers](../heroes/sir-whiskers.md) has met the Turncloak and will not say who he is. The Truce's champions call
the War the cruellest chapter, because it is a place where the Truce was never sworn.
