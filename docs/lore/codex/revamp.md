# The revamp plan: matching the heroes to their peoples

The user, 2026-11-14: "I think we need revamps of some heroes now, they seem all the same, like human and stuff ... and let's
match heroes to lore". This page gives every hero its people ([the peoples](peoples/README.md)) and says whether its figure
should change to show it. **Nothing here is built.** The plan goes to the user for a yes; the models are made later, one at a
time in Blender, starting with one sample. Back to the [codex](README.md).

Three answers:
- **No:** the figure already reads as its people.
- **Touch:** a costume or colour detail of its people: skin, eyes, a mark, a horn. The figure, its clothes and its props stay.
- **Revamp:** a new figure, built the same way (`pipeline/designs/style-v4/README.md`), with the hero's kit, title, colours and
  props kept unless its people demand otherwise.

What was rejected before, and is kept out: hand-built poses and cartoon looks (`CLAUDE.md`, Lessons). What the user accepted, and
is kept: the one realistic style on the pack's body and clips; every hero's colours and held things; the looks that already work.
A revamp changes the body's people, not the hero.

## How the ranking was made

The forty were laid side by side (`pipeline/designs/style-v4/lineup_v4.jpg`) and grouped by what a player sees from the game's
camera: build, outline, main colour, skin and head. Four groups read as one figure dressed four ways:

| Group | Heroes | What they share |
|---|---|---|
| Pale robed women with a staff | Opaline, Silvane, Isolde, Kaia (and Fenna, Lyra, Shiori less so) | a pale face, white or silver hair, a long pale gown in lavender, white or blue, a staff in the left hand |
| Old men in long robes | Mr. Ledger, Wick, Tempo (and Barry, Cantor less so) | a long thin pale face, grey or white hair, a long dark coat or robe |
| Workmen in aprons | Brannoc, Bram, Hank (and Madame Cleaver, Ada less so) | a broad pale man, a beard or grey hair, a brown apron or tunic |
| Small figures with the pack's adult face | Pim, Pom, Grandma Ironknit, Professor Puff, Pip | a short body and an adult face, plain human |

A hero ranks higher the more it looks like another hero now and the more its people can set it apart. The six revamps break up
the four groups; the touches give every human people its own skin and marks, so the line-up reads as many peoples.

**Six revamps, sixteen touches, eighteen unchanged.** The touches are mostly a new skin texture and a mark, and could be done in
two batches (the Kilnborn and Northerners first, then the rest).

## The first three to make

1. **Opaline**, because she and her sister are the two most alike heroes in the game, and the lore makes the difference.
2. **Mr. Ledger**, because he is the clearest case of a hero who becomes another people, and the robed old men are the second
   most alike group.
3. **Kaia**, because she is the hero most often mistaken for another (Isolde) from the game's camera.

One at a time, each shown beside the hero it was mistaken for and beside Sefu and Kaia (or Isolde, for Kaia), and the user's yes
before the next.

## The six revamps

### 1. Opaline, the Lightbreaker (Gloaming folk)

**Built 2026-11-15** (`blender/heroes/real/prism.py`; before and after: `docs/heroes/revamp/index.html`). As built: her
people's violet eyeshine added (the one mark every Gloaming hero carries); the crystal glows faintly, the skin does not.

- **Figure:** still slim and calm, still the floating ring of prisms. The change: where she went down the Prism Stair, the Deep's
  light grew into her. Facets of clear amethyst crystal along the cheekbones, the collarbones and the backs of the hands,
  built as small pieces on the body; her skin with a faint inner light where the crystal meets it. Her hair goes from silver to
  a deep violet-black, cut at the jaw (she cut it on the Stair). The gown changes from lavender to deep amethyst over cream, with
  a cutter's leather apron at the hip. The wand stays in the left hand. Silvane keeps the pale lavender and silver, so the two
  sisters read as night and the Deep.
- **Kit and title:** unchanged. Prismatic, banking bolts, the crystal wall, the ring of shards.
- **Lore that changes:** her people is Gloaming folk, born in Gloaming's mirror quarter, which LORE.md did not say. The crystal
  is new: the Deep's light took root in her the year she broke her first great crystal, and she is slowly becoming glass. Her
  [page](heroes/opaline.md) has it.

### 2. Mr. Ledger, the Tithe-Keeper (the Long Dead)

**Built 2026-11-15** (`blender/heroes/real/ledger.py`). As built: the skin painted and the cheeks moved in on the pack's own
face, small bone knuckles built on the hands; his brows are gone.

- **Figure:** the same tall black-and-oxblood robes, the great red book chained to his wrist, the long quill, the gold coin
  chains. The change is under them. His skin goes to old vellum, grey-gold and drawn tight on the bone; the cheekbones, the brow
  ridge and the jaw show bone through it; his hands are long and thin with bone at every knuckle; his grey hair is kept, thin
  and slicked. The faint red eyes become two clear embers in deep sockets. Wick keeps the living old man's face, so the Truce's
  two old officers read as the living and the dead.
- **Kit and title:** unchanged. Debts that come due are, if anything, more his now.
- **Lore that changes:** "Nobody knows" becomes: he died some sixty years before the Fall, in the middle of a sum, and got up to
  finish it. He is one of the Long Dead, the only one who works for the Truce. His Reason is the Tally itself. Vesper's debt to
  him is a debt to a dead man, which makes it worse. Ironknit's scarf is the only warm thing he owns. His
  [page](heroes/mr-ledger.md) has it.

### 3. Kaia, the Tempest Caller (Coast folk)

**Built 2026-11-15** (`blender/heroes/real/storm.py`). As built: her figure was already a coat with legs, so the change is
the skin, the bared forearms with the tide lines painted on, the hair (the hood and long cloak are gone), the earrings, the high collar
and short cape, the sea boots. The storm clouds on her shoulders are kept.

- **Figure:** a storm caller of the Spires, not a lady of a court. Sea-tanned skin; her silver hair kept, salt-bleached, with
  sea-green streaks, bound up; tide lines round both forearms (she has lived through nine great floods); sea-glass in the ears.
  The long blue gown becomes a knee-length rain-blue oilskin storm coat with a high collar and a short cape, over dark leggings
  and sea boots, so her outline changes from a bell to a coat with legs. The staff that catches lightning stays in the left hand,
  with its hook and spark.
- **Kit and title:** unchanged.
- **Lore that changes:** none of her story; her figure now matches it. Her [page](heroes/kaia.md) describes the new look.

### 4. Brannoc, the Ashen Smith (Kilnborn)

**Built 2026-11-15** (`blender/heroes/real/forge.py`). As built: his breastplate covered the chest, so the plate and shirt open
in a V above the grate and the seams climb from the forge to the throat; the bracers cover only the wrist half, so the darker forearms
show. His beard, red on the old model, is ash as the lore always said.

- **Figure:** the same huge smith in the scorched leather apron with the hammer. His skin goes ash-grey, darker at the forearms;
  **ember seams** cross his chest, throat and arms, glowing forge orange from the fire in his chest (the trait's Heat could later
  brighten them; not part of this plan). His ash beard stays. His eyes are pale amber.
- **Kit and title:** unchanged; the title becomes literal.
- **Lore that changes:** he is Kilnborn, of the first generation born grey; the forge in his chest is the Caldera's fire in a
  Kilnborn body, taken in on purpose. His [page](heroes/brannoc.md) has it.

### 5. Hank, the Moss Hewer (Wood folk)

- **Figure:** the same huge woodcutter, braided beard, moss-green tunic, fur mantle and one-bit axe held low. New: **antlers**,
  broad and branching, sawn flat at every tip, because he cuts them back each spring and plants the tips (a felled tree must be
  paid back). His skin goes ruddy and freckled; his eyes moss green. The antlers give him a silhouette no other hero has, and set
  him apart from Brannoc and Bram for good.
- **Kit and title:** unchanged.
- **Lore that changes:** he swore the Moot's oath at twenty-two, the year after the Fall, and has grown and sawn antlers every
  year since. Fenna says it is the only thing he plants willingly. His [page](heroes/hank.md) has it.

### 6. Grandma Ironknit, the Weaver (Briarkin)

- **Figure:** still tiny, old and kind, in the dark wine shawl with the rose bands, the spindle with the three glowing spools,
  the two long needles and Mr. Biscuit. New: long pointed ears through the hood's rolled edge; eyes of one colour, silver from lid
  to lid; skin with the faint dapple of light through leaves; fingers a little too long. From the game's camera she reads as a
  small hooded figure with ears, not as another short human.
- **Kit and title:** unchanged.
- **Lore that changes:** she is a Briarkin of the Spindle court who chose to live among mortals at the Wood's edge three hundred
  years ago, which is why the courts left the changeling twins on her doorstep. The Hourkeepers have always known; the Wood folk
  pretend not to. Her [page](heroes/grandma-ironknit.md) has it.

## The touches, in a line each

- **Wick:** deep umber skin, his white hair in braids under the hood, star lines along the jaw. A Northerner who keeps doors.
- **Silvane:** silver eyeshine, skin cooled to blue-grey, dark lips; nothing else changes.
- **Bram:** ash-grey skin and pale amber eyes; Gerald unchanged.
- **Tempo:** umber skin, his white hair kept; star lines along the jaw drawn as a clock's ticks.
- **Vesper:** crimson-violet eyeshine, blue-grey skin; her crown and gown unchanged.
- **Ada:** ash-grey skin, dim ember seams at the knuckles; the copper coat and goggles unchanged.
- **Referee Whistler:** ash-grey skin; the tabard, helm and horn unchanged.
- **Madame Cleaver:** deep brown skin, kohl to the temples, henna on the backs of the hands; the crimson headwrap unchanged.
- **Barry Cloudsley:** sea-tanned skin, tide lines on the forearms.
- **Zephyr:** sea-tanned skin, sea-glass in one ear, tide lines.
- **Marin:** tide lines and sea-glass; her slippers go, she dances barefoot as her catalog page said.
- **Cantor:** golden-brown skin, the dawn line, gold thread in his hair.
- **Pip:** golden-brown skin with the freckles kept, hair bound with gold thread.
- **Shiori:** the star lines of her birth hour drawn in ink on the backs of her hands.
- **The Great Zambini:** pointed ears under the hat, violet eyes of one colour.
- **Anka:** a warm emissive rim at the edges of her skin and the roots of her hair (kept under the floor's glow).

## The Climb's people (later, low priority)

The 25 people of the Climb were built as plain humans before the peoples were written (`docs/tower/models/`). The lore covers
them as they are (the Wood's five swore no oath, so no horns; the Deep's five are from the far halls, so not grey). If the user
wants their peoples to show, the cheapest touches are skin textures: the Sands' five to Dune folk skin and kohl (some already are),
the Sea's five to sea-tan and tide lines. Nothing else is needed.

## What this plan does not change

No kit, no spell, no number, no trait. No hero's title. No colour that a spell uses. The rule that a staff goes in the left hand
and a weapon in the right. The pack's body and clips. The game's people labels (on the Play card and the Lore panel) are shown
from the lore as soon as the lore ships, before any model changes; six of them (the revamps) describe a figure the player will
not see until the revamp is made.
