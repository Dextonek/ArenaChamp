# Mabel, the Deepdelver

*The Underforge · [Mole-folk](../peoples/mole.md) · from [the Deep](../realms/deep.md)* · [Heroes](README.md)

## Biography

Mabel was born in the warren of the Deepest, the lowest of the mole-folk's towns under the southern hills, twenty years before the Fall by the Hourkeepers' count and
"in the year of the warm seam" by her own people's. The Deepest is a warren of round rooms joined by tunnels exactly as wide as a mole-folk's shoulders, in rock that is
warm and still and dark, and Mabel grew up in the dark as her people always had: digging, listening to the rock, learning the seam-songs from her mother, who learned them
from hers. She was stout, dark-furred and curious, and she dug faster than any girl in the warren.

She was twenty and at the face of a new seam on the day of the Fall. She heard the rock sing, a high long note she had never heard before, and then she saw a light in the
rock: a vein of crystal she had uncovered that morning, dull and grey, filling from inside with pale rose light, as if something were pouring into it from above. The light
ran along the vein faster than she could follow, into the next crystal and the next, through the whole of the Deep, until every crystal in the halls glowed. The mole-folk call
it **the Shining**. Mabel saw it first.

Her people are divided about it still. The old say it ruined the dark, which they had loved for nine hundred years, and that a light that comes from above is not to be trusted.
Mabel's generation says the light came to them, through their rock, to their crystals, and that it belongs to the miners who dug down to it. Mabel says so loudest. She is a
miner of the deepest seams, and a lamp-keeper: she keeps the **Lamp Road** open, the mole-folk's great tunnel from the Deep's mouth down to the crystal halls, and she has fitted
every crystal along it with a bronze collar so that it can be carried to light another tunnel. She wears a bronze helm topped with a glowing amber crystal lantern of her own, cut
from the seam where she saw the Shining.

The Noonsworn want every drop of the old light carried up to the sky, and [Opaline](opaline.md), the Lightbreaker, goes down the Prism Stair every day and breaks it out of the
crystals and sends it to Aubade. Mabel has told her that the light belongs to the miners. She has told her with her claws, which are as long as daggers and tipped in gold, in the
Ring, three times. The Deep is still arguing. Mabel rather likes Opaline, which makes it worse.

She digs with [Tova](tova.md), the Underforge's sapper, a younger mole-folk who uses powder instead of claws, which Mabel considers cheating and very efficient: they have opened
more fallen halls together than any pair in the Deep. And she sorts glass with [Itzel](itzel.md), the Herald of the Caldera: Mabel digs the black glass out of the Caldera's
cooled edges, where the sun's fire turns to obsidian, and Itzel cuts it, and the two of them run the trade in black glass that pays for half the Underforge's iron.

In the Ring she goes under the floor and comes up under you. She does not like the open, or the light at the Ring, which hurts her small eyes; she fights fast and leaves.

Since the Climb opened she has been up to the Deep's chapter, the far halls on the night their crystals went out, and walked them in
the dark without a lamp, as her people did before the Shining, and found it, she says, restful. The Echo-Hermit there clicks his tongue
to find his way, a trick he learned from a mole-folk Old Mother, and Mabel taught him two more seam-songs before she came down.

## People and appearance

Mole-folk of the Deepest, and Noon-touched (one of the few: most of her people's elders were too deep to be touched by anything): twenty at the Fall, she looks forty in mole-folk
years. Stout and dark-furred, with a long muzzle and a small rose nose, calm black eyes, a studded leather cuirass, a bronze helm with a gold band and brass goggles pushed up, a
glowing amber crystal lantern on top, a slung pick, and huge gold-tipped claws. In the [revamp plan](../revamp.md) her figure is unchanged: she is already her people.

## Voice

Brisk, friendly and literal, with the mole-folk's round vowels and a habit of sniffing before she answers. She says exactly what she means and is puzzled when others do not.
She hums seam-songs when she digs.

## Voice lines

- **Picked:** "Down we go."
- **Winning:** "Dug in."
- **Winning:** "Up from under. Hello."
- **Losing:** "Too bright up here. I'm going down."
- **Losing:** "Cave-in. Happens."
- **Meeting Opaline:** "That's our light, glass-girl. Put it back."
- **Meeting Tova:** "Claws first, powder second. Agreed?"

## Secrets

- The crystal in her lantern still holds the first light of the Shining. On some nights it sings the high note she heard in the rock. She thinks it is calling the rest of the
  light home, and she does not know where home is.
- She has dug deeper than any mole-folk has ever dug, below the Deepest, and found a tunnel going down that was dug from below. She filled it in. She has told only her Old Mother.
- She agrees with Opaline more than she lets on. The light does not belong to anyone.

## What she wants from the Tower

To see the light from above, once, where it came from; and then to bring it home to the Deep, where it is safe.

## Ties

- **[Opaline](opaline.md)**, rival: Opaline takes the Deep's light up to Aubade; Mabel wants it left for the miners who dug to it.
- **[Tova](tova.md)**, friend: they dig the Deep together, one with claws and one with powder.
- **[Itzel](itzel.md)**, comrade: Mabel digs the black glass out of the Caldera, and Itzel cuts it.
