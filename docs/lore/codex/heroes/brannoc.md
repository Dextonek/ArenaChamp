# Brannoc, the Ashen Smith

*The Underforge · [Kilnborn](../peoples/kiln.md) · from [the Deep](../realms/deep.md)* · [Heroes](README.md)

## Biography

Brannoc was born in the southern hills thirty years before the Fall, the son of a smith in a hill town over the Deep, when the hill people were still
called hillmen and nobody's skin was grey. He was a master smith by twenty-five, a huge, ash-bearded, short-tempered man who could draw a sword blade in
a morning and argue about it all afternoon. The quarry above his town was a cold crater then, where the hill people cut stone.

On the day of the Fall the crater filled with fire from below. Brannoc was at his anvil and felt the ground warm under his feet, and went out and stood at
the crater's edge and watched the fallen sun's heat come up through the rock: a lake of slow fire where the quarry had been, cooling at the edges into black
glass. Everyone else ran. Brannoc fetched his hammer. Within a month he had built a forge on the crater's rim and was working iron in the sun's own fire,
and iron worked in that fire, he found, came out like nothing anyone had ever made: harder, brighter, and warm for days. The hill people followed him down to
the heat, and built the great forge round the **Ember Caldera**, and called themselves the Underforge.

The fire changed them, as it changed him. Over a generation the hill people's skin went grey as ash and their eyes pale as a coal's edge; they began to be called
the Kilnborn. Brannoc went further. In the third year after the Fall, when the great forge's first furnace would not draw, he opened his own chest to the Caldera's
fire, by a working nobody has repeated, and took a coal of it in. A forge glows in his chest now. It is why he is called the Ashen Smith. He has no cooldowns,
only heat: every working he does heats him, and at white heat he burns, and must quench. Every Kilnborn smith since has been grey; Brannoc is grey and
cracked, with lines of ember light across his chest and arms where the forge shows through.

He is the Master of the great forge, sunk in the Ember Caldera, where the fire of the fallen sun still rises through the rock. The Ring's iron is his, and the
Gaol's chains, and half the blades on the Dial. He taught [Ada](ada.md) everything about metal and nothing about patience, and she built a hammer that swung
itself, and he has never forgiven her for it or been prouder of anything.

He has loved two women. In the war, [Madame Cleaver](madame-cleaver.md), the cook who fed both armies from one fire: in year 30 her cleaver came to his forge
for a new edge, and went back to her with a promise neither of them kept, to open an inn together at the Waymeet when the war was really over. And in year 40,
[Isolde](isolde.md), the Winter Warden of Frostfall. He followed her north, fire to her frost, and set up his forge on the shore of Frostfall Lake, and the ice
cracked, and the library under it began to drip. She froze his forge solid to send him home. It took him a year to light it again. She says it was the kindest
thing she knew how to do. He has not followed anyone since, and neither of them has married.

He wants the Ember of Aubade for his fire. The Noonsworn's last piece of the old sun, carried on [Orren](orren.md)'s back, would burn in the great forge, he
believes, until the Caldera's fire was a sun underground, lighting the Deep and warming the Dial. He fights Orren for it whenever the Ring allows. Orren
respects him, which he finds very annoying.

## People and appearance

Kilnborn, of the first generation, and Noon-touched: thirty at the Fall, he looks fifty. Huge, with an ash beard, a broad iron helm, a scorched leather apron,
and a hammer; the forge glowing in his chest. Today his skin is pale. In the [revamp plan](../revamp.md) he is a revamp: ash-grey skin, darker at the forearms,
cracked with ember seams over the chest, throat and arms where the forge-light shows through, and pale amber eyes, so that the Ashen Smith's title is literal.

## Voice

Loud, rough and impatient, a voice made for shouting over furnaces. He speaks in short commands and long complaints. When he is pleased he says nothing at all,
and hits something.

## Voice lines

- **Picked:** "Hotter. Again. Hotter."
- **Picked:** "Stand back. I'm working."
- **Winning:** "Forged."
- **Winning:** "Quenched. Next."
- **Losing:** "Cracked in the cooling. My fault."
- **Losing:** "Back in the fire with me."
- **Meeting Orren:** "Give me the Ember, boy. It wants a proper forge."
- **Meeting Isolde:** "Still cold, Warden? I'm still hot."

## Secrets

- The coal in his chest is going out. The Caldera burns a little lower every year, and so does he. He has measured both. He wants the Ember because he thinks it
  is the only thing that can relight him.
- He still has the deed to the Waymeet inn, signed by Cleaver. She does not know he kept his copy.
- The Underforge's drill toward the Hall of Noon was his idea.

## What he wants from the Tower

The Heart of Noon, below, where it belongs, and its fire brought up through the rock; and if he cannot have that, the Ember, in his forge.
