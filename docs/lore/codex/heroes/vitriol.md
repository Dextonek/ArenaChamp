# Vitriol, the Plague Alchemist

*The Dune Throne · [Dune folk](../peoples/dune.md) · from [the Sands](../realms/sands.md)* · [Heroes](README.md)

## Biography

Nobody knows how old Vitriol is, because nobody has seen his face. The embalmers of the Sands take no name and show no face when they put on the mask: they are
called by their trade, and Vitriol is the word for the oil the Throne's embalmers use to keep a king's heart from rotting before it is weighed. His hands are old:
long, dry, stained to the wrist with the resins of the trade, steady as stone. His voice could be forty or a hundred. He has been the master of the embalmers' house
in Tamerit since before the Fall, the court thinks, though the court's oldest members remember him only as he is: a dark hood, a pale glass beak mask with round green
lenses, a long coat with a bandolier of vials.

He is the alchemist who keeps the Throne's dead kings whole. The embalmers' house is a windowless house of white plaster in Tamerit's old quarter that smells of resin
and myrrh, where the kings and great nobles of the Sands are prepared for the Valley of Lids: their hearts drawn and weighed against Nebet's feather, their bodies dried
in natron and wrapped in linen and sealed with oils that keep them for a thousand years. It is the oldest office in the Sands, older than the crook, and the embalmer
has always been feared, because a man who knows every reagent that preserves also knows every one that does the opposite.

Vitriol knows them all. In the Ring he lays one on you, then another, and lets them meet: a dart of toxin, a vial of fire, a splash of acid, a walking bomb of resin and
salt that he calls a homunculus and the Arbiter calls illegal. He is fascinated by reactions: what two substances do when they touch, what a substance does to a body,
what a body does to a substance. He is not cruel, he says. He is curious. He says it without any warmth at all.

He served the old king, and then the old king's daughter, Asenet, in the two years she ruled. In the last month of her reign, as the river towns rose and the court turned
against her, Asenet came to the embalmers' house at night and asked him for a curse: not a poison, a working, to lay on the crown so that it could never stay on the head of
whoever took it from her. He brewed it. He has never told anyone what was in it. On the night she was driven out she laid it on the crown at the gate of Tamerit, and it
has passed from head to head ever since, and she never paid him for it. He has not forgotten. [Asenet](asenet.md) owes him, and the debt is not in the Tally.

He serves her brother now, as he served her, because the embalmer serves the Throne and not the head under the crown. He brews Sefu a draught every night that keeps the
curse from taking the king. It is shortening the king's life. Vitriol has told him so, once, and Sefu said thank you.

In the seventy-first summer, on the Brass Road where it skirts the Walking Wood's south-eastern edge, he tested a new fume on a garden by the road: a mile of wildflowers
planted, he found later, by [Fenna](fenna.md), the Moot's Grove Warden, to hold the soil where the Wood had walked. Every plant died in an hour. He was delighted, and wrote
to the Moot to thank it for the use of the garden, calling it a successful test. Fenna has not forgotten. They have fought in the Ring six times, and she has planted a flower
on every spot where he fell, which he finds a very interesting reaction.

## People and appearance

Dune folk, by his hands, his trade and his tongue; nobody has seen more. A dark hood, a pale glass beak mask with round green lenses, a dark coat with a bandolier of vials
of green and amber liquid, gloves stained to the wrist. In the [revamp plan](../revamp.md) his figure is unchanged: masked, his people do not show, and that is his trade's
custom.

## Voice

Soft, dry and clinical, slightly muffled by the mask; he describes what is happening to you as it happens, like a lecturer. He never raises his voice. He laughs, rarely,
at results.

## Secrets

- He is two hundred and ten years old. He has taken a drop of the embalmers' preserving oil every morning since he was forty, and it has kept him, as it keeps the kings;
  under the mask his face has not changed since the year he began. He does not know what will happen if he stops, and is curious.
- The curse he brewed for Asenet cannot be broken by anyone but its maker. He could lift it tonight. He is waiting for her to pay.
- The fume that killed Fenna's garden was meant for the Tower. He wanted to see what it would do to a thing that drinks the Dial.

## What he wants from the Tower

Samples. Of the Heart of Noon, of Ash and Ember, of the Sun Warden, of whatever is in the Noon Seat. He wants to know what a sun is made of, and what it reacts with.

## Ties

- **[Fenna](fenna.md)**, sworn enemy: his fumes killed a whole garden of hers, and he called it a successful test.
- **[Asenet](asenet.md)**, owed by her: he brewed the curse she laid on the crown, and she never paid him for it.
