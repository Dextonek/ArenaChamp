# Wick, the Doorwarden

*The Truce of the Ring · [Northerners](../peoples/north.md) · from [the Ring](../realms/tower.md)* · [Heroes](README.md)

## Biography

Wick was born in Hearthmere at the south end of Frostfall Lake, about fifty years before the Fall, to a family of lamp-keepers: the
people who light the lamps of the observatory's long corridors and the library's caverns under the ice, and know every door in both.
He grew up with a ring of keys at his belt and a taper in his hand. He was a quiet, thin, courteous boy who liked doors more than people,
because a door, he said, is a promise that the other side is still there.

He kept the observatory's doors for thirty years. He knew which ones stuck in the cold and which ones the Readers left open, which led to
rooms the Hourkeepers had forgotten and which led to rooms they would rather nobody remembered. When the sun fell he was fifty and
already grey-white, standing at the observatory's great door with a lamp in his hand while the Readers rushed past him to the Lens. He
remembers that nobody shut the door. He shut it.

In year 3, when the Kilnborn masons dug the foundations of the Ring's stands at the Tower's foot, they found beneath them a gallery of
a hundred doors, already hung, already locked, opening onto places that were not under the Ring: the Sands, the Deep, the white north,
the Wood's edge and further. Nobody had built them. The Truce sent to Frostfall for someone who understood doors, and the Hourkeepers
sent Wick.

He has kept the **Hundred Doors** for ninety-seven years. Champions come to the Ring from every realm of the Dial, and every one of them
comes through a door that Wick keeps. He has learned what most of them do. Some open onto the realms; some onto places in the realms that
are not on any map; a few onto rooms that are the same room on both sides; and one, the hundredth, he has never opened, and keeps a lamp
lit in front of it day and night. He wears a long oxblood coat with gold braid down the front, the hood lying back on his shoulders, and
carries a ring of keys with an amber gem among them that glows when a door nearby is open.

He hates the Tear. A door has a frame, a hinge, a lock and another side; the Tear above the Tower has none of them. It is a hole in the
world with no frame and no other side, and the thing that came through it, [Oblivion](oblivion.md), makes holes where no door should be.
Wick has sworn to send it back through its own hole and close it after it, and he is the one person on the Dial who might know how to
close a hole that is not a door. In the Ring he opens his own doors in pairs and shows his enemies out, politely, through the one they
did not expect.

Since the Tower's gate opened in year 90 he has been asked, many times, whether the gate is one of his. It is not. He has stood in front
of it with every key on his ring and felt nothing answer. But he has noticed that on the nights after a champion goes up the Climb, one of
the Hundred Doors is warm to the touch in the morning, never the same one twice, as if something on the other side had leaned against it
in the night. He writes down which, and sends the list to Frostfall.

His friend is [Thessa](thessa.md) the Sky-Stitcher, who sews places together with a golden thread: he keeps the doors and she keeps the
seams, and they agree that the Tear is neither. They meet once a month at the Waymeet inn, where his family's old cousin keeps the fire,
and compare notes on the Tear, which is getting wider. This year Wick has started locking doors that have never been locked.

## People and appearance

A Northerner of Hearthmere: tall, thin and old, with a long calm face, level brows and a thin smile; his white hair, which went white
before he was thirty as Northern hair does, swept back. Today his skin is pale and he wears no star lines, which the
[revamp plan](../revamp.md) touches: deep umber skin, his white hair in braids under the hood, the star lines of his birth hour along the
jaw. The oxblood coat, the gold braid, the ring of keys and the amber gem stay.

## Voice

Soft, courteous and slightly old-fashioned, the voice of an usher at a very good house. He gives directions even when nobody asked. He
apologises before he hits you.

## Voice lines

- **Picked:** "This way, please. No, the other this way."
- **Picked:** "Mind the step. And the one after."
- **Winning:** "You've been shown out."
- **Winning:** "And the door closes. Thank you for visiting."
- **Losing:** "Locked out. How very rude."
- **Losing:** "I'll find another way in. I always do."
- **Meeting Oblivion:** "You have no door. You have no business here."
- **Meeting Thessa:** "Still sewing? It's wider today."

## Secrets

- He opened the hundredth door once, in year 4, for a moment. On the other side was the Ring, at night, empty, a hundred years from now.
  He has kept the lamp lit in front of it since.
- He believes the Hundred Doors and the Tower were made by the same hand, and that the Tower is, in the end, a door.
- He knows which door Asenet used to leave the Gaol of Oaths, and that it is not one of his. It frightens him.

## What he wants from the Tower

To find its other side. Every door has one, and the Tower is the largest door on the Dial. And to close the Tear behind Oblivion.

## Ties

- **[Oblivion](oblivion.md)**, sworn enemy: the keeper of every door, against the thing that makes holes where no door should be.
- **[Thessa](thessa.md)**, friend: he keeps the doors and she keeps the seams, and they agree that the Tear is neither.
