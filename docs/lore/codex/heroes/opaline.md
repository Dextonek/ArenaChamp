# Opaline, the Lightbreaker

*The Noonsworn · [Gloaming folk](../peoples/gloaming.md) · from [the Deep](../realms/deep.md)* · [Heroes](README.md)

## Biography

Opaline was born in the mirror quarter of Gloaming, eight years before the Fall, the younger daughter of a glazier of the Mirror house. Her sister
Silvane was four years older, and the two girls grew up in their father's workshop among sheets of silvered glass, learning to cut, grind and
polish, to set a mirror so that it caught a lamp across a room, and to read faces in reflections. Silvane loved the mirrors because they kept
things. Opaline loved the prisms that came in the workshop's crates from the Deep, because they let things go: white light went in, and colours
came out.

On the day of the Fall the light left Gloaming first. Opaline was eight; she remembers her sister at the workshop window with their father's best
hand mirror, holding it up to the last of the sun as it went, and catching something in it, and covering it with her hand. Then the long dusk
came, and the year without a day, and the change. Both sisters' eyes began to shine back the lamplight. Silvane said it was beautiful. Opaline
said it was a cage.

She left Gloaming in year 18, still a child to look at, with a bag of glazier's tools, and walked south along the night roads and the Brass Road
to the Deep, where the light of the fallen sun had run down into the crystals. She wanted to see where it had gone. At the top of the Prism Stair,
the stair of three thousand steps cut down through a vein of clear crystal, a Kilnborn glass-cutter took her in as an apprentice, and when he died
she kept his house. She has lived there since, and gone down the Stair every day.

What she does there is break light. The sun's light hid in the Deep's crystals when it fell, which is why the halls glow, and Opaline goes down and
breaks it out again: she cuts a crystal open along its grain, and the white light that was trapped in it comes out as bands of red, gold, green and
blue. She is looking for the one colour the sun has lost, the one that is missing from every band she has ever cut; she believes that when she finds
it, she will know why the sun fell. The Noonsworn, who want every drop of the old light carried up for the sun, took her into the Friends of the Dawn
in year 60, and she sends them what she frees. She is calm, pale and patient, with a ring of prisms circling her head and a voice that never rises.

The miners do not like it. They hold that the light belongs to those who dug down to it, and [Mabel](mabel.md) of the mole-folk, who keeps the Lamp
Road, has told her so, with her claws, more than once; they have settled it in the Ring three times and the Deep is still arguing.

And her sister. [Silvane](silvane.md) stayed in Gloaming and became the Mirror Duchess of the Court of Dusk, keeper of its secrets in its mirrors,
and still carries the hand mirror with the sun's last light inside it. Opaline went to the Noonsworn to set light free; Silvane stayed to keep it.
They met for the last time in year 40, at the Masque of Houses, and quarrelled about the mirror: Opaline asked her to break it and let the light
go, and Silvane said no. They have not spoken since. They have fought in the Ring twice, and both times walked away without a word.

The Deep's light has changed her. She has broken more crystal than anyone alive, and the crystal has gone into her: there are facets now along her
cheekbones and the backs of her hands, clear amethyst, which she cannot cut out, and which glow faintly in the dark. She is slowly becoming glass.
She holds the gate of the Climb's Deep, where the halls are lightless, because she is the only champion who can make them bright.

## People and appearance

Gloaming folk by birth, of the mirror quarter, with her people's eyeshine, and Noon-touched: eight at the Fall, she looks twenty-eight. Today she is
slim and calm, with long pale hair, a ring of small prisms floating behind her head, a pale lavender robe with crystal epaulettes and a crystal brooch,
and a crystal wand in the left hand, much like her sister. In the [revamp plan](../revamp.md) she is the first revamp: facets of amethyst crystal
grown along her cheekbones, collarbones and hands, a faint inner light, hair cut short and deep violet-black, a gown of deep amethyst over cream with a
cutter's apron, so that the sisters read as the night and the Deep.

## Voice

Quiet, even and precise, a cutter's voice: she says exactly what she means and then stops. She never raises it. People lean in to hear her, which
she has learned to use.

## Voice lines

- **Picked:** "White is only every colour, waiting."
- **Picked:** "Hold still. You're in the light."
- **Winning:** "Broken clean."
- **Winning:** "There. Every colour you had."
- **Losing:** "A flaw in the cut. I'll find it."
- **Losing:** "Light bends. It doesn't break."
- **Meeting Silvane:** "Still keeping it, sister? Let it go."
- **Meeting Mabel:** "It isn't yours either, Mabel."

## Secrets

- She has found the missing colour, once, in a crystal from the bottom of the Prism Stair: a colour with no name, for one heartbeat. She
  told no one. She thinks the same colour is inside Silvane's mirror.
- The crystal in her skin is spreading. She has calculated that she will be glass through in about thirty years.
- She still has the glazier's tools her father gave her, and polishes a small mirror with them every night, which she would never admit.

## What she wants from the Tower

To bring the light she frees to the Noon Seat, all of it, every colour; and to find out whether the sun lost its colour before it fell, or after.

## Ties

- **[Silvane](silvane.md)**, sister: two sisters of the glass: one breaks the sun's light free for the Noonsworn, one keeps it in a mirror for the
  Court.
- **[Mabel](mabel.md)**, rival: Opaline takes the Deep's light up to Aubade; Mabel wants it left for the miners who dug to it.
