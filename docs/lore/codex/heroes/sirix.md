# Sirix, the Swarm Warden

*The Greenwood Moot · [Briarkin](../peoples/briar.md), the Hive court · from [the Walking Wood](../realms/wood.md)* · [Heroes](README.md)

## Biography

The hive-trees of the Walking Wood are hollow oaks the size of towers, full of amber wax and wasps, in the south of the forest where the sun used
to fall longest; and inside them, in rooms of wax that the wasps build round them, lives the Hive court of the Briarkin. The Hive court is the least
like people of all the courts. Its Briarkin wear chitin at the shoulders and the brow, see with faceted eyes, grow two pairs of thin wings, and think,
the other courts say, a little too much like the hive: together, in order, without much argument.

Sirix was born in the great hive-tree called the Amber Mother some time before the Fall; the Hive court does not count years, only summers of honey,
and he says he was born in the summer the honey ran red, which the Moot's records put about twenty years before the Fall. He was a warden's child: in the
Hive court, the wardens are the ones who leave the tree, who speak with the folk and the other courts, and who guard the flowers the hives need. He grew
up flying (in short hops; the court's wings are better for steadying than flying) between the hive-trees and the meadows, learning which flowers would
bloom when, and which folk could be trusted not to rob the trees in the first frost.

He was a young warden when the Wood began to walk, and the hives woke. The wasps of every hive-tree came out at once, in the first hour after the Fall,
and swarmed over the forest in a cloud that darkened the Wood's sky for a day, as if looking for the sun. When they came back they would not go into the
trees. They gathered round Sirix instead, a cloak of wasps, and would not leave him. The Hive court made him its Swarm Warden that week, because the
swarm had chosen. He says the wasps are not his: he is theirs.

The hives fly where he asks, because he keeps the Wood's flowers safe, and since the Wood began to walk the flowers need keeping: a meadow walked over by
a grove is a meadow lost, and the hives go hungry. He guards [Fenna](fenna.md)'s gardens because the hives need them, and works the Wood's edges with her,
replanting. He wears black chitin plate with hexagonal pauldrons and a crested insect helm, which is not a helm, and a swarm follows him like a cloak. In the
Ring the swarm fights with him: a barb, a sting, a cloud, a column of wasps.

His friend is [Vela](vela.md), the luna-moth priestess of the Moth court, who lays the Wood to sleep at night. The hive keeps the day and the moth keeps the
night, and between them, the Moot says, the Wood's flowers are never alone. They meet at dusk at the edge of the night meadows, when his wasps go in and her
moths come out, and talk for exactly as long as the light lasts.

His enemy, though Sirix would not use the word, is [Professor Puff](professor-puff.md), the Windward's aeronaut. In year 55 the Professor, mapping the sky over
the Wood from his basket, let a bubble of his rose gas drift down onto the Amber Mother, which swelled, lifted its roots out of the ground, and floated a mile
out of the Wood before it came down in a field of the Field of Banners, upside down, with the whole court inside. Nobody died. The swarm has not forgotten his
face. When the Professor flies over the Wood now, it rises to meet him.

## People and appearance

A Briarkin of the Hive court: black chitin plates at the shoulders and brow with an amber rim, hexagonal pauldrons, a crested insect head with faceted green eyes,
two pairs of pale wings carried folded like a cloak, and a cloud of small striped wasps. In the [revamp plan](../revamp.md) his figure is unchanged: the insect
helm and the two pairs of wings are his court's sign, grown, not worn, and they already read as his people.

## Voice

A dry, humming voice with a buzz under the consonants, calm and literal. He says "we" for himself and the swarm. He is courteous to the folk and very direct,
and finds jokes difficult.

## Voice lines

- **Picked:** "Hold still. They only sting what moves."
- **Picked:** "We are ready. All of us."
- **Winning:** "The hive is satisfied."
- **Winning:** "Stung. Swarmed. Done."
- **Losing:** "The swarm retreats. The swarm returns."
- **Losing:** "We are many. You are one. We remember."
- **Meeting Professor Puff:** "The swarm remembers your face, aeronaut."
- **Meeting Vela:** "Dusk already. Good night, moth."

## Secrets

- The swarm that woke at the Fall did not come from the hives. It came out of the ground under the hive-trees, from somewhere below the Wood, and moved into the
  hives later. Sirix does not know what it was before.
- He can hear, through the swarm, every flower in the Walking Wood at once. Since the Climb opened, he can hear flowers that are not in the Wood. They are in the
  Tower.
- He is very fond of the Professor, and has never managed to tell the swarm.

## What he wants from the Tower

To find the flowers he can hear inside it, and bring the hive to them. The swarm wants to go up. He does not know why.

## Ties

- **[Professor Puff](professor-puff.md)**, rival: Puff's gas once floated a whole hive-tree out of the Wood, and the swarm remembers his face.
- **[Vela](vela.md)**, friend: the hive keeps the day and the moth the night, and between them the Wood's flowers are never alone.
