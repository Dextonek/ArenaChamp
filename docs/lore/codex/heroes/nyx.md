# Nyx, the Umbral Stalker

*The Court of Dusk · [the Fallborn](../peoples/fallborn.md), of the Cast · from [Gloaming](../realms/gloaming.md)* · [Heroes](README.md)

## Biography

At noon on midsummer day a hundred years ago, as the sun stopped over the Tower, its last light came across the Dial low and hard, and threw every
shadow long. In Gloaming, at the westernmost edge of the light, a woman was standing at the foot of the Night Temple's west wall, and her shadow fell
across the wall, tall and thin, a hand raised to shade her eyes. The sun cracked. The light changed. The woman walked away. Her shadow did not.

It stood on the wall for the rest of that first long dusk, while the Gloaming folk lit their lamps and the Court gathered to keep the Naming, and at
midnight, during the Naming, it stepped off the wall. The priests who saw it say it stood in the forecourt for a long time, looking at its hands, as
if it had never had hands before. Then it looked at the dark mirror in the temple, which reflects nothing, and nothing looked back, and it seemed
pleased. That was Nyx, the first of the Cast.

Nobody knew what she was. The Hourkeepers, when they heard, came to measure her, and she let them, and they wrote "Fallborn: of the Cast", and could
say no more than that. She had no name, no voice for the first year, no memory of the woman whose shadow she had been, and no shadow of her own. She
faded when she was left alone and came back sharper when someone looked for her. She did not eat or sleep. She watched.

The person she watched most was the Countess. Vesper, in the hungry winter of the first year, was the only noble in Gloaming who walked the night roads
alone to bring food to her tenants, and the only one who looked straight at the thing standing in the temple forecourt and said good evening to it. Nyx
followed her home. She has stood in Vesper's shadow ever since: not the shadow Vesper casts, which is her own, but beside it, close enough that in some
lights the Countess seems to have two. When the Court of Dusk was founded, Nyx became its knife: the Countess's guard, the Court's assassin, the one who
hunts whoever the Court names. She chose her own name from the old Gloaming word for the hour after the last lamp.

She wears matte black with a violet edge and nothing that could catch the light, a hood over a face that is not there, a slit of mint where eyes would be.
In the Ring she fades when she is left alone, and her first strike out of the fade is the one that counts. She hunts mostly in the thickets of the Walking
Wood, where the Court's enemies hide and the light is broken.

She is not the only one of her kind, and that troubles her. The Hourkeepers count perhaps forty of the Cast across the Dial, most of
them thin and confused, standing on the walls they peeled from; the Court has taken in the ones it could find, and Nyx trains them. A few
have done things in the dark that the Court did not order. Nyx deals with those herself, quietly, and the Countess does not ask.

Two people on the Dial can find her. [Lyra](lyra.md), the Hourkeepers' star archer, shoots arrows that draw constellations, and her stars are the one light
Nyx cannot step out of: a shadow needs the light to come from somewhere, and starlight comes from everywhere. They have duelled in the dark more often than
either has told anyone, and Lyra has won more than half. And [Arden](arden.md), the Huntmaster of the Moot, has tracked her through the thickets for ten
years without finding a single footprint, which he takes personally. A shadow leaves no footprints. Arden says everything leaves a trail. Nyx has started
leaving him small things to find, on purpose, to see what he does.

## People and appearance

The first of the Cast, the shadows thrown by the sun's last light that walked away ([the Fallborn](../peoples/fallborn.md)). A slim figure of matte,
light-drinking black, with a thin hard violet edge where the light catches it, a hood, and no face: a mint slit where eyes would be. In the
[revamp plan](../revamp.md) her figure is unchanged: she already reads as what she is.

## Voice

A whisper, dry and very close, always seeming to come from just behind you. She learned to speak in the second year, from the Court, and speaks its
courtly Ring-speech with no accent at all. She uses few words, and no names but the Countess's.

- **Losing:** "I'll fade. I'll come back."

- **Meeting Arden:** "Still looking at the ground, hunter? Look up."

## Secrets

- She knows who put the two Noonsworn bell-ringers to sleep on the night road in year 86. It was another of the Cast, a shadow that walked off a wall in
  Aubade, and that hates the dawn more than Vesper ever could. Nyx has been hunting it for fourteen years. She has not told the Countess. She calls it the Dawnless, and she knows where it takes its sleepers: to the
  Tower's roots.
- She has begun to remember the woman whose shadow she was. A Gloaming glazier, from the mirror quarter, the morning of the Fall. She does not know her
  name. She thinks Silvane does.
- She is not sure she can die. She is sure she can be forgotten, and that it would be the same thing.

## What she wants from the Tower

The Noon Seat kept empty, for the Countess's sake; and to stand where the sun stood and see what shadow it throws now.
