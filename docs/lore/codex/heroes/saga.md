# Saga, the Northern Light

*The Hourkeepers · [Northerners](../peoples/north.md), lake-born · from [Frostfall](../realms/frostfall.md)* · [Heroes](README.md) · new, on the cards

## Biography

Saga dances the aurora over Frostfall: the curtains of green and violet light that hang over the lake on winter nights and tell the Hourkeepers the hour when the stars are hidden.
She was born in the same shore village of Frostfall Lake as her sister Isolde, of the same lake-born family, eighteen years before the Fall: frost-pale, white-haired from birth, and
from the first night she could stand, drawn to the sky. Her sister went onto the ice. Saga went onto the terraces.

The **dancers of the northern light** are an old order of the lake-born, nearly as old as the Wardens: when the aurora hangs over the lake, the dancers dance on the stone terraces of
the east shore, and the curtains move with them, and the Hourkeepers read the hour from the curtains' shape. It is a working, though the dancers will not call it that. The light is
there already; the dancers only ask it to move. A good dancer can make it fold; a great one can make it hang where she likes, across a valley, across a fight. Saga was the best of her
generation at fifteen, and the order's first dancer at twenty-five.

She was eighteen and dancing on the terraces at noon on midsummer day, in daylight, to practise, when the sun fell; and as the sky went white and then dim over Frostfall, the aurora
came out at noon, for the only time in recorded history, and hung over the lake in green and violet for nine heartbeats. The Hourkeepers wrote it in the Noon Folio. Saga, eighteen,
had been the only dancer on the terraces. She has never been sure whether she called it, or it came to her.

Since the Fall the stars are often hidden by the masterless weather, and the aurora has become the Hourkeepers' second clock. Saga dances on every night it shows, and on many it does
not, coaxing it out. She hangs her curtains across a fight the way she hangs them across the sky, and whoever walks through one comes out changed: slowed, silenced, exposed. She is a tall
dancer in deep night-blue with a white fur mantle, silver embroidery like star charts, long pale braids and a crown of thin silver rays.

She lives on the aurora terraces in a long low house of turf and stone with its roof open to the sky in winter, where the dancers of the
order sleep by day and dance by night. She teaches the young dancers, lake-born girls and boys who can feel the light move, and tells them
the first rule of the order: you do not command the light, you invite it, and it can say no. In the hundredth summer the light has said no
more often than in any year since the Fall, and the curtains, when they come, hang lower over the lake, as if they were tired.

[Isolde](isolde.md), the Winter Warden, is her elder sister: the warden of the cold and the dancer of its light, daughters of the same frozen lake. [Lyra](lyra.md), the star archer, is
her partner at the observatory: one charts the stars, the other the lights between them, and on nights the stars are hidden, Lyra reads the hour from Saga's curtains. [Vela](vela.md),
the moth priestess of the Wood, is a friend of the night: two dancers of the night, one who lays the Wood to sleep and one who lights the north; Vela flies north in midwinter to watch
Saga dance, and it is the only time Vela sleeps. And [Kaia](kaia.md), the storm caller of the Spires, is her rival: lightning and the northern light have never shared a sky politely. When
Kaia's storms go north they foul Saga's curtains, and when Saga's curtains come south the Spires get green flashes at sea that sailors take for omens, and blame on her.

## People and appearance

A Northerner of the lake-born, Noon-touched: eighteen at the Fall, she looks thirty-eight. Tall and frost-pale, with long white braids and the star lines of her birth hour drawn in silver
on her jaw; deep night-blue with silver embroidery like star charts, a white fur mantle, a crown of thin silver rays. Her look is not built yet; its brief, which keeps her pale braids as
the lake-born's, is in the [revamp plan](../revamp.md).

## Voice

Light, musical and far away, as if she were speaking from the terraces across the lake. She describes people by their colours. She laughs often and softly.

- **Meeting Isolde:** "Keep the cold, sister. I'll keep it lit."

## Secrets

- She did call the aurora at noon on the day of the Fall. She was trying to call the sun back, as the dancers' oldest song says can be done. The aurora came instead. She has never tried
  again, and never told Isolde.
- The curtains she hangs across a fight do not change whoever walks through them. They show them as they will be. She has learned not to look.
- She has seen an aurora over the Tower, inside the Tear, in colours that are not on any chart.

## What she wants from the Tower

To dance on its top at noon, as she danced on the terraces on the day of the Fall, and finish what she tried: call the sun back. She thinks the aurora came at noon because the sun
answered, and could not come.
