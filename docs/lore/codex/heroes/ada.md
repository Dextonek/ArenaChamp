# Ada, the Artificer

*The Underforge · [Kilnborn](../peoples/kiln.md) · from [the Deep](../realms/deep.md)* · [Heroes](README.md)

## Biography

Ada was born in the seventy-fourth summer after the Fall in a workshop above the Underforge's great lift, the daughter of the lift's engineer: the woman
who kept the iron cages running up and down the shaft from the hilltops to the forge halls on water and counterweights. Ada was born grey, as every Kilnborn
child of her generation was, and grew up in the noise of the lift, with grease to her elbows by five, taking apart anything that would come apart. At eight
she rebuilt the workshop's clock so that it ran on the lift's counterweights and told the time by the slip. At ten she built a mechanical mouse that cleaned the
cages. At twelve she asked to be apprenticed at the great forge, and her mother, who knew what was good for her, sent her to Brannoc.

[Brannoc](brannoc.md), the Ashen Smith, taught her everything about metal: how to draw, temper and fold iron in the sun's own fire, how to read a blade's colour
as it cools, how to listen to an anvil. He taught her nothing about patience, because he had none to teach. She was his apprentice for eight years, and his best,
and the most argumentative person in the forge, which was saying a great deal. In her eighth year, given a hammer to forge as her journeyman piece, she built a
hammer that swung itself: a forge hammer with a clockwork heart and a spring of Caldera steel, which would strike, lift and strike again on its own, at the right
weight, for an hour. Brannoc looked at it for a long time, and said, "That's not smithing," and Ada said, "No, it's better," and walked out of the forge and set up
on her own.

She is the Underforge's artificer now, the Master of Engines in the Hall of Marks: a slim inventor in a copper coat, with a backpack of brass gears and goggles
pushed up on her leather cap, who builds turrets, drones and hooks faster than she can name them. Most of the Underforge's lifts, pumps and lamps are hers, and she
rebuilt her mother's great lift when her mother retired, so that it now runs on steam from the Caldera and carries a hundred people at once. Her workshop hangs over
the shaft on iron struts, and she works with the door open so she can hear the lift. In the Ring she fights with what she built that morning: give her ten seconds
and something to bolt it to.

She thinks the Heart of Noon is the finest engine the Dial has ever had, and she would like to run the Deep on it. Not burn it, as Brannoc wants, and not hang it in
the sky, as the Noonsworn want: harness it, with pipes and turbines and gears, so that its heat drives every lift and pump and lamp from the Deep to the Ring. She has
drawn the plans. They are very large. Brannoc says it is not smithing. Ada says it is better.

She argues engines with [Professor Puff](professor-puff.md), the Windward's aeronaut, at every Truce dinner: two inventors at one table, seated across from each
other by Madame Cleaver on purpose. She wins every argument about engines, and he wins every one about flying, and they have argued for six years whether a balloon
is an engine. She has secretly built a balloon. It works. She has not told him, because then he would win.

In the hundredth summer she has been asked by the Hall of Marks to look at the Underforge's drill, the shaft it is boring from the
Caldera toward the Hall of Noon, which has slowed in rock that the drill-heads will not bite. She went down it alone with a lamp and a
notebook, and came back up two days later with three slivers of glass in her pocket and a face nobody had seen on her before.

## People and appearance

Kilnborn of the great lift, born after the Fall: twenty-six, slim and quick, with a bobbed head of red hair, goggles pushed up on a leather cap, a copper coat, and a
backpack of brass gears that hum. Today her skin is pale. In the [revamp plan](../revamp.md) she gets a touch: the ash-grey skin she was born with, and faint ember
seams at the knuckles from her years at Brannoc's forge.

## Voice

Fast, clipped and technical; she thinks aloud, in numbers, and interrupts herself. She is cheerful in a crisis and bored in peace. She names her machines after
people she is annoyed with.

## Secrets

- The clockwork heart in her self-swinging hammer runs on a sliver of Caldera glass that still holds a little of the sun's fire. She has three more slivers. She
  has not told the Hall of Marks where she got them: from the bottom of the Underforge's drill shaft, from very near the Hall of Noon.
- She has built the balloon. It is moored in a cave above the great lift, and she has been up in it six times.
- She is afraid that if the Heart of Noon is harnessed, it will go out. She has run the numbers four times.

## What she wants from the Tower

To see the engine inside it. Anything that keeps a sun up, or held one, or drinks one, is a machine, and she would very much like to look at the gears.

## Ties

- **[Professor Puff](professor-puff.md)**, rival: two inventors at one table; she wins every argument about engines, he wins every one about flying.
- **[Brannoc](brannoc.md)**, her teacher: he taught her everything about metal, and she built a hammer that swung itself.
