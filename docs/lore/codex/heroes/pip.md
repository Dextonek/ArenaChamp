# Pip, the Wingfoot

*The Truce of the Ring · [Aubade folk](../peoples/aubade.md) · from [the Ring](../realms/tower.md)* · [Heroes](README.md)

## Biography

Pip was born in the couriers' loft above the Ring's north stand in the eighty-first summer after the Fall, to an Aubade courier who ran
the Dawn Road and a Coast boatwoman who ran the Truce's ferries. His given name is Pellam-of-the-Stair, after the Dawn Stair where his
mother's family lived; nobody has called him that since he could walk, because he did not walk. He ran.

He grew up in the Ringside the way the Ringside's children do: under the stands, in the kitchens of the Truce Table, between the legs of
champions. He carried messages for coppers from the time he was six: a wager to the Tally-house, a love letter from a Coast oarsman to a
Gloaming glazier, a challenge from a Dune house to an Aubade bell quarter. He was fast, honest and incurious, which is the whole of a
courier's art: he never opened a letter, never lost one, and never asked what was in one, even when it was on fire.

A challenge to the Ring is not real until it is delivered by hand, and the Truce's couriers carry them across the whole Dial: sealed
scrolls from countess to king, from the Deep to the white north. At fourteen Pip was the youngest courier the Truce had ever sworn, in a
courier's pack wider than he was. At sixteen he was the fastest. At seventeen he did the thing that made his name: a challenge had to
reach the Hourkeepers' Council at Frostfall from the Dune Throne at Tamerit, corner to corner of the Dial, before the next midsummer's
reading, which was two days away. Every courier said it could not be done. Pip crossed the whole Dial in a day and a night, through the
Storm Canyon, round the Tower, up the Frostfall road and over the moors, and put the scroll in the Council's hands as the Nail rose.

The Truce gave him gold wings for his circlet and his heels, and the title of First Courier, and the right to wear the Truce's colours
in the Ring. He is the youngest of the Truce's officers and by far the fastest. He has never been paid on time, which he holds against
[Mr. Ledger](mr-ledger.md), who writes the bills Pip delivers and has never once tipped him. They are comrades, in their way: Pip is the
only person who goes into the Tally-house without knocking, and Ledger is the only person who knows Pip's real name.

His rival is [Zephyr](zephyr.md) of the Spires, the fastest blade on the Dial against the fastest feet. They met when Zephyr, on the wind,
overtook Pip on the Dawn Road and laughed; Pip caught him at the Waymeet and did not laugh. They have raced across the Dial every spring
since, and the score is even, which both of them find intolerable.

He sleeps in the couriers' loft where he was born, under a map of the Dial covered in pins, one for every delivery, so thick in places
that the realms underneath have disappeared. He knows every road, every ferry, every short cut through the Wood that the Wood has not yet
moved, and the hours each realm keeps this month by the Slip-sheet, which he learns the way other boys learn songs. The other couriers
call him the Wingfoot, after the gold at his heels, and bet on him. The Truce has never asked him to slow down.

He fights in the Ring to settle races, mostly, and once to deliver a challenge to a champion who would not take it any other way. He is
not a great fighter and knows it. He wins by being somewhere else.

Now he carries more letters than ever. Every faction is writing to every other about the Climb, and some of the letters he carries are
addressed to people on the Climb's floors, people who have been in the Tower for a hundred years. He has delivered three. Nobody has
told him how.

## People and appearance

Aubade folk by his father, born at the Ring: young, wiry and freckled, with ginger hair and the eager face of someone who is always about
to leave. A deep blue capelet, a leather courier's pack with sealed scrolls and a glowing seal, a gold winged circlet and gold wings at the
heels, and a sealed scroll in a raised hand. In the [revamp plan](../revamp.md) he gets a touch: the golden-brown skin of his people with
his freckles kept, and his hair bound with gold thread, as a courier who keeps his oaths.

## Voice

Quick, bright and out of breath, even when he is standing still. He talks in deliveries: "for you", "sign here", "no reply needed". He is
very cheerful about bad news, because it is not his.

## Voice lines

- **Picked:** "Sign here. And here. And there, where it's on fire."
- **Picked:** "Special delivery! For you. It's a punch."
- **Winning:** "Delivered. No reply needed."
- **Winning:** "First again. Somebody write that down."
- **Losing:** "Return to sender."
- **Losing:** "Delayed. Not lost. Never lost."
- **Meeting Zephyr:** "Spring's a long way off. Want to race now?"
- **Meeting Mr. Ledger:** "Your bills are all out, sir. Any chance of a tip?"

## Secrets

- One letter he carried was addressed in his own handwriting, from a floor of the Climb he has never been to. He has not opened it. He
  never opens letters.
- He is afraid of standing still. When he stops, he can feel the slip of the hours, and it makes him sick.
- He let Zephyr win one race, the year Zephyr's mother died. Zephyr does not know.

## What he wants from the Tower

To run it, top to bottom, faster than anyone. And to deliver the letter in his pack to whoever it is for.

## Ties

- **[Mr. Ledger](mr-ledger.md)**, comrade: Pip delivers every bill Ledger writes, and has never once been tipped.
- **[Zephyr](zephyr.md)**, rival: the fastest feet against the fastest blade; they race across the Dial every spring and the score is even.
