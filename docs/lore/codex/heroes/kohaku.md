# Kohaku, the Foxfire

*The Court of Dusk · [Briarkin](../peoples/briar.md), the Fox court · from [Gloaming](../realms/gloaming.md)* · [Heroes](README.md) · new, on the cards

## Biography

The Fox court is the strangest of the Briarkin's courts and the only one that does not live in the Walking Wood: it keeps to the thickets and
valleys round Gloaming, where the dusk is long and the night roads run, and has done since before the Long Count. Its people are foxes by night
and people by day, or the other way round, depending on whom you ask; nine-tailed in the old stories and one-tailed in daylight; and they live by
taking names. A Briarkin will not give its true name, because a name given can be taken, and the Fox court is why.

Kohaku is a fox of that court, and she will not say how old she is, because the answer is strange. She was a girl of the court three hundred years before
the Fall, at a midsummer revel at the Tower's foot that the Tower took whole, and she fell out of the Tower on the morning after the Fall, when its broken grip
on the sun shook its oldest keepings loose, into a Fox court that no longer remembered her. She looks perhaps twenty-five, and has looked it since at least the War of the
Banners, when the Ninefold knight Sir Wystan of Nine (whom the Ring now calls [Sir Whiskers](sir-whiskers.md)) first saw her steal a crimson captain's
battle-cry off his lips in the middle of a charge, so that he opened his mouth and nothing came out. Her use-name means amber, for the colour of her
eyes and her foxfire, and she has others.

She steals names. Say a working aloud near her, its name, the word that calls it, and she can take it: you forget it for a while, and she casts it back
at you in blue foxfire. She steals other names too, the names of things and people, which is worse; the Gloaming folk say that if a fox spirit says your
name three times in your hearing you will forget it for a night, and wake not knowing who you are. The Court of Dusk tolerates her because she steals
mostly from its enemies, and pays her in what she likes best: secrets, cream, and an audience.

She once stole the word from the brow of [Hesk](hesk.md)'s first golem. Hesk of the Underforge writes words on the brows of clay and iron bodies, and a
golem obeys whoever wrote the last word on it; Kohaku, visiting the Deep for reasons of her own in the sixtieth summer, read the word, liked it, and took it.
The golem stopped, turned, and walked into the black water of the Deep, and was never seen again. Hesk has made a hundred golems since and keeps them closer,
and has sworn to have the word back. Kohaku says she cannot give it back; she has forgotten which one it was.

Two others on the Dial are her particular game. [Morwen](morwen.md), the Truce's Saltwarden, pours a line of salt round the Ring every dawn, and a line of
salt is the one fence a Briarkin cannot cross. Kohaku has tried every night for nine years to find a gap in it, to prove she can, and has left her paw prints
along it right up to the edge. And [Arden](arden.md), the Huntmaster of the Moot, who has caught every fox in the Walking Wood except one, and whom she has
fooled exactly once, and never again. They meet in the thickets every few months and he tries to catch her, and she lets him get close, and they both enjoy it
more than they would admit.

And Sir Whiskers, who has been trying to catch her for longer than either will admit, and whom she has been letting. They met in the War, on opposite banners;
he had just spent his second life of the new count the Noon Grace had given him, and she stole the name of that life, so that he could not remember what had killed him, which
he considered the most charming thing anyone had ever done to him. He has chased her since. She stays just out of reach.

She wears white and pale grey with a rust-red sash and a carved fox mask pushed to the side of her head; she wears the mask when she is about to be honest, and
nobody has seen her wear it.

## People and appearance

A Briarkin of the Fox court: slim and quick, with long pointed ears, amber eyes of one colour from lid to lid, and one fox tail by day; white and pale grey
with a rust-red sash, a carved fox mask pushed to the side of her head, blue foxfire at her fingers. Her look is not built yet; its brief, which adds the tail
and the eyes of her court, is in the [revamp plan](../revamp.md).

## Voice

Light, amused and musical, with a habit of repeating the last thing you said in your own voice. She never says a name she has not stolen. She laughs at the
wrong moments, on purpose.

- **Meeting Morwen:** "There's always a gap, salt-witch. I'll find it."

## Secrets

- She remembers exactly which word she took from Hesk's golem. It was her own true name, written there by someone who should not have known it. She took it
  back. She is afraid of who wrote it.
- She is older than the Fox court's oldest memory, because she spent three hundred years of it in the Tower, at one night of a revel. In year 40 she found the door
  her brother was behind, the thirty-first of the Hundred Doors, and when Wick opened it she stole her brother's true name as he stepped out, so that the Tower could
  never call him back. He is The Great Zambini. The rest of the revel is still inside.
- She has let Sir Whiskers catch her once, at the Masque of Houses, in a mask. He did not know it was her.

## What she wants from the Tower

Its name. Everything has one, and nobody has ever said the Tower's.
