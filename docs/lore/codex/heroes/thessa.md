# Thessa, the Sky-Stitcher

*The Hourkeepers · [Northerners](../peoples/north.md) · from [Frostfall](../realms/frostfall.md)* · [Heroes](README.md) · new, on the cards

## Biography

Above the Tower, where the sun hung, the sky is torn: the Tear, black with a burning pink edge, a hand's breadth wider every year. Thessa means to sew it shut.

She was born in Hearthmere in the sixty-fourth summer after the Fall, the daughter of a sail-maker who had come north from the Coast and married a Northern Reader, and she grew up
with a needle in her hand. At eight her mother, who could see the girl's stitches were better than her own, walked her down the Frostfall road to the stone cottage at the Wood's edge
where, everyone in Hearthmere knew, the best needlewoman on the Dial lived: [Grandma Ironknit](grandma-ironknit.md), the Hourkeepers' Weaver. Ironknit looked at the girl's stitches,
said "hm", and took her in for the winter. Thessa stayed nine years.

She learned to sew at Ironknit's knee: cloth, then leather, then silk, then the things Ironknit sews that are not cloth at all. At fourteen she sewed a tear in a sail so well that the
sail would not tear again anywhere near the seam. At fifteen she sewed a split in a cracked bowl, with a gold thread, and the bowl held water. At sixteen, bored, she sewed two places:
the cottage's back door and the well at the bottom of the garden, a running stitch of golden thread from the doorframe to the well's rim, and when she pulled it tight, the well was
just outside the back door, and has been since. Ironknit looked at it for a long time and said, "Well. That's that, then. Cloth is too easy for you." From then on she taught her to
sew places.

Thessa sews places, not cloth: two points of the Dial drawn close with a golden thread, so that what happens at one happens at the other. A seam can be short or long, a stitch or a
journey; it holds as long as the thread holds, and her thread, spun by Ironknit, holds a long time. The Hourkeepers asked for her at seventeen, and she went back to Frostfall as their
seamstress of the sky: the one who sews the Dial back together where the Fall has pulled it apart. She has sewn a ford across a river that the Walking Wood moved, a pass across a
landslide in the southern hills, and a lane between the observatory and the library so that the Readers no longer have to cross the ice in winter.

She is slim and grave, in a night-blue coat stitched with silver seams, a gold thimble on her finger and a silver needle as long as her arm. And since the twentieth year of her life
she has been sewing the Tear. She climbs to the Ring's highest stand at night, where the Tear is closest, and casts her golden thread up into the dark, and sews a stitch across the
burning edge, and pulls; and the Tear closes, a little. And the next night the stitch is gone, unpicked, and the Tear is a little wider than before. [Oblivion](oblivion.md) has
unpicked every stitch she has put in it. She keeps sewing.

[Wick](wick.md), the Doorwarden of the Ring, is her friend: he keeps the doors and she keeps the seams, and they agree that the Tear is neither. They meet once a month at the Waymeet,
the old inn where the Frostfall road crosses the Dawn Road, and compare notes over salt tea: which doors were warm, which stitches held, how much wider the Tear has grown. This year
they have started to meet twice a month.

## People and appearance

A Northerner of Hearthmere, born after the Fall: thirty-six, slim and grave, with deep brown skin, hair gone white early in many thin braids, and the star lines of her birth hour on her
jaw; a night-blue coat stitched with silver seams, a gold thimble, and a silver needle as long as her arm with a golden thread. Her look is not built yet; its brief is in the
[revamp plan](../revamp.md).

## Voice

Quiet, steady and exact, the voice of someone counting stitches; she speaks in short sentences and finishes every one. She is gentle with everyone and immovable about the Tear.

## Voice lines

- **Picked:** "Here and there. Now they are the same place."
- **Picked:** "One stitch at a time."
- **Winning:** "Sewn shut."
- **Winning:** "Tight. It'll hold."
- **Losing:** "A stitch pulled. I'll redo it."
- **Losing:** "The thread is still in my hand."
- **Meeting Oblivion:** "I'll sew it again tonight. And tomorrow."
- **Meeting Wick:** "Was a door warm last night? Which one?"

## Secrets

- The stitches she puts in the Tear are not unpicked from outside. She has watched, through Lyra's lens, and they are unpicked from inside the Tear, by small, careful fingers.
- The lane she sewed between the observatory and the library passes, for one step, through somewhere that is not Frostfall. Everyone who uses it feels it. Nobody has asked.
- She has sewn one thread to the Tower's top. It is still there. Something at the other end pulls it, sometimes, gently, three times.

## What she wants from the Tower

To reach the top, where the Tear is nearest, and sew it shut from close to; and to find whoever is unpicking her stitches, and ask them to stop.

## Ties

- **[Oblivion](oblivion.md)**, sworn enemy: she sews the Tear shut; it unpicks every stitch.
- **[Wick](wick.md)**, friend: he keeps the doors and she keeps the seams, and they agree that the Tear is neither.
- **[Grandma Ironknit](grandma-ironknit.md)**, her student: Ironknit taught her to sew, and to sew places once cloth was too easy.
