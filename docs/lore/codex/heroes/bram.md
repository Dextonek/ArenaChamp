# Bram, the Stonewright

*The Underforge · [Kilnborn](../peoples/kiln.md) · from [the Deep](../realms/deep.md)* · [Heroes](README.md)

## Biography

Bram was born in the masons' halls of the Deep eighteen years before the Fall, the son of a mason, the grandson of a mason, and so back further than the family
can count: his family's maker's mark, a square with a dot in its corner, is cut on pillars in the Deep's oldest halls, the ones the mole-folk say they dug first.
He was a broad, calm, slow-spoken boy who liked stone because it waited. He learned to cut at six, to dress at ten, to set at fourteen, and to build at sixteen,
and he has built the halls of the Underforge stone by stone ever since.

He has one friend, and the friend is a pillar. Gerald is a limestone pillar about as tall as Bram, with a carved face, a grumpy one, on one side. Bram found him at
eighteen, on the day of the Fall, in the oldest gallery of the masons' halls, where the shaking of the rock had brought down a wall and exposed a pillar that was not
on any plan, older than the masons' oldest marks, older than the Deep, Bram says, with a face cut into it that nobody had cut. Bram put his hand on it to steady
himself as the ground moved, and the face looked at him, and said, quite clearly, "Mind your feet." Nobody else has ever heard Gerald speak. Bram has never doubted him.

Gerald goes everywhere with him. Bram carries him, or rolls him, or, in the Ring, sets him down, and Gerald stands there being stone in the most useful place possible:
a wall, a pillar to hide behind, a stone that topples. Bram is broad and calm, with carved limestone pauldrons, a leather apron, a rune-cut maul and a glowing amber rune
on his chest, his mark, cut on his apron's bib in a gold ring. "Gerald says hello," he says before a bout, and, after, "Gerald says goodbye," and he is not joking.

He carved the ledge on the Night Temple's eaves in Gloaming in the fiftieth summer, for a statue the Court commissioned and never made, and twenty years later the Court
set a guard turned to stone on that ledge, and at dusk the guard woke: [Corbel](corbel.md), the Stonewing. Bram went to Gloaming to see what had happened to his ledge,
and found the stone had grown into the guard and the guard into the stone, and that Corbel had wings Bram had carved in the niche. They are, oddly, friends. Bram visits
once a year, and they talk on the eaves until morning about the grain of rock.

Every year at the Ring's opening he pushes the same great stone against [Big Yoko](big-yoko.md), the Mountain: a block of the Ring's own foundation that the founders
set in the sand in year 3 as a test of strength. Neither has ever moved it farther than the other. Bram considers Yoko the only man on the Dial who understands that a
stone is not an enemy.

Two of his fellow craftsmen offend him. [Hesk](hesk.md), the golem-maker, writes words on the brows of clay and iron bodies and makes them obey: Bram thinks a friend
should be made of stone and should choose you, and that a servant made of clay who obeys is a sad thing, and Hesk thinks that is very sentimental. And
[Tova](tova.md), the sapper, who knocks down what Bram builds, with powder; he builds what she knocks down, and she knocks down what he builds, and they have rebuilt and
re-demolished the same gallery of the old Deep four times out of principle.

## People and appearance

Kilnborn of the masons' halls, and Noon-touched: eighteen at the Fall, he looks thirty-eight. Broad and calm, with a square-jawed face under iron-grey hair, carved
limestone pauldrons, a leather apron with the amber rune of his mark glowing on its bib, a rune-cut stone maul; and Gerald. Today his skin is pale. In the
[revamp plan](../revamp.md) he gets a touch: the ash-grey skin and pale amber eyes of his people; Gerald is unchanged.

## Voice

Slow, deep and even, with long pauses; he speaks for Gerald as well as himself, and Gerald is ruder. He never raises his voice. He talks to stone as if it were
listening, because he thinks it is.

## Voice lines

- **Picked:** "Gerald says hello. Gerald says goodbye."
- **Picked:** "Stand behind Gerald. He doesn't mind."
- **Winning:** "Set in stone."
- **Winning:** "Gerald says you were very brave."
- **Losing:** "Cracked. Stone cracks. It doesn't break."
- **Losing:** "Gerald says get up. I'm getting up."
- **Meeting Big Yoko:** "The stone again, Mountain. Same as every year."
- **Meeting Tova:** "Don't you dare. I just finished that."

## Secrets

- Gerald has told him what the pillar was before the Deep was dug: a marker, one of nine, set in a ring round the Tower's foot, before anyone remembers. Bram has looked
  for the other eight. He has found three, one of them under the Ring.
- He can hear stone, a little, as Corbel can. The Tower's stone is silent. It frightens him.
- He let Tova win the gallery the fourth time.

## What he wants from the Tower

To touch its stone and find out who cut it. Every stone has a mason; even Gerald. Bram has never seen a seam in the Tower, and he would like to know how it was done.

## Ties

- **[Big Yoko](big-yoko.md)**, rival: every year at the Ring's opening they push the same stone, and neither has moved it farther than the other.
- **[Corbel](corbel.md)**, friend: Bram carved the ledge where Corbel woke, and the stone remembers its mason.
- **[Hesk](hesk.md)**, rival: a friend made of stone who chooses you, against a servant made of clay who obeys.
- **[Tova](tova.md)**, rival: he builds what she knocks down, and she knocks down what he builds.
