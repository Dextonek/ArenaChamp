# Anka, the Firebird

*The Noonsworn · [the Fallborn](../peoples/fallborn.md), an Ember · from [the Drowned Coast](../realms/coast.md)* · [Heroes](README.md)

## Biography

When the sun broke at noon on midsummer day a hundred years ago, it threw sparks across the Dial. Most of them went out where they fell:
in the Sands, where they turned to glass; in the Wood, where they set small fires the Briarkin put out; in the sea. One of them fell into
the surf off the Drowned Coast, a mile south of Sallowmouth, where a girl was dancing on the shore with a fan of gull feathers, as the Coast
girls do at midsummer for the turning of the tide. The spark went into the water. It did not go out.

The next morning, while the sun rose thin and late from the Tower and the drowned harbour lay under the water, a woman walked out of the
surf: barefoot, in ember-red and gold, with a fan of feathers that burned at the tips and did not burn away. She did not know her name. She
did not know what she was. She knew how to dance, because the first thing she had seen, from inside the water, was the girl on the shore,
and she had taken her shape from her the way an Ember takes its shape from its first sight. The girl had drowned in the flood that
afternoon. Anka has carried her face for a hundred years, and has never stopped feeling that she owes it back.

The Coast folk called her the sea's fire and were afraid of her. The Noonsworn, when word reached Aubade, called her the sun's promise: proof
that what falls can rise. They brought her to the white city and gave her a name, Anka, from an old cliff word for the bird that sings at
dawn, and a place among the Friends of the Dawn. She has served the Noonsworn since, gladly. She is not sure she believes everything they do.
She believes in rising.

Because she does rise. She is fragile, more fragile than she looks: cold hurts her, water held long enough puts her out, and a blade goes
into her as into anyone. When she dies, she burns. Her body goes up in a column of fire and leaves embers on the ground, and from the embers,
after a time, she rises, whole, about twenty years old in a human's eyes, remembering every life. She always knows where she will come back,
because she leaves her embers on the ground for it, and in the Ring she plants them on purpose. She has burned and risen many times since the
first morning. Each time it takes a little longer.

[Mortis](mortis.md), the Ossuary Lord, has buried her three times. He considers it his duty: the dead should stay where they are put, and an
Ember that will not stay in the ground offends everything the Long Dead keep. The first time was in year 40, after a duel on the Field
of Banners, when he buried her ashes in a lead box under Gloaming's ossuaries; she rose in the box, melted it, and walked out through the Night
Temple during the Naming. The second and third times were more careful. She has never stayed. He fights in the Ring for the chance to bury her
a fourth time, and she fights him because she will not be kept.

The Dune Throne's judge of the dead, [Nebet](nebet.md), weighs every royal heart in the Sands against a single feather in her scale, and the
feather in her pan, they say, is one of Anka's, picked up from the surf on the morning after the Fall. Nebet would like a fresh one, from the
bird herself, and has said so in public. Anka has said that if Nebet wants a feather she can come and take it, and that she will need a very
long arm.

She climbs to find the rest of the fire she came from: the Heart of Noon, and Ash and Ember in the Cinder Pit, who are, she believes, her
elder siblings. She wants to give it back to the sky. She has reached the Wood's crown, and stood in front of Ash and Ember, and they knew her,
and she did not fight them. She came down, and burned that night for no reason, and rose late.

## People and appearance

The first and best known of the [Embers](../peoples/fallborn.md), the sparks of the sun that took a shape. She looks twenty: a dancer with
warm skin, dark-red hair cut at the jaw, a gold circlet with a three-feather crest and a turquoise stone. An ember-red bodice, a layered skirt of
flame-coloured scalloped feathers, a mantle of red and gold feather wings at the arms, an open feather fan in her hand. In the
[revamp plan](../revamp.md) she gets a touch: a warm glow at the edges of her skin and the roots of her hair, so that she reads as an Ember
and not as a woman in a feather dress.

## Voice

Light, quick and teasing, with a laugh just under every sentence; she speaks the Coast's Ring-speech with Aubade's formal words, and talks
to fire as if it were a person. When she is about to burn she goes very quiet.

## Voice lines

- **Losing:** "Not out. Just resting."
- **Meeting Mortis:** "A fourth box? You never learn."
- **Meeting Nebet:** "You want a feather? Reach."

## Secrets

- She is slower to rise each time. Twenty heartbeats the first time; nearly a minute now. She thinks that when the old sun's light is gone
  from the Dial she will not rise at all, and she has not told the Noonsworn.
- She remembers the girl on the shore: her name was Ama, and she was nine. Anka has visited Ama's mother's grave every midsummer.
- Ash and Ember spoke to her at the Wood's crown. They said: "Not yet."

## What she wants from the Tower

To find the rest of the fire she came from, and give it back to the sky. And, though she does not say it, to find out whether an Ember can
choose to go home.

## Ties

- **[Mortis](mortis.md)**, sworn enemy: he keeps the dead in the ground; she will not stay there. He has buried her three times.
- **[Nebet](nebet.md)**, rival: Nebet weighs hearts against one of Anka's feathers, and would like a fresh one from the bird herself.
