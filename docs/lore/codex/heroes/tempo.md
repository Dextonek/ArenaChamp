# Tempo, the Unwound Hour

*The Hourkeepers · [Northerners](../peoples/north.md) · from [Frostfall](../realms/frostfall.md)* · [Heroes](README.md)

## Biography

Tempo was born in Hearthmere at the south end of Frostfall Lake, forty years before the Fall, the son of a clockmaker, and he was a clockmaker himself by twenty:
a careful, quiet, white-haired young Northerner (the Northerners go white early) who could take apart any mechanism on the Dial and put it back together a little
better. At thirty-five the Hourkeepers made him keeper of the great clock in the observatory's central hall, the clock that told the Dial's true time by the
Tower's shadow on a brass dial fed by a mirror from the dome. It was the finest clock on the Dial. It had not lost a second in four hundred years.

He was forty and standing at the great clock at noon on midsummer day, watching the shadow cross the brass, when the shadow stopped. He remembers reaching out to
touch the dial, as if the clock had stuck, and then he remembers nothing, and then he remembers reaching out to touch the dial. He did it twice. Everyone in the hall
saw him do it twice. He has done everything twice since.

The Hourkeepers call him an **unwound hour**: a man who fell out of time at the moment of the Fall, and has lived every moment twice since. The truth is plainer:
when the Tower closed its hand on the hour of noon he was, for once, a heartbeat behind his own clock, and it took half of him. He is not a ghost or an
echo; he is one man, living twice. Every step he takes, he takes again a heartbeat later, and the first step and the second are both real. Every word he says, he says
again, and both are heard, though most people learn not to notice. Every working he casts plays again. He has lived a hundred years since the Fall, every moment twice,
which makes two hundred, and he looks forty, because the Noon-touched age slowly and he is not sure which of his two lives to count.

He keeps the great clock still. Since the Fall it has had no shadow to read, and he has rebuilt it to keep the slip instead: it measures how far the Dial's hours have
slipped from where they should be, and the Hourkeepers' monthly Slip-sheet is read from its face. He wears a long dark coat with an ivory dial plate on the shoulder,
carries a staff shaped like a minute hand, and has a clock-dial halo, rings of radium numerals in chartreuse, that hangs behind his head and ticks. He races the dark
through the Deep for practice, because the dark there has no hours at all, and he wants to know if he can outrun it twice.

Living twice is lonely. His wife, a lens-grinder of Hearthmere, left him in the third year after the Fall, kindly, saying that she could
bear being told everything twice but not being kissed twice and only feeling one. His friends grew old at the normal rate and died, and
he attended every funeral twice. He lives now in a room off the great clock's hall, with two of everything (two cups, two chairs, two
pens), and the Readers bring him tea, two cups, and drink the second one themselves so that he will not feel the lack.

He has sworn to wind the Dial up again before [Oblivion](oblivion.md) unmakes the hours altogether. The thing that came through the Tear on the day of the Fall makes
things unable to end; Tempo believes it is what stopped the shadow, and that it is slowly stopping time itself. They have fought in the Ring nine times. Tempo has won
four, twice each. He works with [Shiori](shiori.md), the library's great calligrapher: he keeps the hours and she writes them down, and both are always a little behind.
She is the only person on the Dial who can write down both of his sentences at once.

## People and appearance

A Northerner of Hearthmere, and Noon-touched, or something stranger: he looks forty, with white hair swept back, a monocle, a long dark coat with an ivory dial plate on
the shoulder, the minute-hand staff in the left hand, and the glowing clock dial behind his head. His skin is the umber of his people, and their star lines run
along his jaw drawn as a clock's ticks (Touched on 2026-11-16 by the [revamp plan](../revamp.md): he was pale before).

## Voice

Calm, precise and doubled: every line is said twice, the second a heartbeat behind the first, which listeners learn to hear as one voice with an echo. He speaks in
measurements. He is patient with everyone, because he has time.

## Secrets

- The second Tempo, the one a heartbeat behind, is slowly falling further behind. A heartbeat in year 1; nearly two now. He thinks that when the gap is wide enough, there
  will be two of him, and he does not know which one will be himself.
- The great clock still has the shadow's last position on its dial, scratched into the brass at the moment it stopped. It does not point at noon. It points nine heartbeats
  past it.
- He has seen himself on the Climb's War floors, a heartbeat ahead, going up.

## What he wants from the Tower

To wind it. If the Tower was the Dial's clock, it can be wound; and he means to find the key before Oblivion finds the last hour.

## Ties

- **[Oblivion](oblivion.md)**, sworn enemy: one keeps the hours; the other wants none left.
- **[Shiori](shiori.md)**, comrade: he keeps the hours and she writes them down, and both are always a little behind.
