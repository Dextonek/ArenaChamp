# Ramet, the Lion of the Gate

*The Dune Throne · [the Ninefold](../peoples/ninefold.md), a lion · from [the Sands](../realms/sands.md)* · [Heroes](README.md) · new, on the cards

## Biography

The gate of Tamerit, in the north wall of the city of the Throne, is kept by a great lion of sandstone and gold, the size of a house, that lies across the gate by day and
wakes when its rider sits on its back. It was carved in the first king's reign, the tomb paintings say, and woken by a working the Sands have lost, and for six hundred years
its rider has been the captain of the gate: one of the Five Offices of the Throne. For the last three generations the captains have been lions of the Ninefold.

The lion line of the Ninefold came to the Sands about eighty years before the Fall, when a lion knight of the Keep of Nine swore an oath to a king of the Sands who had saved
her life, and was given the gate. Her son kept it after her, and her grandson after him. Ramet is the grandson. He was born in the gatehouse of Tamerit twenty-five years
before the Fall, gold-furred and green-eyed, with a mane that came in early, and grew up riding the stone lion as other children ride ponies. At twenty he swore the Ninefold's
oath to the Throne, as his grandmother had, and took the gate.

He was twenty-six and on the gate on the night in year 1 when Sefu took the crown from his sister in the Throne Hall at midnight. Asenet came to the gate in the dark, alone,
with the court's guard behind her and the river towns in arms, and laid her curse on the crown she no longer wore, standing in the gate's shadow; and then she looked at Ramet.
He opened the gate and let her go. He has never told anyone why, and Sefu has never asked him. Asenet owes him for it, and has never paid. The Throne kept him on the gate,
because a Ninefold's oath is to the Throne and not to its king, and because nobody else could ride the lion.

He is the Throne's gate captain, a strong, steady lion in gold-banded armour with a long spear, and he has kept the gate for ninety-nine years, Noon-touched, looking now about
forty-five. He has spent two of his nine lives on it: one to a raider chief of the Storm Canyon in year 30, and one in the great storm of year 61, holding the gate shut against
the sand. In the Ring he fights on the lion's back; break the lion and he fights on foot, and he is still a captain.

The gatehouse of Tamerit is his home and his charge: a tower of sandstone over the north gate, with the stone lion asleep across the
arch below it and a long room at the top where the captains of the gate have kept their spears for six centuries. Ramet keeps it as his
grandmother did, with nine copper hooks by the door for the line's nine lives and a bowl of water always full on the step for whoever
comes to the gate thirsty, at any hour. The Dune folk of the river towns bring him their quarrels as they would bring them to a judge,
because he is the only officer of the Throne who has never once taken a side in the court. Children dare each other to touch the stone
lion's paw. It has never minded.

Since the Climb opened he has been asked to take the Throne's champions up the Sands' chapter, and has done so twice, as far as the
Brass Sphinx on the twenty-fourth floor: a lion of brass with a woman's face, from the gate of a city the Sands buried long ago, who
asked him her riddle. He answered it. She did not let him pass anyway. He considers this the worst manners he has met in a century.

Every year at the Homecoming he races [Zephyr](zephyr.md), the Spire-born wind blade, across the Glass Flats, the lion on the glass against the boy on the wind. The lion has
never lost on sand. Zephyr has come within a length twice, and Ramet, who likes him, has started to worry about the third time.

## People and appearance

A lion of the Ninefold, of the line that keeps the gate of Tamerit, and Noon-touched: twenty-five at the Fall, he looks forty-five. A great gold-furred lion's head with a long
dark-gold mane braided with copper rings, green eyes, fur at the throat and hands, gold-banded armour over Dune linen, a long spear in the right hand; nine copper rings on the
spear's cord, two of them blackened. His card drew him as a man in a lion-mane hood; in the [revamp plan](../revamp.md) his look brief makes him a lion of the Ninefold.

## Voice

Deep, warm and formal, with a growl under it he cannot help; he speaks like a soldier and a courtier both. He never hurries and never boasts. He calls everyone by their rank.

## Voice lines

- **Picked:** "The gate is shut. Climb over me."
- **Picked:** "Captain of the gate. State your business."
- **Winning:** "The gate holds."
- **Winning:** "Turned back. With my compliments."
- **Losing:** "A life spent. Well spent."
- **Losing:** "The lion falls. The captain stands."
- **Meeting Zephyr:** "The Flats are hot this year, wind-boy. Bring water."
- **Meeting Asenet:** "Majesty. The gate is still open. For you."

## Secrets

- He opened the gate because Asenet asked him to with the old courtesy of the Ninefold, by his grandmother's name, which only she at court knew. She had been his grandmother's
  friend. He would open it again.
- The stone lion is not stone. It is the first lion of the line, who spent her ninth life holding the gate, and did not lie down.
- He has seven lives left, and he means to spend the next one on Asenet.

## What he wants from the Tower

To keep its gate. He believes the Tower's gate opens only for a champion of the Ring because no one has been set to keep it, and he would like the post.

## Ties

- **[Zephyr](zephyr.md)**, rival: the wind and the lion race across the Glass Flats every year, and the lion has never lost on sand.
- **[Asenet](asenet.md)**, owed by her: he opened the gate and let her go free the night her brother took the crown.
