# Shiori, the Last Brush

*The Hourkeepers · [Northerners](../peoples/north.md) · from [Frostfall](../realms/frostfall.md)* · [Heroes](README.md)

## Biography

The Hourkeepers write the Dial down before it changes. The frozen library under Frostfall Lake holds the record of every year since the Long Count began, in ink that the
cold keeps from fading, and for six hundred years its finest writing has been done in the library's eastern script: an old brush-script from a far shore of the Outer Sea that
a shipwrecked scribe brought to Frostfall in the library's first century, and which the Hourkeepers kept because nothing else could write so much so precisely. Its masters
are called the great brushes. There were twelve before the Fall, and the work of a great brush, the Hourkeepers say, is not only precise but true: what a great brush writes
comes a little true.

Shiori was born in Hearthmere in the seventieth summer after the Fall, to a lens-grinder and a Reader of the observatory, and given a scribe-name at birth because her
grandmother, who was the last great brush then living, held her and said so. Her grandmother taught her the brush at three. By seven she could write the library's script as
well as any Reader. By twelve she could write a stroke that did not dry: her ink lay wet on the paper for a day, and the paper under it changed. By fifteen her grandmother was
dead, and Shiori was the last of the great brushes, and the library's.

She is the Hourkeepers' great calligrapher, the one whose writing comes true. She wears a rice-paper robe with its hem washed in ink and an indigo sash, long black hair held by
a brush-shaped pin, and carries a brush as tall as she is, with paper talismans floating round her. She writes the year's book on the Longest Night. She writes the Slip-sheet
from Tempo's clock. And in the Ring her strokes lie wet on the floor, and where two cross, the ink bursts: what she writes on the world, the world must read.

She lives in the library itself, in a cell cut into the ice at its western end with a window onto the lake's underside, where the light
comes through green and the fish pass. She writes there by lamplight eleven hours a day. The Readers bring her the year's records, and she
copies them into the library's book in the eastern script, and once a month she walks up the stair to the observatory to watch Tempo's
clock and write the Slip-sheet. She has left Frostfall four times in her life, every time to the Ring, every time for a duel, and every
time she has come home and written the duel down before she slept.

She has taught two people outside the library, both in a single year, and both have made something of it. [Lian](lian.md) of the Spires came north at fourteen to learn paper,
and Shiori taught her to fold: a fold made once, cleanly, a figure from a single sheet, no cut, no glue. Lian went home and folded a dragon. And [Hesk](hesk.md) of the Underforge
came north at forty-five to learn a better script for his golems' brows, and Shiori, sixteen, wrote him one word; the golem it was put on stood up and bowed. The word on every
golem's brow in the Underforge is in her hand now.

She thinks [Mr. Ledger](mr-ledger.md)'s book is the worst-written history on the Dial: every debt and no story, every sum and no picture. He thinks hers has too many pictures and
no totals. They meet every year at the Truce dinner to compare records, and part in perfect mutual contempt, and Shiori has copied every page of the Tally that he has let her see,
in her own hand, with illustrations. And she works with [Tempo](tempo.md), the unwound hour: he keeps the hours and she writes them down, and both are always a little behind.
She is the only person who can write down both of his sentences at once, and she writes them in two colours.

## People and appearance

A Northerner of Hearthmere, born after the Fall: thirty, with long black hair held by a brush-shaped pin, a rice-paper white robe with the hem washed in ink, an indigo sash, a
huge brush in the left hand, and floating paper talismans. In the [revamp plan](../revamp.md) she gets a touch: the star lines of her birth hour drawn in ink on the backs of her
hands, as every Northerner's are, in her own hand.

## Voice

Quiet, exact and a little severe, a teacher's voice; she speaks as she writes, one clean stroke at a time, and never corrects herself. She is funny in a dry way that only
the library understands.

## Voice lines

- **Picked:** "Hold still. You are being written."
- **Picked:** "One stroke. No corrections."
- **Winning:** "Written. And so it is."
- **Winning:** "Your ending is legible."
- **Losing:** "A blot. I'll start a fresh page."
- **Losing:** "The ink is still wet. So am I."
- **Meeting Mr. Ledger:** "Still no pictures, Ledger? How sad."
- **Meeting Tempo:** "Slow down. I can only write so fast. Twice."

## Secrets

- What she writes comes true only a little, and only for a while. Except once: at fifteen, the night her grandmother died, she wrote her grandmother's name on the year's book,
  and her grandmother's ink has never dried. The page is still wet.
- She has seen the blank page in the Tally, from the duel of year 90. She has tried to write on it, with her brush. The ink would not take.
- The word she gave Hesk was the library's word for "awake". She did not tell him what it does to a thing that was never asleep.

## What she wants from the Tower

To write it down: every floor, every person, every realm the Tower holds, so that whatever happens at the top, the record is kept. And to read whatever is written at the top,
because she believes something is.

## Ties

- **[Mr. Ledger](mr-ledger.md)**, rival: two keepers of the record; she says his book has no pictures, he says hers has no totals.
- **[Lian](lian.md)**, her student: Shiori taught her to fold paper; the dragon is folded from one sheet, as Shiori's way requires.
- **[Hesk](hesk.md)**, her student: she taught him the script; the word on every golem's brow is in her hand.
- **[Tempo](tempo.md)**, comrade: he keeps the hours and she writes them down, and both are always a little behind.
