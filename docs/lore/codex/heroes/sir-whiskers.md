# Sir Whiskers, the Lynx Duelist

*A loner · [the Ninefold](../peoples/ninefold.md), a lynx · from [the Field of Banners](../realms/banners.md)* · [Heroes](README.md)

## Biography

His name is Sir Wystan Aurelio of Nine, knight of the Keep of Nine, and he will recite it if asked and sometimes if not. The Ring calls him Sir Whiskers, which he pretends to dislike
and secretly adores. He was born at the Keep of Nine on its ridge above the Field of Banners, twenty years before the Fall, a lynx of the Ninefold: tawny-furred, black ear tufts, amber
eyes, and from the start extremely pleased with himself. He was the youngest knight of the keep to swear the oath, at sixteen, and he swore it, as Ninefold knights do, to a court: the
crimson kings of Harrowmark, whose kingdom the keep stood in.

He counts his life in nines, as the lynx knights of old did, and spends lives like coin: on a wager, a lady, a point of honour. He spent his first three before he was twenty: one on a wager
that he could cross the Field's river on its winter ice (he could not), one on a lady of Harrow whose honour he had already compromised, one in a duel with a crimson knight over the correct
way to hold a rapier. He was on his fourth life when the sun fell, and like every Ninefold knight alive that day, he felt his count go back to nine. The Noon Grace, the Ninefold call it.
Sir Whiskers called it a second helping.

He fought the War of the Banners for the crimson, as his oath required, and spent two lives on the Field, one in the first battle and one in the Winter of Mud, saving a Briarkin fox who
had stolen a crimson captain's battle-cry and was about to be hanged for it. She stole the name of the life he had just spent, so that he could not remember what had killed him, which he
considered the most charming thing anyone had ever done to him. That was [Kohaku](kohaku.md), of the Fox court. He has been trying to catch her ever since, for longer than either will admit,
and she has been letting him. When the crimson banner fell and King Orlan died in the mud, Sir Whiskers' oath fell with them, and he has not sworn to another court since. He has served many.

He serves for the duration. A Ninefold who is between oaths takes service where it is interesting, and Sir Whiskers has been the champion of a Coast captain, the escort of a Dune caravan,
the fencing master of a Gloaming house, the second of an Aubade paladin in a duel, and once, briefly, the Arbiter's deputy, which ended badly. He duels for whoever is most interesting, and
lands on his feet. In the sixty-ninth summer he was hired for an evening to ride beside the carriage of the Mirror Duchess of Gloaming, [Silvane](silvane.md), on the night roads, because the
Duchess had been told to expect an assassin and Sir Whiskers had said she would need someone interesting. A crossbow bolt came out of the dark at the carriage window. He took it in the heart,
died, and got up again three minutes later on his fourth life of the new count, and finished the ride. He has never asked her for anything in return. She has never paid it back.

He is an upright tawny lynx in gunmetal plate with gold filigree and a short wine-red cape, a gold gorget, a dark helm with his ear tufts through it, and a long rapier whose amber gem glows,
and he has been thirty-one in this life for thirty-one years.

## People and appearance

A lynx of the [Ninefold](../peoples/ninefold.md), of the Keep of Nine: a tawny lynx's head with black ear tufts and amber eyes, fur at the throat and hands, gunmetal plate with gold filigree,
a gold gorget, a short wine-red cape, a cream halo of nine lives, and the long rapier with its amber gem in the right hand. In the [revamp plan](../revamp.md) his figure is unchanged: he
already reads as his people.

## Voice

Rich, amused and theatrical, with rolled consonants and elaborate courtesy; he narrates his own fights. He never says a short sentence when a long one will do. He purrs, very slightly,
when pleased, and denies it.

## Secrets

- He knows the Turncloak on the Climb's War floors. It is his brother, Sir Aldous of Nine, who swore to the azure, spent eight lives on the Field and went into the Tower on his ninth.
  Sir Whiskers has met him there. He has not told the keep.
- He has five lives left, not six as he tells everyone. He spent one in secret, in year 88, on someone he will not name.
- He has let Kohaku steal his name once. For a night he did not know who he was, and was, he says, very happy.

## What he wants from the Tower

To see what is at the top, because it is the most interesting thing on the Dial; and to bring his brother down before his ninth life runs out.

## Ties

- **[Silvane](silvane.md)**, owed by her: he spent one of his lives for her, and she has not paid it back.
- **[Kohaku](kohaku.md)**, rival: the fox and the cat; he chases, she lets him, and neither will say how long it has been.
- **[Kagen](kagen.md)**, rival: they spar every winter, and he calls the swordsman slow, always from a safe distance.
