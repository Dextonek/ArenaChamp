# The heroes of the Dial

*Sixty champions of the Ring: the forty in the game and the twenty on the cards.* · [Codex](../README.md)

Each hero has a page: a biography, their people and appearance, their voice and eight lines for the game, their secrets, what they want
from the Tower, and their ties. The **figure** column is the [revamp plan](../revamp.md)'s answer: whether the figure changes to show
its people (no, touch, revamp), or, for the twenty not yet built, *new*.
