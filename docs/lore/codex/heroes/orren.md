# Orren, the Sunbearer

*The Noonsworn · [Aubade folk](../peoples/aubade.md) · from [Aubade](../realms/aubade.md)* · [Heroes](README.md) · new, on the cards

## Biography

Orren was the son of a lamplighter in the low quarter of Aubade, under the cliff, where the light comes last. He was born in the
fifty-eighth summer after the Fall, big from the first day, slow to speak and slower to fall over, and he spent his boyhood
carrying things: oil up the cliff stairs, water down them, his younger sisters everywhere. At fourteen he could carry a
bell-founder's mould on his back up the Dawn Stair without stopping, and Campana's foundry took him on as a carrier. At twenty
he asked to join the White Barracks, and the paladins laughed: he could not ride, could barely read, and swung a sword like a
man swinging a door. They took him anyway, because nobody could knock him down.

Before the Fall a piece of the sun's light was kept in a lantern-shrine in Aubade: the **Ember of Aubade**, lit from the sun
itself at its last noon. The shrine's keepers say the choir carried a lamp up the Tower that morning, as they did every midsummer,
lit it at noon from the sun's own light, and sent it down by runner before the Hymn began. The lamp reached Aubade at dusk. By then
the sun had fallen, and the Ember was the last of its light on the Dial that anyone could hold. The Noonsworn kept it in its
shrine for seventy years, fed it oil of cliff olives, guarded it day and night, and argued about what it was for.

Some said it was a relic, to be kept. Some said it was a seed: if it were carried to the top of the Tower and set in the Noon
Seat, it would grow into a new sun. The second party won in the ninetieth summer, when the Tower's gate began to open to champions
of the Ring, and the question became who could carry it. The Ember's shrine is brass and glass and heavy, and the Ember cannot be
taken out of it without going out; whoever carries it must carry the whole shrine, on their back, through every fight on the way
up. The paladins tried, in turn, for a season. Most could carry it a mile. None could fight under it.

Orren carried it to the Ring and back without being asked, because it needed carrying. Then he fought a practice bout with it on
his back, and won, and found something nobody had known about the Ember: in its light, the one who carries it grows strong. Where
he stands, the light falls; in the light, his blows land harder and his wounds close. The elders put the oath to him that night.
[Auriel](auriel.md) spoke it, because he asked for her: he swore never to set the Ember down except to fight, and she swore to walk
in front of him wherever he carried it. They have kept both oaths since, which is to say for ten years.

He is not a clever man and does not pretend to be. He is slow and patient and very hard to move, and when anyone tries to take the
Ember from him, which happens more often than the Truce would like, he lets them try. Nobody has carried it far. The Court of Dusk
wants it out: [Vesper](vesper.md) has sworn to put it out, and it burns her to come near, which she has done anyway, twice. The
Underforge wants it for its fire: [Brannoc](brannoc.md) believes the Ember belongs in the great forge in the Ember Caldera, where
it could be fed until it burned like a sun underground, and fights Orren for it whenever the Ring allows. Orren respects Brannoc,
which Brannoc finds very annoying.

He has two lives, and keeps them separate. In the Barracks and the Ring he is the Sunbearer, and he lets people call him a hope.
At home in the low quarter he is Orren, who still carries his sisters' children up the stairs on his back, shrine and all, and lets
them warm their hands at the light. The Noonsworn elders do not approve. He has never asked them.

## People and appearance

Aubade folk of the low quarter: broad, slow and very tall even for his people, with warm brown skin, dark hair bound back with gold
thread and the dawn line on his brow. He wears white and gold plate, plain and dented, under a heavy brass lantern-shrine strapped
to his back with leather; the Ember inside it glows warm gold, and its light falls round him. He carries a long, plain sword. His
look is not built yet: its brief is in the [revamp plan](../revamp.md), and it changes nothing from his card.

## Voice

Deep and slow, with long pauses that people mistake for stupidity until they notice that he is choosing his words. He says little,
and almost never says anything unkind. He laughs at himself easily and at others never.

## Voice lines

- **Picked:** "Take it, if you can carry it."
- **Picked:** "I'll go slow. Keep up."
- **Winning:** "Still lit."
- **Winning:** "Up you get. Warm your hands first."
- **Losing:** "Down, but not out. The light's still going."
- **Losing:** "I'll carry it back up. I always do."
- **Meeting Vesper:** "Mind your face, Countess. It's bright here."
- **Meeting Brannoc:** "It's not a coal, smith. It doesn't go in your fire."

## Secrets

- He does not know what the Ember is, and suspects nobody does. On bad nights he wonders whether it is a seed or just the last of
  something, and whether he is carrying a sun or a grave.
- Once, in the dark of the Deep's chapter, the Ember spoke to him, or he thinks it did: one word, which he will not repeat.
- He cannot read well, and the oath he swore was read to him. He has learned it by heart since, every word.

## What he wants from the Tower

To carry the Ember to the Noon Seat and set it down, the one time his oath allows it without a fight; and then to sit for a while,
because it is very heavy.

## Ties

- **[Vesper](vesper.md)**, sworn enemy: he carries a piece of the sun on his back; she would put it out, and it burns her to come near.
- **[Auriel](auriel.md)**, comrade: she swore him to the Ember of Aubade, and walks in front of him wherever he carries it.
- **[Brannoc](brannoc.md)**, rival: Brannoc wants the Ember of Aubade for his forge; Orren will not set it down.
