# Zephyr, the Wind Blade

*The Windward · [Coast folk](../peoples/coast.md) · from [the Drowned Coast](../realms/coast.md)* · [Heroes](README.md)

## Biography

Zephyr was born on the fourth of the Spires, in the seventy-sixth summer after the Fall, in a room so high that clouds came in at the window. The
Spire-born are a lineage of the Coast folk who have lived in the broken sky towers for as long as anyone knows: lighter and longer-limbed than the harbour
folk, with heads that do not spin at heights, and an old knack for the wind that the harbour folk call a working and the Spire-born call walking. A
Spire-born child learns to stand on a ledge in a gale before it learns to walk on a floor. Zephyr learned to walk on the wind itself before he walked on
stone: at three his mother found him standing on nothing, a yard outside the window, laughing.

He grew up quick and bored. The Spires are small, and a boy who can run along the wind between them has run every path by ten. He raced the gulls, then the
kites, then the storm fronts, and won. At fourteen the Windward's elders, who keep the old wind-forge at the top of the fourth Spire, forged him a blade from
a gust: a curved sword of pale metal so light it seems to want to fly out of his hand, with a hot leading edge and a thin dark inner edge, that cuts the air
in sickle crescents. Every dash he makes gives him another gust to spend. He calls it Gale. He named it at fourteen and is embarrassed about it now.

He came down off the Spires at sixteen because there was nobody left on them to race. He found [Pip](pip.md), the Truce's courier, on the Dawn Road, and
overtook him on the wind, and laughed; Pip caught him at the Waymeet, on foot, and did not laugh. They have raced across the whole Dial every spring since, the
fastest feet against the fastest blade, Aubade to Frostfall by any road, and the score is exactly even, which both of them find intolerable. He races
[Ramet](ramet.md), the lion of the gate of Tamerit, across the Glass Flats too, every year at the Homecoming, and loses less: the lion has never lost on sand,
and Zephyr has come within a length of it twice. And he races climbers to the lighthouse on the Climb's Sea floors, for practice, and does not lose at all.

He is lean and quick, in a short teal coat and a very long jade scarf, with swept white hair and a pink blossom tucked behind one ear that he never explains.
He fights in the Ring because standing still bores him: in a duel he is never where the blow lands, and his crescents come from every side at once.

He flies with [Lian](lian.md), the kite master, who flies her paper dragon on his winds and whom he never lets forget it. They grew up on neighbouring Spires
and sent each other messages by kite before either could write; she taught him to read the wind's shapes, and he taught her to ride it, and the two of them
together carry the Windward's messages across the Spires faster than anything on the Dial except Pip.

He has been up the Climb more often than any Windward champion, never to the top and never for long: he goes up a floor or two, looks
round, races something, and comes down bored. He says the Tower's air is wrong, too still, and that its winds blow in circles; Professor
Puff, who has asked him for readings, agrees, and has given him a small brass instrument to carry, which Zephyr has lost twice.

His mother died in the ninety-fourth summer, in a storm at sea. That spring he raced Pip and won, and has never been sure he deserved to.

## People and appearance

Coast folk, Spire-born, born after the Fall: twenty-four, lean and long-limbed, with swept white hair (the Spire-born go white young, from the wind, they
say), a mask of jade cloth across the lower face, a short teal coat, a very long jade scarf streaming behind him, bracers at the wrists, and the curved wind-blade
in the right hand. His skin is sea-tanned, with sea-glass in his left ear and the tide lines
of his people above the bracers (Touched on 2026-11-16 by the [revamp plan](../revamp.md): he was pale before).

## Voice

Fast, light and teasing, always slightly out of breath from laughing. He finishes other people's sentences because they are too slow. He never says goodbye; he
is just gone.

## Voice lines

- **Picked:** "Wind's up. Let's go."
- **Winning:** "Too slow."
- **Winning:** "Did you blink? You blinked."

- **Losing:** "Next time I won't wait for you."
- **Meeting Pip:** "Race you to the Tower and back. Loser buys."
- **Meeting Ramet:** "Same lion? Same loss. For you this time."

## Secrets

- He knows Pip let him win the spring his mother died. He has let Pip win once since, to make it even, and Pip does not know either.
- The blossom behind his ear is from a tree on the Climb's Wood floors, which flowers out of season. He picked it on his first climb and it has not wilted.
- He is afraid of heights. Not of falling: of standing still at the top.

## What he wants from the Tower

To run it, top to bottom, faster than Pip; and to stand on the Noon Seat for one heartbeat, and then jump.

## Ties

- **[Pip](pip.md)**, rival: the fastest feet against the fastest blade; they race across the Dial every spring and the score is even.
- **[Lian](lian.md)**, friend: she flies her dragon on his wind, and he never lets her forget it.
- **[Ramet](ramet.md)**, rival: the wind and the lion race across the Glass Flats every year, and the lion has never lost on sand.
