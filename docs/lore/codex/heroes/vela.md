# Vela, the Dream Moth

*The Greenwood Moot · [Briarkin](../peoples/briar.md), the Moth court · from [the Walking Wood](../realms/wood.md)* · [Heroes](README.md) · new, on the cards

## Biography

The Walking Wood sleeps by night, mostly, and Vela is why. The Moth court of the Briarkin lives in the night meadows in the north of the Wood, open
grassland ringed with birch where the moon comes up early; its people are slender and pale, with great soft wings that fold like a cloak, feathered
antennae and eyes of one colour; and their work, since before the Long Count, has been sleep. The dust of a Moth court Briarkin's wings carries it.
They fly over the forest at dusk and lay it down: the birds, the deer, the bark hounds, the trees, the folk in their villages. In the old days the
Still Wood slept so deeply that travellers on the Frostfall road talked of the silence.

Vela has done it for longer than she will say. She is the Moth court's priestess, the one who flies first and furthest, and before the Fall she laid the
whole Still Wood to sleep every night, alone, and it was easy: a forest that does not walk sleeps like a stone. Since the Fall it has not been easy. The
Wood walks at night, mostly, and a walking forest is a restless sleeper. It turns over. It dreams of the sun. Some nights her dust is not enough, and
groves walk in their sleep into villages and rivers and over the high pastures.

So she has had help. The Moss-Abbess of the Wood folk, a woman of a Wood village with a great curled horn, began in the first year after the Fall to sound the
horn at dusk to lull the forest while Vela flew, and between the two of them the Wood slept most nights. For ninety years they worked together, the moth
and the horn, and became friends, in the strange way a Briarkin and a mortal can: the Abbess grew old, and Vela did not. In the ninetieth summer, when the
Tower's gate opened, the Abbess went to the Ring to see a champion off, and walked into the Tower behind them, and did not come out. Vela has laid the Wood
to sleep alone since. She does not know that the Abbess is the keeper of the Climb's Wood chapter, sounding her horn on ten floors to keep a Wood in its
first year of walking asleep. Nobody has told her.

She hangs paper lanterns in the glades, and travellers sleep safe in their light: under a Moth court lantern nothing in the Wood will harm you. The Truce has asked her for lanterns for the Ringside, and she has hung a hundred on the crater's rim, one
for each of the white stones that mark the market lost at the Fall. She is kind
and frail and in no hurry, slender, with great pale green wings that trail long tails, and she fights in the Ring because sleep is a mercy, and some quarrels
deserve one. A sleeping hero cannot act until damage wakes them, and the hit that wakes them lands harder; she has to choose, every time, whether to let them
sleep.

Her friends are the other keepers of the Wood's hours. [Sirix](sirix.md), the Swarm Warden of the Hive court: the hive keeps the day and the moth the night,
and between them the Wood's flowers are never alone. They meet at dusk at the edge of the night meadows, for as long as the light lasts. And [Saga](saga.md),
the dancer of the northern light, at Frostfall: two dancers of the night, one who lays the Wood to sleep and one who lights the north. Vela flies north to the
aurora terraces in midwinter, when the Wood sleeps on its own, and Saga dances, and Vela sleeps for the only time in the year.

## People and appearance

A Briarkin of the Moth court: slender and pale green, with great luna-moth wings folded as a cloak and long tails trailing, feathered antennae, eyes of one
colour, pale gold; soft grey-green silks; a paper lantern in the left hand. Her look is not built yet; its brief, which adds her court's antennae and eyes, is
in the [revamp plan](../revamp.md).

## Voice

A hush: soft, slow and low, as if everyone near her were already half asleep. She says goodnight instead of goodbye. She never raises her voice, and people
who hear it yawn.

## Voice lines

- **Winning:** "Sleep now. I'll keep the lantern lit."
- **Winning:** "There. Rest is a kindness."
- **Losing:** "So tired. Just a moment."
- **Losing:** "Wake me when it's dark."
- **Meeting Sirix:** "Good evening, warden. Your wasps are yawning."
- **Meeting Saga:** "Dance for me, Saga. I'll sleep through the good part."

## Secrets

- She has not slept, except at Saga's dances, since the Fall. A Moth court Briarkin who does not sleep, the court says, begins to dream awake. She has
  started to.
- In her waking dreams the Wood is walking to the Tower to lie down at its foot and sleep, for good.
- She knows the Moss-Abbess is alive. She dreamed it.

## What she wants from the Tower

To find the Moss-Abbess, and to lay the Tower to sleep, if it can sleep; she thinks it has been awake since the Fall, like her, and is dreaming.
