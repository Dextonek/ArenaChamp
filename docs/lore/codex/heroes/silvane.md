# Silvane, the Mirror Duchess

*The Court of Dusk · [Gloaming folk](../peoples/gloaming.md) · from [Gloaming](../realms/gloaming.md)* · [Heroes](README.md)

## Biography

Silvane was born in the mirror quarter of Gloaming twelve years before the Fall, the elder daughter of a glazier of the Mirror house, and grew up
in her father's workshop among sheets of silvered glass with her younger sister Opaline. She was the serious one: tall, calm, watchful, with a gift
for faces. She could read a customer's mood from his reflection before he had said what he wanted, and her father let her sell the mirrors because
nobody ever left without buying one. She loved mirrors because they kept things: a face, a room, a moment, a light.

On the day of the Fall she was twelve, at the workshop window, holding her father's best hand mirror, a small oval of Aubade silver in a frame of
dark wood, while the light went. The sun was setting at noon over Gloaming as the shadow of the Tower lay across the heights, and she held the mirror
up to the last of it, the way you would hold a cup under a dripping tap, and caught something. The glass went bright and stayed bright. She covered
it with her hand. She has never let it out: its glass still glows pale blue, a hundred years later, the only piece of the old sun's light that anyone
on the Dial keeps in Gloaming.

The long dusk changed her as it changed every Gloaming child: her eyes began to shine back the lamplight, silver, and her skin cooled. She did not
mind. She found that in the dusk she could see more in a mirror than anyone else could, and the Court of Dusk, when Vesper founded it, found a use for
that. The Mirror house was her father's; when he died in year 30 she became its duchess, and the keeper of the Court's mirrors, and through them its
secrets. The **Mirror Hall** of Gloaming is hers: walls of mirrors facing mirrors, in which, the Court says, the Duchess can see any room in the
castle-city that has a mirror in it. She does not deny it.

Her sister left in year 18, for the Deep and the Noonsworn, to set light free. Silvane stayed to keep it. They met for the last time at the Masque of
Houses in year 40, and [Opaline](opaline.md) asked her to break the mirror and let the light go. Silvane said no, and has never told her why: that the
light in the mirror is not just light, that it moves, and that some nights it looks back. They have fought in the Ring twice since, and both times walked
away without a word.

She fights with real mirrors, throwing them off walls and walking to catch them, and the one glass she will not throw is that first mirror, which is the
only thing on the Dial that can turn the gaze of [Lithea](lithea.md), the veiled priestess of the Sands, whose look turns flesh to marble. Lithea came to
Gloaming as the Dune Throne's envoy once, and a guard of the Night Temple looked at her too long and became Corbel the gargoyle; Silvane held up the
mirror in the Night Temple's forecourt, and Lithea's gaze turned back on itself, and the envoy left Gloaming that night with her veil down. They have been
sworn enemies since.

She owes [Sir Whiskers](sir-whiskers.md) a life. In year 69, on the night roads, an assassin from a rival house put a crossbow bolt into the Duchess's
carriage; the Ninefold knight riding beside it, hired for the evening because he was interesting, took it in the heart, died, and got up again three
minutes later on his fourth life. He has never asked for anything in return. She has never paid it back. It is the only debt she has that is not in the
Tally, and it troubles her more than any of them.

## People and appearance

Gloaming folk of the mirror quarter, Noon-touched: twelve at the Fall, she looks thirty-two. Tall and calm, in a dark violet gown with a silver bodice
and a gold edge, with a collar of looking-glass shards fanned behind her head, a calm face and sleek violet hair, and the hand mirror
held out in the left hand with its glass aglow. She has the silver eyeshine of her people, skin cooled to blue-grey
and dark lips (Touched on 2026-11-16 by the [revamp plan](../revamp.md): nothing else changed, so that her sister's revamp sets the two apart).

## Voice

Low, cool and very exact, the voice of someone who has heard every lie in Gloaming and remembers which room it was told in. She asks questions she
already knows the answers to. She rarely uses names; she uses reflections ("you, the one who looked away").

## Secrets

- The light in her first mirror moves, and some nights it looks back. She believes it is not light at all but a piece of whatever looked out of the sun
  before it fell. She is the only person who has seen it, and it is the Court's third plan.
- She has watched her sister in the Mirror Hall every night for sixty years, through a small mirror Opaline polishes in the house at the top of the Prism
  Stair. Opaline does not know.
- The assassin's bolt that killed Sir Whiskers came from the Mirror house: from her own steward, who meant to make her duchess sooner. She found out, and
  dealt with it, and never told the knight.

## What she wants from the Tower

To see what is in the Noon Seat before anyone else, in her mirror, from a safe distance; and if what looks out of her mirror belongs up there, to decide
whether to give it back.
