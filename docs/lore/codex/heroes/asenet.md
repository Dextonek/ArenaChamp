# Asenet, the Uncrowned

*A loner · [Dune folk](../peoples/dune.md) · from [the Sands](../realms/sands.md)* · [Heroes](README.md) · new, on the cards

## Biography

Asenet was born in the Throne Hall of Tamerit twenty-seven years before the Fall, the first child of the old king of the Sands, and raised to rule. She was clever, cold and much
admired, a scholar of the tomb records and the river towns' accounts, and by twenty she understood the Sands' trade better than her father's ministers. Her brother Sefu, three years
younger, was the handsome one, the horseman, the speech-maker; Asenet was the one the court went to when something needed doing. When the old king died, two years before the Fall,
she was crowned queen at twenty-five, and the Sands expected a long, careful reign.

It lasted two years. In the second, at noon on midsummer day, the sun stopped over the Tower and cracked and fell, and the Sands said it fell into their dunes, and the Glass Flats
turned to glass under its heat, and the Father, the sun the kings of the Sands had always called their father, was gone from the sky. The Sands needed someone to blame. They had a queen.
A queen in whose reign the Father fell, the priests said, had failed him: she had not been heavy enough in the pan, or light enough. The river towns muttered all autumn. Asenet did what
she knew how to do: she looked for an answer in the accounts. The crimson banner of King Orlan was collecting the sun's pieces; she joined it, for a share, to buy back what the Sands had
lost. It was the worst decision of her life, and she knew it the month she made it.

In the spring of year 1 the river towns rose, and the court turned, and the Throne's guard went to her brother. Sefu took the crown from her in the Throne Hall at midnight. She was driven
out that night. She went to the embalmers' house first, through the back streets, and asked the master embalmer, [Vitriol](vitriol.md), for a curse: not a poison, a working, to lay on the
crown so that it would never stay on one head. He brewed it. She never paid him. Then she went to the gate of Tamerit, with the court's guard behind her, and laid the curse on the crown she
no longer wore, standing in the gate's shadow: it would never stay on one head, and everyone who wore it too long would fall. And she looked at the captain of the gate, the young lion
[Ramet](ramet.md), and said his grandmother's name, and he opened the gate and let her go. She owes him, and has never paid him either.

The crown has passed from head to head ever since, and everyone who wore it too long has fallen, except her brother. Asenet has walked the Dial for ninety-nine years with no realm and no
court, in faded royal linen of wine and tarnished gold, and she carries the curse with her: a thin circlet, the curse's twin, that will not stay on any head, hers included. In the Ring she
passes it to her enemy, and it goes from head to head with every hit, and whoever has it when the fight turns pays.

She broke the Truce Table's peace in Tamerit in year 97, at the Homecoming, by striking her brother in anger across a festival table she had been invited to; she says he had it coming. The
Truce sent her to the Gaol of Oaths under the Drowned Harbour. On a spring tide in year 99, when the lower cells flooded, she slipped [Kessa](kessa.md)'s chain and walked out by a door that
was not on the Gaol's map. Kessa has not forgiven the sea, the tide or herself, and has come up into the Ring to hunt her.

And [Sefu](sefu.md). Brother and sister: he took her crown, she cursed it, and each has sworn to see the other fall. She is loose in the hundredth summer, and the crown on his head is heavier
than it has ever been.

## People and appearance

Dune folk of the royal house, Noon-touched: twenty-seven at the Fall, she looks forty-seven. Dark-skinned, kohl-eyed, proud and very straight, with the bearing of a queen and the dust of a
long road; faded royal linen of wine and tarnished gold, and a thin circlet hovering just above her head, never resting. Her look is not built yet; its brief is in the
[revamp plan](../revamp.md).

## Voice

Cool, precise and royal, with a bitterness she keeps very well dressed. She speaks in the royal "we" only when she is mocking her brother. She never raises her voice; she lowers it.

## Secrets

- The door she left the Gaol by was shown to her by someone she will not name, who came to her cell on the night of the spring tide and opened it from the other side. It was not Gus, who
  only mopped up after.
- In the year before the Fall she broke the first king's law and opened his tomb, and read the warning on its wall: the pin keeps suns; it kept one before ours;
  it will keep ours; do not sing to it. She tried to warn the court, the Hourkeepers and the master of the choir. That is why the Sands blamed her, and she knows what
  took the Father. Nobody has believed her for a hundred years.
- She did not want the crown back. She wanted Sefu to know what it weighed. Now he does, and she is not sure what she wants.
- The curse can be lifted only by its maker, and the maker is Vitriol. She has never paid him because she is afraid that if she pays, he will lift it, and then she will have nothing.

## What she wants from the Tower

For the crown to stop. She believes the curse is held by the Father's absence, and that if anything sits in the Noon Seat, the curse will break, one way or the other. She would like to be
there when it does.
