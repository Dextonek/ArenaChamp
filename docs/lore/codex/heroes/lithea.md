# Lithea, the Stone Gaze

*The Dune Throne · [Dune folk](../peoples/dune.md) · from [the Sands](../realms/sands.md)* · [Heroes](README.md) · new, on the cards

## Biography

In the Valley of Lids, west of Tamerit, where six centuries of the Sands' kings lie in tombs cut into the cliffs, there was for five hundred years an order of veiled
priestesses who guarded the dead with their eyes. They were called simply **the Veiled**. They were chosen as girls from the river towns, from those whose gaze, the
priests said, was heavy: girls whose look made a bird drop from a branch or a dog lie down. They were taken into the Valley at seven, taught the old workings of the tombs,
and given the veil at fourteen, because by then their gaze could turn whoever met it a little to marble, and whoever met it fully, entirely. They never lifted the veil
except in the tombs. Tomb robbers who met the Veiled in the dark are still standing in the Valley's galleries, in marble, with their tools in their hands.

Lithea was chosen at seven, in the fifty-third summer after the Fall, from a fishing family on the Sefet, because a heron she had stared at in the reeds had stood
on one leg for a week afterwards and could not be made to move. She was the last girl the Veiled ever took. The order had been dying for a century: fewer girls with the
heavy gaze were born each generation, and the Fall, the priests said, had made it worse, as if the sun's coming home had made the eyes of the Sands lighter. By the time
Lithea was given the veil there were four priestesses left in the Valley, all old. By the time she was thirty there was one, and it was Lithea.

She guards the dead kings alone, with her eyes. She wears a sheer verdigris veil over her face, a crown of bronze serpent coils that move a little, made by the Valley's
bronze-smiths in the shape of the snakes that live in the tomb walls and are sacred to the order, and a marble-white gown with bronze edges, and carries a short bronze
staff with a serpent's eye. Whoever meets her gaze turns a little to marble; whoever meets it fully stays that way.

The Dune Throne sends her out of the Valley rarely, because the Valley is unguarded while she is gone. In the seventieth summer it sent her to Gloaming as its envoy, to
treat with the Court of Dusk, because the Court had asked for someone who would not be impressed by it. She went to the Night Temple to pay her respects to its dark
mirror, and lifted her veil to look into it, as the Veiled do before any holy thing; and a guard in the forecourt, who had been told to keep his eyes on the ground, looked
up. He was stone before he could look away. The Court set him on the temple's eaves, and at the first dusk he woke as [Corbel](corbel.md), the Stonewing, grey-winged and
stone-skinned, alive from sunset to sunrise. He has hunted her since.

And in the forecourt that night, while the guard was turning to stone, the Mirror Duchess of the Court, [Silvane](silvane.md), stepped forward and held up her first mirror,
the one with the sun's last light in it, and turned Lithea's gaze back on herself. Lithea felt marble creep up her own throat for the first time in her life. She lowered the
veil and left Gloaming that night, and the Throne and the Court did not speak for a year. Silvane carries the one mirror on the Dial that can turn her gaze. They are sworn
enemies.

## People and appearance

Dune folk of the river towns, born after the Fall: forty-seven, tall and still, dark-skinned, kohl-eyed behind the veil. A sheer verdigris veil over the face, a crown of
bronze serpent coils, a marble-white gown with bronze edges, a short bronze staff with a serpent eye in the left hand. Her look is not built yet; its brief, which keeps
her card, is in the [revamp plan](../revamp.md).

## Voice

Low, slow and very calm, a voice that does not need to be raised because its owner can end any conversation by looking up. She speaks rarely and formally, in the old
desert tongue's courtesies. She likes to be looked at, and says so.

## Voice lines

- **Picked:** "Look at me. Everyone does, once."
- **Picked:** "The veil stays. For now."
- **Winning:** "Still. Very still. Good."
- **Winning:** "You'll make a fine statue."
- **Losing:** "I looked away. My mistake."
- **Losing:** "Marble keeps. So will I."
- **Meeting Silvane:** "Put the mirror down, Duchess. Let's see who blinks."
- **Meeting Corbel:** "You looked up. I remember. I'm sorry."

## Secrets

- She remembers Corbel. She had seen him once before, as a boy, on a caravan, and smiled at him, and he smiled back. She knew him in the forecourt the moment before he looked
  up. She has never forgiven herself.
- Her own gaze, turned back by Silvane's mirror, left a vein of marble in her throat that has never gone away. It is spreading, very slowly.
- She has stood in front of a mirror once since that night, alone, with the veil lifted. Nothing happened. She does not know why.

## What she wants from the Tower

To look at the Heart of Noon, the Father, with her veil lifted, and see whether a sun can be turned to stone. She has not told the Throne, which would forbid it.

## Ties

- **[Silvane](silvane.md)**, sworn enemy: a mirror is the one thing that turns the gorgon's gaze, and Silvane carries it.
- **[Corbel](corbel.md)**, his maker: she turned him to stone with one look; the Court gave him wings, and he has hunted her since.
