# Corbel, the Stonewing

*The Court of Dusk · [Gloaming folk](../peoples/gloaming.md), stone-woken · from [Gloaming](../realms/gloaming.md)* · [Heroes](README.md) · new, on the cards

## Biography

Corbel was born in Lantern-under-Hill, the village of the night-road wardens below the castle-city of Gloaming, in the forty-fourth summer after the
Fall, to a family of wardens. He grew up in the long evening with the Gloaming folk's shining eyes and a warden's lantern of violet glass, and at
eighteen he joined the guard of the Night Temple: the soldiers who stand in its forecourt by night while the names of the dead are said, and keep the
dark mirror from anyone who would look into it for the wrong reasons. He was steady, quiet and proud of his post, and very good at not looking at
things he was not supposed to see.

In the seventieth summer the Dune Throne sent an envoy to Gloaming to treat with the Court of Dusk: [Lithea](lithea.md), the last veiled priestess of the
tombs of the Sands, whose gaze turns whoever meets it to marble. She came to the Night Temple to pay her respects to the dark mirror, and the guard stood
to attention in the forecourt with their eyes on the ground, as they had been told. She lifted her veil to look into the mirror. Corbel, twenty-six, looked
up. He has never been able to say why. He says he wanted to see what kind of face the Sands would send.

He was stone before he could look away: a guard of grey stone in the forecourt, his lantern still in his hand, his eyes on hers. Lithea lowered her veil.
The Mirror Duchess held up her first mirror and turned the priestess's gaze back on herself, and the envoy left Gloaming that night, and the Court of Dusk
and the Dune Throne did not speak for a year.

The Court did not know what to do with a statue of one of its guards. It could not bury him, because he was not dead; it could not keep him in the
forecourt, because the priests said his eyes followed them. In the end the Court set him on the Night Temple's eaves, on a ledge that the mason
[Bram](bram.md) of the Underforge had carved twenty years before for a statue that was never made. And at the first dusk, he woke.

He woke grey-winged and stone-skinned, alive from sunset to sunrise. The wings were not his: they had been carved into the ledge's statue niche by Bram,
for the statue that was never made, and the stone of the ledge had grown into him, or he into it. By night he could move, speak, fly short distances on
stone wings and drop like a falling cornice; by day he slowed back toward stone, and in full sunlight he stopped. The Hourkeepers wrote "stone-woken" and
could say nothing more. The Court made him its watcher from above. He perches on walls and pillars, drops on his prey and climbs back up.

He cannot forgive Lithea, and cannot stop looking for her. He does not want to kill her. He wants her to look at him again, and see what she did, and
he does not know what he will do after that. Bram, who carved the ledge, comes to Gloaming once a year to look at his work, and they talk on the eaves until
morning, the mason and the stone, about the grain of rock and the patience of walls; they are, oddly, friends. And daylight slows him toward stone, which
is why he hates [Auriel](auriel.md)'s dawn more than any blade. Her workings are first light, and in them he freezes. He waits on the eaves for her to tire.
She has not yet.

## People and appearance

Gloaming folk by birth, of the night-road wardens, stone-woken: a guard's body of grey stone, cracked at the joints, his Templeguard armour turned to stone
with him, wings of stone carried folded like a cloak that opens at the shoulders, and the Gloaming folk's eyeshine in a stone face. His look is not built
yet; its brief is in the [revamp plan](../revamp.md) and keeps his card.

## Voice

Deep, gravelly and slow, with the patience of a wall. He speaks from above, always. He is gentle with everyone except those who look at him too long.

## Secrets

- He looked up because he had seen Lithea's face once before, as a boy, on a trade caravan from the Sands, without the veil. She smiled at him. He wanted to
  know if she would remember.
- Each dawn he is a little slower to wake. He thinks one day he will not.
- He can hear the stone of the Night Temple, at night. It talks about the dark mirror, and what is behind it.

## What he wants from the Tower

To reach the top at dusk and look down on the whole Dial; and to find out whether stone that woke can be made flesh again, in the Noon Seat's light, or
whether that light would only turn him back.

## Ties

- **[Auriel](auriel.md)**, rival: her dawn turns him back toward stone, and he waits on the eaves for her to tire.
- **[Lithea](lithea.md)**, made by her: she turned him to stone with one look; the Court gave him wings, and he has hunted her since.
- **[Bram](bram.md)**, friend: Bram carved the ledge where Corbel woke, and the stone remembers its mason.
