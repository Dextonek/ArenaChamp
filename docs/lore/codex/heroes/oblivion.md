# Oblivion, the Unmaker

*A loner · [the Fallborn](../peoples/fallborn.md), the Unmade · from [the Tower](../realms/tower.md)* · [Heroes](README.md)

## Biography

When the sun fell it tore the sky above the Tower, and something came through the Tear that the Dial has no word for. The Hourkeepers call it the Unmaker. The Ring calls it
Oblivion. It does not call itself anything, and has never been heard to use a name.

What is known is what was seen. At noon on midsummer day a hundred years ago, as the sun cracked and fell, a rent opened in the sky where it had hung, black, with a burning pink edge on
one side and a fringe of cyan on the other, and a figure stepped out of the rent onto the Tower's top: a towering knight in black plate cracked with glowing pink rifts, a horned helm with
one burning slit for an eye, a greatsword of void. The Sun Warden, at his post, turned to face it. What passed between them nobody saw. When the two singers of the choir came down the
Tower's stair that night, they passed the knight on the stair, going down ahead of them, and it did not look at them.

It walked out of the Tower's gate before the gate was shut, and across the crater of Noonfall, and into the War of the Banners. It fought for neither side. It walked across the Field,
and where it walked, things stopped being able to end: a wound that would not close and would not kill, a fire that would not go out and would not spread, a battle that would not stop.
The War of the Banners ended outside the Tower in year 2, on the Ninth Field. Inside the Tower, on the Climb's War chapter, it has never ended, and the Hourkeepers believe that is the
Unmaker's doing: that endless war is the one thing on the Dial it has truly unmade. It holds the gate there, the War's gatekeeper, the only gatekeeper who is not of the realm it guards.

It wants no throne. It does not want the Noon Seat, or the sun, or anything any faction can give. What it wants, as far as anyone can tell, is the Dial unmade, hour by hour, door by door:
not destroyed, but undone, so that nothing can begin and nothing can end. While it stands still it pulls in whatever is near, and what it pulls in does not always come back. It has
taken things from the Dial that nobody remembers having had. The Hourkeepers have a list. It grows.

Between duels it is seldom seen. It stands, some nights, on the Ring's highest stand, under the Tear, quite still, with the shards of its
cloak drifting round it, and the Ringside's people keep their doors shut. Sometimes it walks the Field of Banners, where the plough turns up
bone, and the stones the people of Harrow have laid for the dead are found the next morning moved into a ring. Once, in year 77, it walked
into the Walking Wood, and the Wood stopped walking for a month, everywhere, and the Moot does not like to speak of it.

It comes to the Ring, which nobody understands. It fights under the Truce, keeps the rules, eats nothing at the Table, and leaves. The Arbiter has called its duels for a hundred years and
says it has never once broken a rule, and that this frightens him more than anything else about it. Some say it fights because the Ring is the one place on the Dial where things end:
every duel has a winner. Some say it is waiting for the gate.

Three people have sworn to send it back. [Wick](wick.md), the Doorwarden, keeper of every door, against the thing that makes holes where no door should be. [Tempo](tempo.md), the unwound
hour, who keeps the hours, against the thing that wants none left. And [Thessa](thessa.md), the Sky-Stitcher, who sews the Tear shut every night and finds every stitch unpicked in the
morning. It fights all three, in the Ring, whenever they ask. It has never once asked them.

## People and appearance

The only one of [the Unmade](../peoples/fallborn.md) that has walked the Dial: a shape, not a body. A towering knight in black plate cracked with glowing pink rifts, spiked pauldrons, a
ragged cloak whose hem breaks into floating shards, a horned helm with a single rift slit for an eye, and a greatsword of void. In the [revamp plan](../revamp.md) its figure is unchanged:
it already reads as what it is.

## Voice

Rarely heard: a low, flat voice with no breath in it, like a sentence read out with the punctuation removed. It speaks in questions. It never answers them.

## Secrets

- It believes it did not come through the Tear by its own will: that something on this side pulled, at noon. It has been looking for what pulled for a
  hundred years, and has told only Wick, who did not believe it.
- It loses in the Ring sometimes on purpose. It wants to know what losing is.
- What it does not know: it is the evening of Senmere, the world the Tower kept before the Dial, the ending that world was never allowed to have, which the Tower
  drags behind it from sky to sky. What pulled it through the Tear was the Tower's taking of the sun.
- The Hourkeepers' list of things it has unmade includes, near the top, in Shiori's hand, a word: the old name of the Dial. Nobody remembers it. The word is blank.

## What it wants from the Tower

The Tower unmade, with everything else. Or, if the Arbiter is right that it fights in the Ring because the Ring is where things end: to reach the top, and end.
