# Isolde, the Winter Warden

*The Hourkeepers · [Northerners](../peoples/north.md), lake-born · from [Frostfall](../realms/frostfall.md)* · [Heroes](README.md)

## Biography

At the top of the Dial, where the stars turn round the pole, the cold keeps time still, and Isolde guards it. She was born twenty-eight years before the Fall in a shore village of
Frostfall Lake, to one of the lake-born families: the old Northern lineage that has kept the lake's ice since the first star-watchers, frost-pale from generations of the cold,
white-haired from birth. The lake-born have always given the Dial its **Wardens of the Winter**, the Northern warriors who hold the ice, and every generation one of them is the
Winter Warden, who keeps the cold itself: the cold that keeps the frozen library dry and still, so that nothing written in it fades.

Isolde was chosen at twelve, because she could walk on ice no one else could stand on, and could make water freeze by holding it. She was trained by the old Warden on the lake for
fifteen years, in the long nights, with an ice spear and a crown of ice spikes that grows on the Warden's head and is not worn but kept, and at twenty-seven she was the Winter
Warden. A year later the sun fell, and the winter that came after it did not end until the next summer, and the herds starved on the moors, and the library under the ice began to
drip for the first time in its history, because the slipping hours had confused the cold. Isolde stood on the lake for that whole winter and the summer after, alone, holding the
cold still with her spear in the ice, until it remembered how to keep. The Northerners call it the Long Hold. The library has not dripped since.

She is the Winter Warden, in white and frost-blue, with a crown of ice spikes fanned behind her head and a long ice spear, and the Hourkeepers' library at Frostfall lies in ice under her
watch, where nothing written can fade. Her hits stack the cold on her enemies until they freeze. The lake-born age slowly, and the Noon-touched slower, and the Warden's cold has crept
into her skin over seventy years until it is nearly white with a blue undertone; she looks forty-eight and has not felt warm since the Long Hold.

She lives on the lake. The Warden's house is a round hut of black stone on an island at the lake's centre, reached over the ice in winter
and by boat in the brief summer, with a door that faces the library's entrance across the water. She goes down into the library every
night and walks its shelves with a lamp, touching the ice of the walls to feel whether the cold is holding, and the Readers working late
know the sound of her spear on the floor. She trains the Wardens of the Winter on the ice at dawn, the lake-born young who will hold it
after her, and has chosen none of them to be the next Winter Warden yet, which worries the lake villages.

Except once. In year 40, the smith of the Underforge, [Brannoc](brannoc.md), came north to Frostfall Lake with a forge in his chest, following her, fire to her frost: they had met
at the Ring the summer before and he had decided, in his way, that she was the one person on the Dial who could stand his heat. He set up his forge on the lake's shore, and the ice
cracked, and the library under it began to drip. Isolde froze his forge solid to send him home. It took him a year to light it again. It was the kindest thing she knew how to do: she
could not have him there, and she could not have asked him to leave, so she made him. Neither of them has married.

[Saga](saga.md), who dances the northern light, is her younger sister: the warden of the cold and the dancer of its light, daughters of the same frozen lake. They are close in the
quiet Northern way, which means they say little and are always within reach. Isolde fights to keep the cold, and the past, as they are.

## People and appearance

A Northerner of the lake-born families, Noon-touched: twenty-eight at the Fall, she looks forty-eight, frost-pale with a blue undertone and white hair, as her lineage is and as the
Warden's cold has made her more so. White and frost-blue robes in a bell to the floor, a frost fern pattern at the hem, a crown of ice spikes fanned behind her head, and the long ice
spear in the left hand. In the [revamp plan](../revamp.md) her figure is unchanged: hers is the lake-born look, and Kaia's revamp (a sea-tanned storm caller in an oilskin coat) sets the two apart.

## Voice

Slow, cool and quiet, with long pauses; she never says more than she needs to. She speaks to fire as if it were a child. Her kindness sounds like a reprimand.

## Voice lines

- **Picked:** "Slower. Slower. There."
- **Picked:** "Cold keeps. Let it keep you."
- **Winning:** "Frozen. Kept."
- **Winning:** "Be still. The winter is patient."
- **Losing:** "A thaw. It won't last."
- **Losing:** "The lake will freeze again."
- **Meeting Brannoc:** "You're dripping on my ice, smith."
- **Meeting Saga:** "Dance, sister. I'll keep the cold."

## Secrets

- During the Long Hold, the cold spoke to her. It said the hours were slipping because something had stopped them at the Tower, and that if the Tower's hour were set going again,
  the cold would have to let go of everything it keeps: the library, the lake, and her.
- She keeps the forge she froze. It is still on the lake's shore under a dome of ice, and on the coldest nights, through the ice, it glows.
- She has never been warm since the Long Hold. Brannoc was the only time.

## What she wants from the Tower

To keep the hours as they are until the Hourkeepers know why they slipped. If the sun is hung again, the cold will have to let go, and she is not sure she can.
