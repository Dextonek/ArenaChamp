# Mum, the Silent One

*The Court of Dusk · [Aubade folk](../peoples/aubade.md) · from [Gloaming](../realms/gloaming.md)* · [Heroes](README.md)

## Biography

Mum was born in the bell quarter of Aubade, twenty-six years before the Fall, into a family of ringers. Her name then was not Mum; it was a
long Aubade bell-name that she has not used for a hundred years and which the codex will not print, because she would not want it. She sang
before she spoke, the family says, and she sang so purely that by sixteen the choir had taken her, the youngest soprano in its history. The Sun
Warden, the choir said, turned his head when she came in.

She sang the Hymn of Noon at the Tower's top every noon for ten years, at the choir master's right hand, with a young baritone called
[Cantor](cantor.md) at his left and a tenor called Ansel at her other side. The master liked her. In the last week before the Fall, the week
before midsummer, he did something he had never done: he taught her the Hymn's last verse, which only the master knew and which was sung only at
midsummer, in case, he said, anything happened to him. She learned it in three days. She was to sing it with him for the first time on midsummer
day.

On the day of the Fall the choir sang the Hymn at noon, and somewhere in the second line, Mum believes, she was a hair behind the beat. She had
been thinking about the last verse. She felt the beat slip under her, and tried to catch it, and the sun stopped overhead and cracked and fell.
When the light came back the Sun Court was empty except for the Warden, Cantor and herself, and she was on her knees, and she had nothing to say.
She came down the Tower's stair beside Cantor in the dark, and she has not made a sound since. Not a word, not a note, not a laugh, not a cry.

She came down from the Tower in a porcelain mask she made in the first month, to hide a face she could not control, and left Aubade within the
year, because every bell in the city rang the hundredth short for the choir she believed she had failed. She walked the night roads west, and found
a home at the Court of Dusk, which values people who say nothing. The Court made her a harlequin, a player of its revels, and in time the first of
them. She plays the Court's revels in her coat of ivory diamonds; she reads its secrets from faces across a ballroom; she carries its messages
without speaking them. Nobody at court has heard her laugh. The Countess trusts her absolutely, because she has never told anyone anything.

In the Ring her walls and her ball cannot be seen, only the chalk lines she leaves on the floor, as if the world had to be drawn back in by hand. Her
workings are silence: a wall that is not there until you walk into it, a ball you only see when it hits you. The Arbiter has had to rule, more than
once, that a wall nobody can see is still a wall. [Sahar](sahar.md), the archer of the mirage, builds walls nobody can trust, and each calls the
other a cheat; they have fought in the Ring seven times and never once looked at each other. And [Tova](tova.md), the sapper of the Underforge, has
broken every wall she ever met except Mum's, which she cannot find, and takes it as a personal insult.

Cantor still sings to her. Every midwinter he comes to Gloaming for the Masque of Houses and sings under her window, every verse of the Hymn but the
last. She has never answered. She has never left the room.

## People and appearance

Aubade folk of the bell quarter, though nobody can see it: Noon-touched, twenty-six at the Fall, she looks forty-six, narrow-shouldered in a dark violet
coat with ivory diamonds and an ivory ruff, a porcelain mask in a dark hood with dark eyes, gold tear lines and a gold brow diamond, a long violet ribbon
behind her shoulder, and big pale gloves with gold cuffs. In the [revamp plan](../revamp.md) her figure is unchanged: masked and gloved, her people do not
show, and the lore says why.

## Voice

None. She speaks with her hands, in the harlequins' sign, and with chalk. Her voice lines are gestures, written in brackets. When she is truly angry
she becomes completely still.

## Voice lines

- **Picked:** "(Nothing. A pale glove rises, palm out. The wall is already there.)"
- **Picked:** "(She bows, very low, and does not come back up until you look away.)"
- **Winning:** "(She draws a chalk line under you, and dusts her gloves.)"
- **Winning:** "(A slow, silent round of applause, for you, from three feet away.)"
- **Losing:** "(She mimes a door closing. Gently.)"
- **Losing:** "(She lies down exactly where she fell, and folds her hands.)"
- **Meeting Cantor:** "(She raises one gloved finger to the lips of the mask, and turns her head away, and waits.)"
- **Meeting Sahar:** "(She knocks twice on the air between them. It answers.)"

## Secrets

- She believes she was the hair behind the beat, and that the sun fell because of her. Cantor believes it was Ansel. She has never told him.
- She knows the Hymn's last verse. She has never sung it, even in her head. She is afraid of what will happen if she does.
- The porcelain mask has a crack behind the left eye. She made it in the first month, and the crack was there from the start.

## What she wants from the Tower

To climb to the Sun Court and stand where she knelt, and decide there whether to sing. She has not decided.

## Ties

- **[Cantor](cantor.md)**, old flame: they sang the Hymn of Noon side by side on the day it failed. She has made no sound since; he has not stopped
  singing to her.
- **[Sahar](sahar.md)**, rival: she builds walls nobody can see, he builds walls nobody can trust, and each calls the other a cheat.
- **[Tova](tova.md)**, rival: the only walls Tova has never broken are the ones she cannot find.
