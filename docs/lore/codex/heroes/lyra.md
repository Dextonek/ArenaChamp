# Lyra, the Star Archer

*The Hourkeepers · [Northerners](../peoples/north.md) · from [Frostfall](../realms/frostfall.md)* · [Heroes](README.md)

## Biography

The stars still keep time, even with the sun gone wrong. The sun's shadow stopped at the Fall, and the hours slip, and the days run long and short; but the stars over Frostfall
still turn round the Nail every night exactly as they did for the first star-watchers twelve hundred years ago, and the Hourkeepers set their clocks by them. Lyra reads them.

She was born in the observatory itself, in the seventy-eighth summer after the Fall, in a room under the dome where her mother, a Reader of the night sky, had been charting a
comet and refused to stop. She grew up on the observatory's roof terraces at night, wrapped in reindeer furs, with the Great Lens behind her and the whole sky in front, learning
the constellations the way other children learn the faces of their family. She could name every star the Hourkeepers had charted by eight, and found her first uncharted one at
ten, a faint red star low over the ice coast that the library now calls Lyra's Lamp.

She learned the bow on the moors from the Wardens of the Winter, because the observatory's Readers go out on the ice at night to measure and the ice has wolves. She was good at
it. She was better at it than at anything else, except the stars, and at fourteen she found that the two were one thing: an arrow loosed at a star-point she had charted drew a
line of light behind it, as a star's motion draws a line on a long exposure of the Lens, and where two lines met, they made a figure, and the figure held. Her arrows draw
constellations between them as they fly: a net, a bow, a stag, gold lines from star to star that snap and fall as shooting stars. She is the Hourkeepers' archer, a night hunter
in a starry violet cloak with a gold crescent bow, and she hits harder the farther away you stand, because a star is always far.

She climbed the Tower for the first time in year 98, with a party of Hourkeepers sent to measure, and went up through the Sea's floors to
its crown with her bow strung and her eyes on the sky. The Tower's floors have skies of their own, and the stars in them, she found, are the
stars of the hour each floor holds: the Sea's sky is the sky of the morning after the Fall, with the old sun's light still fading from it.
She charted it. It is the last chart of the old sky anyone has made, and the library keeps it beside the Noon Folio.

She charts the northern sky with [Saga](saga.md), the dancer of the northern light, from the observatory: partners at the observatory, one who charts the stars and the other the
lights between them. On nights the stars are hidden, Saga dances the aurora and Lyra reads the hour from its curtains, and they have kept the Hourkeepers' night record together for
six years without missing an hour.

Her stars are the one light [Nyx](nyx.md), the Court of Dusk's knife, cannot step out of: a shadow needs light that comes from somewhere, and starlight comes from everywhere at once.
They first met on the night roads in year 95, when Nyx was hunting a Hourkeeper surveyor and Lyra was walking home; Lyra lit the sky, and the surveyor walked home. They have duelled
in the dark more often than either has told anyone, and Lyra has won more than half, and has never once tried to finish it. And [Sahar](sahar.md) of the Sands calls himself the better
archer, and she lets him: two archers who have never shot at the same target, and argue about it at every Truce dinner. She lets him because it is funnier, and because it is not
true.

## People and appearance

A Northerner of the observatory, born after the Fall: twenty-two, with deep brown skin and long dark hair, a starry violet cloak and tunic, a gold constellation halo behind her
head, and the gold crescent bow across her back. In the [revamp plan](../revamp.md) her figure is unchanged: hers was the sample the user accepted for the one style, and her skin
and hair are her people's. Her hair has not gone white yet; Northern hair goes white by thirty, and she has eight years.

## Voice

Bright, clear and a little dreamy, with the habit of looking past whoever she is talking to, at the sky. She names things by stars. She is very kind and very hard to make angry.

## Voice lines

- **Picked:** "Run. The sky is very big, and I can see all of it."
- **Picked:** "Clear night. Good shooting."
- **Winning:** "Charted."
- **Winning:** "You're a constellation now. A small one."
- **Losing:** "Clouds. They pass."
- **Losing:** "The stars are still there. So am I."
- **Meeting Nyx:** "There you are. I can always see you, you know."
- **Meeting Sahar:** "You're the better archer, Sahar. Of course."

## Secrets

- The red star she found at ten, Lyra's Lamp, is moving. It moves toward the Tower, a little every year. She believes it is something coming.
- She could finish any duel with Nyx. She has never wanted to. She thinks Nyx is the loneliest person on the Dial.
- The stars over the Tower are wrong: in the gap above the Tear, there are stars that are on no chart, and they are arranged in a figure she does not know.

## What she wants from the Tower

To stand on the Noon Seat at night and chart the stars above the Tear: the ones on no chart, in the figure she does not know. She thinks they will tell her what came through.
