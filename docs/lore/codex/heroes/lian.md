# Lian, the Kite Master

*The Windward · [Coast folk](../peoples/coast.md) · from [the Drowned Coast](../realms/coast.md)* · [Heroes](README.md) · new, on the cards

## Biography

The Windward send their messages from Spire to Spire by kite: a letter tied to a kite's tail, flown on a long line from one Spire's kite-post until it
crosses the wind into the next Spire's catch-hooks, which can be a mile of open sky. It is an old craft, older than the Fall, and since the Fall, when
the winds stopped keeping their seasons, it has become an art: a kite-flier who can read the wind's shapes in the moment can get a message across the
Spires in a storm. Lian is the best flier the Windward have ever had.

She was born on the Kite Spire, the third of the nine, in the seventy-seventh summer after the Fall, to a family of kite-makers: her mother made the
frames, her father the paper, her grandmother the lines. She flew her first kite at four from her bedroom window, and lost it, and flew the second at
five, and did not. She grew up next door, across a mile of sky, to a boy on the fourth Spire called [Zephyr](zephyr.md), who could walk on the wind; they
sent each other messages by kite before either could read, pictures drawn on the paper, and when they were older she taught him to read the wind's shapes
and he taught her to ride it. They have been friends for twenty years. He has never once let her forget that she flies on his wind.

At fourteen she went north to Frostfall to learn paper. The best paper on the Dial is made at the frozen library, and the Hourkeepers' great calligrapher,
[Shiori](shiori.md), only twenty-one then herself but already the last of the great brushes, took her for a season and taught her to fold. Shiori's way is
strict: a fold must be made once, cleanly, and a figure must be folded from a single sheet with no cut and no glue, because a cut is a lie and glue is a
promise you cannot keep. Lian came home with a paper crane in her pocket that could fly by itself on a windless day, and a new idea.

Her great kite is a paper dragon, folded from a single sheet, as Shiori's way requires: one sheet of Frostfall paper forty feet square, folded over a winter
in the Kite Spire's great hall into a dragon with a jointed body, a long tail and wings that catch the wind like sails. It flies where she points. It is not
alive, she says; it is only very well folded. In the Ring the kite is a second place she fights from: her bolts come from the dragon as often as from her
hand, and her enemies never quite know which of the two to watch.

She is light and quick, in a saffron silk coat with indigo cuffs and paper ribbons braided into her hair, and she leads Kite Day on the Drowned Coast every
windy month, when the whole coast flies kites from the Spires and her dragon goes up first. The Windward's messages cross the Spires faster for her than they
ever have, and the Council of Captains has asked her to string a kite-line from the Spires to the Ring, which she says is impossible, and has started.

She has been up the Climb only once, to the Sea's lighthouse floor, to fly the dragon from its gallery; the Tower's air, she says, has no
wind in it at all, only something that pretends to be wind, and the dragon would not climb in it. She came down and flew it from the
Spires for a week without stopping, to make it feel better. She means to go back up with a longer line.

## People and appearance

Coast folk of the Spires, born after the Fall: twenty-three, light and quick, with sea-tanned skin, sea-glass in her ears, black hair braided with paper
ribbons, a saffron silk coat with indigo cuffs, and the great paper dragon kite on its line. Her look is not built yet; its brief is in the
[revamp plan](../revamp.md).

## Voice

Bright, quick and precise, with a habit of pointing at things in the sky that nobody else can see yet. She talks to her kite. She is very polite to Shiori and
very rude to Zephyr, and both know why.

## Voice lines

- **Picked:** "Look up. No, the other up."
- **Picked:** "One sheet. No cuts. Watch."
- **Winning:** "Folded."
- **Winning:** "Right where I pointed."
- **Losing:** "Tangled. I'll unpick it."
- **Losing:** "The wind changed. It does that."
- **Meeting Zephyr:** "Your wind's a bit weak today. Mine's fine."
- **Meeting Shiori:** "Teacher. I kept the crease."

## Secrets

- The dragon is alive, a little. On the night she finished the last fold it moved by itself in the hall, and she has never told anyone, not even Shiori,
  who would say that a fold made once and cleanly does that.
- She has flown a kite up the outside of the Tower, on a long line from the Ring's stands. It went up into the cloud and the line went slack. When she reeled
  it in, the kite had a message tied to its tail, in a hand she did not know.
- She is in love with Zephyr and has decided to wait until he notices, which she has calculated will take about forty years.

## What she wants from the Tower

To fly the dragon from the Noon Seat, the highest kite-post on the Dial; and to answer the message on her kite's tail.

## Ties

- **[Zephyr](zephyr.md)**, friend: she flies her dragon on his wind, and he never lets her forget it.
- **[Shiori](shiori.md)**, her teacher: Shiori taught her to fold paper; the dragon is folded from one sheet, as Shiori's way requires.
