# Professor Puff, the Aeronaut

*The Windward · [Coast folk](../peoples/coast.md) · from [the Drowned Coast](../realms/coast.md)* · [Heroes](README.md)

## Biography

Professor Puff was born on the Kite Spire forty years before the Fall, under a name that is in the Spires' register and that he will not say. He was a
small, round, serious child who did not like heights, which on the Spires is a kind of disability, and who solved it the way he has solved everything
since: by studying it. At twelve he had read every book on the air in the Spires' library. At twenty he had read every book on the air in the frozen library
at Frostfall, where the Hourkeepers made him a Correspondent. At twenty-five he built his first balloon, a bladder of oiled silk filled with a gas he had
distilled from the rose-coloured salt of the Spires' lowest rooms, and went up in it, and was very sick, and came down having mapped the winds over the
Drowned Coast to a height nobody had reached.

He has been mapping the sky ever since. Before the Fall he mapped it from a basket under glass spheres of his glowing rose gas, and published the first true
chart of the Dial's winds, the **Puff Atlas**, which every Windward captain carried. The Spires gave him the title of Professor, which he has used instead of
his name since. He was forty and aloft over the Lantern Coast at noon on midsummer day, taking readings, when the sun stopped and cracked and fell, and he
recorded the moment with an instrument for the first and only time in history: the wind changed direction nine times in nine heartbeats. The page is in the
frozen library.

Since the Fall the sky has changed, and he is mapping it again. The old atlas is useless; the winds keep no seasons. He goes up every week from whichever Spire
has room for his mooring, and comes down with readings, and the readings never agree. He is completely serious about it, about everything; he has been called
a comic figure, by people who have not read his work, and he finds this puzzling rather than hurtful. He is a small round scholar of the air in an aubergine coat
with a high collar and two rows of brass buttons, goggles up on his brow, silver tufts of hair, big calm eyes, and a brass tank on his back. Above and beside his
head float glowing rose spheres of gas in brass cradles.

His gas makes whatever it touches swell, which makes enemies larger and easier to hit, and which, in year 55, floated a whole hive-tree out of the Walking Wood.
He was mapping the sky over the Wood's south and let a bubble of gas drift down onto the great hive-tree called the Amber Mother; it swelled, lifted its roots
and floated a mile out of the Wood before it came down upside down in a field of the Field of Banners, with the Hive court inside. [Sirix](sirix.md), the Swarm
Warden, has not forgiven him, and the swarm rises to meet him whenever he flies over the Wood. He has written Sirix eleven letters of apology, with diagrams.

He argues engines with [Ada](ada.md) of the Underforge at every Truce dinner: two inventors at one table; she wins every argument about engines, he wins every
one about flying, and they have never agreed whether a balloon is an engine. And [Barry Cloudsley](barry-cloudsley.md) is his oldest flying companion: one reads
the sky, the other maps it. They have gone up together hundreds of times, Barry calling the wind and the Professor steering into it.

## People and appearance

Coast folk of the Spires, Noon-touched: forty at the Fall, he looks sixty, small and round with silver tufts of hair, brass goggles up on the brow and big calm
eyes; the aubergine coat with two rows of brass buttons, a brass tank on his back, and glowing rose gas spheres in brass cradles above and beside his head. In the
[revamp plan](../revamp.md) his figure is unchanged: his balloons set him apart, and he says the Spires' sun does not tan him.

## Voice

Precise, polite and academic, with long words and footnotes spoken aloud. He explains things during fights. He is never sarcastic and does not understand when
others are.

## Secrets

- The wind that changed direction nine times in nine heartbeats at the Fall was blowing toward the Tower each time, from nine places. He believes the Tower
  breathed in. He has told only the Hourkeepers.
- His rose gas is distilled from salt found only in the Spires' lowest rooms, the ones nobody built. He does not know what it is, and has never said so.
- He is still afraid of heights.

## What he wants from the Tower

To map it, inside and out, top to bottom, every floor's sky. Each floor has its own weather, and he has never been allowed to bring the basket in.
