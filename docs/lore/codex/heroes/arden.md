# Arden, the Huntmaster

*The Greenwood Moot · [Wood folk](../peoples/wood.md) · from [the Walking Wood](../realms/wood.md)* · [Heroes](README.md) · new, on the cards

## Biography

Arden was born in the forty-eighth summer after the Fall in a hunting lodge in the west of the Walking Wood, a lodge that has stood on eleven
different sites since then, because the Wood moves and the lodge is on skids like every other building in it. His father was a hunter of the Moot,
his mother a tracker, and he could read the ground before he could read a page: who had passed, how long ago, how heavy, how frightened. By twelve he
was tracking bark hounds alone, the low, long, amber-eyed dogs of bark and root that the Wood breeds in its dark, which come out of the thickets and
take sheep and children. By sixteen he had killed his first. By twenty he had sworn the Moot's oath, and grown antlers broad enough that he has to turn
his head to get through a door.

The **Hunt of the Moot** is the Wood folk's answer to what the Walking Wood breeds. Since the Fall the forest has not only walked; it has grown things in
its walking: bark hounds, moss giants, thorn sprites, things with no names that come out at the edges where the forest meets the villages. The Hunt rides
elk through the moving paths, keeping them clear, and its riders are the best trackers on the Dial. Arden rode with it from sixteen, led a wing of it at
thirty, and became its Huntmaster at forty, when the old Huntmaster's elk walked into a grove that walked into a river.

He is a tall, weathered hunter in olive and russet leathers, with an antler-tipped longbow, a hunting horn at his hip, and the broadest antlers in the Moot,
which he does not saw, unlike his friend Hank, because a hunter should look like what he hunts. He reads tracks the way the Hourkeepers' calligraphers read
ink. Everything that walks leaves a trail, he says, and every trail ends somewhere. In the Ring he shoots at where his enemy has been as well as where they
are, and the trail they leave is his to read.

He hunts with [Hank](hank.md), the Moot's woodcutter: the Wood's two hunters, Hank says, one of trees and one of what lives under them. They work the edges
together, Hank clearing the walking groves while Arden clears what walks out of them, and they have saved more villages between them than either would
count. And he eats with Bo, and argues with Fenna, and sleeps very little.

There are two quarries he has never brought in. The first is [Kohaku](kohaku.md), the fox spirit of the Fox court, the one fox in the Wood he has never caught:
she lets him get close, every few months, and then is not there. She has fooled him exactly once, in his first year as Huntmaster, and never again; he has
never caught her, and she has never fooled him twice. They both enjoy it more than they would admit.

The second is [Nyx](nyx.md), the Court of Dusk's knife. Ten years ago, in year 90, a Moot speaker was killed in the thickets near the Gloaming road, and the
Moot named the Court; Arden took up the trail. He has followed the Court's knife through the thickets of the Walking Wood for ten years since, and in all that
time he has never found one footprint. She is a shadow; a shadow leaves no tracks. Arden does not accept that. Everything that walks leaves a trail. He has
started finding small things on his path, left for him: a violet thread, a black feather, a footprint, once, a single one, perfect, in a patch of mud, pointing
at him. He takes it personally.

## People and appearance

Wood folk, sworn to the Moot at twenty, and born after the Fall: fifty-two, and looks it, tall and weathered, with ruddy skin, moss-green eyes, greying auburn
hair in a hunter's braid, and broad antlers, unsawn, the largest in the Moot. Olive and russet leathers, an antler-tipped longbow, a hunting horn at the hip.
His look is not built yet; its brief, which puts the antlers on the man as well as the bow, is in the [revamp plan](../revamp.md).

## Voice

Quiet and outdoor: a voice that does not carry, because a hunter's should not. He speaks in observations ("you've been running", "you're favouring the left")
and rarely says what he thinks of them. He whistles to his elk.

## Voice lines

- **Picked:** "You walked here. I can see every step."
- **Picked:** "Fresh trail. Let's go."
- **Winning:** "The trail ends here."
- **Winning:** "Brought in."
- **Losing:** "Lost the trail. Not for long."
- **Losing:** "Every track fades. Mine come back."
- **Meeting Nyx:** "I found your footprint, knife. Just the one."
- **Meeting Kohaku:** "Not twice, fox. Never twice."

## Secrets

- The Moot speaker killed in year 90 was not killed by Nyx. Arden found the real trail in his second year: it led to the Climb's gate. He has not told the Moot,
  because the Moot would stop him hunting Nyx, and he does not want to stop.
- The footprint in the mud was not Nyx's. It was his own, made the next morning, in the same patch of mud. He has thought about it a great deal.
- He is afraid of the Wood. He is the only Huntmaster who has ever said so, and he said it to Hank.

## What he wants from the Tower

To follow the trail that goes into it. Every trail ends somewhere, and the one from year 90 ends inside.
