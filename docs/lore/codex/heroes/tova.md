# Tova, the Hallbreaker

*The Underforge · [Mole-folk](../peoples/mole.md) · from [the Deep](../realms/deep.md)* · [Heroes](README.md) · new, on the cards

## Biography

When the old halls of the Deep fall in, someone has to open them, and when something lives in them, someone has to close them. Tova does both, with powder.
She is the Underforge's sapper: a mole-folk of the Lamp Road warren, born in the sixty-ninth summer after the Fall, who was told from her first dig that the
mole-folk open the rock with their claws and nothing else, and who decided at nine, watching a Kilnborn miner blow a seam with a charge of crystal powder, that
claws were very slow.

The mole-folk did not approve. A cave-in, to the mole-folk, is the worst thing that can happen, and powder makes cave-ins on purpose. Her Old Mother forbade it. Tova
learned it anyway, from the Kilnborn powder-makers at the great forge, who were delighted to have a mole-folk apprentice because she could feel the rock's grain with
her claws and tell them exactly where to set a charge. By fifteen she could bring down a wall to the inch; by twenty she could bring down a hall and leave its pillars
standing. The Underforge made her its Master of Sapping at twenty-six, the first mole-folk ever to hold a seat in the Hall of Marks by craft and not by warren.

She is the Underforge's sapper, in soot-dark leathers with a bandolier of charges, a coil of fuse at her hip and a long iron pry-bar, and she is called whenever a
wall must stop being a wall. Her charges burn a bright fuchsia, from the crystal powder of the Deep, ground from the dead crystals the Shining left behind. The old
halls of the Deep, nine centuries of them, fall in all the time since the Fall, as the Caldera's heat moves the rock, and Tova opens them: to rescue miners, to reach
seams, and, more and more since the far halls went dark, to close galleries where things have come up out of the deep rock that should have stayed there.

She digs with [Mabel](mabel.md), the Deepdelver, who keeps the Lamp Road: they dig the Deep together, one with claws and one with powder, and have opened more fallen
halls than any pair in the Deep. Mabel considers powder cheating and very efficient; Tova considers claws honest and very slow; they have lunch together every day in
whatever tunnel they are opening. She takes her powder from Brannoc's forge. And she argues with [Bram](bram.md), the mason, who builds what she knocks down: they have
rebuilt and re-demolished the same gallery of the old Deep four times, out of principle, and she won the fourth, and suspects he let her.

She has never failed to break a wall, with one exception. [Mum](mum.md), the Court of Dusk's silent harlequin, builds walls that cannot be seen, only the chalk lines
she leaves on the floor; Tova met them in the Ring and could not find them to set a charge. She has fought Mum three times and lost three times, and taken it as a
personal insult every time, and is building a charge that does not need to know where the wall is.

Her warren still does not quite know what to do with her. She lives in the Lamp Road warren in a round room like any other mole-folk's,
with one difference: its walls are lined with iron, because of the powder, and its door is the only door in the warren that locks. The
young mole-folk come to her to learn, against their Old Mothers' wishes, and she teaches them, and tells them every time that a charge set
wrong kills the sapper first. Three of them are Underforge sappers now. The Old Mothers call it a disgrace in council and send for her
whenever a seam falls in.

## People and appearance

A mole-folk of the Lamp Road, born after the Fall: thirty-one, stout and quick, with a mole's head and velvet dark fur, a long muzzle and small bright eyes, broad
hands with gilded claws scarred by powder burns, soot-dark leathers, a bandolier of charges, a coil of fuse and a long iron pry-bar. Her card drew her as a human
woman; in the [revamp plan](../revamp.md) her look brief makes her a mole-folk, so that the Underforge's sapper is of the people who dug the Deep first.

## Voice

Cheerful, quick and loud, a voice used to shouting "clear!" down tunnels; she counts down from three before everything. She talks about walls as if they were
personal enemies.

## Voice lines

- **Picked:** "That was a wall. Now it's a door. You're welcome."
- **Picked:** "Three. Two. Run."
- **Winning:** "Cleared."
- **Winning:** "Doorway. Made to order."
- **Losing:** "Short fuse. My fault."
- **Losing:** "Bury me in something I can blow open."
- **Meeting Mum:** "Where is it? I know it's there. Where is it?"
- **Meeting Bram:** "Built it again? I'll be quick."

## Secrets

- The things that come up out of the deep rock in the far halls are lurkers, the same as on the Climb's Deep floors. Tova has closed eleven galleries on them. She
  thinks they are coming up from the tunnel Mabel found and filled in, dug from below.
- Her Old Mother has never forgiven her for the powder, and has never once missed one of her duels.
- The charge that does not need to know where the wall is would bring down the Ring. She has not finished it, on purpose.

## What she wants from the Tower

To find out what is under it. Everything that stands is standing on something, and the Underforge's drill toward the Hall of Noon has stopped in rock that will not
break. She has offered to help. The Hall of Marks has said no.

## Ties

- **[Mum](mum.md)**, rival: the only walls Tova has never broken are the ones she cannot find.
- **[Bram](bram.md)**, rival: he builds what she knocks down, and she knocks down what he builds.
- **[Mabel](mabel.md)**, friend: they dig the Deep together, one with claws and one with powder.
