Wave 1a tuning: Cantor, Brannoc, Vesper

2026-11-12. The three heroes rebuilt last night, tuned until each wins about half its duels on either side. Every number is from the fight simulator (the computer playing both heroes, on the game's own code). Before: 32 fights a pairing (64 where both are of the three). After: 64 a pairing. A bar is how often the hero wins that pairing; the lighter strip is 20 to 80%, the aim. * not one of the six benchmark heroes.

In short. All three now sit inside the band, both sides together and as the player's hero alone. Cantor has no pairing past 78 to 22 (he had five). Brannoc has one, Kaia, which is older than his rework. Vesper is even overall but her pairings are still far apart: five of eight are one-sided, some of them the other way round than before. Three faults were in how the computer played them, not in their numbers, and one is in the engine and is not fixed here (the two computer players do not treat a basic attack alike).

Why so many pairings were 32 of 32

A duel between two computers is close to a race with no chance in it: the same casts, the same hits, fight after fight. A hero who is 12% ahead at the end wins every time, so a small edge reads as 100%. The older heroes miss with skillshots, which puts chance back in; these three hit with almost everything (a cone, a targeted needle, a hammer in melee). That is why a few points of damage turned Cantor against Kaia from 32 of 32 to even, and why some of Vesper's pairings flip with every small change. The same shows among the older heroes: Kaia beats Isolde 57 of 64 and loses to Lyra's side 4 of 32 with nothing of theirs changed.
