Hunting plains on the Climb's floors

A plan for your idea (2026-11-19): open plains on each floor where mobs keep spawning, a place to fight between the quests. Nothing is built yet; the questions at the end decide it.

Why the floors need it

The floors were made five times bigger on 14 November, but the fights did not grow with them. Floor 4 is 274 m across and holds 6 packs; floor 24 also 6; floor 44 has 12. Most of the map is walking. A pack you clear stays cleared for your climb (online it comes back after 3 minutes).

Where they could go

Each floor drawn from its own data. The orange rings are the widest open ground clear of the roads, the places, the packs, the people and the cover, found by measuring the floor. packs todaya plain

The Wood is the tightest (its trees fill the ground: plains of about 10 m there); the Sands and the War have room for 16–18 m.

How a plain would work

  • One or two plains a floor, each with a name on the map and a signpost at the road (the Gull Flats, the Thorn Meadow, the Glass Pan, the Black Shelf, the Burnt Field...), drawn as open wild ground: no paving.
  • Mobs that keep coming: 6 to 10 of the floor's weaker enemies (the Sea's crabs and dockers, the War's soldiers) wander the plain in ones and twos. One that falls comes back 20–30 s later at the far side, out of sight, never on top of you.
  • They fight like the packs: the same enemies, the same waking and leash (they never chase you far off the plain), the same models.
  • Online it is shared like the packs already are: everyone on the floor sees the same mobs, a kill counts for whoever hit it, and they get tougher with more players.
  • Built on what exists: the plain's mobs are small packs with a short comeback; the floors' other packs, quests and people stay where they are.

How it would be built

  1. One Sea floor, alone (no server change): the plain, its mobs and the comeback; pictures and a clip for your look.
  2. All 50 floors: the plains placed by measuring each floor (as on the maps above), their names and signposts; every floor played through by the route bot.
  3. Online: the plains' mobs shared on the server (a new message: open tabs reload at the deploy) and a two-player test. Live only with a deploy you ask for.
