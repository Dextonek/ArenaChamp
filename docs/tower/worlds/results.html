What every floor has

  • A camp with a keeper (main quest), a second person (two side quests) and a trader who sells for shards.
  • Three kinds of enemy in packs, an elite, and a named enemy with a bounty.
  • Three hidden caches, a lore stone, and waystones you wake at after a fall.
  • A rival climber: a race on floors 1 and 4, a duel on floors 2, 3 and 5. Beat them and they fight beside you against the ruler.
  • A trial door into one room of the weekly Tower, for a relic.
  • The ruler behind a door, then the gatekeeper's duel, where your boons are set aside.
  • Three lives, four stars, and one boon kept as a sigil for later climbs. Your floor is saved in the browser if you leave or reload.

What is not done

  • Nobody has played it. The computer finds stuck quests and unreachable spots, but not whether it is fun or clear.
  • Difficulty is rough. The win rates above are from 8 to 12 fights each.
  • Looks are first pass. Grounds, props and enemies are plain shapes drawn in code; each ruler is a hero's model drawn big. This is where your eye is needed.
  • Not measured on a phone. On this computer a floor costs 10 to 14 ms a frame, a duel about 8.
  • Alone only, and kept in the browser, not on the account. Friends on a floor is a later step.

To try it

  1. Open the game, press Tower, stay on the Climb tab.
  2. Pick a hero and press Start floor 1.
  3. Talk to Gus in the camp (walk up to him), then follow the arrow.
