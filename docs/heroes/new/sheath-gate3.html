Kagen, the Scarlet Sheath

His four spells and his passive with every number. Each spell has an answer, checked against the game's walking speed (4 m/s) and a player's reaction (0.25 s). Say yes, or change spells while they are still words.

One correction to the card first. The card said no hero stores damage on the enemy and spends it later. Mr. Ledger does: his spells put damage on your tab, and it comes due later. Kagen's marks are kept, but they follow different rules, so they play differently (the table below). If that is still too close, there is one other way at the bottom of this page.

The idea

He fights close with the sword. Every blow that lands leaves a thin cinnabar cut on the enemy. The cuts do nothing until he puts the blade home: click, and they all open at once.

The kit

Under each spell: the red bar is how long its warning shows, the pale bar how long a player needs to get out (grey: reacting).

PScarlet Sheathhis passive-

Each of his hits that lands (a sword blow, Issen, each wave of Senbon) leaves a cut mark on you: a thin cinnabar line, for both players to see. 4 at most. They never open by themselves and fade 5 s after his latest cut. Only Noto (W) or Sekai-giri (R) opens them: 24 each. All four at once also slow you 30% for 1 s.

Answer: Count the lines on yourself. With marks, keep out of his ring, or go 5 s without being cut: they are gone.

QIssenOne Flash6 s

A thin white line shows down his aim (6 m) for 0.45 s, then he crosses it in a flash and stands at its end. Whoever the cut passes: 32 and a mark. His way in, or out.

Answer: Step off the line: 0.6 m to its edge.

WNotoThe Click7 s

No aim. He slides the blade home: a ring of 3.5 m shows round him for 0.5 s, then the click. Every mark on an enemy inside the ring opens: 24 each, 96 at most. The parry: for 0.4 s after the click, the next hit on him does nothing and gives the attacker a mark.

Answer: Step out of the ring: 0.9 m from his sword reach to its edge. Do not hit him just after the click.

ESenbonThousand Swords11 s

A cinnabar ring (3 m) is painted on a spot within 8 m for 1 s, then katanas rain into it in three waves, 0.4 s apart. Each wave that finds you inside: 10 and a mark.

Answer: Walk out: 3 m from the very middle.

RSekai-giriWorld Sever26 s

One cut along a 12 m line, drawn on the floor. He stands still for 1.3 s while the world goes grey round it (only a look: nobody else is held). Then it tears: 110 on the line, and every mark on you opens wherever you stand, at 36 each.

Answer: Leave the line: 1.2 m. Carry few marks into it. He stands still 1.3 s: hit him.

Why it is fun

  • For him: click now for what he has, or fight on for the fourth mark and its slow, while you may step away. Spend the marks close with the click, or keep them for World Sever, which opens them anywhere and for more.
  • For you: his whole threat is written on your body. Fight him in close and carry the cuts, or step out and let them fade. When the ring shows, you have half a second.
  • His weakness: any damage of his that waits can be walked away from, and he has 640 HP and no shield. World Sever holds him still for 1.3 s.

His E and R, as they look now

The numbers in short

  • Damage now: blow 9, Issen 32, Senbon 10 a wave (30 if you stay for all three), Sekai-giri 110.
  • Damage that waits: 24 a mark (36 with Sekai-giri). The biggest combo, four marks then World Sever: 254, about half of the lowest HP in the game, and only if you take four cuts in 5 s and then stand on the line.
  • The computer as Kagen fights close, saves the click for 3 or 4 marks, uses Issen to close in or get away, and keeps World Sever for when you carry 2 or more marks. Against him it backs off when it carries marks, and steps out of the ring.
  • Exact numbers are set by the fight simulator at gate 5 (between 40% and 60% wins).

The other way (only if the marks feel too close to Mr. Ledger)

Cuts in the air. His blade leaves thin cinnabar lines on the floor where it passed, not on you. The click opens every line, and whoever stands on one then is hit. It is closer to Shiori's ink strokes, which also lie on the floor and burst with her ultimate. The kit above keeps the marks you agreed to.
