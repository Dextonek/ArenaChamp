Kagen, the Scarlet Sheath

The new swordsman you asked for: a real hero whose kit is the showpieces' moves, so someone actually fights with them. This is gate 1 of the new-hero rules: the idea, before any model or spell. Say yes, or change anything while it is still words.

One sentence
A swordsman who never fights with a drawn blade: he cuts, and the cuts only open when he sheathes it.
Signature shape
The scarlet sheath with two paper seals, and the cut line that opens like a burning almond.
Colours
Cinnabar (a vermilion, warmer and more orange than the enemy's red) pale rice-paper foil, on black lacquer with gold fittings.
Role
Assassin / duelist, melee. Low health, high delayed damage.

The new thing (rule 2)

Delayed cuts. No hero in the game stores its damage on the enemy and spends it later. Kagen's hits leave cut marks (thin red lines on the enemy, up to 4), and they only deal their damage when he sheathes the blade (W, the click) or when World Sever tears. Both players see every mark.

The question he asks
"How many cuts will you let me put on you before I click?" Every few seconds the opponent must decide to take marks and run, or press him before he sheathes.
His weakness
His damage comes late. Kill him, stun him or push him out of reach before the click, and the marks fade unspent (5 s). Low health, no shield.
His player's second decision
When to click: now, for the marks he has, or later, for more, risking that they fade or that he dies first. The click's short parry window is a second reason to time it.

His moves, as they look now

Lore (rule 0)

People and home
A Northerner, from a mountain shrine in Frostfall where the Tear can be seen in the sky on clear nights.
Faction
Loners (with Oblivion, Sir Whiskers, The Great Zambini, Ostrel, Asenet).
Age
Looks 38. He was a boy of 9 sweeping the shrine on the day the sun fell (Noon-touched).
Story
His master drew once at the Tear the night it opened, and never sheathed his sword again: the cut he made was never finished. Kagen keeps the master's sheath, lacquered red in the last noon light, and swears to finish that cut: to close the Tear with one draw.
Ties
Oblivion came through the Tear: the one he waits to meet in the Ring. Zephyr wants to race his draw and never quite sees it. Shiori inks the paper seals on his sheath. Sir Whiskers spars with him and calls him slow, from a safe distance.
Line
“It is already cut. You just have not noticed.”

Nearest heroes (rule 1: not mistaken for them)

  • Zephyr, the Wind Blade: also a sword, but wind crescents, teal and jade, and fast dashes that build gusts. Kagen is still, red-black, and his damage is stored.
  • Sir Whiskers, the Lynx Duelist: a duelist, but comic claw strokes and ginger. Kagen is grave and mythic.
  • Shiori, the Last Brush: ink and paper too. Kagen keeps paper only on his sheath's seals; his look is lacquer, steel and cinnabar, not brush strokes.
  • Vesper, the Crimson Countess: crimson. That is why his accent is cinnabar (orange-red) with a pale paper foil, not her crimson and not the enemy's red.

How he looks (for gate 2)

A tall, lean man of about 38, one continuous realistic body like every hero: dark hair tied back, a calm, scarred face. A black kimono-like coat over grey, a cinnabar sash and cord, black lacquer armour on one shoulder only, straw sandals. The katana in his right hand; when sheathed, at his left hip. Mythic and quiet, never cartoon. His moves use the animation pack's sword clips.
