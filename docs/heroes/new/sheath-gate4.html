Gate 4 · the spells · a draft, not in the game

Kagen, the Scarlet Sheath

His four spells and his passive, built and drawn. Every picture is taken in a real duel in the Frost arena from the game camera's angle. The enemy (Brannoc) is held still and silenced, so each sheet shows only Kagen's spell. Read each sheet left to right, top to bottom.

PScarlet Sheaththe cut marks

Each blow that lands leaves one thin cinnabar slash on the enemy's body, with a rice-paper rim, one to four, each at its own angle. Small red marks over the head count them too. Both players see them.

QIssenOne Flash

A fine paper line is laid down his aim and a ring of light closes on his feet. Then a white-steel streak runs the length of the line as he crosses it, and a thin cinnabar line hangs after him and splits open like a cut. The enemy gets one mark.

WNotoThe Click

A pale ring with tick marks closes round him while a gold glint pulses at the sheath's mouth. At the click a cinnabar ring pulse goes out and the cuts burst open on the enemy's body. In this picture the enemy carried three marks: 72 damage. A faint pale arc in front of him is the parry window.

ESenbonThousand Swords

One brush stroke paints the ring on the floor. Katanas gather points-down under a thin ring that hangs high in the air, then fall in three volleys and stick in the floor with a steel flash. Each volley that finds the enemy: 10 and a mark.

RSekai-giriWorld Sever

The cut is drawn along the floor and the world turns grey around it while he stands still. After a dead beat comes one black impact frame. Then the line tears open along its whole length like a burning almond, and shards of the floor drift up out of the slit. In this picture the enemy stood off the line, so its four marks opened anyway: 144 damage.

Icons and portrait

Issen, Noto, Senbon, Sekai-giri and the passive at 128, 64 and 32 pixels, with his portrait, beside Anka and Zephyr for scale.

Checked

  • The damage matches the kit in these very pictures: Issen 32, the click with three marks 72, Senbon's three waves 30, World Sever opening four marks off the line 144.
  • No errors in the engine's frame-by-frame test of all four spells, or in 15 computer-against-computer fights. The type check and the build pass.
  • The computer plays him and uses all four spells. In 15 quick fights before any tuning he won 9. Balance is the next gate.

Worth your eye

  • Senbon's hanging ring floats 4.6 m up, so from the steep camera it shows well above the floor ring, round where Kagen stands. Say if it should hang lower.
  • Only World Sever blacks out the screen for a moment. Issen and the click had blackouts too; I took them out, because a full-screen flash on spells cast every few seconds was too much.
  • Opening all four marks slows the enemy by 40%, the game's one slow, not the 30% on the sheet.
  • The icons were exported with a stand-in. The icon tool (VectorCraft) is not on this computer, so they need one run of the real export on the computer that has it. They will look the same.
