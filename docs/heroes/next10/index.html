Ten new heroes

Gate 1 cards (docs/pipeline/new-hero.md): each a mythic role in one sentence, a shape you can draw as an icon, an accent and a pale foil, and one thing the game does not have yet. Under each: the question it asks the opponent, its weakness, the choice its own player keeps making, its five spells with what the effect looks like, and how the figure looks.

Added 2026-11-14: each card now names the hero's faction and gives a line of their legend; the whole world, its factions and every hero's story are on the lore page, the second ten new heroes on their own page, and full kit sheets for all twenty on the kits page.

Nothing here is built. Pick the ones you like (or change them while they are words): each picked hero then goes through the look gate (the model, beside Sefu and Kaia) before any spell is made.

1 · gorgon

Lithea

the Stone Gaze
controller · ranged
A veiled gorgon priestess whose look turns whoever meets it to marble.
New: the gaze. Her spells care which way you face. Facing her when they land turns you to stone a little; facing away, they barely touch you. No hero uses facing today.
Question
"Can you fight me without looking at me?" Walking at her means facing her; backing off means turning your back.
Weakness
She must face you too: from behind or the side she has nothing, and her big casts are slow.
Her choice
Veil down (armour, weak gaze) or lifted (full gaze, no armour): she changes it, and both screens show it.
PMarble Grace. Each gaze hit on a face turned to her adds a stone stack (three: stone 1 s). Marble creeps up the legs in veined white plates.
QSerpent Strike (line). A bronze snake lunges 9 m. A verdigris serpent ribbon, the hit flaking stone dust.
WGarden of Statues (ground). Raises three marble statues: cover for anyone, and they gaze too. Statues rise out of cracking floor, eyes glinting green.
ECoil (dash). A slither 6 m, leaving a coil that slows. A scaled S-curve drawn on the floor in bronze.
RUnveiling. 1.5 s wind-up, a great eye opens on the floor; whoever faces her within 12 m at the end turns to stone 1.6 s. Answer: turn away. The veil lifts, the eye's slit pupil narrows, a white flash, marble shell.
verdigrismarble foil· nearest: Sefu (gold sand), Isolde (stone-like frost)
Look: tall, a sheer verdigris veil over the face, bronze serpent coils as a crown that move a little, a marble-white gown with bronze edges, a short bronze staff with a serpent eye.
Faction: The Dune Throne. Legend: The last veiled priestess of the tombs of the Sands. A guard of the Night Temple who looked at her too long became Corbel the gargoyle, and Silvane carries the one mirror that turns her gaze. Lore
Changed on the kit sheet: The veil lifts by itself for 2.5 s after each spell (the card had her switch it; there is no fifth key). Q is a serpent that seeks a clicked enemy, not a line: as a line her four shapes were Silvane's (line, terrain, dash, self). W raises two statues, not three (the budget). Colour clash: the card's accent `#3fbf9a` is 10 from Mortis's colour (under 12 reads as the same). A nearby colour that keeps apart: `#2cb28b`. Or keep it and let the figure tell them apart; to decide at the look. Kit sheet
2 · moth

Vela

the Dream Moth
support · ranged
A luna-moth priestess whose wing dust carries sleep.
New: sleep. A sleeping hero cannot act until damage wakes them, and the hit that wakes them lands harder. Nobody puts heroes to sleep today, so she has to choose: let them sleep, or wake them with something big.
Question
"Will you stay in my dust?" Three dust stacks in four seconds and you sleep.
Weakness
Her own damage wakes you. She is frail, and dust is slow to build.
Her choice
Wake the sleeper with a big hit, or keep them asleep to walk away, heal, or set up her lanterns.
PPowdered Wings. The first hit on her every 6 s is softened 30%, and a melee attacker gets a dust stack. A puff of pale green powder off the wings.
QDream Dust (cone). Adds a drowsy stack. A soft cone of glittering mint powder that hangs a moment.
WMoonlit Lantern (ground, 2 charges). A paper lantern on a post: enemy bolts within 5 m bend into it (it takes two), and allies in its light heal. A pale green paper lantern, bolts curving into it and going out in a puff.
EFlutter (dash). A quick flight to one of her lanterns or 5 m. Wings open wide, a trail of drifting scales.
RLullaby of Wings. 1.2 s wind-up, then a ring 7 m: two drowsy stacks to all inside, and every lantern flares. A giant moth's silhouette spreads over the floor, dust falling like snow.
luna greenpowder ivory· nearest: Fenna (deep green and pink), Grandma Ironknit (support)
Look: a slender priestess with great pale green moth wings with long trailing tails and two ivory eye spots, a soft fur collar, a grey-lilac gown, feathered antennae as a circlet; a lantern staff in the left hand.
Faction: The Greenwood Moot. Legend: The moth priestess who lays the Walking Wood to sleep each dusk and hangs paper lanterns in its glades, so that travellers sleep safe; a friend of Sirix and of Saga. Lore
Changed on the kit sheet: Sleep lasts 1.5 s at most (the lock rule) and leaves 5 s without stacks. The ultimate is a 4.5 m circle at a point, not a 7 m ring round her: from its middle a 7 m ring needs 2 s to leave and warned 1.2 s. Kit sheet
3 · salt

Morwen

the Saltwarden
controller · ranged
A hedge witch who draws circles of salt that no spell can cross.
New: lines that stop spells but not feet. Walls stop everything. Her salt lines let anyone walk across but no spell, from either side. To trade spells with her you have to come in.
Question
"Will you step inside my circle?"
Weakness
Up close she is soft; the circle shelters the enemy from her spells too.
Her choice
Draw the circle round herself (a shelter) or round the enemy (a cage for their spells).
PHearthward. Inside her own salt she heals slowly. Candle-small flames on the line nearest her.
QIron Nail (line). A thrown iron nail that slows. A black nail with a cold blue-white glint, rust flakes.
WSalt Circle (zone, 4 m). Drawn over 0.6 s, holds 5 s: no spell crosses its edge. A pouring white salt line, crystals catching light, spells breaking on it in sparks.
EHex Bundle (target). The enemy's next dash fizzles. A bundle of herbs tied with red thread smoulders above them.
RThe Boundary. A 12 m line: for 2 s nothing crosses it, then it stays 6 s as a salt line. A wall of blowing salt and iron filings that settles into a line.
cold ironsalt white· nearest: Mum (walls), Referee Whistler (no spells)
Look: a hooded hedge witch, grey-blue wool and a pale linen apron, iron keys and herb bundles at the belt, a pouch of salt pouring from the left hand, a black iron staff with a candle cage.
Faction: The Truce of the Ring. Legend: The hedge witch who salts the Ring's edge every dawn so that no spell reaches the crowd. Mortis's dead cannot cross her lines, and Kohaku has tried every night. Lore
Changed on the kit sheet: None to the idea. The numbers are new: circle 4 m for 5 s, the Boundary a wall for 2 s and a salt line for 6 s more. Kit sheet
4 · kite

Lian

the Kite Master
marksman · ranged
A kite master whose paper dragon flies where she points and strikes from the sky.
New: a second place to cast from. Her kite flies on a 9 m string toward the pointer. Two of her spells start at the kite, not at her, and the string between them cuts whoever stands across it.
Question
"Where is the danger: the kite, or me?"
Weakness
The kite can be hit down (three hits; 6 s to fly it again), and she walks slower while flying it.
Her choice
Fly the kite close to the enemy (strong, but the string is in reach) or keep it safe.
PLong String. The string cuts lightly whoever crosses it. A thin silk line that hums and glints indigo.
QPaper Dart (from the kite). A folded dart to the target. Saffron paper folding in the air, a crisp tear on hit.
WReel In. The kite flies home fast; the string sweeps everything between. The string glowing as it whips round, paper streamers.
EFirecracker (from the kite). A string of crackers drops below it, 1 s fuse. Red-gold paper crackers popping in a chain of small flashes and smoke.
RDragon Dance. The kite becomes a long paper dragon that follows its own path 4 s, cutting what it crosses. A paper dragon with a saffron head and indigo scales, ribbons streaming.
saffronpale indigo· nearest: Lyra (marksman), Professor Puff (things in the air)
Look: a light, quick figure in a saffron silk coat with indigo cuffs, a wide sash, braided hair with paper ribbons; the reel in the left hand, the dragon kite flying above.
Faction: The Windward. Legend: The best kite flier of the Spires. Shiori taught her to fold paper, and she flies her paper dragon on Zephyr's winds, which he never lets her forget. Lore
Changed on the kit sheet: The kite is placed where she flies it, not steered: a thing that moves on its own drifts apart on the two screens (units do). W is Let Fly, with Reel In as its recast. Colour clash: the card's accent `#ffb21e` is 6 from Sirix's colour (under 12 reads as the same). A nearby colour that keeps apart: `#ffa119`. Or keep it and let the figure tell them apart; to decide at the look. Kit sheet
5 · chain

Kessa

the Chainwarden
juggernaut · melee
The warden of a drowned prison, who never lets a prisoner go.
New: trading places. Chained to the enemy, she can swap places with them in a blink: off a ledge of fire, behind a wall, into her own trap.
Question
"Where will you be in a second: here, or where I stand?"
Weakness
Everything needs the chain: dodge the shackle and she is a slow fighter. A dash breaks the chain.
Her choice
Swap now, or hold the chain and drag them.
PIron Will. Takes 15% less while a chain holds. Links glow warm along her arm.
QShackle (hook). Chains the enemy 4 s: they cannot go beyond 8 m from her (dragged and slowed). A heavy copper-iron chain uncoiling, a shackle snapping shut with sparks.
WBulwark of Links (self). Armour; recast breaks the chain in a stunning slam. Chains wrapping her like mail, then bursting out in a ring.
EExchange. Swaps places with the chained enemy. Both figures blur into links and land in each other's place.
RThe Iron Post. A post drops between them; both are chained to it 3 s (4 m). An iron post hammered into the floor, two chains drawing tight.
tarnished copperpale steel· nearest: Brannoc (forge), Ada (hook)
Look: a broad warden in dark plate with copper rivets, a long sea-worn coat, a great ring of keys, chains wound round both forearms; a heavy shackle in the right hand.
Faction: The Truce of the Ring. Legend: Warden of the Gaol of Oaths under the Drowned Harbour, the Truce's prison for oath-breakers. Asenet, the Uncrowned, is the one prisoner who ever slipped her chain. Lore
Changed on the kit sheet: The ultimate is renamed The Iron Post: a published game is called The Iron Oath. Exchange is named for what it is, a swap of places, which moves the enemy farther than the engine's 4 m pull cap; it reaches only as far as the chain (7 m). The chain's reach is 7 m (the card said 8). Colour clash: the card's accent `#c08a4a` is 8 from Wick's colour (under 12 reads as the same). A nearby colour that keeps apart: `#d89f77`. Or keep it and let the figure tell them apart; to decide at the look. Kit sheet
6 · aurora

Saga

the Northern Light
mage · ranged
A sky dancer of the far north who hangs the aurora's curtains across the battle.
New: lines that change whoever crosses them. Her curtains don't stop anyone. Walking through one gives its colour: green slows, violet silences, rose marks you for more damage. She gets speed from her own.
Question
"Will you cross the light, or walk around?"
Weakness
Curtains are lines: going around them is always possible. She is frail.
Her choice
Her next curtain's colour (green, violet, rose, in turn): cast now, or hold for the colour she needs. Both screens show the next one.
PPolar Light. Crossing her own curtain: haste 1.5 s. The curtain ripples where she passes.
QShimmer (line). A streak that leaves a short curtain where it lands. A thin luminous ribbon folding upward.
WCurtain. A 10 m curtain of the next colour, 6 s. Tall soft folds of light, bright at the hem, fading up into stars.
ESkate (dash). Glides 6 m on rime. A white frost trail with sparkles.
RThe Sky Opens. Every curtain swings round its middle over 3 s: lines that move. The whole floor washed in green and violet, curtains turning like doors.
aurora greensnow· nearest: Isolde (frost), Opaline (light)
Look: a tall dancer in deep night-blue with a white fur-trimmed mantle, silver embroidery like star charts, long pale braids, a crown of thin silver rays; a slim silver wand in the left hand.
Faction: The Hourkeepers. Legend: She dances the aurora that tells the hour at Frostfall: Isolde's sister, Lyra's partner at the observatory, and Kaia's rival for the sky. Lore
Changed on the kit sheet: None to the idea. The ultimate swings each curtain 90 degrees in 2.5 s, and its fans show on the floor 1.0 s before. Kit sheet
7 · obsidian

Itzel

the Obsidian Herald
bruiser · melee
A herald with a black glass blade who splits the ground, and only she can cross what she splits.
New: ground that only she can cross. Her strikes split the floor: for 3 s each crack is a chasm to the enemy (go round, or dash over) and a road to her (she walks across, and runs along it). Changed on the kit sheet: the first version made the danger where two cracks cross, and that is Shiori's ink.
Question
"Which side of the crack do you want to be on?"
Weakness
Every crack is straight, at most 9 m, shows as a bright line and closes in 3 s; any dash jumps it. Without cracks she is an ordinary fighter.
Her choice
Cut the enemy off from cover and keep them near her, or cut a road away for herself.
PFault Lines. Her cracks are chasms to the enemy for 3 s, then harmless scars; she walks across them. Black glass lips, turquoise deep inside: bright while a chasm, dim as a scar.
QObsidian Edge (cone). A sweep that leaves a 6 m crack along its middle. An obsidian arc with a turquoise inner edge.
WRift (line). A 9 m crack from her feet, opening over 0.5 s. A turquoise glint running ahead, the ground splitting behind it.
EShatterstep (dash). Runs 6 m, cracking the floor behind her. Glass shards kicked up at each step.
RObsidian Sun. A star of eight cracks from her opens after 1.2 s, then walls the ground round her into eight wedges for 3 s. A disc of black glass with a turquoise rim rises behind her as the floor bursts.
turquoiseobsidian sheen· nearest: Bram (ground bursts), Mabel (holes)
Look: a lean warrior in black lacquered armour inlaid with turquoise, a feathered mantle in deep jade and white, a gold collar; a broad black glass blade in the right hand.
Faction: The Underforge. Legend: Herald of the Underforge, who speaks for the fallen sun's fire where it cools into black glass in the Ember Caldera. She thinks Campana's bronze would crack in a real fire. Lore
Changed on the kit sheet: Her new thing changed. The card made the danger where two cracks cross; that is Shiori's ink, whose strokes already burst where they cross (rule 2). Now her cracks are ground only she can cross. Glass Edge is renamed Obsidian Edge (glass edge is taken), and Black Sun is renamed Obsidian Sun (the black sun is a far-right symbol). Colour clash: the card's accent `#30d0c0` is 3 from Sefu accent's colour (under 12 reads as the same). A nearby colour that keeps apart: `#47b2ae`. Or keep it and let the figure tell them apart; to decide at the look. Kit sheet
8 · fox

Kohaku

the Foxfire
assassin · melee
A fox spirit who steals the name of a spell and calls it with her own fire.
New: stealing a spell. She takes the last spell you cast: it waits 4 s longer for you, and she gets one foxfire copy of its kind (a bolt, a dash, a ring or a shield).
Question
"What did you cast last?" Feed her a small spell and keep the big one safe.
Weakness
She has to get within 7 m; she is fragile, and the steal is wasted on a weak spell.
Her choice
Steal now, or wait for the enemy's big spell.
PTails of Foxfire. Each stolen spell lights one of her tails; nine lit: her next hit burns blue. Tails of pale blue flame, counted on her back for both players.
QFoxfire Bite (dash-strike). A blue-white fox head of flame snapping shut.
WGlamour (self). Leaves a fox-shaped decoy and is unseen 1 s. A fox of white smoke that runs on and puffs out.
EBorrowed Name (target, 7 m). The steal. The stolen spell's icon drawn in blue fire, pulled from the enemy into her paw.
RWisp Hunt. Nine foxfire wisps circle her, then hunt the enemy one by one. Flickering blue flames with white hearts, streaking in curves.
foxfire bluesnow-fox white· nearest: Sir Whiskers (feline), Kaia (blue-white)
Look: a slim woman in a white and pale grey layered robe with a rust-red under-sash, a carved white fox mask pushed to the side of her head, a long white-tipped fox tail of fur; a fan in the left hand.
Faction: The Court of Dusk. Legend: A fox spirit of Gloaming who steals the names of spells. She once stole the word off Hesk's first golem, and Sir Whiskers has chased her for longer than either admits. Lore
Changed on the kit sheet: The steal seals the key for 4 s rather than adding to its cooldown, so it works on Vesper, Brannoc and Cantor, who have no cooldowns. Nine tails became three lit tails (a round is too short for nine), and the passive is renamed Tails of Foxfire (the Nine-Tails is a famous character of another series). Kit sheet
9 · bell

Campana

the Bellwright
tank · melee
A temple bell-founder whose bronze bells ring each other into a chorus.
New: chain reactions between her things. Hit one of her bells and its ring spreads out; a ring reaching another bell rings that one too. One blow can set off the whole floor.
Question
"Inside the ring, or past it?" Only the edge of a ring hurts and stuns.
Weakness
Without bells she is a slow tank; bells can be broken (two hits).
Her choice
Where to set each bell, so that the chain runs through the enemy.
PResonance. A ring that reaches a bell rings it. Thin bronze sound rings expanding on the floor, pale patina at the rim.
QClapper (melee). A heavy strike that rings the nearest bell within 6 m. A bronze hammer arc and a deep shiver in the air.
WCast a Bell (ground, 2 charges). Sets a bronze bell. A bell dropped from above, landing with dust and a hum.
EToll (dash). Charges to a bell and rings it. A bronze streak, the bell swinging.
RThe Great Bell. 1.0 s shadow warning, then a great bell drops over the enemy: inside it 1.5 s, no spells, then it rings every bell in a chain. A huge bronze bell falling, a deep boom, rings racing bell to bell.
bronzepale patina· nearest: Big Yoko (tank, rings), Cantor (sound)
Look: a strong founder in a heavy leather apron over temple robes of deep plum, bronze bracers, a small bell on a cord at the hip; a bronze hammer in the right hand.
Faction: The Noonsworn. Legend: She cast every bell of Aubade, the bells that ring the thin sun up each morning, and believes enough bells rung at the Tower's top would call the sun back. Lore
Changed on the kit sheet: Rings are born at the bell's lip (1.5 m), so the safe places are next to a bell or past 5 m. Bells have 90 HP rather than "two hits". Colour clash: the card's accent `#d29a4a` is 4 from Sefu's colour (under 12 reads as the same). A nearby colour that keeps apart: `#b2962c`. Or keep it and let the figure tell them apart; to decide at the look. Kit sheet
10 · ferry

Ostrel

the Ferryman
skirmisher · ranged
The ferryman of the dead, who lays rivers of pale light and rides them.
New: ground that carries you. His rivers are strips that move whoever stands on them along their flow, enemy or him. Downstream is quick, upstream is a crawl.
Question
"Will you cross the river, or be carried?"
Weakness
The rivers are narrow (2.5 m): a step across is always there. Away from water he is ordinary.
His choice
Which way each river flows: toward him (to bring them close) or away (to carry himself out).
PFerry Toll. On his own river his spells come back faster. His lantern burns brighter on the water.
QPole Sweep (cone). A push of 3 m. A long pole arc with a spray of pale water.
WRiver of Souls. A 12 m strip flowing 3 m/s one way, 6 s. Pale blue water with drifting lantern lights and faint faces in the current.
EToll Mark (target). A marked enemy carried by a river takes damage per metre. A green-gold coin floating over their head, spinning.
RThe Last Crossing. A river across the floor that slowly turns into a whirl, drawing to its middle 4 s. A wide pale river curling into a whirlpool, lanterns spinning in.
lantern green-goldfog grey· nearest: Marin (tide), Gus (wet floor)
Look: a tall gaunt figure in a long fog-grey hooded coat with a deep blue lining, a pale calm face, coins sewn on the hem; a long ferry pole with a hanging lantern in the left hand.
Faction: a loner. Legend: Ferryman of the dead, who serves no faction because every faction's dead ride with him. He brings the Sands' dead to Nebet's scale; Mortis keeps the dead back, and they are sworn enemies. Lore
Changed on the kit sheet: The way a river flows is chosen by a recast (away from him on the cast, toward him on the recast). The ultimate's whirl lasts 2.2 s and carries 4 m at most, inside the pull cap. Kit sheet

What happens next

Pick any of the ten (or change them while they are words). Each picked hero goes through the gates one at a time: the look first (the model beside Sefu and Kaia, for your yes), then the kit on paper with every number and answer, then the spells, the CPU and balance (40-60%), then release. The colours above are first picks; each gets checked against the palette rows before the look.
