Ten more heroes

Gate 1 cards for heroes 11 to 20 of the new twenty, made for the world of the Dial (the lore): they fill the thin factions (two more for the Dune Throne, one each for the Noonsworn, the Greenwood, the Court of Dusk, the Hourkeepers and the Underforge, one more loner), give the strongest rivalries their missing side (Sefu and his sister Asenet, Nyx and the Huntmaster, Vesper and the Sunbearer, Oblivion and the Sky-Stitcher), and spread the roles (two tanks, two juggernauts, two marksmen, an assassin, a summoner, an engineer, a support).

Each brings one thing the game does not have yet, not shared with the forty or with the first ten. The full kit of each, every number and answer, is on the kits page and in its sheet. Nothing here is built; each picked hero goes through the look first.

11 · scale

Nebet

the Heartweigher
juggernaut · melee
The judge of the dead of the Sands, who weighs every heart against a feather.
New: The scale: she compares the two heroes. A scale over the fight tips toward whoever has more of their health left, and her spells grow with how far it tips against her. No hero compares the two health bars today.
Question
"Do you want to be ahead of me?" Pulling ahead makes her hits heavier.
Weakness
Ahead, she is a plain fighter who hits 10% softer, and her ultimate is weak when she is winning. Keeping the bars level takes her edge away.
Her choice
When to weigh: her ultimate pays most when she is far behind, so she can wait for it, or spend it early to stop the bleeding.
PThe Feather and the Heart (passive). A scale hangs over the fight for both players. A small gold scale drawn over the two health plates, its beam tipping, a feather in one pan and a heart in the other; her blade's lapis edge brightens as she falls behind.

12 · mirage

Sahar

the Mirage
marksman · ranged
A desert archer who shoots from shade that is not there.
New: False cover. His mirages look like the arena's walls and palms but stop nothing: not feet, not bolts, not sight. Every hero hides behind cover; his cover lies.
Question
"Is that wall real?"
Weakness
A mirage casts no shadow and its foot shimmers; walking into one breaks it for both players. Without mirages he is a plain archer with 540 HP.
His choice
Raise a mirage where the enemy will want to hide, or in front of himself, where his arrows pass through it stronger.
PHeat Haze (passive). His arrows that pass through a mirage come out 30% stronger. The mirage: the arena's own sandstone wall or palm, with a faint heat shimmer at the foot and no shadow; broken, it ripples into heat lines and is gone.
QGlass Arrow (line). 0.3 s wind-up, 24 m/s. A glass-tipped arrow with a heat-lilac streak that flares as it leaves a mirage.
WMirage (terrain). A false wall or palm rises at a point and stands 8 s. Heat lines rising off the ground and settling into the shape of a wall or a palm.
EShimmer Step (blink). He blinks 5 m and leaves a mirage of himself where he stood for 2 s. He flickers like air over sand; his image stays and wavers.
RThe Glass Flats (line). The sand of a 6 m wide, 12 m long strip flashes to glass after 1.2 s: 90 and blind 1 s to the enemy in it, and every mirage in the strip becomes real for 3 s: a wall that stops feet and bolts.
