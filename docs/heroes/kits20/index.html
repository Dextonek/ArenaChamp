Twenty new kits

Gate 3 on paper for all twenty new heroes (cards 1-10, cards 11-20): role, HP, speed and attack from the role budget; every spell with its cooldown, range, size, damage and rule; every answer checked with the game's numbers (a hero walks 4 m/s, reacts in 0.25 s; a circle warns 0.25 + radius / 4 s; a bolt reaches 6 m no sooner than 0.5 s; the ultimate never lands at the cast). The checks are run by python pipeline/tools/kits20.py, which also writes each hero's sheet (pipeline/designs/kit-<theme>.md): engine needs, the screen that decides each rule, the CPU, and the effects part by part.

Written before the look. The rules put the look (gate 2) first; the user asked for the full design now, so every number here may move once a hero's look is agreed.

The build order

Heroes that need no new engine work first, then small, medium and large. One hero at a time goes through gates 2 to 5.
