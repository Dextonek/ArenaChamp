21 zones are not judged by this rule, each with its reason (sure hits, placements, quick melee swings, lingering fields). Two readings of d. By the radius (a victim in the middle): 18 zones fail. By the walk (for a zone round the caster, the radius minus his attack reach, where the victim already stands in a fight): 0 fail. Bolts too fast (6 m sooner than 0.5 s): 0.
