# League of Legends kit reworks and failures: evidence for what "good" means

Compiled 2026-11-04. All text is paraphrase unless in quotation marks.

Source labels used on every citation:
- **[Riot]**: Riot-authored page, read directly.
- **[Riot via wiki]**: Riot-authored post, read as reproduced on the League wiki Development page. Original not fetched.
- **[Riot via outlet]**: a named Riot person's statement as reported by an outlet or tracker.
- **[Community]**: outlet or player opinion, not Riot.
- **[Excerpt]**: seen only in a search excerpt. The page was not opened or could not be opened, so treat it as lower confidence.

## 1. What Riot said was wrong in each kit, what changed, and whether the rework worked

### Takeaway
Riot's stated reasons for older reworks (Sion, Urgot, Swain, Aatrox, Poppy, Irelia) are mostly that the kit did not match the character and offered few real decisions. Later ones (Akali, Yuumi, Skarner, Irelia in 2021) are mostly about missing counterplay and a gap between experts and everyone else. Riot-authored verdicts on whether a rework worked exist for only about half of these cases.

### Cited Findings

#### Sion
- Riot's stated problem: his identity was unclear, a caster with an axe who also partly tanked. Some abilities scaled with attack damage and others with ability power, so any build wasted part of the kit and left two abilities redundant. He had few interactions, one fixed play pattern, no skillshots and few chances to show mastery. — [Riot via wiki: Dev Blog: Reimagining Sion, Conor Sheehy and others, date not shown, text refers to 2014 standards](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Sion/Development)
- What changed: rebuilt as a slow, tough undead war machine with heavy attacks opponents can see coming, plus built-in trade-offs such as limited steering on his charge. The comedic tone was dropped so story and art supported one idea. — [same post](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Sion/Development)
- Did it work: no Riot-authored verdict found. The nearest signal is that Riot named Sion and Urgot as the comparison for how far the Aurelion Sol rebuild would go. — [Excerpt: NME on the April 2022 roadmap](https://www.nme.com/news/league-of-legends-roadmap-teases-entirely-new-kit-for-aurelion-sol-3207544)

#### Urgot
- Riot's stated problem: kit, visuals, story and theme were tangled together. He was a ranged character with a knife hand, a marksman who also tanked, and a champion who swapped himself into the enemy team. Riot staff themselves struggled to make sense of him. — [Riot via wiki: State of Champion Update, Reav3, January 2017](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Urgot/Development)
- What changed: Riot kept the oddness but sharpened it into a ranged juggernaut. — [Riot via wiki: Champion Roadmap, Reav3, June 2017](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Urgot/Development). Six legs on separate cooldowns came from wanting his visible legs to matter in play, per David Capurro. — [Riot via wiki: quotes in a contributor-written Champ Insights piece, August 2017](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Urgot/Development)
- Did it work: no Riot-authored verdict found. Capurro only reports surprise at how much players liked the knee shotguns, which is reception of one feature. — [same piece](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Urgot/Development)

#### Poppy
- Riot's stated problem: the old ultimate made her immune to everything except her chosen target. It was very strong yet unappealing, defined the whole kit, pushed her toward bursting down backline targets against her theme, and forced the rest of the kit to stay weak. Riot considered a nerf but judged that a nerf would cost the ability's appeal anyway, so removed it. — [Riot via wiki: Champion Insights: Poppy, Conor Sheehy with SolCrushed, date not shown](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Poppy/Development)
- What changed: a knockback ultimate replaced it, and her niche became tanky disruption with a small body and disproportionate strength. — [same post](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Poppy/Development)
- Did it work: no Riot-authored verdict found.

#### Swain
- Riot's stated problem: he played like a drain tank while billed as a master tactician. He had little impact when behind, became nearly unkillable and high-damage when ahead, and much of his power sat in abilities enemies could barely answer. — [Riot via wiki: Teaser, Reav3, July 2017 roadmap](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Swain/Development). In play he simply walked at enemies until he died or ran out of mana. — [Riot via wiki: Champion Insights: Swain, January 2018 per URL](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Swain/Development)
- What changed: kit rebuilt from scratch around a ruthless ruler who fights on the front line, chosen over the tactician idea as more satisfying to play. The transformation moved to his ultimate and now keeps his other spells, because the earlier demon form silenced him and undercut the idea that he controls the demon. Early versions were either underwhelming or high power with little counterplay. Ravens were cut, then restored when he stopped feeling like himself. — [same posts](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Swain/Development)
- Did it work: no Riot-authored verdict found.

#### Irelia
- Riot's stated problem: apart from the ultimate, few abilities matched her blade-dancer fantasy. Her reliable kit produced predictable stat checks and leaned too hard on snowballing. — [Riot via wiki: Champion Roadmap, Reav3, October 2017](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Irelia/Development)
- What changed: Bladesurge was kept, with more ways to reset it, and the rest was overhauled around surging in and cutting down targets, with long poke for a fighter and strength against ranged champions. — [Riot via wiki: Champion Roadmap, Reav3, January 2018](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Irelia/Development)
- Did it work: partly, and it moved the problem. Three years later Riot said her power was concentrated in high-level play, where she crushes early and stays ahead, while most players had a poor win rate because the lane lead was hard to reproduce. Riot then lowered early power, capped the passive at 4 stacks, made those stacks visible to enemies, and fixed E's blade timing at 0.25 seconds. — [Riot: Quick Gameplay Thoughts: June 25, Riot Phlox, June 25, 2021](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-gb/news/dev/quick-gameplay-thoughts-june-25/)

#### Aatrox
- Riot's stated problem: gameplay did not live up to his art and theme, and he kept topping requests for an update. — [Riot via wiki: Champion Roadmap, Reav3, June 2017](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Aatrox/Development). Riot later said he needed rethinking from the ground up. — [Riot via wiki: Champion Roadmap, January 2018](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Aatrox/Development)
- What changed: planned as Riot's largest pure gameplay overhaul, with every ability re-evaluated, ending as a heavy greatsword warlord. The new feel no longer matched his model, so the model was updated too. — [Riot via wiki: Champion Roadmap, April 2018](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Aatrox/Development)
- Did it work: Riot's own verdict is split. A dedicated group played him far more, but many long-time players were alienated and players grew wary of updates to their own favorites. — [Riot: Ask Riot: Lessons from Dev, Reav3, January 28, 2021](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/dev/ask-riot-lessons-from-dev/). Follow-up tuning continued into 2019: testing showed him "a bit overtuned" (Capurro), the revive became conditional on a takedown, and a second E charge returned after backlash. — [Riot via outlet: Upcomer, April 24, 2019](https://upcomer.com/aatrox-changes-league-of-legends-pbe)

#### Akali
- Riot's stated problem: untargetability in shroud under towers was meant to give her dive access and uniqueness, but cost too much in counterplay and frustration, and the team was internally divided over it for a long time. Riot said this class of champion needed more meaningful weaknesses, with cutting the number of mechanics as a secondary goal. — [Riot: Quick Gameplay Thoughts, Riot Meddler, January 25, 2019](https://archive.runeterra.net/na/developer-corner/RgwqT7KP). His caveat: "We don't think that alone will solve everything with Akali." — [Riot via outlet: ComicBook](https://comicbook.com/gaming/news/league-of-legends-removing-part-of-akalis-shroud)
- What changed: the 2018 rework shipped in Patch 8.15. — [Excerpt](https://www.sportskeeda.com/esports/league-of-legends-new-akali-changes-in-10-3-is-set-to-gut-her-completely). Patch 8.24b then added an outline when entering shroud and exposed her to targeted abilities during entry. — [Excerpt: Esports.net](https://www.esports.net/news/patch-8-24b-rundown)
- Did it work: not by Riot's account, through at least February 2020. In June 2019 she averaged about 40% win rate, and Capurro was testing reduced slipperiness with more power in spells. — [Riot via outlet: Dexerto, June 15, 2019](https://www.dexerto.com/league-of-legends/league-of-legends-lead-designer-reveals-future-update-plans-for-akali-719021). In February 2020 Riot cited her pro presence, too many options for entering or leaving fights, and being able to both split push and teamfight without committing to one style. — [Riot: Patch 10.3 notes, February 4, 2020](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/patch-10-3-notes/)

#### Mordekaiser
- Riot's stated problem: the old ultimate created a controllable clone of a slain enemy. The engine was not built to capture and replay a champion, so it caused widespread bugs (a cloned Jhin could get stuck firing his empowered shot), and every future champion would have needed clone support. A summoned copy also did not fit a mace-wielding knight who fights directly. — [Riot: Welcome to the Death Dimension, Joshua Parker, March 31, 2020](https://www.riotgames.com/en/news/welcome-death-dimension). He had also been hard to balance for years as a shielded, manaless, slow burst mage with little crowd control. — [Riot via wiki: Gameplay Update: Juggernauts, Colt Hallam, date not shown](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Mordekaiser/Development)
- What changed: every ability updated or replaced, keeping the magic juggernaut identity and making him a solo laner. — [Riot via wiki: Champions in Season 2019, Reav3](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Mordekaiser/Development). The new ultimate isolates one enemy in a separate dimension for a stat-stealing duel, which needed a new engine concept after an early prototype caused server hitches. — [Riot: Parker, 2020](https://www.riotgames.com/en/news/welcome-death-dimension)
- Did it work: Riot presents the dimension system as a scalable technical base. No Riot gameplay verdict found.

#### Aurelion Sol
- Riot's stated problem: strong art and story, weak gameplay. Players tried him and dropped him, and play rate fell to among the lowest. Riot doubted "patching up his kit with small changes" would deliver the promised fantasy. — [Riot via wiki: Champion Roadmap: April 2022, Reav3](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Aurelion_Sol/Development)
- What changed: gameplay rebuilt from scratch while keeping visuals and story, adding fire breath and flight, and abilities that grow over the game. — [Riot via wiki: LoL Pls posts, Reav3 and Alexia Gao, dates not shown](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Aurelion_Sol/Development)
- Did it work: no Riot verdict found. It needed nerfs two days after release. — [Excerpt: Escorenews, February 2023](https://escorenews.com/en/lol/news/43816-riot-games-nerfs-reworked-aurelion-sol-in-league-of-legends-two-days-after-release-to-prevent-him-from-turning-to-terror). A year later Riot's Yelough announced buffs to fit the new item system and push him toward proactive play. The phrase "failed rework" is Dexerto's headline, not Riot's. — [Riot via outlet, with Community framing: Dexerto, January 28, 2024](https://dexerto.com/league-of-legends/lol-devs-finally-buffing-aurelion-sol-after-failed-rework-2502559)

#### Udyr
- Riot's stated problem: kit and looks had fallen behind the game, stances felt unsatisfying to use, and his Q and R were mainly damage spells. — [Riot: Udyr VGU: Rebuilding the Spirit Walker, August 23, 2022](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-gb/news/dev/udyr-vgu-rebuilding-the-spirit-walker/)
- What changed: stance-changing melee play stayed. New: each stance can be awakened for a stronger effect, a passive periodically awakens the active stance, and R traded damage for a slow so Q and R pair up. — [same post](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-gb/news/dev/udyr-vgu-rebuilding-the-spirit-walker/)
- Did it work: interest yes, strength not at first. On the Korean server pick rate went from about 1% to 14% with an early win rate near 43%, reaching about 48% in the jungle after an August 26 hotfix meant to give options beyond maxing R. — [Community with Riot hotfix reported: Inven Global, September 2, 2022](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/17808/the-long-awaited-udyr-rework-is-finally-here-but-was-it-a-success). One outlet called it a model rework. — [Community, Excerpt: Yahoo](https://au.news.yahoo.com/league-of-legends-udyr-rework-perfect-example-094313057.html)

#### Skarner
- Riot's stated problem: among the lowest ratings for thematic enjoyment and visual appeal and the lowest popularity in surveys. Players remembered his ultimate and little else, and the crystal and scorpion themes clashed. — [Riot: Searching for Skarner's Sting, Skarner VGU team, March 17, 2023](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-gb/news/dev/dev-searching-for-skarner-s-sting/)
- What changed: rejected directions are the useful part. Venom was hard to show on a health bar and left victims of lethal damage over time with few responses. A cycling hextech kit felt flashy and disjointed. Summoned babies felt unsatisfying. Shell-swapping overlapped other champions. The chosen direction was a giant protector with Impale widened into an area teamfight tool. — [same post](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-gb/news/dev/dev-searching-for-skarner-s-sting/)
- Did it work: by Riot's own account, no. Phreak said he failed and wished he had caught the issues before release. Skarner was very strong in pro play, poor in normal games, pick rate fell back to pre-rework levels, and Phreak called him "elite-skewed". The plan included removing the Flash plus E maneuver. — [Riot via outlet: Dexerto, July 12, 2024](https://dexerto.com/league-of-legends/lol-devs-admit-they-failed-with-skarner-rework-vow-to-fix-him-2821206)

#### Yuumi
- Riot's stated problem: indefinite attached untargetability combined with enchanter defenses, hyperscaling and engage left opponents little counterplay. Her power did not depend on her own success because she could switch partners. Pros optimized this while most Yuumi players did not want that style, so she skewed to high MMR, and she was a top pick at two straight Worlds. — [Riot: Yuumi's Rework, Riot Truexy, December 16, 2022](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-gb/news/dev/dev-yuumi-s-rework/)
- What changed: attaching stayed because Riot treats it as her identity. Other openings were added instead: a partner bond that is costly to rebuild, healing limited to combat, a less reliable Q, and the root removed from R. — [Riot: launch post, date and author not shown](https://leagueoflegends.com/en-pl/news/dev/dev-launching-yuumi-s-rework); [Riot: Patch 13.5 notes, March 7, 2023](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-gb/news/game-updates/patch-13-5-notes/)
- Did it work: Riot warned at launch it might not solve everything ([Patch 13.5 notes](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-gb/news/game-updates/patch-13-5-notes/)). Within months she drew heavy 13.12 nerfs. Phroxzon cited about 45% solo win rate and 80% pro presence at the prior Worlds, then 50% and pro presence trending toward 85%, and called about 47% and 2% presence a clear improvement. — [Riot via tracker: Phroxzon tweets, 2023](https://devtrackers.gg/leagueoflegends/p/a911f0b1-riot-phroxzon-tweets-on-the-state-of-yuumi-and-zeri-in-pro-play)

#### Thinner evidence (not counted among the 12)
- Volibear: kit did not land, was least played in the poll, and Riot wanted less feast or famine. Riot dropped a permanent-unstoppability concept during development. — [Riot via wiki: Reav3, May and October 2019](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Volibear/Development)
- Fiddlesticks: "not much about him has aged well" (Reav3). Crowstorm stayed as the anchor, with more counterplay and satisfaction wanted in the base kit, built around ambush and fear. — [Riot via wiki: Choose a Champion Update for 2020, about May 2019](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Fiddlesticks/Development)
- Galio: theme, gameplay and art aged poorly, and he was too dependent on magic resist and weak to attack-damage teams. — [Riot via wiki: Reav3 and Champion Update: Galio, 2017](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Galio/Development)
- Pantheon: framed as modernizing rather than fixing. Riot wanted to keep his aggressive reliability and add options. No specific old-kit defect appeared in the portion I read. — [Riot via wiki: A One Man Army, Reav3, date not shown](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Pantheon/Development)

### Inferences
- Riot's stated rework goals describe "good" as: one idea expressed by kit, art and story together; power the opponent can see coming; and a weakness the opponent can act on. This describes intent. Measured outcomes are much scarcer.
- Keeping one signature piece and rebuilding around it recurs (Irelia's Q, Fiddlesticks' ultimate, Yuumi's attach, Udyr's stances). When the kept piece is itself the source of friction, as with Yuumi, the problem tends to return.
- Skarner's rejected venom direction is a counterplay lesson from a kit that never shipped: Riot rejected it partly because a target already holding lethal damage has no meaningful decision left. This matters for a duel with short rounds.

### Gaps
- No Riot-authored outcome statement found for Sion, Urgot, Poppy, Swain, Mordekaiser, Volibear or Fiddlesticks. I did not use "best rework" listicles to fill this.
- Several wiki-reproduced posts show no date. Wiki pages were also cut off at 100,000 characters when fetched, so later page sections were unread.
- An outlet claimed Riot said old Aatrox felt too much like Riven ([Excerpt: Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/esports/league-legends-developer-reav3-opens-future-champion-vgu-updates)). The page would not open, and the 2021 Ask Riot post I read does not say it, so I do not treat it as Riot's reason.
- A pre-rework Aurelion Sol nerf that Riot reportedly defended despite his low pick rate, said to stem from a few one-trick players, would be a strong expert-skew example. The Dexerto page returned 404, so I could not confirm the reasoning. — [Excerpt: Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/league-of-legends/riot-defends-shock-aurelion-sol-nerfs-after-league-community-backlash-1611147/)

## 2. Repeated failure patterns, tagged for a 1v1 duel

### Takeaway
Eleven of the fifteen patterns below carry over to a 1v1 round-based duel, and they are mostly about what the single opponent can see and do. The four that depend on 5v5 are about gold, items, allies and team coordination. The coordination-driven pro versus solo queue split recurs constantly in Riot's reasons, but it is in that second group, so it is a poor guide for a duel. The tags and one-line reasons are my judgment, and the evidence is cited.

### Cited Findings

#### Theme and gameplay do not match
- **Tag: applies to 1v1.** Why: whether a kit feels like its character has nothing to do with teammates or map.
- Evidence: Sion as a caster with an axe ([Riot via wiki](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Sion/Development)); Urgot's tangled concepts ([Riot via wiki](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Urgot/Development)); Swain as a tactician who played like a drain tank ([Riot via wiki](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Swain/Development)); Aatrox's gameplay under his art ([Riot via wiki](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Aatrox/Development)); Aurelion Sol's unmet fantasy ([Riot via wiki](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Aurelion_Sol/Development)); Skarner's clashing themes ([Riot](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-gb/news/dev/dev-searching-for-skarner-s-sting/)).
- Riot also says complexity should fit theme: a flashy ninja for players wanting challenge, a cuddly character for simpler play. — [Riot: Scruffy, October 30, 2020](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/dev/quick-gameplay-thoughts-october-30)

#### No counterplay window (untargetability, immunity, unanswerable power)
- **Tag: applies to 1v1.** Why: with no team to peel or punish, an unanswerable tool decides the round against the only opponent.
- Evidence: Akali's shroud and the call for meaningful weaknesses ([Riot](https://archive.runeterra.net/na/developer-corner/RgwqT7KP)); Swain's barely answerable abilities ([Riot via wiki](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Swain/Development)); Skarner's rejected venom ([Riot](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-gb/news/dev/dev-searching-for-skarner-s-sting/)); Yuumi's attached untargetability ([Riot](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-gb/news/dev/dev-yuumi-s-rework/)); Poppy's near-total immunity ([Riot via wiki](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Poppy/Development)).
- Caution: Yuumi and Poppy support the principle but are not direct 1v1 precedents. Yuumi needs an ally to attach to, and old Poppy's ultimate left her exposed to her chosen target, which in a duel is the only enemy. Akali and Swain are the cleaner evidence.
- Counter-position from Riot: Phroxzon accepts moments with little counterplay, such as a far-ahead Rengar, and defends hard counters while seeking an upper limit before they become upsetting. — [Riot via outlet: PCGamesN, April 11, 2026](https://www.pcgamesn.com/league-of-legends/phroxzon-debate-mel-balance)

#### Frustration that win rate does not explain
- **Tag: applies to 1v1.** Why: a duel is nothing but the matchup, with no other lane or teammate to absorb it.
- Evidence: Mel's ban rate stayed at about 15 to 30% while win rate fell to 45 to 47% and play rate more than halved, and no balance pass could restore her power without frustration rising sharply. — [Riot: /dev: Mel Midscope, Kevin Huang, October 20, 2026](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/dev/dev-mel-midscope/)
- Unresolved Riot tension, presented as dated positions: in April 2026 Phroxzon called Mel balanced and described near-impossible matchups as one of the game's "secret sauces" ([PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/league-of-legends/phroxzon-debate-mel-balance)). In October 2026 Huang's post removed the mechanic behind exactly that matchup, on the grounds that opponents felt they could not win at any reasonable win rate ([Riot](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/dev/dev-mel-midscope/)).

#### Unclear kit or hidden state the opponent must memorize
- **Tag: applies to 1v1.** Why: the only opponent must read you in real time, and no teammate can call it out or cover for a misread.
- Evidence: a later Ryze update said he was confusing for anyone who had not memorized his stacks, with his power windows described as "two-way mirrors" only the expert Ryze could read ([Riot via wiki: Champion Update: Ryze, the Rune Mage, date not shown](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Ryze/Development)); Irelia's stacks were made visible to enemies ([Riot, 2021](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-gb/news/dev/quick-gameplay-thoughts-june-25/)); Zeri's stacks and on-hits were made more predictable ([Riot: Patch 12.7 notes, April 12, 2022](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/patch-12-7-notes/)); a tested Mel version that stole the first projectile demanded knowing every projectile in the game ([Riot](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/dev/dev-mel-midscope/)).
- Riot's stated rule: playing as a complex champion is opt-in, but facing one should not require extensive study. — [Riot: Scruffy, October 30, 2020](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/dev/quick-gameplay-thoughts-october-30)

#### Stat-check with no decisions
- **Tag: applies to 1v1.** Why: with only one fight to play, a kit with no choices leaves nothing but the numbers to decide it.
- Evidence: Irelia's predictable stat checks ([Riot via wiki](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Irelia/Development)); Sion's fixed pattern and lack of skillshots ([Riot via wiki](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Sion/Development)); Ryze's predictable damage with windows almost always open ([Riot via wiki: Champion Update: Ryze heads to PBE, about 2015](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Ryze/Development)); Udyr's damage-only Q and R ([Riot](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-gb/news/dev/udyr-vgu-rebuilding-the-spirit-walker/)).
- Counterpoint that does not transfer: Phreak wants each lane to keep some low-agency or weakside options, a role that exists only inside a team. — [Riot via tracker: Phreak, 2023](https://devtrackers.gg/leagueoflegends/p/0e34c7f1-why-it-s-hard-to-balance-for-pro-and-preview-of-worlds-meta-phreak)

#### Too many tools in one kit (overload)
- **Tag: applies to 1v1.** Why: with no team to cover weaknesses, each extra tool removes a weakness the opponent could have used.
- Evidence: Akali's too many options, and being able to do everything without committing ([Riot: Patch 10.3 notes, February 4, 2020](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/patch-10-3-notes/)); K'Sante too safe, reliable and powerful in nearly any situation ([Riot: Patch 14.19 notes, September 24, 2024](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/patch-14-19-notes/)); Yuumi's stacked untargetability, scaling, defense and engage ([Riot](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-gb/news/dev/dev-yuumi-s-rework/)); fewer mechanics as a secondary goal ([Riot: Meddler, 2019](https://archive.runeterra.net/na/developer-corner/RgwqT7KP)).
- Language note: in what I read, Riot describes this as options, safety or missing weaknesses. "Overload" comes from community writing. See section 4.

#### One ability defines and constrains the kit
- **Tag: applies to 1v1.** Why: in short rounds, play collapses onto that one cooldown, and its power budget starves everything else.
- Evidence: Poppy's old ultimate limited the rest of her kit ([Riot via wiki](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Poppy/Development)); players knew Skarner almost only through Impale ([Riot](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-gb/news/dev/dev-searching-for-skarner-s-sting/)); removing Mel's reflect freed power for the rest of her kit ([Riot](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/dev/dev-mel-midscope/)).

#### Binary interaction or all-or-nothing matchup
- **Tag: applies to 1v1.** Why: one interaction can shut down the opponent's whole kit, and a duel gives them nowhere else to contribute.
- Evidence: Phroxzon concedes Mel's W can seem to shut down all of Seraphine's kit ([Riot via outlet: PCGamesN, April 2026](https://www.pcgamesn.com/league-of-legends/phroxzon-debate-mel-balance)); the new W can no longer redirect a Seraphine ultimate or Jhin's fourth shot ([Riot](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/dev/dev-mel-midscope/)). Ryze was all-or-nothing in another sense, near-constant pick or ban or never seen, depending on small number changes ([Riot via wiki, about 2015](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Ryze/Development)).
- Evidence for this pattern is thinner than the others. Mel is the only well-documented ability-level case I found.

#### Expert execution gap (a few players break it, others cannot play it)
- **Tag: applies to 1v1.** Why: mechanical and knowledge gaps show directly between two players, with nothing to offset them.
- Evidence: Ryze too hard to learn and too strong once mastered ([Riot via wiki](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Ryze/Development)); Azir, where most players, even in Challenger, struggle ([Riot: Patch 13.5 notes](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-gb/news/game-updates/patch-13-5-notes/)); Irelia's E, nearly guaranteed for experienced low-ping players and hard for others, fixed even though it removed a unique combo ([Riot, 2021](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-gb/news/dev/quick-gameplay-thoughts-june-25/)); Skarner ([Riot via outlet](https://dexerto.com/league-of-legends/lol-devs-admit-they-failed-with-skarner-rework-vow-to-fix-him-2821206)); Mel, as elite players learned her kit ([Riot](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/dev/dev-mel-midscope/)).
- Riot accepts part of this deliberately: Phreak says some champions are built to be aspirational and will fail often at lower ranks, on a sliding scale. — [Riot via tracker: Phreak, 2023](https://devtrackers.gg/leagueoflegends/p/0e34c7f1-why-it-s-hard-to-balance-for-pro-and-preview-of-worlds-meta-phreak)

#### Rework erases what the kit's players valued
- **Tag: applies to 1v1.** Why: loyal players of a kit exist in any game with a fixed roster, and this is not a team effect.
- Note: this is a failure of the rework process, unlike the kit-design traits in the rest of this list. It matters once a kit is live and has players attached to it.
- Evidence: Aatrox ([Riot, 2021](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/dev/ask-riot-lessons-from-dev/)); Riot's rule that reworks keep roughly the same complexity for current players ([Riot, 2020](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/dev/quick-gameplay-thoughts-october-30)); a shorter-range Mel reflect rejected as too different ([Riot](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/dev/dev-mel-midscope/)); Volibear art pulled back after mains objected ([Riot via wiki](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Volibear/Development)).

#### Mechanics that copy or swap another kit's state
- **Tag: applies to 1v1.** Why: these bugs come from the engine and per-kit support, not team size, so even a small roster pays for them.
- Evidence: Mordekaiser's clone ([Riot, 2020](https://www.riotgames.com/en/news/welcome-death-dimension)). Viego's form swapping affected how his data entered Riot's pipelines, in Phroxzon's words "some of the form swapping that goes on". — [Riot via outlet: Insider Gaming, April 9, 2026](https://insider-gaming.com/viego-buffs-pulled-as-riot-admits-lol-patch-26-8-mistake/)

#### Balanced for coordinated pro play, weak elsewhere
- **Tag: depends on 5v5.** Why: the gap comes from team coordination, drafts, wave control and map play, none of which exist in a duel.
- Evidence: Ryze's focus areas were wave clear, farming and the coordination his ultimate needs ([Riot via outlet: Meddler, August 22, 2017, Surrender@20](https://www.surrenderat20.net/2017/08/red-post-collection-quick-gameplay_22.html)); Azir's "pro-specific strengths" in guaranteed wave control ([Riot: Patch 25.20 notes, October 7, 2025](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-gb/news/game-updates/patch-25-20-notes/)); Akali split pushing ([Riot](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/patch-10-3-notes/)); K'Sante as a pro staple at the cost of most players' experience ([Riot](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/patch-14-19-notes/)).
- Riot's framework separates audiences: pro uses pick rate rather than win rate, with automatic nerfs at 95% presence in one patch or 85% over two, and it would not buff a champion like Azir just to clear a solo queue bar if that created a pro problem. — [Riot via tracker: Balance Framework Update, 2020](https://devtrackers.gg/leagueoflegends/p/e1c0d421-dev-balance-framework-update)

#### Gold and lane snowball (feast or famine)
- **Tag: depends on 5v5.** Why: items, gold and lane leads do not exist here, and round resets remove the compounding unless the game adds carry-over.
- Evidence: Swain useless behind and unkillable ahead ([Riot via wiki](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Swain/Development)); Volibear's feast or famine ([Riot via wiki](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Volibear/Development)); Irelia's snowball reliance ([Riot via wiki](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Irelia/Development)); losing lane to Yuumi feeling like losing the game ([Riot](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-gb/news/dev/dev-yuumi-s-rework/)).

#### Item-driven power spikes
- **Tag: depends on 5v5.** Why: no items in the duel. This sits in League's gold and build layer, which belongs to the team game's economy.
- Evidence: Zeri with Trinity Force and Yuumi with Helia made overtuned by item changes ([Riot via tracker: Phroxzon, 2023](https://devtrackers.gg/leagueoflegends/p/a911f0b1-riot-phroxzon-tweets-on-the-state-of-yuumi-and-zeri-in-pro-play)); Aurelion Sol buffs to fit the new item system ([Riot via outlet: Dexerto, January 2024](https://dexerto.com/league-of-legends/lol-devs-finally-buffing-aurelion-sol-after-failed-rework-2502559)); Galio's dependence on magic resist items ([Riot via wiki](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Galio/Development)).

#### Power that comes from allies
- **Tag: depends on 5v5.** Why: a duel has no allies.
- Evidence: Yuumi's power did not depend on her own success, and she could leave a lost lane for a stronger partner. — [Riot](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-gb/news/dev/dev-yuumi-s-rework/)

### Inferences
- Priority order for a duel, in my reading: no counterplay window, hidden state, too many tools, and one defining ability. These appear most often in Riot's own words and lose nothing when the team is removed. They probably get worse, since League's team and long match length give hard counters and low-agency kits a reason to exist.
- Phroxzon's defense of hard counters rests on players learning for the next encounter and on other roles carrying the game. In a duel of short rounds, a hard counter may fill the whole match, so the League tolerance level should not be copied.
- Cover may raise the stakes on readability. If line of sight breaks during play, any hidden timer or stack gets harder to track. This is my reasoning, not something Riot has addressed.
- A round-to-round carry-over system would reintroduce the snowball pattern even without items.

### Gaps
- No pattern counts are from Riot. Riot publishes no taxonomy of kit failures that I could find.
- No source tests any of these patterns in a 1v1 format, so every tag is an extrapolation.

## 3. Reworks that failed, were reverted, or were reworked again, and what Riot said it learned

### Takeaway
I found no full kit revert. Riot's usual first response is number changes, with mid-scope changes later, and removing the mechanic outright is rare (Mel, and earlier Poppy). Riot openly admitted failure for Skarner and Mel, and a mistaken goal for Aatrox. The clearest lesson across cases is that tuning cannot fix a mechanic that is itself the problem, and Riot now states this outright about Mel.

### Cited Findings

#### Case by case
- **Aatrox.** Riot said his update led it to swap goals for champion updates, making player trust primary and engagement secondary, even if that makes an update less exciting for the wider playerbase. Polls and repeated dev blogs followed. — [Riot: Reav3, January 28, 2021](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/dev/ask-riot-lessons-from-dev/). A community writer reads Aatrox the opposite way, as proof that discarding an unhealthy kit works. — [Community: Carver Fisher, Dexerto, September 13, 2024](https://dexerto.com/league-of-legends/ksante-is-lols-greatest-failure-patch-14-19-proves-it-2897572)
- **Skarner.** Phreak personally owned the misses, naming the removed slow on W and the unsolved backward E. — [Riot via outlet: Dexerto, July 12, 2024](https://dexerto.com/league-of-legends/lol-devs-admit-they-failed-with-skarner-rework-vow-to-fix-him-2821206)
- **Ryze.** Reworked repeatedly and returning to the same gap. The about-2015 rework added windows of power but left him all-or-nothing in competitive play ([Riot via wiki](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Ryze/Development)). The next one said the previous rework had not fixed reliable, hard-to-answer damage and rooting, and aimed for a similar skill ceiling with a higher floor ([Riot via wiki, date not shown](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Ryze/Development)). In August 2017 Meddler admitted earlier reworks had not addressed his pro strength and that chasing other goals created new problems, and chose a heavy balance pass over another rework ([Riot via outlet: Surrender@20](https://www.surrenderat20.net/2017/08/red-post-collection-quick-gameplay_22.html)). In 2020 he was receiving small buffs because of low pro presence ([Riot via tracker](https://devtrackers.gg/leagueoflegends/p/e1c0d421-dev-balance-framework-update)). In 2021, on about 45 to 46% win rates, Phlox called him "definitely a candidate for some kind of rework" ([Riot via outlet: Dexerto, May 12, 2021](https://www.dexerto.com/league-of-legends/another-ryze-rework-lol-update-1571932)).
- **Azir.** The March 2023 mini-rework was prompted by most players struggling even in Challenger. It moved power out of early poke and added help for struggling players ([Riot: Patch 13.5 notes](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-gb/news/game-updates/patch-13-5-notes/)). Phreak described him as a top-three pro mid with about 45% solo queue win rate ([Excerpt, Riot via outlet: Dot Esports](https://dotesports.com/league-of-legends/news/riot-dev-phreak-breaks-down-azir-changes-coming-to-lol-patch-13-5)). In October 2025 Riot again targeted strengths specific to pro play ([Riot: Patch 25.20 notes](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-gb/news/game-updates/patch-25-20-notes/)). Phreak's stated position is that a steady pro presence near 30% for Azir and K'Sante is acceptable ([Riot via tracker, 2023](https://devtrackers.gg/leagueoflegends/p/0e34c7f1-why-it-s-hard-to-balance-for-pro-and-preview-of-worlds-meta-phreak)).
- **Zeri.** Eight nerfs in her first season, with a rework discussed as possible by Riot August, reported secondhand. — [Riot via outlet: EarlyGame, August 16, 2022](https://earlygame.com/de/lol/zeri-gameplay-update). Patch 12.7 cited continued pro strength ([Riot, April 12, 2022](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/patch-12-7-notes/)). A mid-patch update two days later partly reversed it with no stated reason in the notes ([same notes, April 14, 2022](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/patch-12-7-notes/)). Outlets say Riot called this an overshoot, but I could not open them ([Excerpt: AFK Gaming](https://afkgaming.com/esports/news/riot-games-ships-hotfix-for-zeri-after-brutal-nerfs-in-lol-patch-127)). Patch 12.11 moved her toward more damage and less utility and mobility ([Riot, June 2022](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/patch-12-11-notes/)).
- **K'Sante.** See section 4. Patch 14.19 was a large structural change aimed at his being too safe. — [Riot, September 24, 2024](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/patch-14-19-notes/). A micropatch followed within days, including an armor buff. — [Excerpt: Esports.net](https://www.esports.net/news/lol/lol-patch-14-19-micropatch-fix)
- **Akali.** Iterative trimming rather than a revert, from 2019 through 2020 ([Riot: Meddler, 2019](https://archive.runeterra.net/na/developer-corner/RgwqT7KP); [Riot: Patch 10.3](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/patch-10-3-notes/)), with further changes reported in Patch 11.6 ([Excerpt: GameRiv](https://gameriv.com/akali-to-receive-some-major-changes-in-league-of-legends-patch-11-6/)). The only actual revert I found for Akali concerned voice lines, not the kit. — [Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/league-of-legends/riot-reverts-akali-voiceover-changes-lol-revolt-1877215/)
- **Yuumi.** Riot says it will keep balancing her out of pro and elite play and is open to more drastic changes if needed ([Riot via tracker: Phroxzon, 2023](https://devtrackers.gg/leagueoflegends/p/a911f0b1-riot-phroxzon-tweets-on-the-state-of-yuumi-and-zeri-in-pro-play)). I found no second rework. A changelog shows buffs in Patch 16.8 ([Excerpt: LeagueOfGraphs](https://www.leagueofgraphs.com/overwolf/champions/yuumi)).
- **Mel.** The clearest case. Riot called reflect a bet on a new mechanic that did not work out, and concluded it could not be tuned into a healthy form while on her W. Tested and rejected: a shorter-range reflect, stealing the first projectile, and moving reflect to the ultimate. — [Riot: Huang, October 20, 2026](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/dev/dev-mel-midscope/). A small February 2026 rework had not resolved the frustration. — [Riot via outlet: Insider Gaming, October 20, 2026](https://insider-gaming.com/league-of-legends-mel-midscope-rework-removes-her-reflect/). The change shipped in Patch 26.22. — [Riot, November 3, 2026](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/league-of-legends-patch-26-22-notes/)
- **Mordekaiser.** Riot tried a duo-lane juggernaut experiment before the 2019 update returned him to solo lane, judging that the fantasy fit a lone fighter. — [Riot via wiki: Hallam and Reav3](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Mordekaiser/Development)
- **Aurelion Sol.** No Riot admission found. See section 1.

#### What Riot says it learned
- Trust first: changing a kit beyond recognition costs more than it gains. — [Riot: Reav3, 2021](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/dev/ask-riot-lessons-from-dev/)
- Add weaknesses, not just subtract power. — [Riot: Meddler, 2019](https://archive.runeterra.net/na/developer-corner/RgwqT7KP)
- New mechanics are risky because safe ones tend to be dull, so some bets must be expected to fail. — [Riot: Huang, 2026](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/dev/dev-mel-midscope/)
- Early and late power trade off directly, ping-dependent timing can act as a skill gate, and a simpler passive may lose little mastery. — [Riot: Phlox, 2021](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-gb/news/dev/quick-gameplay-thoughts-june-25/)

### Inferences
- Cases where the disputed mechanic stayed (Yuumi's attach, Azir's soldiers, Ryze's core combo) keep returning to the balance queue. The cases that removed it (Poppy, Mel) have no verdict or are too recent to judge, so "remove it" is promising but unproven.
- Reworks often move the failure rather than end it. Irelia traded a stat-check problem for an expert-gap problem, and Aatrox traded low usage for lost trust.
- Riot's public willingness to admit failure seems to be growing (2021 Aatrox, 2024 Skarner, 2026 Mel), though three cases is a small sample.

### Gaps
- Direct Riot text for the Azir mini-rework's top-three pro framing and the Zeri overshoot could not be opened. Both remain excerpts.
- A later Meddler remark calling further Ryze changes a final attempt surfaced in a search summary, but I could not tie it to a specific page, so it is excluded.
- A 2013 to 2014 Skarner change that was rolled back appeared in older red-post listings ([Excerpt: Surrender@20](https://www.surrenderat20.net/2013/12/red-post-collection-skarner-disabled-on.html)). I did not verify it, and it may be the only true kit rollback.
- The web archive could not be reached with my tools, so no archived originals were used.

## 4. Newer kits criticised for overload or power creep: Riot's response versus community criticism

### Takeaway
Riot's response is nearly always numbers first, then moving power within the kit, and rarely a statement about kit size. I found no Riot-authored post calling Yone, Viego, Briar, Zeri or K'Sante overloaded. Riot's closest language describes K'Sante as too safe and Akali as having too many options. The overload framing in what I read comes from the community.

### Cited Findings

#### K'Sante
- **Riot said:** he became a pro staple since release at the cost of playability for most players. He was too safe, reliable and powerful in nearly any situation regardless of circumstances or opponent skill. Riot wants a difficult champion whose success reflects the player. Patch 14.19 cut range, thinned and sped up Q, slowed E's base dash, removed wall crossing in the ultimate form, and removed the option to cancel the ultimate early. — [Riot: Patch 14.19 notes, September 24, 2024](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/patch-14-19-notes/). Phroxzon said earlier that K'Sante was taking much of the team's attention through overperformance. — [Excerpt, Riot via outlet: Dexerto mirror on Patch 14.4](https://jamtest.dexerto.com/league-of-legends/ksante-set-for-massive-nerfs-on-lol-patch-14-4-that-gut-his-abilities-2526672)
- **Community said:** a design failure that Riot keeps patching in numbers, with pros exploiting a mechanical ceiling average players cannot reach. The writer's own tally is 142 adjustments, about 46% global win rate, and never above 50% below Diamond+. — [Community: Carver Fisher, Dexerto opinion, September 13, 2024](https://dexerto.com/league-of-legends/ksante-is-lols-greatest-failure-patch-14-19-proves-it-2897572). Unusual mechanics widen the skill gap, and numbers cannot fix a core mechanic. — [Community: Florian Frick, EarlyGame, October 23, 2026](https://earlygame.com/news/lol/modern-lol-champions-need-reworks-more-often-does-riot-have-a-design-problem)

#### Zeri
- **Riot said:** sustained pro strength drove repeated nerfs, plus a wish to make her stacks predictable. — [Riot: Patch 12.7 notes](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/patch-12-7-notes/). In 2023, Phroxzon said she was too strong and tanky after her Trinity Force spike, remained optimistic about her mechanics, and preferred a sub-50% win rate long term. — [Riot via tracker: Phroxzon, 2023](https://devtrackers.gg/leagueoflegends/p/a911f0b1-riot-phroxzon-tweets-on-the-state-of-yuumi-and-zeri-in-pro-play)
- **Community said:** she belongs with Ryze, Aphelios and Azir as champions Riot struggles to balance. — [Community: EarlyGame, August 16, 2022](https://earlygame.com/de/lol/zeri-gameplay-update). She and K'Sante are the clearest design problems of recent classes. — [Community: Frick, EarlyGame, 2026](https://earlygame.com/news/lol/modern-lol-champions-need-reworks-more-often-does-riot-have-a-design-problem)

#### Yone
- **Riot said:** Scruffy said Riot was "happy so far" with his balance after release, with more changes possible. — [Excerpt: Dot Esports, August 2020](https://dotesports.com/?p=293226); [Riot via outlet: GameRiv, August 22, 2020](https://gameriv.com/riot-thinks-in-terms-of-balance-lillia-release-was-great-and-yone-release-was-good/). He was nerfed in his first patch after arrival, including his ultimate. — [Excerpt: GGRecon](https://www.ggrecon.com/articles/league-of-legends-nerfs-yone-in-first-patch-since-his-arrival). Riot itself rated him high mechanical, medium knowledge and medium with-or-against complexity. — [Riot: Scruffy, October 30, 2020](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/dev/quick-gameplay-thoughts-october-30). A 2024 Q buff was described as compensation for crit item changes. — [Excerpt: Esports.gg](https://esports.gg/news/league-of-legends/yone-best-mid-laner-at-worlds-2024-getting-more-lol-buffs)
- **Community said:** anger at buffs to a champion seen as not needing them. — [Community, Excerpt: MeinMMO](https://mein-mmo.de/en/lol-hated-champion-gets-buffs-and-players-are-angry-claims-they-dont-need-it,1185315). Calls to remove him outright, title-level only. — [Community, Excerpt: RiftFeed](https://riftfeed.gg/champions/lol-riot-pls-delete-yone)

#### Viego
- **Riot said:** the 2021 aim was to reduce the power of his resets, especially when behind or building tanky, and to make his attack pattern less versatile. Presence was reported at 77.5%. — [Riot via outlet: Dexerto, August 17, 2021](https://www.dexerto.com/league-of-legends/riot-finally-nerfing-viego-lol-update-1632996). In April 2026 planned buffs were pulled after data discrepancies tied to his form swapping. — [Riot via outlet: Insider Gaming, April 9, 2026](https://insider-gaming.com/viego-buffs-pulled-as-riot-admits-lol-patch-26-8-mistake/)
- **Community said:** I found no substantive community analysis of his kit that I could open.

#### Briar
- **Riot said:** after a weak launch and an emergency hotfix adding resilience ([Excerpt: AFK Gaming](https://afkgaming.com/esports/news/riot-games-rolls-out-emergency-hot-fix-for-briar-after-abysmal-win-rate)), August Browning put her at "up to 51% win rate in normal and high MMR", and nerfs targeted her jungle and healing. — [Riot via outlet: PCGamesN, September 21, 2023](https://www.pcgamesn.com/league-of-legends/briar-nerfs)
- **Community said:** a design failure, because a self-taunt stops her choosing targets, so she depends on enemy mistakes. Note this criticism is the opposite of overload, since it is about too little agency. — [Community: RiftFeed, September 16, 2023](https://riftfeed.gg/champions/briar-champion-design-failure)

#### Mel and Ambessa
- **Mel, Riot said:** see section 3. **Community said:** the main subreddit mostly approved and Mel mains were more critical, as reported by the author, who also calls Riot remarkably open. — [Community: Frick, EarlyGame, October 21, 2026](https://earlygame.com/news/lol/mel-is-getting-a-midscope-and-riot-is-finally-giving-up-on-her-reflect)
- **Ambessa:** planned nerfs were delayed to Patch 26.9, and I found no stated design rationale. — [Riot via outlet: Insider Gaming, April 9, 2026](https://insider-gaming.com/viego-buffs-pulled-as-riot-admits-lol-patch-26-8-mistake/)

#### Power creep and pacing
- Riot acknowledged power creep in October 2021 while saying a nerf was not imminent. — [Excerpt: RiftFeed](https://riftfeed.gg/lol-news/will-riot-fix-leagues-power-creep-problem)
- Frick argues modern experimentation is harder to balance in a roster near 200 champions, and that some failures are the cost of creativity rather than decline. — [Community: EarlyGame, October 23, 2026](https://earlygame.com/news/lol/modern-lol-champions-need-reworks-more-often-does-riot-have-a-design-problem)
- A contrary community view argues overloaded kits are good for the game. — [Community, Excerpt: Tech4Gamers](https://tech4gamers.com/why-i-think-overloaded-character-kits-are-good-for-league)

### Inferences
- Community criticism and Riot's wording often point at the same fact from different sides. Riot says too safe in any situation, and the community says overloaded. For a duel, "what situation is this kit bad in?" is the more useful test, since it can be checked per hero.
- A kit can draw overload complaints and too-little-agency complaints at the same time from different players, as the Briar critique shows. Complaint volume is a weak signal.

### Gaps
- I found no Riot-authored statement using the word overload about these champions. This may be a search limit rather than proof of absence.
- Some Dot Esports and Sportskeeda pages returned errors, so several items are excerpts.
- Riot's 2024 position on Yone and Yasuo appeared only in a summary with an unclear source, so it is excluded.

## 5. Low-complexity kits that are widely respected, and what that shows

### Takeaway
Riot wants simple kits to exist on purpose and treats complexity as a cost to be justified by depth, but it also says simple kits tend to be strong at low ranks and weak at high ranks. Evidence that specific simple kits are "widely respected" is thin and community-only. No poll exists that I could find, so the respect claim itself is weakly supported.

### Cited Findings

#### Riot's position
- Complexity should follow a bell curve, with low and high options in each class and position, rated on three axes: mechanical, knowledge, and playing with or against. Examples of low: Rammus and Warwick (mechanical); Miss Fortune, Sona and Lux (knowledge); Ahri and Jinx (with or against). — [Riot: Scruffy, October 30, 2020](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/dev/quick-gameplay-thoughts-october-30)
- The same post rated Volibear low, low, medium and Fiddlesticks low, medium, high, so a kit can be easy to execute yet hard to play against. — [same post](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/dev/quick-gameplay-thoughts-october-30)
- Complexity is a cost, so add as little as possible for as much depth as possible, and build on depth rather than on accessibility. — [Riot via outlet: Ryan Scott at GDC, GamesBeat, March 3, 2015](https://gamesbeat.com/league-of-legends-developer-on-fighting-with-fun-depth-and-complexity/)
- Phreak treats Garen and Jinx as intro-style champions that should not be locked out of pro play, as opposed to aspirational ones like Azir. — [Riot via tracker: Phreak, 2023](https://devtrackers.gg/leagueoflegends/p/0e34c7f1-why-it-s-hard-to-balance-for-pro-and-preview-of-worlds-meta-phreak)
- Simple champions are often much stronger at low ranks, so once balanced they can end up non-viable higher up. Riot still tries to keep them simple. — [Excerpt: Ask Riot, June 2018, the page now redirects](https://nexus.leagueoflegends.com/en-au/2018/06/ask-riot-skins-and-champions/)
- Naafiri was deliberately designed as a beginner-friendly assassin, per designer Glenn Anderson. — [Excerpt, Riot via outlet: Dot Esports](https://dotesports.com/league-of-legends/news/naafiri-is-meant-to-be-easiest-lol-assassin-champion-to-learn). Yet one writer lists her among kits changed often since release. — [Community: EarlyGame, October 23, 2026](https://earlygame.com/news/lol/modern-lol-champions-need-reworks-more-often-does-riot-have-a-design-problem)

#### Community evidence
- Ezreal was hailed in a Reddit thread as "the pinnacle of champion design" for being easy to pick up and very hard to master.
