# Notes: the two Fandom pages the user named

Read on 2026-11-04 in a browser (the Fandom server answers automated fetches with HTTP 402, so these two pages could not be
fetched any other way). Paraphrased; the pages themselves are not reproduced here.

## https://leagueoflegends.fandom.com/wiki/List_of_champions

- Lists the same 173 names as the official wiki's list (https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/List_of_champions).
- It is out of date: its intro counts 170 champions as of January 2025, with Mel the latest. Yunara, Locke and Zaahen have
  "TBA" for class and a placeholder date. Its "last changed" column stops around patch 14.18.
- The official wiki's list has 173 with Locke newest (released 2026-06-24). Its columns: champion, classes, release date,
  last changed, Blue Essence, RP.
- Per-champion research therefore uses the official wiki, which is current and allows fetching.

## https://leagueoflegends.fandom.com/wiki/Champion_ability

The page flags itself as outdated (last edited 2024-04-30). Its classification is still useful as a shared vocabulary, and the
per-champion tags follow it.

**The standard kit.** Nearly every kit is one innate with no ranks, three basic abilities with five ranks each, and an ultimate
with three ranks. The ultimate opens at level 6 and is described as the most characteristic and game-changing ability. Only
one rank of a basic can be taken every other level, so no basic is maxed before level 9.

**Exceptions to the standard shape.**
- Udyr has four stance basics and no true ultimate; Jayce has six ranks on each basic and a stance-swap in the R slot. Both
  count as "pseudo-ultimates" for effects that interact with ultimates.
- Elise, Nidalee, Jayce, Kayn, Gnar, Shyvana, Rek'Sai and Quinn are named as having stances that change their abilities.
- Kha'Zix and Viktor can evolve their abilities into new ones. Kai'Sa, Rengar and Syndra gain extra effects on theirs.
- Lee Sin's basics each have two parts.
- Eight ultimates have two differing casts in one use (Nocturne, Kayn, Ornn, Quinn, Riven, Tahm Kench, Twisted Fate, Urgot).
- A dozen ultimates are global in range.

**Components an ability can have.**
- Scaling: grows with stats from items and runes.
- Cost: mana, energy, health or another resource. Toggles drain continuously. Some champions have no resource at all.
- Cooldown: shortened by the haste stat.
- Ammo: "stock" charges that build up over time, or "barrage" charges granted on cast and lost when it ends.
- Range: from caster to target, centre-based or edge-based; some are global.
- Cast time: commonly 0.25, 0.5 or 1 second, during which the champion cannot act. It stops effects being instant.
- Channel: the champion must keep still or keep channelling for the effect, and can be interrupted. Charged abilities are a kind
  of channel. An interrupted channel usually keeps its cost and cooldown.
- Active, with optional recasts (at most three casts in total).
- Passive: no input needed.
- Toggle: on and off, sometimes swapping between effects.
- Stance: changes the champion or its abilities, with the swap gated to stop effects stacking.
- Summon: brings out other units.
- On-action effects: abilities interact with actions or with each other for synergy.

**Effects an ability can have.**
- Combat: damage, buffs, debuffs, with crowd control as a special debuff.
- Utility: blinks and dashes (mobility, moving round obstacles), healing, shields, stealth, invulnerability, untargetability.

**Rare effects the page counts.**
- Pure invulnerability is on only three abilities; stasis on four; conditional invulnerability on four more. So "can't be hit
  at all" is kept scarce across the whole roster.
- Only three innates allow activity after death.
- Only three innates are summons.
