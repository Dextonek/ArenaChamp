# WoW raid boss mechanics, era 3: Battle for Azeroth (2018-2020) and Shadowlands (2020-2022)

How to read these notes
- Same format and tag vocabulary as the era 1 and era 2 notes. Every boss line names its mechanics, what the raid must do and what failure costs. Tags in square brackets: [tank swap], [spread], [stack/soak], [adds], [interrupt], [dispel], [kite], [LoS] (line of sight), [mind control], [dance] (positional pattern), [hazard] (ground or room hazard), [gimmick] (vehicle or special-action fight), [council] (several bosses at once), [phase], [intermission], [enrage], [resource] (debuff or resource management), [split] (raid divided into groups or realms), [job] (a named player is assigned a task), [dps race], [room change] (the arena itself changes during the fight), [puzzle] (memory, pattern or logic task), [choice] (the raid chooses an order or a buff that changes the fight).
- Source rule: each boss line rests on the Warcraft Wiki (warcraft.wiki.gg) page linked at its start, read through the wiki's own API (page source). Those pages hold the in-game Adventure Guide (Encounter Journal) ability text, role tips, and for some bosses a community "Strategy" section. Where a page had no mechanics text, the line names the guide used instead (Method.gg guides, WorldofMoudi, or search summaries of Icy Veins pages, which refused direct fetches). Numbers are proportions, not absolutes (they are often rescaled for later patches). Anything marked "(unverified)" was not on a fetched page: treat it as a lead, not a fact.
- Raid list check: the wiki's Adventure Guide copies list exactly five Battle for Azeroth raids (Uldir 8 encounters, Battle of Dazar'alor 9 per faction, Crucible of Storms 2, The Eternal Palace 8, Ny'alotha 12) and three Shadowlands raids (Castle Nathria 10, Sanctum of Domination 10, Sepulcher of the First Ones 11): 70 encounters, matching the assignment's list; no raid is missing ([Warcraft Wiki, Adventure Guide Battle for Azeroth raids](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Adventure_Guide_Battle_for_Azeroth_raids); [Adventure Guide Shadowlands raids](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Adventure_Guide_Shadowlands_raids)). The world bosses those pages also list (BfA: Shek'zara, Vuk'laz, Wekemara, Ulmath, T'zane, Ji'arak, Hailstone Construct, Warbringer Yenajz, Azurethos, Dunegorger Kraulok and others; Shadowlands: Valinor, Mortanis, Oranomonos, Nurgash, Mor'geth, Antros) are outdoor encounters, not raid instances, and are not covered.
- Raid size: all eight raids use Raid Finder, Normal and Heroic with flexible size (10-30 players) and Mythic fixed at 20 (the system introduced in Warlords of Draenor; see era 2 notes). Patch and release months in the raid headers are from memory (unverified) except where a race report gives the date.

## 1. For each boss: what are its defining mechanics?

### Takeaway
The two expansions hold 8 raids and 70 encounters, catalogued below. Compared with era 2, the era's signatures are (a) rule-changing fights where the boss or an onlooker imposes laws on the players (the Queen's Court and Azshara decrees, Council of Blood's dance steps, Uu'nat's role-locked relics, Mekkatorque's shrink-and-read-the-code puzzle, Lords of Dread's impostor vote), (b) bosses whose arena is spent as the enrage (Sludgefist's pillars, Wrathion's lava, Dausegne's reservoirs, Sylvanas' Raze, Halondrus' walk through the caves), (c) split realms where part of the raid fights somewhere else (Rastakhan's death realm, Za'qul's three realms, N'Zoth's mind, Kel'Thuzad's phylactery, Anduin's Kingsmourne, Rygelon's singularity), and (d) personal resource bars at raid scale (Jaina's Chilling Touch, Mythrax's health loss, N'Zoth's Sanity, Denathrius' Burden of Sin, Anduin's Willpower, Azeroth's own health bar on the Jailer).

### Cited Findings

#### BATTLE FOR AZEROTH (2018-2020) — Raid Finder, Normal and Heroic flexible 10-30 players, Mythic fixed at 20

##### Uldir (patch 8.0, Sept 2018; 8 encounters; G'huun last) — sources: boss pages linked below
- **Taloc** ([wiki](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Taloc)) — Plasma Discharge on players leaves Blood Storm pools (one every 2 s for 4 s) that fill the room [hazard]. Cudgel of Gore: he charges his target and slams, knocking back everyone within 30 yd with damage falling off from the impact, and the slam absorbs all Blood Storms within 18 yd, so the tank aims his charge at the pools to clean the floor (a boss attack used as the raid's cleaning tool) [job][hazard]. Sanguine Static: bolts surge outward, knocking back anyone in their path [dance]. Stage 2 at 35% ("Ruin's Descent"): he Powers Down (99% damage reduction), Coalesced Blood adds trail Blood Storms and Volatile Droplets run at players and explode (kill them first) while Uldir Defensive Beams sweep the arena (marked Deadly) [adds][hazard][phase]; that the arena floor is an elevator carrying the raid down the shaft in this stage (unverified, not stated on the page). Mythic: Hardened Arteries (slows the target over 6 s, then explodes on everyone within 8 yd: spread) and Enlarged Heart (damage split among nearby players: soak) [spread][stack/soak].
- **MOTHER** ([wiki](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/MOTHER_(tactics))) — A three-room fight: the raid passes through Defense Grids (lasers) into the next chamber; every crossing hurts the whole raid and summons an Embodiment of Contamination 3 s later, so crossings are coordinated [room change][adds][split]. In each chamber MOTHER counts down Cleansing Purge, after which the room burns every second, so the raid must move on in time (a timer per room) [enrage]. Reaching the third chamber cleanses all corruption and gives her Depleted Energy (+100% damage taken) [phase]. Sanitizing Strike is a frontal cone that stacks +50% damage taken: tank swap [tank swap]. Wind Tunnels push players toward Purifying Flame jets [hazard]; Remnants of Corruption cast an interruptible raid DoT [interrupt]. Mythic: Resistant Bacterium adds (each outbreak buffs other embodiments by 10%) and Viral Contagion's Endemic Virus, which spreads to everyone within 10 yd when it ends, so its target steps away [spread][adds].
- **Fetid Devourer** ([wiki](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Fetid_Devourer)) — Thrashing Terror: every fourth melee swing (Terrible Thrash, 300% of a melee hit) goes to the player closest to the tank, so the second tank stands next to the first and soaks it (a "two tanks at once" design instead of a swap) [tank swap][stack/soak]. Waste Disposal Units dump Corruption Corpuscles; if not killed they release Enticing Essence and he walks over and eats them (heals 10%, +20 energy) [adds][dps race]. At 100 energy, Rotting Regurgitation: a frontal cone that knocks back and applies Malodorous Miasma, a stacking DoT that turns into Putrid Paroxysm when it ends, so healers must manage stacks [dance][resource]. Fetid Frenzy at 50%: +25% damage dealt, +50% damage taken [enrage]. Heroic: Shockwave Stomp raid knockback; Mythic: Mutated Mass adds.
- **Zek'voz, Herald of N'Zoth** ([wiki](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Zek%27voz)) — Titan Discs come online one after another, each giving the fight a theme and turning the previous one off [phase]. Stage 1 (Chaos, C'Thun): Eye Beam leaps to nearby allies for more damage (spread) and Silithid Warriors ignore threat [spread][adds]. Stage 2 at 65% (Deception, Yogg-Saron): Roiling Deceit leaves an Ominous Cloud that drifts and spawns a Guardian of Yogg-Saron if anyone strays close (its Shadow Nova hits everyone); Nerubian Voidweavers cast interruptible Void Bolt [hazard][interrupt]. Stage 3 at 30%: Orb of Corruption explodes on the raid unless a player intercepts it; the catcher gets Corruptor's Pact (+100% health, damage and healing) but is then mind-controlled (Will of the Corruptor) and must be interrupted and CC'd [job][mind control]. Surging Darkness at 100 energy: a series of eruptions plus raid damage [dance]. Might of the Void: Void Lash cones (-50% healing, stacking) and Shatter (+50% physical taken) [tank swap].
- **Vectis** ([wiki](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Vectis)) — Landmark "debuff-routing" fight. Several Omega Vectors exist from the pull; each sits on a player 10 s and then jumps to the closest player, leaving a permanent Lingering Infection stack (+3% Nature damage taken, no duration, cannot be removed) on its former host [resource][job]. The raid plans who takes each jump so stacks spread evenly and tanks avoid them; Contagion hits everyone, scaled by stacks [stack/soak]. Gestate stuns a player and hatches a Plague Amalgam whose Immunosuppression absorbs healing raid-wide [adds]. Stage 2: he Liquefies into a pool (touching it adds infection stacks), Plague Bombs must be soaked or they become Amalgams [stack/soak][phase]. Mythic: stack thresholds trigger on Contagion: 6 stacks Bursting Lesions (spreads stacks to players within 5 yd), 12 an Engorged Parasite add, 25 Terminal Eruption (damage to everyone) — so the whole raid's stack totals are a soft enrage [resource][enrage].
- **Zul, Reborn** ([wiki](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Zul,_Reborn)) — Stage 1 is an add fight: Call of Blood waves of Nazmani Crushers (Thrumming Pulse +50% damage stacking to nearby allies: tank them apart), Bloodhexers (their Animated Ichors walk to Zul and heal him with their remaining health: kill them on the way; interruptible Bloodshard) and Bloodthirsty Crawgs that heal for 200% of the damage they deal; the Forces of Blood regenerate after death unless the magic debuff Decaying Flesh is on them, which jumps to an add of another type when it ends [adds][interrupt][dispel][job]. Minions of Zul fixate and cannot be tanked; dispelling their Bound by Shadow kills them [dispel][kite]. Dark Revelation: 10 s, then damage falling off with distance from the target, who runs out [spread]. Stage 2 at 40%: Locus of Corruption converts all his energy into permanent Corrupted Blood stacks (1 per 5 energy), a raid DoT for the rest of the fight, so the energy he banked in stage 1 is the enrage [enrage][dps race]; Deathwish makes players walk to the platform's edge and jump off unless dispelled (dispel or jump hits the raid) [dispel][mind control]. Heroic: Pools of Darkness explode unless someone stands in them [stack/soak]; Bloodhexers within 20 yd of each other heal all adds.
- **Mythrax the Unraveler** ([wiki](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Mythrax_the_Unraveler)) — Annihilation stacks reduce maximum health by 1% each; hits from his abilities add stacks, and Existence Fragments that spawn near a player who just gained a stack remove 2 stacks when touched, so the raid manages a shrinking health bar by collecting pickups [resource]. Oblivion Sphere charms everyone near it and keeps adding stacks; freed players (knocked out) trigger Crashing Oblivion on the raid, so the raid keeps several players from being caught by one sphere [mind control][spread]. Imminent Ruin on 2 players explodes after 12 s [spread]. Stage 2: he hides behind Oblivion Veil (99% damage reduction), Obliteration Beams sweep, Visions of Madness clones of up to 5 players cast interruptible Mind Flay, N'raqi Destroyers gain +80%/−80% when within 40 yd of another (tank them apart) [interrupt][adds][phase]. Heroic: at 20% Essence Shatter stops fragments [enrage]. Mythic: beams and blasts eat the fragments they pass over [resource].
- **G'huun** ([wiki](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/G%27huun_(tactics))) — Landmark "orb run". Phase 1: G'huun is sealed in the wall and heals itself; the raid fights his adds in the center (Cyclopean Terrors' interruptible Torment, Dark Young whose Dark Bargain circle gives +25% damage/healing/damage taken to everyone inside, players and mobs, so the tank parks it on other adds; 2 stacks = charmed) [adds][interrupt][job]. To dislodge him, Power Matrices (orbs) spawn on alternate sides and must be carried up to the Reorigination Drive on the upper platform; the carrier slows by 20% every 2 s until rooted, so the orb is thrown (up to 20 yd) along a relay of players; an uncaught throw destroys it, and anyone who held one cannot hold another for 3 minutes (Imperfect Physiology), so each run needs fresh runners; Matrix Surge chases the carrier and cysts slow them [job][gimmick][split]. Each drive charge (35 energy per orb, 3 orbs) fires Reorigination Blast: raid damage every 6 s for 24 s, but G'huun and all adds are stunned and take +100% damage [phase][stack/soak]. Phase 2: he emerges; Growing Corruption (+15% bite damage per consecutive swing on the same target) forces tank swaps; Wave of Corruption gives everyone stacking Putrid Blood, which is only reset by standing on a Blood Feast (a rooted player) — which then spawns a Gibbering Horror whose health grows 5% per stack consumed and which cannot die (comes back after 40 s) except under a Reorigination Blast; so the raid chooses many small adds or few big ones and times its one remaining blast [tank swap][resource][stack/soak][adds]. Phase 3 at 25%: he destroys the drive and the ceiling falls (Collapse knockback), Blood Feast stops (Putrid Blood stacks forever: a soft enrage), Gaze of G'huun fears anyone facing him (turn away) [room change][enrage][LoS]. Bursting Boils hit the 2 closest players; 2 stacks = permanent thrall [stack/soak][mind control]. Mythic: slain minions burst into Spawns of G'huun that fixate and burrow into players (Blood Host), Blightspreader Tendrils with interruptible Decaying Eruption that burrow elsewhere, Blood Shield on adds, Tendrils of Corruption on the upper paths [adds][interrupt].

##### Battle of Dazar'alor (patch 8.1, Jan 2019; 9 encounters; Lady Jaina Proudmoore last) — sources: boss pages linked below
Faction note: the first three encounters exist in an Alliance and a Horde version (Champion of the Light, Jadefire Masters, Grong), with different enemies and the same design; the raid list confirms separate pages for each ([Warcraft Wiki, Adventure Guide Battle for Azeroth raids](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Adventure_Guide_Battle_for_Azeroth_raids)). The pages read here are the Alliance versions; the Horde fights mirror them (Horde boss names (unverified): Frida Ironbellows, Grong the Jungle Lord, Mestrah and Manceroy Flamefist). The Alliance raid also begins the instance at a different boss than the Horde one, playing the battle from its side (order (unverified)).
- **Champion of the Light** ([wiki](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Champion_of_the_Light_(Alliance))) — A paladin boss with a seal cycle: she gains energy under Seal of Retribution (aura buffs her allies; her melee becomes a raid-wide wave) or Seal of Reckoning (every player attack on her gives her a Zealotry stack, so DPS must stop attacking her) and at 100 energy releases the seal for a payoff (a +200% damage blessing on an ally, or a raid-wide Holy blast) [resource][phase]. Call to Arms brings 1 Crusader + 2 Disciples whose heals and Penance must be interrupted; Crusaders' Consecration halves damage taken by adds standing in it and Blinding Faith disorients anyone facing them [adds][interrupt][LoS]. Wave of Light heals adds it passes through, so tanks keep adds moving and out of Consecration [dance]. Sacred Blade stacks: tank swap [tank swap]. Heroic: Divine Protection (99% reduction on an ally, then Forbearance). Mythic: Prayer for the Fallen: every crusader and disciple, alive or dead, acts (dead disciples heal her to full, dead crusaders throw mallets) — so how many adds died matters [adds].
- **Jadefire Masters** ([wiki](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Jadefire_Masters_(Alliance))) — Two-boss council (monk and fire mage) [council]. Harmonious Spirits: the healthier one grows stronger as its partner falls lower, so their health is kept even [council]. Multi-Sided Strike knocks the tank up and images strike from several sides; the tank must face each one or take +100% physical damage (Tested) [job]. Rising Flames stacks on tanks and ends in a raid-wide Burnout scaled by stacks, so tanks swap to manage it [tank swap]. Searing Embers must be dispelled (lowest duration first) or explode on the raid [dispel]. Combined Power drives set-piece team attacks [phase]: at 30, Living Bombs in Chi-Ji's Song mist zones that block targeting in and out (healers must step inside) [LoS][adds]; at 60, A Flash of Hostility teleports the raid to one side of the room behind a maze of Rings of Hostility (entering one expels you) with a Spirit of Niuzao chasing through it while phoenix strikes fall, and the raid breaks a barrier and interrupts them [room change][dance]; at 100, they transform for good (Jade Serpent and Blazing Phoenix with constant raid damage) [enrage]. Heroic: Magma Trap launches players so high the fall kills; Force Orb carried through the maze opens another barrier weak point [job].
- **Grong** ([wiki](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Grong,_the_Revenant)) — Death Energy rises over time (faster below 70% and 40%) and at 100 he casts Death Knell, a raid-wide pulse that stacks Death Touched (+30% damage from the next Knell) [resource][enrage]. Death Specters add energy and must be interrupted and killed; their death blast also feeds him if he stands in it [adds][interrupt]. A player picking up the specter's Necrotic Core can Discharge it at Grong for 5% of his health, which also forces an early Death Knell if he is above 25 energy, letting the raid control when the Knell comes [job]. Bestial Smash + Crushed (+500% from the next Smash): tank swap [tank swap]. Heroic: Ferocious Roar fears anyone not standing near an ally (stay in pairs) [stack/soak]; Bestial Throw hurls the tank at another player.
- **Opulence** ([wiki](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Opulence)) — Landmark split gauntlet. Stage 1: the raid splits into two groups that fight through two trapped vault hallways (flame jets, a Ruby Beam that follows a player, a hex that hurts while moving) and each kills a guardian (The Hand of In'zashi, Yalat's Bulwark) whose Crush stuns everyone on one side of it; Consuming Flame sets a whole room ablaze to push the group forward [split][hazard][room change]. Each player takes one of seven Crown Jewels that give role-specific powers (Diamond: tank absorbs 50% of damage up to 300% of health, recharges in 30 s, so tanks swap on its depletion; Amethyst: healers' heals make allies take −90% Shadow damage; Topaz: grows stacks while nobody stands nearby, then becomes a crit aura for allies; Emerald: stacks while standing still; Ruby: makes the target take more damage) — a loadout puzzle where every player picks a jewel [choice][job][tank swap]. Stage 2: Hoard Power doubles his damage and health for each guardian still alive when engaged [dps race]. Coin Shower is split among everyone within 7 yd [stack/soak]; Wail of Greed is a ramping raid channel that gives him a permanent +15% stack [enrage]; Spirits of Gold channel bursting gold piles until killed [adds]. Mythic: an Arcane Trap swaps players' positions in the halls; Spirits of Gold heal in Molten Gold pools [hazard].
- **Conclave of the Chosen** ([wiki](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Conclave_of_the_Chosen)) — Loa council: two aspects are active at once; killing one heals the others to full and gives them +15% damage (Loa's Wrath, stacking), then a new aspect joins; aspects within 7 yd of each other get +75% damage and −90% damage taken (keep them apart) [council][tank swap]. Each new aspect adds its loa's "Wrath" to a growing rotation of raid events: Pa'ku's Wrath (everyone must run to the bird within 10 yd or take lethal damage), Gonk's raptors that fixate and become CC-immune and fast when close together, Kimbul's leaps leaving stacking Bleeding Wounds, Akunda's explosions on players that release radiating Static Orbs, Krag'wa's leaps to the farthest player [stack/soak][kite][spread][adds]. Akunda's Mind Wipe and Gonk's Crawling Hex (spreads within 8 yd when it ends) must be dispelled or managed [dispel]. Mythic: Bwonsamdi's Wrath makes a player immune to healing and jumps to the nearest unaffected player on death or dispel [dispel][job].
- **King Rastakhan** ([wiki](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/King_Rastakhan_(tactics))) — Stage 1: Bind Souls, all damage to the king transfers evenly to his bodyguards, so the guards die first [adds][council]. Stage 2: Bwonsamdi (absorbs all damage) radiates Aura of Death within 30 yd; Death's Door leaves Death Rifts. Stage 3 at 60%: Bwonsamdi shifts into the death realm and takes the closest half of the raid with him [split]. Players in the death realm stack Deathly Withering and clear it by stepping through a Death Rift, which detonates Withering Burst on the living side; death-realm players also steer Dread Reaping waves into rifts to send them across — the two halves of the raid hurt each other by their choices [split][resource][job]. Inevitable End pulls everyone toward a zone of instant death around Bwonsamdi [kite]. Zombie Dust Totems mind-control two players until destroyed [mind control]. Bwonsamdi at 50% leaves and returns everyone to the living realm; stage 4 Rastakhan grows +2% damage every 15 s (Bwonsamdi's Boon) [enrage]. Scorching Detonation: the tank's explosion hurts the raid less the farther the tank is from the boss, so the tank runs away [spread]. Plague of Fire spreads between players within 7 yd [spread]. Mythic: phantoms return to the death realm when killed, leaving rifts; Grievous Axe Flurry.
- **High Tinker Mekkatorque** ([wiki](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/High_Tinker_Mekkatorque_(tactics))) — Landmark communication puzzle. Spark Bots with a 99% damage shield chase players [adds][kite]. World Enlarger shrinks several players for 30 s (damage −99%, trampled by full-size allies); shrunk players climb inside a Spark Bot and must enter its shutdown code three times correctly; the code is shown to everyone except the player inside, so others call it out; a wrong code or 1 minute shocks and stuns the tamperer. On Heroic and Mythic only players inside other Spark Bots can see a bot's code, so shrunk players read codes to each other; in Raid Finder everyone sees them [puzzle][job][gimmick]. Gigavolt Charge: at expiry it blasts everyone in line of sight of the victim, so they hide behind the room's objects [LoS]. Electroshock Amplification +15% per consecutive swing: tank swap [tank swap]. At 40% he flies up and drops Exploding Sheep; on landing Hyperdrive adds +30% attack speed and an extra bot [phase][enrage]. Heroic: Wormhole Generator teleports all players to one player. Mythic: one player becomes Enormous instead and tramples allies (and kills shrunk ones by Squish), Gigavolt leaves radiation zones, Spark Shield reflects damage [job][hazard].
- **Stormwall Blockade** ([wiki](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Stormwall_Blockade)) — Laminaria's energy rises from every empowerment; at 100 she casts Catastrophic Tides and kills the raid, so the fight is an energy clock [enrage][resource]. Stage 1 on two ships: two tidepriests (Sister Katherine, Brother Joseph) whose empowerment channels must be interrupted once their absorb shields break; killing one makes its soul channel a faster empowerment, so they die together; the raid crosses between ships by a Translocation Pedestal [council][interrupt][split][dps race]. Tempting Song makes a player walk to the Siren and jump overboard [mind control]. Heroic: tidepriests teleport to the other ship at health thresholds [split]. Stage 2 on the docks vs Laminaria: Ire of the Deep must be soaked by players or it hits everyone and spawns many pools [stack/soak]; Storm's Wail overcharges a player for 13 s (who can absorb Freezing Tidepools meanwhile) and then spawns an Energized Storm; Energized Storms walk to Laminaria and feed her energy (Energizing Wake) if they arrive, so they are killed or slowed by the tank's Kelp-Wrapping (+10% damage taken per stack on non-players); a killed storm recasts Storm's Wail on a random player (Normal) or leaves an Energized Remnant that pulses raid damage until a player picks it up and takes Storm's Wail (Heroic/Mythic) — a loop the raid must keep turning [job][resource][adds]. Mythic: Spawn of Laminaria's Sea Swell; tidepools strip kelp stacks from storms; Ire's knockback is divided among the soakers; the tidepriests' Souls of the Deep return to aid Laminaria [stack/soak].
- **Lady Jaina Proudmoore** ([wiki](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Lady_Jaina_Proudmoore_(tactics))) — Landmark "keep warm" resource fight. Chilling Touch stacks from her frost spells and the ice slow players 2% per stack; at 20 stacks and below 80% health a player is Frozen Solid and must be broken out [resource]. Heat is the answer: standing in Searing Pitch (which also hurts) or near a Burning Explosion barrel clears stacks [hazard][resource]. Stage 1 on a Zandalari ship: Kul Tiran Corsairs bombard the deck; marines' Set Charge barrels must be picked up and thrown overboard; players man ballistas to repel corsairs [gimmick][job]. Ring of Ice freezes everyone within 30 yd and hurts anyone farther [dance]. Intermission 1, Howling Winds: she blinks away into a storm that limits vision and radiates Glacial Shards that freeze on touch; Blistering Tornadoes knock players around [intermission][hazard]. Stage 2 on the frozen sea: the ground itself adds stacks (Arctic Ground); frozen Unexploded Ordinance barrels must be cracked with the flagship's Broadside, then become the warmth sources; Siegebreaker Blast knocks the raid back [room change][job]. Intermission 2, Flash Freeze: an expanding zone of cold that freezes on contact, and at the end she shatters everyone; Jaina's Tide Elemental casts Frost Nova and interruptible Water Bolt Volley [intermission][interrupt] (how the raid survives the shatter (unverified): not stated on the page). Stage 3: Prismatic Images copy her spells; a frozen player draws a Shattering Lance (triple damage to frozen targets), so frozen allies are freed at once; Orb of Frost loses power with each player who body-blocks it [stack/soak][adds]. Mythic: Gathering Blizzard cuts vision and requires each player to stay within 10 yd of 1, then 3 (at 50 power), then 5 other players (at 75) or freeze after 6 s — a forced-clumping rule against every spread mechanic; in the first intermission Icebound Images shield her (immune to damage and interrupts) until all are killed; ballistas get frozen (Frozen Siege) [stack/soak][intermission][adds].

##### Crucible of Storms (patch 8.1.5, April 2019; 2 encounters; a short raid between two tiers) — sources: boss pages linked below
- **The Restless Cabal** ([wiki](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Restless_Cabal)) — Two-boss council (Zaxasj the Speaker at range, Fa'thuul the Feared in melee) with three Relics of Power on the floor [council]. Each time a boss loses 25% health (75/50/25%) it activates the relic nearest to it: Void Stone (100% healing reduction on the raid until its absorb shell is broken), Trident of Deep Ocean (a bubble takes 75% of the bosses' damage and erupts with the absorb that remains), Tempest Caller (15 s of raid damage, and anyone hit below 25% health dies instantly) [resource][dps race]. Using the same relic twice per boss gives Power Overwhelming (+1,000% damage, a wipe), so the tanks drag each boss to a relic it has not used before each threshold: the room is a chessboard [job][dance][puzzle]. Pact of the Restless: they cannot die unless both reach 1 health at the same time [council][dps race]. Leaving the central platform: −99% damage and healing for 30 s [hazard]. Aphotic Blast gives +150% threat; on Heroic it turns its target into a hostile Agent of Demise, so one ranged player takes it [job][mind control]. Promises of Power (dispel, shrinks max health), Eldritch Abominations' Witness the End (interrupts staggered across players), Visage from Beyond (bring to 1 hp to interrupt its fear) [dispel][interrupt][adds]. Shear Mind stacks: tank swap [tank swap]. Mythic: each relic leaves an essence after use (Void Essence pulls everyone to the center, Oceanic Essences stun on touch, Storm Essences zap the nearest player with growing damage) that persists until the relic is used again [hazard].
- **Uu'nat, Harbinger of the Void** ([wiki](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Uu%27nat,_Harbinger_of_the_Void)) — Landmark "players carry the boss's tools". The same three relics are now picked up by players, each by one role only: healers carry the Void Stone (its Embrace of the Void stops all healing on creatures and players, used to deny Uu'nat's healing from Oblivion Tears, and ended by breaking the carrier's shell), tanks the Trident (a bubble that shelters players), DPS the Tempest Caller (its storm instantly destroys minions below 25% health, so it kills the otherwise-unkillable Undying Guardians); each use locks the carrier out of that relic (Feedback) [job][gimmick][resource]. Unstable Resonance marks players Storm, Ocean or Void for 15 s; a marked player must touch the matching relic, and touching a player of another mark or letting the timer run out explodes on everyone within 10 yd [job][spread][puzzle]. Undying Guardians cannot fall below 1 hp (Shroud of Whispers), regenerate, and reduce Uu'nat's damage taken by 99% within 10 yd, so tanks keep them away and the DPS relic finishes them [adds][tank swap]. Kingsmourne Hungers (4 times in Phase 1): everyone standing in it is pulled into the sword, a separate realm holding 1 Anduin's Despair (tankable; killing it gives 18 Willpower; failing to kill it in time is a wipe), 4 Anduin's Doubt (4 Willpower each, CC-able, despawn if they reach the center) and 4 Anduin's Hope (friendly; must be dispelled of Gloom and escorted, they cost 4 Willpower each if they reach the outside) [split][job][dispel]. Willpower is the fight's resource: at 100 Anduin manifests the source of his domination, which players can fight for Grasp of Domination; missed Willpower makes Anduin stronger [resource]. Blasphemy: players pair up so each pair holds two different debuffs; matching pairs must split and find a new partner within 16 s [puzzle][job]. Befouled Barrier: players must stand in it to be healed (its healing is consumed by those inside) [stack/soak]. Intermissions: walls of Remnant souls fill lanes and the raid moves into the lane with two walls; Monstrous Souls explode at low health [room change][adds]. Phase 2: four Grim Reflections whose Psychic Terror must be interrupted, each kill making the rest harder to kick (Grim Fate), so later ones are hard-CC'd [interrupt][adds]. Phase 3: Hopelessness stacks are cleansed in Beacon of Hope (center puddle); Empowered Wicked Stars bounce around the room [dance][resource]. Dark Zeal stacks until he attacks another target: tank swap [tank swap]. Mythic: a Severed Soul left behind by each Kingsmourne group casts Banish Soul until killed (stun/blind rotation); in Beacon of Hope each player fires a Fragment of Hope a teammate must catch to cleanse [job][interrupt]. A German report of an Ion Hazzikostas interview says Anduin had so many mechanics it felt like a final boss ([Mein-MMO](https://mein-mmo.de/en/wow-chief-says-the-mythic-race-was-unhealthy-raids-will-be-easier,816476)).
- **Lords of Dread** ([wiki](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Lords_of_Dread); [Method](https://www.method.gg/guides/sepulcher-of-the-first-ones/lords-of-dread)) — Two dreadlords (Mal'Ganis, Kin'tessa); each dies into a Rampaging Swarm until the other dies too [council]. At 100 energy Mal'Ganis triggers Swarm of Decay and Swarm of Darkness: heavy raid damage for 20 s while both take +100% damage (a burn window) [phase][dps race]. Landmark imposter phase: at 100 energy Kin'tessa makes both dreadlords vanish and infiltrate the raid disguised as players (Infiltration of Dread); unless they are unmasked quickly, Shatter Mind kills everyone. Method's guide calls it the "Among Us" mechanic: some players see "demon wings" on the impostors, the raid calls out names, and once the same two names have been called three times, those two are confirmed and attacked [puzzle][job]. After it, Aura of Shadows horrifies anyone more than 8 yd from another player for 50 s (clump) [stack/soak]. Cloud of Carrion grows on its target until passed to another player or cleansed (Bursting Dread fears if cleansed on someone without it) [dispel][job]. Opened Veins + Leeching Claws heals Mal'Ganis if the tank is hit while bleeding: tank swap [tank swap]. Slumber Cloud lingers [hazard].
- **Rygelon** ([wiki](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Rygelon)) — Cosmic energy is the clock: at 100 Rygelon casts Massive Bang, killing every player outside The Singularity, which players reach through the field left by a killed Unstable Matter; inside, Rygelon rematerializes, everyone takes Eternal Radiation (rising damage the longer they stay), then he Shatter Spheres a remaining Unstable Antimatter to return all players [room change][split][phase]. Dark Eclipse targets explode (Umbral Eruption) unless they stand in a Collapsing Quasar Field; Collapsing Quasars walk to Rygelon and give Plasma Radiation on contact [job][hazard]. Unstable Cores' Gravitational Collapse hurts targeted players heavily at 0 health [adds]. Manifest Cosmos: Cosmic Cores snare the nearest player [adds]. Corrupted Wound stacks: tank swap [tank swap].
- **The Jailer** ([wiki](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/The_Jailer_(tactics)); [Method](https://www.method.gg/guides/sepulcher-of-the-first-ones/the-jailer)) — Landmark "protect Azeroth" finale. Azeroth has its own health bar: World Crusher opens by dropping it to 37%, and players heal it by soaking pools of Blood of Azeroth (soaking stacks Azerite Radiation, more damage taken but more damage dealt); at the end, Desolation damage that players do not intercept harms Azeroth (Method: at least 17 soakers needed), and if Azeroth's life force reaches 10% the Jailer executes it and unmakes existence (a wipe) [resource][stack/soak][job][enrage]. Relentless Domination permanently mind-controls any player in his line of sight, so the raid hides behind pillars (Method) [LoS][mind control]. Unholy Attunement raises pylons that are knocked down by the tanks' Shattering Blast, and connecting a line of pylons to the machine starts The Unmaking [room change][job]. Rune of Damnation bombs: players jump into assigned holes in the floor (pylon conduits) so the final eruption does not hit allies [job][hazard]. Rune of Compulsion charms 3 players behind a shield who walk off the platform unless the raid breaks the shield [mind control][job]. Chains of Oppression pull the raid in (escaped with a warlock gateway) [kite]. Chains of Anguish: allies take the tank's physical damage [stack/soak]. Martyrdom needs at least 5 soakers [stack/soak]. Decimator knocks players back by position, with a thin line that kills, and later Falling Debris circles [dance][hazard]. Rune of Domination's absorb is split among the players hit [stack/soak]. Mythic: a secret extra phase revealed during the race (25 March 2022), with Death Sentence (dispels that split onto catchers) and Meteor Cleave (a cone split among soakers with knockback); the boss heals about 15% and shields ([Esports.gg](https://esports.gg/news/gaming/jailer-defeated-echo-takes-home-victory-in-the-wow-rwf-sepulchre-of-the-first-ones/) via search; Method guide) [phase][stack/soak][dispel]. Wowhead's copy of the Encounter Journal lists three stages (Origin of Domination, Unholy Attunement, Eternity's End): in stage 2 the raised pylons spread Unholy Ground (damage near each pylon) and Shattering Blast destroys pylons caught in its blast, so the tanks aim it to clear the floor; on Mythic, Relentless Misery explodes the ground if fewer than 5 players are hit, World Crusher/Cracker/Shatterer scatter the Blood of Azeroth pools around the chamber, and in stage 3 Falling Debris: "Corrupted pylons crash to the floor" (heavy damage within 25 yd); Defile grows each time it damages a player (and over time on Mythic); Chains of Anguish binds the tank to 4 allies and breaks with raid damage if one moves 30 yd away ([Wowhead, The Jailer NPC page](https://www.wowhead.com/npc=180990/the-jailer)) [room change][stack/soak][job]. That the platform itself breaks apart is (unverified): the journal's room changes are pylons raised, destroyed and crashing down, and holes for the bombs (Method).

### Inferences
- The era moved the "one rule per fight" idea from era 2 into the players' heads: many landmark fights here are won by reading a rule and organising people (who reads which code, who takes which relic, which half sees which illusion, who votes for which impostor), not by dodging alone. These designs need communication and they scale badly to pick-up groups, which is why Raid Finder versions show the codes to everyone (Mekkatorque) or ignore used relics (Restless Cabal).
- Pairing is everywhere: Chain Links (Sludgefist), Dark Recital (Council of Blood), Paranoia (N'Zoth), Mind Tether (Za'qul), Blasphemy (Anduin), Mythic Lihuvim's alignments, Arcing Azerite (Ashvane). For a 2-40 player game, a "pair" rule is the smallest coordination unit and works at every size.
- "Opposite answers to look-alike threats" is a reusable pattern: Wings of Rage vs Reverberating Refrain (The Nine), Painful Memories vs Longing (Azshara's lovers), Frost Mark vs Toxic Brand's movement rules (Sivara, Heroic), odd vs even standing players turning a ring (Roh-Kalo).
- The heal-the-boss inversion (Sun King's Salvation), friendly targets to protect or heal (Bargast's soul ghost, Anduin's Hope, Azeroth's health bar) and targets to body-block (Halondrus' motes, Ashvane's waves) give healers and tanks new verbs.

### Gaps
- Radiance of Azshara, Maut, Xanesh, Hivemind, Drest'agath, Il'gynoth, Vexiona, Ra-den and Carapace rest on short Adventure Guide overviews only; their Mythic-only mechanics are not covered. Maut's wiki page carries a copy of Wrathion's ability list, so only its overview was used.
- Radiance of Azshara rests on search summaries of guides, not on fetched pages (Icy Veins refused fetches).
- Mythic-only mechanics are well covered for Uldir, Dazar'alor, the Eternal Palace and Sanctum of Domination (the wiki marks them in the ability lists), but thin elsewhere: Castle Nathria has Mythic details only for Shriekwing and Sun King (Inerva's and Denathrius' Mythic ("Through the Mirror") are named but not described, and none were found for Altimor, Xy'mox, Hungering Destroyer, Council of Blood, Sludgefist or Stone Legion Generals — the raid's highest pull counts); in Sepulcher only Lihuvim, Anduin, Halondrus (bombs) and the Jailer's secret phase are covered; Uu'nat's Mythic and most Ny'alotha Mythics (beyond Wrathion, Skitra, Shad'har, Drest'agath and N'Zoth) are not covered.
- Horde versions of the first three Dazar'alor fights were not fetched (assumed mirrored).

## 4. How did raid size work, and which mechanics scaled with it?

### Takeaway
On Normal and Heroic (10-30 players), Blizzard scaled boss health and damage with group size and scaled some target counts in steps; Mythic is fixed at 20, so Mythic counts are absolute (e.g. "at least 17 soakers"). The clearest concrete examples are in a February 2021 Castle Nathria tuning post: Stone Legion Generals' Seismic Upheaval targets 4 to 10 players and Heart Rend 1 to 3 across 10 to 30 players; Lady Inerva throws 1 to 3 bottles (Normal) and 2 to 4 (Heroic) depending on raid size.

### Cited Findings
- "Seismic Upheaval now targets 4 to 10 players, scaling from 10 to 30-player raid sizes, on Normal and Heroic difficulties"; on Raid Finder 3 to 5. "Heart Rend now targets 1 to 3 players, scaling from 10 to 30-player raid sizes, on Heroic difficulty." Stone Legion Generals' overall damage was cut 25-33% for 10-player raids and raised for large raids; "Wicked Laceration damage now scales with the number of players" — [Blizzard, Dungeon and Raid Tuning, Feb 9 2021](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/dungeon-and-raid-tuning-february-9/863057)
- "Lady Inerva Darkvein now throws fewer bottles for smaller raid sizes, scaling from 1 to 3 (was 2 to 3)" on Normal and "from 2 to 4 (was 3 to 4) on Heroic"; Anima Web up to 20% less damage for raids under 30; Shared Suffering up to 20% less for 10-player raids; add health lower for raids under 30 — [same post](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/dungeon-and-raid-tuning-february-9/863057)
- Xy'mox's Glyph of Destruction and Withering Touch, Sludgefist's Chain Slam and Colossal Roar, and Denathrius' Crimson Cabalist health were all tuned "depending on group size" — [same post](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/dungeon-and-raid-tuning-february-9/863057)
- Mechanics whose strength depends on how many players join (not on raid size, but a scaling rule designers can use at any size): Eye of the Jailer's Dragging Chains pull "reduced by the number of players being chained" ([wiki](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Eye_of_the_Jailer_(tactics))); N'Zoth's Harvest Thoughts drains 250 sanity split among those hit, and if fewer than 5 are hit it drains everyone ([wiki](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/N%27Zoth_the_Corruptor)); Guardian of the First Ones' Mythic Obliterate drains 20 energy if it hits fewer than 4 players ([wiki](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Guardian_of_the_First_Ones)); Mythic Ire of the Deep's knockback "divided among all players struck" ([wiki](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Stormwall_Blockade)); the Jailer's Desolation needs at least 17 soakers on Mythic ([Method](https://www.method.gg/guides/sepulcher-of-the-first-ones/the-jailer)); Kel'Thuzad's Necrotic Surge increases the number of players targeted by Oblivion's Echo and Glacial Wrath each cycle ([wiki](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Kel%27Thuzad_(Sanctum_of_Domination_tactics))).
- Difficulty, not size: Queen's Decree gives each player 1 decree on Raid Finder and Normal, up to 2 on Heroic, up to 3 on Mythic ([wiki](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Queen_Azshara_(Eternal_Palace_tactics))); Mekkatorque's Spark Bot codes are visible to everyone on Raid Finder but only to players inside other bots on Heroic and Mythic ([wiki](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/High_Tinker_Mekkatorque_(tactics))); Restless Cabal on Raid Finder ignores relics already used ([wiki](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Restless_Cabal)).
- Background (Mists of Pandaria, Flex): a developer described moving target counts from hard breakpoints (e.g. Shadow Word: Bane hits 2 targets at 10-14 players, 3 at 15) to weighted randomness (a 13-player raid would get 3 targets 60% of the time), while keeping fixed counts where groups must pre-plan (Imprison on Sha of Pride) — [Wowhead news via search summary](https://classic.wowhead.com/news=225217) (whether this system continued into BfA and Shadowlands (unverified))

### Inferences
- The scaled values are "targets per cast" and "damage per hit"; mechanics whose solution is a fixed formation (Form Ranks positions, Mekkatorque's code readers, Roh-Kalo's rune counts) are hard to scale and are where small raids feel the squeeze.
- Rules phrased as "split among those hit" or "at least N players" scale themselves to raid size and are the cleanest template for a 2-40 player game; "at least N" needs N set per size.

### Gaps
- No BfA-era example of a target count scaling with raid size was found in a fetched source. A developer note about easing "space constraints imposed by larger raid sizes" surfaced in a search about Sylvanas' Normal/Heroic changes but could not be tied to a boss or date (unverified).

### Inferences
- Pull counts peaked on fights with many simultaneous jobs and little room for error (Uu'nat's relics plus resonance, Stone Legion Generals' intermission, Halondrus' bombs on the move), not on the final bosses' raw tuning.
- The long multi-location final fights (Sylvanas, the Jailer's four phases plus a secret phase) were where Blizzard and the press talked about length; a designer for a 2-40 player game should keep each location's "retry cost" short (checkpoints, shorter phases).

### Room-changing fights (summary)
- New room or location mid-fight: MOTHER, Opulence, Stormwall Blockade, Jaina, Jadefire Masters, Orgozoa, Il'gynoth, Carapace, Blackwater Behemoth, Eye of the Jailer, Denathrius, Sylvanas, Halondrus, Rygelon (see section 3 for how).
- Floor fills or shrinks (the room as enrage): Wrathion, Hungering Destroyer (Heroic), Kel'Thuzad, Denathrius, Sylvanas stage 3, Dausegne, N'Zoth (Mythic), Shriekwing, Dormazain.
- Objects spent: Sludgefist pillars, Azshara's wards, Inerva's containers.
- Floor patterns or rearranged floor: Painsmith, Roh-Kalo, Jadefire Masters' maze, Azshara Mythic walls, Uu'nat's closing void ring, G'huun's collapse, Sivara's pool inversion (Heroic), the Jailer's pylons raised and destroyed.

### What scaled with raid size (summary)
- Normal/Heroic, 10-30 players: target counts (Seismic Upheaval 4-10, Heart Rend 1-3, Inerva bottles 1-3 / 2-4), damage per hit and add health "depending on group size" (Xy'mox, Sludgefist, Denathrius' Cabalists, Stone Legion Generals) — Feb 2021 Blizzard tuning post.
- Self-scaling rules: split damage among those hit; "at least N hit or everyone suffers" (N'Zoth 5, Guardian Mythic 4, Jailer Desolation 17 on Mythic); force divided among those chained or struck.
- By difficulty rather than size: decrees per player 1/2/3, code visibility, relic memory on Raid Finder.
