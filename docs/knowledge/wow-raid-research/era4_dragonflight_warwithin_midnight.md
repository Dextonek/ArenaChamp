# WoW raid boss mechanics, era 4: Dragonflight (2022-2024), The War Within (2024-2026), Midnight (2026)

How to read these notes
- Every boss line names its phases and its mechanics, what the raid must do, and what failure costs. Mechanic types are tagged inline in square brackets: [tank swap], [spread], [stack/soak], [adds], [interrupt], [dispel], [kite], [LoS], [dance] (positional pattern), [hazard] (ground or room hazard), [room] (the room itself changes), [gimmick] (vehicle or special-action fight), [council] (several bosses at once), [intermission], [enrage], [resource] (debuff or resource management), [split] (raid divided into groups or realms), [job] (a named player is assigned a task), [puzzle] (memory or logic), [aimed] (players aim or place the mechanic), [phase] (a health or event threshold changes the fight), [private aura] (a debuff addons cannot read), [dps check], [heal check].
- Source rule: each raid heading names its sources, and a boss line with its own link rests on that page. Midnight boss lines rest mostly on Method's **Mythic** strategy guides (method.gg, pages updated May-November 2026), read through a summarising fetch tool: they describe the Mythic version, often name what is new on Mythic compared with Heroic, but rarely give the Normal version. Where Method itself did not label a mechanic Mythic-only, these notes say "per Method's Mythic guide" instead of "Mythic-only". Marker colours and class assignments from those guides were left out. Dragonflight and The War Within boss lines rest on Warcraft Wiki boss pages, which reproduce the in-game Encounter Journal (ability text, Heroic/Mythic marks, hotfix notes).
- Connecting detail: the named abilities, numbers, timers and Heroic/Mythic differences come from the linked pages. The short connecting clause inside a line ("so the raid soaks it fully", "so tanks keep him off them") follows the page where it said so and the evident purpose of the ability, or common practice, where it did not; treat those clauses as interpretation.
- Quotation marks: text in quotation marks is wording a fetched page showed; all other wording is a paraphrase made through a summarising fetch tool.
- Dates: Warcraft Wiki pages, Method's raid-history pages and pullcount.gg pages are undated living pages, read on 12 Nov 2026; Method boss guides carry their own "last updated" dates, given per boss.
- Anything marked "(unverified)" was not confirmed on any fetched page and comes from the researcher's memory; treat it as a lead, not a fact.
- Count: as of 12 November 2026 these three expansions hold 12 raid instances (3 Dragonflight, 3 The War Within, 6 Midnight: The Voidspire, The Dreamrift, March on Quel'Danas, The Venomous Abyss, plus the one-boss Sporefall and the one-boss Lair, The Tidebound Grotto) and 69 encounters.

## 1. For each boss: its phases and its defining mechanics

### Takeaway
Twelve instances and 69 encounters, all catalogued below. Almost every fight of this era runs on the boss's energy bar: a fixed loop of abilities ends in a big ability at 100 energy, and a stacking soft enrage (a damage buff per cycle, or a floor that fills up) ends the fight. On top of that loop, each boss has one or two rules of its own that set it apart: a tic-tac-toe board, a duck, a garbage ball the players steer, a memory game split across three realms. Mythic usually adds one more rule that ties players to one another, such as players who must touch to clear a debuff, or an add that must not reach the boss. Midnight's bosses (2026) are built the same way as Dragonflight's and The War Within's.

### Cited Findings

#### DRAGONFLIGHT (2022-2024)

Raid list: Vault of the Incarnates (8 bosses, patch 10.0, Mythic world first race December 2022), Aberrus, the Shadowed Crucible (9, patch 10.1, May 2023), Amirdrassil, the Dream's Hope (9, patch 10.2, November 2023) — [Method, Dragonflight raid history](https://www.method.gg/raid-history/dragonflight). The list in the brief is complete and correctly named. Normal and Heroic are flexible (10-30 players), Mythic fixed at 20 (see question 5). Boss pages on Warcraft Wiki (warcraft.wiki.gg) reproduce the in-game Encounter Journal: the ability text with damage numbers shown as placeholders, and Heroic/Mythic notes.

##### Vault of the Incarnates (8 bosses) — sources: Warcraft Wiki boss pages (linked per boss, read 12 Nov 2026); race dates from Method
1. **Eranog** — [Warcraft Wiki](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Eranog). Stage One "Army of Talon": Flamerift wreathes several players; when it ends a rift under each spawns a Flamescale Tarasek that fixates one player (Kill Order) and leaves Lava Flow, so marked players drop rifts at the edge and kite [adds][kite][aimed]; Molten Cleave frontal; Incinerating Roar is raid damage with a stacking DoT and blows up the Molten Spikes left earlier into Molten Fissures with knock-ups [heal check][hazard]; Burning Wound stacks on tanks [tank swap]. Stage Two "Army of Flame" at 100 energy: Primal Flames sweep the room with Incinerate lines (deadly, 3 s stun) while Pulsing Flames hit the raid for 21 s [dance][heal check]. The stages repeat (every 90 s per a search summary). Mythic-only: Greater Flamerift on the current target spawns a Flamescale Captain (Leaping Flames bouncing between players, interruptible Pyroblast), and more Primal Flames [adds][interrupt].
2. **Terros** — [Warcraft Wiki](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Terros). A stationary boss whose room fills up: Rock Blast is split among players near the target (Mythic: hits all players) and raises Awakened Earth pillars, which hurt the raid until destroyed, and destroying one triggers Seismic Assault [stack/soak][adds]; Concussive Slam, a tank line, stacks +40%/50% (Mythic) damage taken [tank swap]; Shattering Impact and Fractured Rubble. At 100 energy, **Resonating Annihilation** slams a frontal cone, breaks the pillars and permanently covers part of the chamber in Resonant Aftermath; once the whole chamber is covered, Frenzied Devastation kills the raid — a soft enrage made of floor [room][enrage][aimed]. Heroic: Fractured Rubble also leaves Resonant Aftermath; Mythic: Infused Fallout dusts every player, and players touching clear each other (Explosive Reaction), leaving Reactive Bedrock [puzzle][spread]. Tectonic Barrage if nobody is in melee.
3. **The Primal Council** (Kadros Icewrath, Dathea Stormlash, Opalfang, Embar Firepath) — [Warcraft Wiki](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/The_Primal_Council). Four bosses; each one killed starts a Convocation, raid damage that grows until the council is dead, so once the first member dies the rest must die fast, and the kill order decides which Convocations stack [council][enrage]. Kadros: Primal Blizzard stacks Frost; at 10 stacks a player is Frost Tombed and must be freed; stacks are removed by standing in Embar's Scorched Ground (consumed on Mythic) [resource][hazard]. Dathea: Conductive Mark spreads to players within 5 yards and is removed by an Earthen Pillar (Heroic 20 s, Mythic never expires) [spread]. Opalfang: Earthen Pillars at several players (Heroic: Quaking Pulse until destroyed), tank Crush stacks [tank swap]. Embar: Meteor Axes on two players, damage split among players within 10 yards, destroying pillars (Heroic: +300% to later impacts for 15 s, so the two axes need two separate soak groups) [stack/soak][split]; interrupts on Frost Spike and Lightning Bolt [interrupt].
4. **Sennarth, the Cold Breath** (giant spider) — [Warcraft Wiki](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Sennarth,_the_Cold_Breath). Stage One "Ice Climbers": a short fight below, then she **climbs a staircase to an icy summit and the raid follows**, killing a Frostbreath Arachnid at each platform; the floor is Icy Ground (sliding) [room][adds]. Enveloping Webs drop Sticky Webbing that stacks into Wrapped in Webs (Heroic: a 30 s stun); Caustic Spiderlings erupt on death and remove webs (Mythic: they also apply Dissolved Defenses) [resource][adds]; Gossamer Burst pulls everyone inward, and the raid must not be pulled off the ledge or into webs [hazard]; Mythic Gusting Rime knocks players back. Stage Two "The Frozen Precipice" at the top: Apex of Ice makes her take 99% less damage while pulsing growing raid damage until interrupted (an "Important, Interruptible" cast); Repelling Burst pushes everyone away (edge danger); Pervasive Cold stacks raid Frost damage taken until she dies (soft enrage); Permafrost fills the lower lair [interrupt][enrage][hazard].
5. **Dathea, Ascended** — [Warcraft Wiki](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Dathea,_Ascended). Wind and knockbacks. Coalescing Storm summons Volatile Infusers that channel Diverted Essence (interruptible, +10% boss damage stacking) and knock the raid back when they die (Blowback) (Heroic: Thunder Callers too; Mythic: infusers stay dormant until energised) [adds][interrupt]. Raging Burst lands tornadoes (Raging Tempest) that knock up whoever touches them; Crosswinds moves every tornado to a new place [hazard][dance]. Cyclone pulls everyone toward Dathea for 10 s, then knocks up [hazard]. Conductive Mark copies itself to players within 4 yards (Mythic: never expires, and touching a Dormant Infuser triggers Static Discharge) [spread]. Zephyr Slam knocks the tank back with stacking damage [tank swap]. (Unverified, from memory and not on the page: some infusers stand on ledges above the arena and are reached by players riding a knockback.)
6. **Kurog Grimtotem** — [Warcraft Wiki, Kurog Grimtotem (tactics)](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Kurog_Grimtotem_(tactics)). The room holds four altars (Earth, Fire, Frost, Storm) [room]. Stage One "Elemental Dominance": Kurog builds dominance over the element of the altar he stands near, and tanks move him between altars, each giving a kit — Fire: Magma Burst pools, Searing Carnage; Frost: Absolute Zero split among nearby players (Heroic: Frost Bite, two stacks freeze), Heroic Frigid Torrent orbs; Earth: Erupting Bedrock ripples, Seismic Rupture, Heroic Enveloping Earth healing absorbs; Storm: Shocking Burst on charged players, Heroic Lightning Crash, Thunder Strike that hits everyone if it hits no player [dance][stack/soak][spread]. Standing in the centre away from the altars causes Elemental Surge raid damage; Heroic Primal Shift hits the raid on each attunement; Sundering Strike tank knockback [tank swap]. Mythic: an elite elemental per altar (Flamewrought Eradicator, Frostwrought Dominator whose Icy Tempest forces grouping, Earthwrought Smasher, Stormwrought Despoiler), each with a tank cone [adds]. At 100 energy he summons elementals according to his dominance levels. Stage Two "Elemental Creations": Primal Barrier (99% less damage) while the raid kills elemental bosses (Blazing Fiend, Frozen Destroyer with interruptible Frost Binds, Tectonic Crusher, Thundering Ravager), each with a stacking Presence aura [intermission][adds][interrupt][split]. Stage Three "The Primal End": Primal Attunement, all altar abilities at once plus stacking damage every 0.5 s (soft enrage). Mythic hard enrage 10 minutes (raised from 9 in a December 2022 hotfix) [enrage].
7. **Broodkeeper Diurna** — [Warcraft Wiki](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Broodkeeper_Diurna). Phase 1: Broodkeeper's Bond keeps her alive above 2% while eggs remain and buffs nearby allies, so the eggs must all be broken [gimmick][dps check]. Greatstaff of the Broodkeeper gives a player a stack they spend to fire a beam that **breaks every egg in its path**: the raid aims the beam through rows of eggs [aimed][job]; each egg broken triggers Clutchwatcher's Rage, stacking raid damage, so eggs are broken in measured batches [resource][heal check]; Rapid Incubation hatches the 2 nearest eggs into adds (Primalist Reinforcements also arrive in waves: mages with interruptible Ice Barrage, Earthreavers, Flamebenders, proto-dragons, Stormbringers) handled by add tanks [adds][interrupt]; Wildfire, Icy Shroud absorbs healing [dispel][heal check]; Mortal Stoneclaws reduces healing on the tank by 65% (Mythic 50%) [tank swap]; Diurna's Gaze (Mythic: Lingering Gaze stacking) [hazard]. Phase 2 "A Broodkeeper Scorned": Broodkeeper's Fury, a damage increase every 30 s (soft enrage); Frozen Shroud roots players in attackable rime; Mythic Mortal Stoneslam splits damage among players within 15 yards [enrage][stack/soak]. Mythic: Storm Fissures along the beam paths.
8. **Raszageth the Storm-Eater** (final) — [Warcraft Wiki](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Raszageth_the_Storm-Eater). Three stages, two intermissions.
   - Stage One: Hurricane Wing at 100 energy pushes the raid, longer each time [hazard]; Static Charge on players blasts the raid for less the farther away they are, so carriers move out [spread][aimed]; Volatile Current leaves Volatile Sparks that burst for raid damage unless interrupted or cleared (Heroic 2 charges, Mythic 3) [interrupt][job]; Lightning Breath frontal; Electrified Jaws on tanks [tank swap].
   - Intermission "The Primalist Strike": she takes 99% less damage while "vault integrity" drains (faster on Heroic and Mythic); Surging Ruiners channel into the vault behind shields and summon a Primalist Force when their shield is removed; adds include Oathsworn Vanguard, Stormseeker Acolyte (interrupt), and on Mythic Frostforged Zealot and Flamesworn Herald; Lightning Devastation lines sweep the platform; the next stage begins at 50% and 0% integrity [intermission][adds][interrupt][dps check].
   - Stage Two "Surging Power": Stormsurge gives every player a positive or negative charge: standing near the same charge gives Focused Charge (bonus), near the opposite gives Scattered Charge (damage), so the raid sorts into two charge groups (Mythic: Inversion flips several players) [split][puzzle][dance]; Fulminating Charge explodes and jumps to the nearest player with a shrinking radius [aimed]; Tempest Wing push.
   - Intermission "The Vault Falters": Colossal Stormfiends teleport (Storm Break), leaving Seeking Stormlings that run back to heal and empower them; Ball Lightning; Crackling Energy stacks raid damage [adds][kite].
   - Stage Three "Storm Incarnate": Raging Storm engulfs the platform over time, shrinking the safe area (soft enrage); Mythic Storm-Eater pulls motes from the storm that heal and empower her; Heroic Magnetic Charge pulls players to the blast; Thunderous Blast tank beam [room][enrage].
   - Room: she fights on the central platform and dives onto the side prisons (Fyrakk, Vyranoth, Iridikron) between stages (wiki).

##### Aberrus, the Shadowed Crucible (9 bosses) — sources: Warcraft Wiki boss pages (linked); race dates from Method. After Kazzara the raid picks between two wings of three bosses before Echo of Neltharion and Sarkareth (search summary of guides).
1. **Kazzara, the Hellforged** — [Warcraft Wiki](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Kazzara,_the_Hellforged). Health-threshold fight: at 80/60/40% Hellsteel Carnage breaks off an armour plate, which speeds up Infernal Heart's raid pulses and adds an extra Dread Rift target each time (an escalating fight) [phase][heal check]. Dread Rifts mark several players and open rifts under them 5 s later; Rays of Anguish hunt players from the rifts and leave Molten Scar fire; on Heroic and Mythic the rays keep hunting until a Dread Rift consumes them, so marked players walk rifts into the rays' paths (Heroic: a ray still alive at the next cast speeds up and pulses raid damage +50% per pulse; Mythic: a ray crossing an existing scar hits the raid, Infernal Fusion) [aimed][kite][hazard]. Hellbeam at 100 energy (frontal beam plus raid damage); Wings of Extinction raid knockback; Terror Claws tank bleed [tank swap].
2. **The Amalgamation Chamber** (Essence of Shadow and Eternal Blaze, then the Shadowflame Amalgamation) — [Warcraft Wiki](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/The_Amalgamation_Chamber). Phase 1: two elementals on two sides of the room, each pulsing a stacking aura to players within 50 yards (Corrupting Shadow lowers max health; Blazing Heat burns), so players choose a side [split][resource]. Shadow: Coalescing Void (less damage farther away), Umbral Detonation marks leave pools. Blaze: Fiery Meteor split among players within 8 yards (detonates on everyone if it hits no one), Molten Eruption fireballs that must each be caught by a player [stack/soak]. Heroic: Elemental Instability, both take 200% more damage within 25 yards of each other (a reason to bring them together briefly); Mythic: a player with Blazing Heat touching one with Corrupting Shadow erupts (Volatile Shadowflame) [council][dance]. Intermission merges them. Phase 2 Shadowflame Amalgamation: Shadowflame Contamination stacks on everyone every 10 s (max-health loss, a soft enrage); Gloom Conflagration meteor needs at least 5 players or it detonates on all; Blistering Twilight knocks back; Withering Vulnerability makes tanks swap [stack/soak][enrage][tank swap].
3. **The Forgotten Experiments** (Neldris, Thadrion, Rionthus) — [Warcraft Wiki](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/The_Forgotten_Experiments). Three drakes woken in turn (Normal/Heroic: each at 50% of the previous; Mythic: on the previous one's death) [council][phase]. All share Infused Strikes: tank stacks that are cleared by touching another afflicted player, which triggers Infused Explosion on the raid [tank swap][resource]. Neldris: Rending Charge on several players (bleed 15 s Normal, 30 s Heroic, never expires on Mythic), Massive Slam cone, Bellowing Roar (less damage farther away). Thadrion: Unstable Essence grows on a player; when removed it explodes, leaves an Erratic Remnant and jumps to another player, so it is passed on at a chosen moment and place [aimed][resource]; Violent Eruption raid pulses. Rionthus: Deep Breath across the room leaving cinders [dance]; Temporal Anomaly vortices drift to him and shield him for 10/15/20% of his health (Normal/Heroic/Mythic) unless players body-block them and are knocked away [job][hazard]; Disintegrate slows and leaves zones.
4. **Assault of the Zaqali** (Warlord Kagni and Ignara) — [Warcraft Wiki](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Assault_of_the_Zaqali). A siege on **two battlements**: the raid splits to hold both, Kagni leaps between them (Devastating Leap, fissures, knockback), Zaqali Wallclimbers climb the walls and must be stopped before they attack the Elder's Door, which triggers Barrier Backfire, stacking raid damage (never expires on Heroic) [split][adds][room]. Adds: Magma Mystics with Molten Barrier shields, interruptible Lava Bolt, Huntsman Blazing Spears, Obsidian Guards; Siege Arbalests fire along the ramparts [adds][interrupt][hazard]. Heavy Cudgel tank cone with stacking vulnerability [tank swap]. Mythic: Ignara's Phoenix Rush plants plumes that hatch fixating hatchlings which revive 10 s after death, and Vigorous Gale pushes one battlement toward the edge [adds][kite][hazard]. Phase 2 at 25%: Kagni assaults the door; Catastrophic Slam is a shockwave toward the door split among players in its path, applying Weakened Vitality (Heroic 200%, Mythic 500% more from the next), so two groups alternate soaks [stack/soak][split]; Desperate Immolation heals him 35% and adds Ignara's Fury raid pulses.
5. **Rashok, the Elder** — [Warcraft Wiki](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Rashok,_the_Elder). Energy-control fight: tanks drag Rashok into the Elder's Conduit, which drains his energy and incapacitates him while Scorching Heatwave hits the raid (Heroic+: stacking 2% per wave); after two drains a conduit shatters and cannot be reused; if he reaches 100 energy, Ancient Fury pulses raid damage every second (wipe) [gimmick][resource][enrage]. Charged Smash is split among players within 10 yards and gives him up to 15 energy minus the number of players hit, so the raid soaks it fully [stack/soak]. Searing Slam leaps to a player and creates a Lava Vortex; every vortex in the room adds 20% to the next slam, and a slam landing on a vortex causes Lava Explosion, so slam targets drop vortices away from earlier ones [aimed][room]. Doom Flames missiles must each be caught or they hit everyone. Mythic: Unleash Shadowflame sends orbs across the room. Tank Wrath of Djaruun: +500% (Normal) or +3000% (Heroic+) damage taken for 6 s, a forced tank swap [tank swap].
6. **The Vigilant Steward, Zskarn** — [Warcraft Wiki](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/The_Vigilant_Steward,_Zskarn). Trap room: Dragonfire Traps on the floor burn players within 11 yards; Animate Golems summons Dragonfire Golems that walk to inactive traps to activate them (interruptible) [adds][interrupt][room]. Players **salvage parts from dead golems** (Salvage Parts) to learn how to disarm traps, then Deactivate Dragonfire Trap — a player-interaction job [job][gimmick]. At 100 energy, Tactical Destruction makes dragon statues erupt across the area [hazard]. Shrapnel Bomb detonates on the raid after 30 s unless a player triggers it early (smaller hit on that player) [job][stack/soak]. Heroic: Unstable Embers damage allies within 5 yards [spread]; Mythic: golems become immune to CC at 50% health or full energy, and Elimination Protocol blasts in the direction of Ember targets [aimed]. Blast Wave knockback; Searing Claws tank stacks [tank swap].
7. **Magmorax** — [Warcraft Wiki](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Magmorax). A **floor-filling** cycle: Molten Spittle targets explode when it ends and leave Magma Puddles that grow over the fight; players standing in a puddle gain Searing Heat (Heroic: killed at 25 stacks, Mythic 40), which shrinks the puddle — so players are sent to soak puddles down [aimed][stack/soak][room][resource]. If Magmorax stands in a puddle he gains energy and pulses raid damage (Blazing Tantrum), so tanks keep him off them; at 100 energy Catastrophic Eruption stacks raid fire damage taken (+50%, Mythic +100%) — a soft enrage [enrage][dance]. Igniting Roar launches Lava Ejections that must be caught or they explode raid-wide and leave a puddle; Overpowering Stomp knockback; Incinerating Maws tank stacks [tank swap]. Mythic: one Spittle target becomes Explosive Magma split among nearby players, whose puddle is smaller the more players soak.
8. **Echo of Neltharion** — [Warcraft Wiki](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Echo_of_Neltharion). Stage One "The Earth-Warder": Twisted Earth raises **earth walls** across the room; Echoing Fissure repeats at each earlier impact point and leaves collapsed earth and lava [room][hazard]; Rushing Darkness knocks several players back and shatters any wall they hit (Shatter: raid damage; Mythic: stacking damage taken), so knockback targets are aimed to break chosen walls [aimed]; Volcanic Heart: players explode after a timer [spread]; Calamitous Strike tank knockback +200% physical damage taken for 37 s [tank swap]. Intermission at 60%: remaining walls erupt; Surrender to Corruption. Stage Two "Corruption Takes Hold": Corruption turns several players **hostile to their allies for 30 s, letting them attack hidden Voice From Beyond adds**; breaking a Voice's shield clears it; Umbral Annihilation raid damage doubles with each cast (soft enrage) [gimmick][job][adds][enrage]. Stage Three "Reality Fractures": walls become void portals spawning Twisted Aberrations; players standing in a portal take damage but are protected 99% from Ebon Destruction (Mythic: each portal protects only one player), so the raid assigns portals [LoS][job][adds]. Volcanic Heart was the first raid mechanic hidden as a private aura (see question 6).
9. **Scalecommander Sarkareth** (final) — [Warcraft Wiki, Sarkareth (tactics)](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Scalecommander_Sarkareth_(tactics)).
   - Phase 1 "The Legacy of the Dracthyr": Oppressing Howl pushes everyone back and raises damage from his next big abilities; the debuff is removed when a player is struck by Glittering Surge, Mass Disintegrate lines or Searing Breath (each of which removes it and triggers Echoing Howl raid damage) — a debuff the raid must spend in order (Mythic: 2 applications) [resource][puzzle]; Scorching Bomb explodes after 12 s leaving Burning Ground; Mass Disintegrate lines on marked players [aimed]; Searing Breath cone ×3 [tank swap].
   - Phase 2 "A Touch of the Forbidden": Void Bombs explode after 20 s, and a player can absorb one (Void Fracture) to delay it [job][stack/soak]; Abyssal Breath line knockback leaves void residue and creates images of Sarkareth with interruptible Cosmic Volley and self-heal Rescind (Mythic: tank-type and DPS-type images) [adds][interrupt]; Desolate Blossom bulbs pulse and absorb healing; Infinite Duress triggers Dread knockback when removed [dispel]; Ebon Might buffs the adds (at 10 stacks, Heroic 5, immune to CC and interrupts). When badly wounded **he destroys the room** [room].
   - **Emptiness Between Stars**: most void abilities add Oblivion stacks; at 10 a player is sent to this realm, deals 99% less damage, and dies for good unless they recover in time (25 s Normal, 20 s Heroic+) by absorbing Astral Flares; Mythic Destabilize damages the raid per entry and Dimensional Puncture opens holes players can fall through for 3 minutes [resource][split][hazard].
   - Phase 3 "The Seas of Infinity": Cosmic Ascension crashes through the floor, **the wall breaks open onto the void**; Astral Formation asteroids give cover from Scouring Eternity (a supernova knockback) [LoS][room]; Embrace of Nothingness puts a black hole on a player that sucks in nearby asteroids, so the target runs away from the cover [aimed]; Motes of Oblivion add 5 Oblivion stacks; Void Might stacks +10% damage (soft enrage) [enrage].

##### Amirdrassil, the Dream's Hope (9 bosses) — sources: Warcraft Wiki boss pages (linked); private-aura examples from [Raider.IO, "Private Auras - Were They Successful?"](https://raider.io/news/613-private-auras-were-they-successful) (undated, written after 1 Feb 2024)
1. **Gnarlroot** — [Warcraft Wiki, Gnarlroot (tactics)](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Gnarlroot_(tactics)). Energy cycle: Flaming Pestilence patches explode and some become dormant Tainted Lashers; Controlled Burn marks players who scorch nearby players and the ground [spread][adds]; Tortured Scream raid damage; Shadowflame Cleave; Dreadfire Barrage tank stacks [tank swap]. At 100 energy, Potent Fertilization: Doom Cultivation shields him and **Doom Roots** burst up and siphon energy; players who are Ember-Charred (from Splintering Charcoal) ignite the roots, and destroying all roots gives Uprooted Agony (he takes extra damage for 20 s) [job][dps check]; when it ends he gains a Rising Mania stack (+10% damage, soft enrage) [enrage]. Toxic Loam near him; Tainted Treants blind; interruptible Blazing Pollen [adds][interrupt]. (Heroic/Mythic differences not on the page.)
2. **Igira the Cruel** — [Warcraft Wiki](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Igira_the_Cruel). A choice fight: at 100 energy, Marked for Torment ignites **three areas**; players stand in the areas to shrink them while taking Gathering Torment (doubling after 10 s via Flesh Mortification), while Igira forges a weapon; her next weapon comes **from the areas the raid did not extinguish**, and if none were left she uses Ruinous End (deadly) — the raid chooses which weapon it faces (Mythic: two weapons are forged) [stack/soak][puzzle][split]. Blistering Spear tethers players in place and pulses until the spear is destroyed [adds]; Twisting Blade; Vicious Swing cleaves the next-closest player [tank swap]; Harvest of Screams stacks her damage each 100 energy (soft enrage) [enrage]; Umbral Destruction: damage to everyone is reduced by the number of players near the impact, who become Brutalized and cannot help with the next [stack/soak][split]. Heroic+: Smashing Viscera leaps with Devastation waves; Heart Stopper absorbs healing and explodes, Vital Rupture when removed [heal check].
3. **Volcoross** (lava serpent) — [Warcraft Wiki](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Volcoross). Fought from rings beside a lava pool; at 100 energy **Flood of the Firelands** drops two lava globs, each split among nearby players; if either hits fewer than 4 players the raid takes damage and Hellboil consumes the chamber, so two soak groups of at least 4 are needed [stack/soak][split][room]. Scorchtail Crash knocks back players in the ring (Heroic+: Tidal Blaze lava); Serpent's Fury sets players' Coiling Flames, which tighten and hurt allies nearby (Twisting Singe) [spread]; Mythic Coiling Eruption explodes on nearby players and moves on, and a player hit by two at once takes Eviscerating Flames [spread][aimed]; Volcanic Disgorge leaves Hellboil; tanks swap on Molten Venom [tank swap]; Combusting Rage if nobody is in melee; Serpent's Wrath raid damage growing over time [enrage].

##### The Voidspire (6 bosses) — sources: [Method, Voidspire guides index](https://www.method.gg/guides/the-voidspire) (Averzian summary) and Method's Mythic guide for each boss (linked per boss); Race to World First data from [pullcount.gg](https://www.pullcount.gg/raids/vs-dr-mqd)
1. **Imperator Averzian** — one phase built around a **tic-tac-toe board**. Shadow's Advance spawns three Abyssal Voidshapers that try to claim tiles of the floor; players soak Umbral Collapse to destroy two of the three each wave; if the boss claims **three tiles in a line**, the raid fails [puzzle][stack/soak][adds][room]. Imperator's Glory buffs the boss and adds standing within 10 yards of claimed tiles, so tanks keep them off the claimed squares [dance]. Voidshapers reaching 100 energy become Obsidian Endwalkers, so they die first; interrupt Pitch Bulwark; kill Voidmaws [adds][interrupt]. Dodge Void Fall and Oblivion's Wrath [hazard]; tanks swap at about 8-10 stacks of Blackening Wounds [tank swap]; Dark Upheaval is unavoidable raid damage [heal check]. World first: Incentive, 4 pulls.
2. **Vorasius** — [Method Mythic, 10 May 2026](https://www.method.gg/guides/the-voidspire/vorasius). Boss phase alternating with up to three add phases. Shadowclaw Slam is soaked by the tank; its impact sends out Aftershock rings, which the raid avoids by stepping into the impact spot at once [dance]. Parasitic Expulsion starts an add phase: Blistercreeps fixate players, explode shortly after dying, and leave **Dark Goo**, a puddle that is permanent and on Mythic grows over time, shrinking the floor for later lasers [adds][kite][hazard][room]. **Void Crystal walls** shatter only when Blistercreeps die on top of them (2 deaths per wall on Heroic, 3 on Mythic), so players drag fixates and use grips and knockbacks to place the deaths [aimed][room]. Failure: adds dying near the middle make the room "unplayable"; a third add phase leaves no time before the enrage that follows the third laser [enrage]. World first: Incentive, 2 pulls (pullcount marks it "Nurfed").
3. **Fallen-King Salhadaar** — [Method Mythic, 10 May 2026](https://www.method.gg/guides/the-voidspire/fallen-king-salhadaar). A repeating loop. Concentrated Void orbs drift toward the boss; an orb reaching him wipes the raid; each orb killed puts Dark Radiation on the raid, so kills are spaced one at a time [adds][resource]. Void Convergence brings killed orbs back with less health (on Mythic, Enduring Void: every Convergence), so orb deaths are stacked in one spot for AoE [aimed]. Shattering Twilight roots the boss for a few seconds, so tanks keep distance from the nearest orb beforehand. Fractured Images must all be interrupted before they finish casting or the raid wipes; on Mythic, Nexus Shield makes some Images unkickable until others are kicked, forcing a fixed kick order [interrupt][puzzle][job]. At 100 energy, Entropic Unraveling hits the raid hard (up to three times); after the third he enrages [heal check][enrage]. Per Method, on Mythic missed kicks and orbs are fatal instead of leaving puddles.
4. **Vaelgor & Ezzorak** (two dragons) — [Method Mythic, 11 May 2026](https://www.method.gg/guides/the-voidspire/vaelgor-ezzorak). Twilight Bond: keep the bosses more than 15 yards apart and within 10% health of each other, or they deal double damage [council][dps check]. Gloom: a puddle soaked at a marked spot by two teams of six that alternate [stack/soak][split]. Nullbeam is taken by tanks while the raid snaps tethers (Nullzone); the first snap spawns Nullscatter circles to dodge [dance]. Void Howl spawns orbs that cast Voidbolt until gripped, stunned and AoE'd [adds][interrupt]. Dread Breath puts a fear-like debuff on one player, dispelled at once after the target runs to a safe spot (Mythic: the target also gains speed) [dispel][aimed]. Intermissions: on Heroic the bosses fly; on Mythic they stay grounded and Cosmosis makes reflections of the bosses cast Dread Breath, Nullbeam and Void Howl during intermissions; an intermission add is held by a third tank [intermission][adds]. World first: Liquid, 5 pulls.
5. **Lightblinded Vanguard** (three paladin bosses: General Amias Bellamy, Commander Venel Lightblood, War Chaplain Senn) — [Method Mythic, 16 May 2026](https://www.method.gg/guides/the-voidspire/lightblinded-vanguard). Council with shared health balance: Retribution ramps the survivors if one dies early, so damage is spread across all three [council][dps check]. Each boss at 100 energy casts an ultimate and leaves a permanent Consecration where it stood, so tanks move only that boss to the room's edge [hazard][room][aimed]. Avenger's Shield debuffs must be removed fast (dispels, racials) [dispel]; Sacred Toll is periodic raid damage, everyone must be topped before the next [heal check]; Tyr's Wrath gives healing absorbs to players inside a circle (assigned soakers); Divine Toll shields are the main random killer and must be dodged; Execution Sentence marks players for soaks by four fixed groups of five [stack/soak][split]; Elekk Charge is baited by a few mobile ranged [aimed]. World first: Liquid, 52 pulls.

Epu: Frostfire patches, do not run into the opposite type; leftover Frostfire stacks wipe the raid on Mythic (Elemental Explosion) [resource][spread]. Nema: Mighty Thud soaks in a line, each knocking players into the next; Shell Spin baited by melee to the edge; Blink Nova [stack/soak][aimed]. Box spawn circles can one-shot [hazard].    - Intermission: three teams follow one of three slam patterns, identified by the first slam's marker [split][dance][puzzle].
   - Phase 3: across **four platforms that break**, reached by water bubbles: Serpent's Bite on three soaker teams; Volatile Purge makes a soaker release Caustic Waves in the direction of an arrow, aimed away from the raid (aimed at the raid it almost always wipes); Slithering Clutches crawl to venom and become Vipers if a wave hits them; Toxic Incubation, a spectral Ula'tek's venom stream at the Wretch, stacks Mother's Boon unless a player blocks it with their body and takes stacking Toxic Burn; on the last platform one group is expected to die [aimed][room][job]. Mythic adds enrage after 25-30 s (Boiling Venom, 100% haste and damage, Acidic Expulsion) [enrage]. Damage checks: ≤66% after the first damage amp, ≤44% after the second. Blizzard Watch: late in phase 3 the platform shrinks; Liquid's winning strategy had non-debuffed players jump into the venom pool, and Blizzard announced a nerf to that strategy on the race's final day — [Blizzard Watch, 3 Sept 2026](https://blizzardwatch.com/2026/09/03/world-first-ulatek-venomous-abyss-liquid-win/).

### Inferences
- The 100-energy cycle is the era's default skeleton: of the bosses whose pages describe a trigger, most cast their signature ability at 100 energy (Eranog, Terros, Kazzara, Rashok, Magmorax, Igira, Volcoross, Smolderon, Sikran, Ovi'nax, Ky'veza, Rik Reverb, Stix, Lockenstock, Plexus Sentinel, Salhadaar (Voidspire), Lightblinded Vanguard). Sszorak inverts this on Mythic: 100 energy is the failure state the raid must prevent. Design reading: players learn one rhythm per boss, and the designers vary what happens at the peak.
- Soft enrages are usually written as stacks that grow per cycle (Rising Mania, Harvest of Screams, Heating Up, Upgraded Bloodtech, Purging Lightning, Unstable Infusion, Colossal Clash) or as the third cast of the big ability never ending or ending the fight (Ky'veza's Eternal Night, Rik's Hype Fever, Salhadaar's enrage after the third Entropic Unraveling). Hard enrages written as room changes: Terros (floor full), Raszageth Stage Three (storm closes in), Nexus-King Salhadaar (spikes close in), Queen Ansurek (Cataclysmic Evolution), Midnight Falls (safe zones shrink, per CogConnected only).
- Mythic most often adds a "players touching each other" rule: Terros (Infused Fallout, touch to clear), Amalgamation Chamber (Volatile Shadowflame on contact), Lockenstock (opposite charges explode), Entombed Sentinels (Shifting Protovenom), Raszageth (Inversion of charges), Midnight Falls (Severance: touching the other dimension is death). Another common Mythic addition is a death penalty that spreads to the raid (Twin Fangs: a dead player drops globules).
- Splitting the raid into realms or dimensions recurs in each expansion: Sarkareth (Emptiness Between Stars), Bloodbound Horror (The Unseeming), Echo of Neltharion (hostile corrupted players), Chimaerus (two realms), Midnight Falls (three realms for the memory game, then two dimensions).
- Vehicles and special player actions are given by the encounter, not by classes: bikes (Vexie), rolling garbage balls (Stix), ducks (Council of Dreams), dragonriding (Tindral), flying mounts (Dimensius), Phase Blink through a beam (Plexus Sentinel), feeding the boss (Ulgrax), salvaging golem parts to disarm traps (Zskarn), mushrooms to jump a Blast Wave (Lost Explorers).

### Gaps
- Midnight: Method's guides describe Mythic; the Normal and Heroic versions of most Midnight bosses (what Mythic removes or adds) were not found, except where Method named a Mythic addition. Sporefall (Rotmire) rests on a search summary; Nymrissa Wavecaller rests on one boosting site.
- Pages that did not give phases or triggers: Dathea (no phase list), Gnarlroot (no difficulty marks), Rasha'nan (how the fight moves through the room), Fractillus (lane rule; the wall-off-a-lane failure is from memory). Enrage timers were mostly absent from the wiki pages (they show only Kurog's 10-minute Mythic enrage and Ulgrax's after-fourth-frenzy enrage).
- Damage numbers: the wiki pages show placeholders ("X", "Y") for most damage values, so none are given here.
- "The Unbinding of Kith'ix" (one search summary) and any raid after The Venomous Abyss (a guide title mentions "Eclipse") could not be confirmed.

## 2. Which mechanic type does each belong to?

### Takeaway
Every boss line above carries its type tags. The era uses every type in the brief; the most common are [stack/soak] (soak counts and split damage on almost every boss), [aimed] (players place puddles, beams, walls or knockbacks), [tank swap] (nearly universal through stacking vulnerability debuffs), [adds] and [enrage] (soft, by stacking). Least common: pure [LoS] (Echo of Neltharion portals, Sarkareth asteroids, Larodar's bramble barriers, Mug'Zee's gaol walls stopping spears) and [kite] of the boss itself.

### Cited Findings
- Tags rest on the per-boss sources in question 1. The closing table (end of this file) lists the clearest example per type.
- Private auras turned one more type into a designed mechanic: players must sort themselves by a debuff the interface shows but addons cannot read (Echo of Neltharion's Volcanic Heart; Smolderon's Seeking Inferno; Fyrakk's Flamebound/Shadowbound) — [Raider.IO, "Private Auras - Were They Successful?"](https://raider.io/news/613-private-auras-were-they-successful).

### Inferences
- Compared with era 1 (Classic to Wrath), the share of [aimed] and [puzzle] mechanics is far higher: the boss now hands players objects to place (beams, walls, puddles, balls, bombs) and rules to solve (tic-tac-toe, rune memory, orb counts), and fewer fights are decided by class tools alone.
- [job] mechanics now usually belong to the encounter (a debuff, a carried object, an extra button), not to a class: anyone marked carries the egg, the crystal, the bomb.

### Gaps
- No source classifies the era's mechanics statistically; the counts above are the researcher's reading of the catalogue.

## 3. Which fights changed the room itself, and how?

### Takeaway
About a third of the era's bosses change the room during the fight: floors that fill with permanent puddles (the most common), walls that rise and break, platforms the raid crosses or flies between, arenas that shrink as an enrage, and realms the raid is split into. The full list is at the end of this file.

### Cited Findings
- Floors that fill: Terros (Resonating Annihilation permanently covers part of the chamber; a full chamber is a wipe), Magmorax (Magma Puddles grow; players soak them down), Vorasius (Mythic Dark Goo permanent and growing), Ovi'nax (Sanguine Overflow), Rashok (Lava Vortices), Lightblinded Vanguard (a permanent Consecration per ultimate), Entombed Sentinels (Blood Venom in a pattern), Vexie (Oil Slicks up to 5 minutes), Rasha'nan (Acid Pools 1.5 min) — sources in question 1.
- Walls that rise and break: Echo of Neltharion (Twisted Earth walls broken by knocked-back players; later portals), Fractillus (crystal walls created by the tank's shockwaves, broken by knockbacks), Vorasius (Void Crystal walls broken by add deaths), Loom'ithar (tangle ring broken by the tank's strand).
- Platforms and moving: Sennarth (climb to the summit), Tindral (fly between platforms), Raszageth (central platform and prisons), Queen Ansurek (ascends to her chamber; conduits teleport players), Crown of the Cosmos (platform swaps by gateway), Ula'tek (four platforms that break, reached by water bubbles), Dimensius (fly between Remnants), Zaqali (two battlements), Twin Fangs (cross the room by gateway on Submerge), Chimaerus and Bloodbound Horror (realms).
- Room destroyed or opened: Sarkareth (he destroys the room in phase 2; the wall breaks open to the void in phase 3), Gallywix (from the House of Chrome down into the arena), Ulgrax/Bloodbound Horror (platform collapse cutscenes on the raid page).
- Arenas that shrink: Raszageth Stage Three (Raging Storm), Nexus-King Salhadaar (World in Twilight spikes), Ula'tek (platform shrinks late in phase 3 — [Blizzard Watch, 3 Sept 2026](https://blizzardwatch.com/2026/09/03/world-first-ulatek-venomous-abyss-liquid-win/)), Midnight Falls (safe zones shrink — CogConnected only, a weak source), Nymue (matrix lanes reshaped each cycle), Imperator Averzian (claimed tiles).

### Inferences
- The "permanent puddle" is the era's cheapest room change: it needs no new art, turns positioning into a resource spent over the whole fight, and gives a natural enrage when the floor runs out.

### Gaps
- Several wiki pages do not describe room transitions (Rasha'nan, Ulgrax, Tindral's platform layout), so the descriptions of those fights' room changes are thinner.

## 5. How do these raids scale with raid size?

### Takeaway
Normal and Heroic remain flexible (10-30 on The War Within's Nerub-ar Palace), Mythic fixed at 20 for the main raids, and Midnight adds a third model: one-boss raids (Sporefall, the Lair format) with a flexible Mythic for 15-25 players. The scaling that is documented is mostly health, damage and the number of targets per ability on Normal and Heroic (hotfixes cut damage most at 10 players); the soak thresholds written into abilities ("at least 4", "fewer than 5", "14 players") are fixed numbers, and they are tuned for Mythic's fixed 20.

### Cited Findings
- Nerub-ar Palace raid size 10-30 — [Warcraft Wiki](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Nerub-ar_Palace). The Venomous Abyss infobox lists "Player limit: 25" — [Warcraft Wiki](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Venomous_Abyss) (meaning not explained; see Gaps). Method's Mythic guides all assume 20 players (2 tanks, 4-5 healers, 13-15 DPS).
- Flexible Mythic 15-25: Sporefall's Rotmire (patch 12.0.7) — search summary of guides; The Tidebound Grotto Lair, the only Patch 12.1 raid boss with it — [Frostyboost](https://frostyboost.com/blog/nymrissa-wavecaller-guide-tidebound-grotto-lair-strategy-difficulties-loot).
- Counts that change with group size (from hotfix notes on Warcraft Wiki boss pages):
  - Broodtwister Ovi'nax (Sept 2024): smaller groups got lower add health, a longer Poison Burst cast, **fewer Sticky Web targets**, less Volatile Concoction damage.
  - Ky'veza (25 Sept 2024): lower health and Assassination/Queensbane/Starless Night/Regicide damage for smaller groups.
  - Queen Ansurek: health and damage reduced for smaller groups.
  - Liberation of Undermine (March 2025, most bosses): damage lowered in LFR/Normal/Heroic for smaller groups, largest cut at 10 players; Rik Reverb's Heroic **Lingering Voltage duration scales with group size**; Mug'Zee's health scaling factor was raised (LFR, Normal, Heroic).
  - Nexus-King Salhadaar (4 Sept 2025): **Banishment targets fewer players** in smaller groups; Behead and add health/damage reduced, most at 10 players, no change at 30 or on Mythic.
  - Dimensius: Living Mass, Voidwarden and Nullbinder health reduced for smaller groups on Normal/Heroic.
- Fixed thresholds written into abilities (do not scale): Volcoross Flood of the Firelands needs at least 4 players per glob; Smolderon's Brand at least 5; Amalgamation's Gloom Conflagration at least 5; Rasha'nan's Web Reave at least 4; Plexus Sentinel's Mythic Salvo at least 5; Loom'ithar's Mythic Writhing Wave at least 5; Ulgrax's Mythic Contest at least 6; Twin Fangs' Mythic Feast at least 4 per hit; Sszorak's Mythic Serpent's Fury 14 players; Raszageth's prisons and Kurog's altars set group counts by room layout — question 1 sources.
- "Several players" targets (count unstated on the pages) are the norm: Dread Rifts (Kazzara: Extra targets are added for each shattered armor piece), Phase Blades, Assassination, Molten Spittle, Flamerift.
- Story and Raid Finder simplifications: Story Mode for Queen Ansurek (17 Sept 2024, no loot) — [Warcraft Wiki](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Nerub-ar_Palace); Story Mode for The Voidspire and March on Quel'Danas ([Blizzard](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24223664)) and The Venomous Abyss, where a patch note says dungeon followers now properly lead players when Dungeon Assistance is on (patch note, 26 Aug 2026 — [Warcraft Wiki](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Venomous_Abyss)); Council of Dreams' Dream Team (shared damage) exists only in Raid Finder; Sennarth's Gossamer Burst and Repelling Burst web players only on LFR and Normal; Ulgrax's and Sennarth's harsher versions are Heroic/Mythic — question 1 sources.

### Inferences
- The designers scale a flexible raid through numbers (health, damage, target count, durations) and keep the rules the same; the one rule that scales by count in the documents is "how many players are targeted". Fixed soak thresholds (4, 5, 6, 14) assume a full raid and become relatively harder at 10 players, which may be why hotfixes repeatedly cut damage at 10.
- The 15-25 flexible Mythic on one-boss raids is new in Midnight and is the closest model in WoW to a variable-size top difficulty.

### Gaps
- What the Venomous Abyss "Player limit: 25" means (a lower Normal/Heroic cap than 30, or a wiki error) was not confirmed.
- No source lists which abilities scale their target count with players across a whole raid; the examples are from hotfix notes only.

## 6. What new design tools did these expansions bring, and what did the designers say?

### Takeaway
Three tools stand out: private auras (patch 10.1, 2023), debuffs that addons cannot read, used to stop addons assigning players automatically; Story Mode (The War Within, 2024), a difficulty for players who want the story (the only evidence of NPC followers in it is a Venomous Abyss patch note about dungeon followers and Dungeon Assistance); and Midnight's "addon disarmament" (announced 13 Nov 2025), which removes the combat advantage of boss and aura addons and promises built-in warnings and clearer, longer telegraphs instead. Encounter-specific buttons and vehicles (bikes, balls, ducks, flight, Phase Blink) are everywhere, but no source on an "extra action button" policy was found.

### Cited Findings
- Private auras: introduced in patch 10.1 with Aberrus, used sparingly (three abilities); the first noticed was Echo of Neltharion's Volcanic Heart (five players exploding after a timer); Blizzard later shrank its radius; Amirdrassil flagged 20 abilities; the goal was to "force players to actively make a decision themselves"; players answered with macros that report their own debuff to an aura addon; Blizzard removed the tag from Fyrakk's Flame/Shadow debuffs on 1 Feb 2024 — [Raider.IO, "Private Auras - Were They Successful?"](https://raider.io/news/613-private-auras-were-they-successful); [Raider.IO, "An inside look at private auras"](https://raider.io/news/570-an-inside-look-at-private-auras) (search summary); [mein-mmo](https://mein-mmo.de/en/addons-in-wow-are-too-powerful-now-blizzard-is-restricting-them,963581) (search summary).
- Midnight addon disarmament: Blizzard forum post "Combat Philosophy and Addon Disarmament in Midnight" by Game Director Ion Hazzikostas, 13 Nov 2025: "Addons should no longer offer a competitive advantage in WoW combat"; they "should not be able to make a player more likely to succeed in combat against an encounter or another player"; the alternative was "signing up for a couple more years of designing our content around powerful addons"; "we have tried to take a surgical approach"; Blizzard is "making new access points available to addon developers" — [Blizzard forums, 13 Nov 2025](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/combat-philosophy-and-addon-disarmament-in-midnight/2199207) (quotes as reproduced in replies). Hazzikostas: Blizzard had "inevitably had to design AROUND them for the past several expansions"; addons left players who skip them "at a clear disadvantage, making WoW less approachable" — [Attractmo, 14 Nov 2025](https://attractmo.de/game-design/wow-director-addresses-major-combat-addon-overhaul-in-midnight).
- Stated encounter direction for Midnight (search summary of Icy Veins interviews, not fetched): bosses with "clear telegraphs ahead of time, and sufficient time to react", group mechanics with slightly longer reaction windows, built-in boss warnings, cooldown tracking, audio alerts and damage meters in the base UI, and challenge at the top tiers "about the same" — [Icy Veins, "Your Raid Nights Are About to Feel Very Different in Midnight"](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow/news/your-raid-nights-are-about-to-feel-very-different-in-midnight/); [Icy Veins interview](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow/news/blizzard-confirms-major-midnight-changes-in-exclusive-icy-veins-interview/). PC Gamer headline: the director "admits it's too hard without combat mods" and "We're not looking to turn all our raid encounters into giant puzzles for people to solve" — [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/world-of-warcraft/world-of-warcraft-director-admits-its-too-hard-without-combat-mods-and-explains-how-bosses-will-change-in-midnight-were-not-looking-to-turn-all-our-raid-encounters-into-giant-puzzles-for-people-to-solve/) (headline only).
- In practice, Method's Midnight Mythic guides (May-Nov 2026) still recommend raid tools for assignments ("NSRT", Northern Sky Raid Tools, and "M33kAuras") on Midnight Falls, Ula'tek and Salhadaar — Method guides cited in question 1. A news item titled "lightblinded vanguard dispel weakaura hotfix" (Seramate, 23 May 2026) suggests Blizzard hotfixed an aura-based answer on that boss (title only, unverified).
- Story Mode: Nerub-ar Palace's Ansurek (Sept 2024, questline entry, no loot); Midnight's Voidspire and March on Quel'Danas; The Venomous Abyss with dungeon followers — sources in question 5.
- Encounter-given abilities: Phase Blink through the Atomizer (Plexus Sentinel), Soaring Reshii flight (Dimensius), Fallen Feathers for dragonriding (Tindral), bikes (Vexie), Rolling Rubbish (Stix), Polymorph Bomb with Quack and Preen (Council of Dreams), Salvage Parts and Deactivate Trap (Zskarn), Seeds of Amirdrassil (Fyrakk), Dawn Crystals (Midnight Falls), eggs (Ula'tek) — question 1 sources.
- New raid formats in Midnight: three raids released together in one season (6 + 1 + 2 bosses), a bonus one-boss raid mid-season (Sporefall), and the one-boss "Lair" with flexible Mythic (The Tidebound Grotto) — question 1 sources.

### Inferences
- Private auras and Midnight's disarmament are two steps of one policy: first hide single debuffs from addons, then remove the combat advantage of addons altogether. Both push assignment work back onto the encounter's own interface and onto raid leaders, which the Raider.IO analyst argued widened the gap between top guilds and others.
- The Midnight Mythic fights documented here (memory game across realms, ghosts visible only to their target, hidden orb counts) still demand heavy coordination; the "simpler mechanics" stance shows more clearly in the stated plans than in the Mythic fights found.

### Gaps
- No primary Blizzard text on telegraph timing or the built-in boss warnings was fetched (only search summaries). No interview with the encounter team about specific Dragonflight or War Within fights was found within the call budget.
- No source on an "extra action button" policy or count.
