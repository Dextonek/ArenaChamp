# WoW raid boss mechanics, era 1: Classic, The Burning Crusade, Wrath of the Lich King

How to read these notes
- Every boss line names its mechanics, what the raid must do, and what failure costs. Mechanic types are tagged inline in square brackets, e.g. [tank swap], [spread], [stack/soak], [adds], [interrupt], [dispel], [kite], [LoS] (line of sight), [mind control], [dance] (positional pattern), [hazard] (ground or room hazard), [gimmick] (vehicle or special-action fight), [council] (several bosses at once), [phase], [enrage], [resource] (debuff or resource management), [split] (raid divided into groups or realms), [job] (a named player is assigned a task), [threat] (aggro rules are the mechanic), [dps race].
- Source rule: each raid heading names its sources, and a boss line with its own link rests on that page. The named abilities, numbers, timers and quoted phrases come from those pages. The short connecting detail inside a line (who usually does a job, how a raid usually stands) follows the page where it said so and common practice where it did not. Anything marked "(unverified)" was not confirmed on any fetched page and comes from the researcher's memory; treat it as a lead, not a fact.
- Version caveat: the Classic and TBC raid guides used (Warcraft Tavern) were written for the Classic re-releases (2019 and 2021), which reproduce the last patch state of each raid, not its launch state. Warcraft Wiki boss pages were used for the original numbers and patch changes where fetched.
- Outdoor world bosses (Classic: Azuregos, Lord Kazzak, the four Emerald Dragons; TBC: Doom Lord Kazzak, Doomwalker) are not raid instances and are not covered. The TBC overview only says of them "You can face these World Bosses with a group of up to 40 players" — [Warcraft Tavern, TBC raids overview](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/tbc/guides/raids/).

## 1. For each boss: what are its defining mechanics?

### Takeaway
The three eras hold 25 raid instances and about 170 encounters (59 in Classic, 51 in TBC, 57 in Wrath with the Ruby Sanctum's three mini-bosses, counting the reused Naxxramas and Onyxia once per era), all catalogued below with two to five defining mechanics each. Classic fights are mostly one strong rule plus class tools and resistance gear; TBC fights stack several overlapping jobs for assigned specialists; Wrath fights are movement patterns for everyone, offered in 10- and 25-player sizes with optional harder versions.

### Cited Findings

#### CLASSIC (2004-2006)

Raid list and sizes: Onyxia's Lair 40, Molten Core 40 (10 bosses), Blackwing Lair 40 (8), Zul'Gurub 20 (10 encounters, of which the Edge of Madness rotates 4 bosses), Ruins of Ahn'Qiraj 20 (6), Temple of Ahn'Qiraj 40 (9), Naxxramas 40 (15). "There are no difficulty levels or variable raid sizes in Vanilla WoW." — [Warcraft Tavern, Classic raids overview](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/wow-classic/guides/raids/)

##### Molten Core (40) — source: [Warcraft Tavern MC guide](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/wow-classic/guides/mc/)
- **Lucifron** — Raid-wide curse (doubles the cost of spells and abilities) and a raid-wide magic debuff (about 2k damage after 10 s): two different dispel types to clear at once [dispel]. Two guards mind-control players; priests dispel it [mind control][adds].
- **Magmadar** — AoE fear about every 30 s; the tank needs fear protection (fear ward, tremor totem, stance dance) [dispel]. Frenzy about every 20 s must be removed with a hunter's Tranquilizing Shot [job]. Fire patches on the ground [hazard].
- **Gehennas** — Gehennas' Curse cuts healing received by 75%; decurse the tank first [dispel]. Rain of Fire circles to leave [hazard]. Two guards with a stun and a stacking Sunder Armor, each pulled to its own off-tank [adds].
- **Garr** — A pack of Firesworn adds (eight: unverified on the fetched page): each explodes on death (about 2k in 50 ft), Garr grows stronger with each one killed, and an add dragged too far from him enrages (Separation Anxiety). Warlocks banish several while the rest are tanked [adds][job]. Garr strips one buff from nearby players (Antimagic Pulse).
- **Baron Geddon** — Living Bomb: one player becomes a bomb that explodes after 8 s for 3k+ and throws nearby players in the air; that player runs out of the raid [spread][job]. Inferno: pulsing, ramping AoE around the boss; melee run out [hazard]. Ignite Mana burns mana until dispelled [dispel]. At 2% he blows himself up; all run away.
- **Shazzrah** — Blinks to a random player about every 45 s and wipes threat, then casts point-blank Arcane Explosion; the raid spreads so few are near where he lands [threat][spread]. Curse doubling magic damage taken (decurse) and a self-buff halving magic damage to him (purge) [dispel].
- **Sulfuron Harbinger** — Boss plus four Flamewaker Priests that heal each other and the boss; each priest gets its own tank and they are pulled apart, interrupted and killed one at a time [adds][interrupt][council].
- **Golemagg the Incinerator** — Boss plus two Core Ragers that cannot be killed before him (they heal below 50% and reset the fight if pulled too far). Two off-tank teams hold the dogs for the whole fight [adds][job]. Magma Splash stacks an armour-reducing fire DoT on melee attackers (up to 10 stacks) [resource]. At 10% he enrages (Earthquake) [enrage].
- **Majordomo Executus** — Council of eight adds (four healers, four elites); the boss is not killed, he surrenders when the adds die. Healers are polymorphed while elites die one by one; after four adds are dead the rest become immune to polymorph [adds][council][job]. He shields all adds alternately with a melee-reflect shield and a spell-reflect shield, so damage dealers must stop on the wrong shield [resource]. Teleports the tank into the fire pit [hazard].
- **Ragnaros** — Stationary boss in lava. Wrath of Ragnaros: melee knockback that cuts threat; Hammer of Ragnaros: hits a mana user and knocks back everyone near them, so casters stand spread [spread][threat]. If nobody is in melee range he Magma Blasts the raid [job]. After 3 minutes he submerges for 90 s and eight Sons of Flame (mana-burning adds) must be killed or banished; banishing all eight brings him back at once [phase][adds]. The fight is a race to kill him before or just after the second submerge [dps race].

##### Onyxia's Lair (40) — source: [Warcraft Tavern Onyxia guide](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/wow-classic/guides/onyxias-lair/)
- **Onyxia** — Three phases by health [phase]. P1 (100-65%): a dragon with a frontal Flame Breath and Cleave, a rear Tail Sweep that can throw players into the whelp eggs, and a tank knockback that cuts threat by 25%; the raid stands on her flanks only [dance][threat]. P2 (65-40%): she flies; whelp waves spawn from the side caves and two assigned groups AoE them [adds][split]; she fireballs the highest-threat player with splash, so the raid spreads [spread]; Deep Breath: an emote, then she flies a straight line across the room breathing fire, and everyone in the lane must step sideways or die [dance]. P3 (40-0%): she lands with threat wiped; Bellowing Roar fears the whole raid every 10-30 s while lava cracks in the floor erupt under fleeing players [hazard][threat]. She is immune to taunt, so damage dealers must hold back while the tank rebuilds threat.

##### Blackwing Lair (40) — source: [Warcraft Tavern BWL guide](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/wow-classic/guides/bwl/)
- **Razorgore the Untamed** — Phase 1 is an objective, not a boss kill: one player channels the Orb of Domination to mind-control Razorgore and uses him to destroy every egg in the room, while the raid, split into four corner groups, kills mages and kites the endless legionnaires and dragonkin [mind control][job][kite][split]. When the last egg breaks he heals to full and becomes a normal fight; Conflagration burns and disorients the tank and drops their threat, so two tanks stand 90 degrees apart [tank swap].
- **Vaelastrasz the Corrupt** — Essence of the Red gives the raid unlimited mana, rage and energy for 3 minutes, after which healers run dry: a hard damage race [dps race][resource]. Burning Adrenaline every 15 s: the target does +100% damage and dies after 20 s; they run out of the raid before exploding. Every third cast hits the main tank, so the fight consumes tanks in sequence (3-4 tank deaths expected, five warriors queued in threat order) [tank swap][job][spread].
- **Broodlord Lashlayer** — A gauntlet before the boss: suppression devices slow everything by 80% and must be disarmed by rogues over and over while whelps respawn [hazard][job]. Boss: Mortal Strike on the tank (5k+ and a healing debuff), Knock Away halves the tank's threat, Blast Wave around him; several tanks keep threat in order [threat][tank swap].
- **Firemaw** — Flame Buffet: a stacking fire-vulnerability debuff on everyone in his line of sight; players step behind a wall to let stacks expire at 5-7 [LoS][resource]. Wing Buffet knocks back and cuts threat (an off-tank taunts to take it); Shadow Flame is a frontal breath whose burning after-effect is prevented only by wearing the Onyxia Scale Cloak, so tanks wear it [tank swap].
- **Ebonroc** — Shadow of Ebonroc: for 8 s every hit on the cursed tank heals the boss for 25,000; another tank must taunt at once [tank swap].
- **Flamegor** — Frenzy with a raid-wide Fire Nova every other second until a hunter removes it with Tranquilizing Shot [job][dispel].
- **Chromaggus** — Two of five breath attacks, fixed per raid ID for the week (fire, acid armour-melt, frost slow, stacking burn, and Time Lapse which stuns, halves health and wipes threat); the raid hides behind a wall for each breath [LoS]. Five Brood Afflictions land on players, each a different dispel type (the bronze one needs a looted item, Hourglass Sand); a player holding all five turns into a hostile drakonid [dispel][resource]. Frenzy needs Tranquilizing Shot [job]. Shimmer changes which one magic school hurts him. Enrages at 20% [enrage].
- **Nefarian** — P1: the boss is untouchable and mind-controls and fears players while 42 drakonids of two random colours (fixed per week) pour from two doors; the raid splits to hold both doors [adds][split][mind control]. P2: he lands with a raid-wide Shadow Flame (Onyxia Scale Cloak or die), then makes class calls: every member of one class is punished at once (warriors forced into Berserker Stance, priests' heals turn into damage, mages polymorph allies, hunters' ranged weapons break, rogues are teleported in front of him, and so on) [resource][job]. AoE fear and Veil of Shadow (healing-reduction curse on the tank) [dispel]. P3 at 20%: all dead drakonids rise as Bone Constructs and must be AoE'd at once [phase][adds].

##### Zul'Gurub (20) — source: [Warcraft Tavern ZG guide](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/wow-classic/guides/zg/)
- **High Priestess Jeklik (bat)** — P1: charges and silences players near the tank; bat swarms every minute [adds]. P2 at 50%: a Great Heal (about 25% of her health) and a chained Mind Flay that must be interrupted [interrupt]; bomber bats drop fire patches the raid must leave [hazard].
- **High Priest Venoxis (snake)** — Four snake adds first [adds]. In troll form he is fought at range (the guide has the raid drain his mana); at 50% he becomes a snake and leaves spreading poison clouds; the tank drags him out of them and melee stay away [phase][hazard][kite].
- **High Priestess Mar'li (spider)** — Spawns spiders that grow stronger the longer they live; kill at once [adds]. Enveloping Webs roots everyone in melee and she runs for the ranged group, where an off-tank waits [tank swap]. Drain Life heals her unless interrupted [interrupt].
- **High Priest Thekal (tiger)** — P1 is a three-boss council: Thekal and Zealots Lor'Khan and Zath must die within about 10 s of each other or the living ones resurrect the dead; Lor'Khan's heal must be interrupted [council][interrupt]. P2: Thekal returns as a tiger, summons tiger adds, and knocks the tank away (Force Punch) [phase][adds].
- **High Priestess Arlokk (panther)** — She marks one player; a stream of weak panthers hunts the marked player. She vanishes and reappears in panther form with a whirlwind cleave; panthers are held with fears and AoE'd while she is gone [adds][kite][phase].
- **Bloodlord Mandokir (optional)** — Threatening Gaze: he watches one random player, who must stop every action until the gaze ends or be charged and killed [job][resource]. He charges players who stand alone, so the raid stays grouped [stack/soak]. His raptor Ohgan stacks Sunder on its tank [tank swap]; when Ohgan dies Mandokir enrages. Dead players can be revived by spirits, but every third player death makes him gain a level (more damage, less damage taken) [enrage].
- **Edge of Madness (optional; one of four bosses is available per lockout, in rotation)** — *Gri'lek*: periodically grows, drops threat and fixates a random player who must run; roots must be dispelled so the runner is not caught [kite][dispel]. *Hazza'rah*: mass sleep in melee range, chain mana burn, and nightmare illusions with huge damage and little health that ranged must kill at once [adds]. *Renataki*: vanishes and ambushes random players; AoE reveals him [threat]. *Wushoolay*: lightning clouds on the ground to leave [hazard].
- **Gahz'ranka (optional, summoned by fishing)** — Massive Geyser throws the whole melee group into the air and resets threat; fought in water to cancel the fall damage [hazard][threat].
- **Jin'do the Hexxer (optional)** — A target-priority fight [adds][job]: Shades of Jin'do are invisible except to players carrying his curse (Delusions), which therefore must not be removed [resource]; Brain Wash Totems mind-control a player until the totem dies [mind control]; Powerful Healing Wards heal him; he teleports a random player into a pit of skeletons that mages must AoE; Hex on the tank must be dispelled or he turns on the next player [dispel]. He can only be damaged safely when no shade or totem is up.
- **Hakkar the Soulflayer** — Blood Siphon every 90 s: stuns the whole raid and drains life to heal himself. The answer is a deliberate self-poisoning: a Son of Hakkar is kept crowd-controlled, killed just before each siphon, and the raid stands in its poison cloud to gain Poisonous Blood, which turns the siphon into damage on the boss [resource][job][adds]. Corrupted Blood: a DoT that jumps to players nearby, so ranged spread [spread]. Cause Insanity mind-controls the tank; polymorph them while an off-tank takes over [mind control][tank swap]. Each of the five animal priests left alive gives Hakkar that priest's ability, an early form of chosen difficulty [resource].

##### Ruins of Ahn'Qiraj (20) — source: [Warcraft Tavern AQ20 guide](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/wow-classic/guides/ruins-of-ahnqiraj-aq20/)
- **Kurinnaxx** — Mortal Wound stacks a healing-reduction debuff on the tank; swap at about 5 stacks [tank swap]. Sand Traps appear under players and burst after a moment (damage, silence, -75% hit chance for 20 s) [hazard]. Enrages at 30% [enrage].
- **General Rajaxx** — A gauntlet of seven waves, each led by a captain, fought beside friendly NPCs (Lieutenant General Andorov and four elites) who must be healed [adds][job]. The general himself: Thundercrash knocks the raid back and removes 40-50% of everyone's current health; he disarms the tank.
- **Moam (optional)** — A mana clock: he drains the raid's mana, and at full mana casts Arcane Eruption (a wipe). Hunters, priests and warlocks are assigned to drain him [resource][job]. After 90 s he turns to stone for 90 s and three Mana Fiends spawn (two banished, one killed) [phase][adds].
- **Buru the Gorger (optional)** — He fixates a random player and cannot be hurt until 20%; the chased player runs him over an egg and the raid detonates it under him. Each egg explosion damages him and hatches an add [kite][job][adds]. At 20% his armour breaks and it becomes a race against a growing raid-wide DoT (Creeping Plague) [phase][dps race].
- **Ayamiss the Hunter (optional)** — P1: she hovers out of melee reach, tanked by a ranged player on threat, with a stacking poison on her target [job][tank swap]. She pins a random player on the altar (Paralyze) and larvae crawl toward them; the larvae must die before they arrive or the player dies and a stronger add spawns [adds]. At 70% she lands [phase].
- **Ossirian the Unscarred** — He has a permanent buff (Strength of Ossirian) that triples his damage. It is switched off for 45 s only by dragging him onto a crystal; a mounted scout finds the next crystal and the tank kites him from crystal to crystal for the whole fight [kite][job][resource]. Each crystal also makes him weak to one magic school. Tornadoes roam the room [hazard]; he is immune to taunt [threat].

##### Temple of Ahn'Qiraj (40) — source: [Warcraft Tavern AQ40 guide](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/wow-classic/guides/temple-of-ahnqiraj-aq40/)
- **The Prophet Skeram** — At 75, 50 and 25% he splits into himself plus two clones [adds][phase]. He teleports between three platforms and Earth Shocks freely if nobody is in melee range, so players are pre-placed on each platform [job][split]. True Fulfillment mind-controls a player (triple damage, instant casts); polymorph them [mind control]. He casts Arcane Explosion over and over whenever five or more players are next to him, so at most four stand in melee, and the clones' casts are interrupted [stack/soak][interrupt].
- **Bug Trio: Lord Kri, Princess Yauj, Vem (optional)** — A council where the kill order is the difficulty choice and decides the loot: Kri leaves a lethal poison cloud on death, Yauj spawns a brood on death, Vem's death enrages the others; the last one alive absorbs the dead and enrages [council][enrage]. Yauj fears and casts Great Heal [interrupt]; Kri's Toxic Volley must be cleansed [dispel]; Vem charges and knocks back.
- **Battleguard Sartura** — She and three Royal Guards Whirlwind constantly and each Whirlwind wipes threat, so they wander through the raid; the four are tanked far apart and stunned on a rotation [adds][threat][spread]. Hard enrage at 10 minutes [enrage].
- **Fankriss the Unyielding** — Mortal Wound on the tank, -10% healing per stack; swap at 3-5 [tank swap]. Worm adds (Spawn of Fankriss) appear at three tunnels and go berserk if not killed in seconds, one-shotting players [adds].
- **Viscidus (optional)** — A pure gimmick [gimmick]: the slime is immune to finishing damage until it is frozen by a set number of frost hits (counted in hits, not damage), then shattered by a set number of melee hits in a short window (again counted in hits). It bursts into 20 globs that crawl back to the centre; every glob killed removes 6% of his health [adds]. Poison volley and clouds throughout.
- **Princess Huhuran** — A resistance and soak check [stack/soak]: below 30% she is berserk and fires a Poison Volley every 3 s at the 15 nearest players, so 15 chosen players in nature-resistance gear stand closest and absorb it [job][enrage]. Wyvern Sting sleeps players and does heavy damage if dispelled (do not dispel) [dispel]. Frenzy needs Tranquilizing Shot.
- **The Twin Emperors (Vek'lor and Vek'nilash)** — Two bosses with a shared health pool; Vek'nilash is immune to magic and Vek'lor to physical damage, so melee and casters each have their own boss [council][split]. About every 35 s the twins swap places and wipe threat, so the whole damage group crosses the room; a warlock tanks the caster twin with shadow spells [job][tank swap]. If the twins come within 60 yd they heal each other. Bugs in the room are exploded or mutated [adds]. Hard enrage at 15 minutes [enrage].
- **Ouro (optional)** — A sand worm that submerges whenever no one is in melee range (or after 3 minutes), then travels under the raid as quake mounds [hazard][phase]. Sweep knocks tanks away and Sand Blast is a frontal stun cone that wipes threat [tank swap][threat]. At 20% he goes berserk and stays up [enrage].
- **C'Thun** — P1 (the Eye): Eye Beam chains between players within 10 yd and doubles with each jump, so the raid is arranged in fixed spread positions before the pull; walking into the room carelessly wipes the raid [spread][dance]. Dark Glare: a lethal beam that sweeps slowly through 180 degrees; the raid rotates around the room ahead of it [dance]. Tentacles spawn all the time [adds]. P2 (the body): invulnerable. Players are swallowed into the stomach, where they must kill two Flesh Tentacles while a stacking acid debuff forces them to leave in time; killing both makes C'Thun vulnerable for 45 s [split][resource][phase]. Giant Claw and Giant Eye tentacles outside must be tanked and killed meanwhile [adds].

##### Naxxramas (original, 40) — source: [Warcraft Tavern Classic Naxxramas guide](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/wow-classic/guides/naxxramas/)
*Arachnid Quarter*
- **Anub'Rekhan** — Locust Swarm about every 90 s: a 30 yd silencing, stacking DoT aura around the boss for its duration; the tank kites him around the edge of the room while the raid stays away [kite]. Impale throws a line of players into the air [spread]. Crypt Guards and corpse scarabs (a scarab swarm bursts from every dead player and guard) [adds].
- **Grand Widow Faerlina** — She enrages every minute. The enrage is removed only by sacrificing one of her own Worshippers: a priest mind-controls a Worshipper and uses its Widow's Embrace on her. There are four Worshippers, so the fight is on a fixed budget [mind control][job][resource][enrage]. Rain of Fire to leave [hazard].
- **Maexxna** — Web Wrap every 40 s: three players are thrown onto the wall in cocoons and die unless an assigned ranged team frees them [job]. Web Spray every 40 s: the whole raid is stunned for 8 s while she keeps hitting the tank, so healers must pre-load heals before it [resource]. Spiderlings must die before each spray [adds]. Necrotic Poison (-90% healing) must be cleansed at once [dispel]. Enrage at 30% [enrage].

*Plague Quarter*
- **Noth the Plaguebringer** — Curse of the Plaguebringer on the raid: harmless by itself, but if it is not removed in time it erupts into Wrath of the Plaguebringer on everyone, "an instant wipe" [dispel]. Blinks and wipes threat [threat]. On a timer he teleports to a balcony and waves of skeletons must be handled, each visit longer than the last [phase][adds].
- **Heigan the Unclean** — The floor is divided into four zones that erupt in sequence, leaving one safe zone at a time. Phase 1 (90 s): slow sequence, boss tanked on the move, casters on his platform out of mana-burn range. Phase 2 (45 s): he teleports to the platform and the eruptions come fast; the whole raid walks back and forth in step, the "dance". Standing in an eruption is near-fatal [dance][hazard]. Decrepit Fever disease to cleanse [dispel].
- **Loatheb** — A healing-denial fight: Corrupted Mind lets each healer cast one healing spell per minute, so healers heal in a called rotation [resource][job]. Spores spawn every 13 s; five players at a time kill one to gain Fungal Bloom (+50% crit, no threat), taken in turn by groups [job]. Inevitable Doom hits the raid at shrinking intervals (every 30 s, then every 15 s after 5 minutes): a soft enrage [enrage].

*Construct Quarter*
- **Patchwerk** — No movement at all. Hateful Strike hits, among the players in melee range, the one with the most health other than the main tank; off-tanks keep their health high to soak it instead of melee damage dealers [stack/soak][job]. Berserk at 7 minutes: a gear check for tanks, healers and damage [dps race][enrage].
- **Grobbulus** — Mutating Injection: after 10 s the injected player explodes and leaves a poison cloud, so they run to the room's edge to drop it [spread][hazard][job]. The boss also leaves clouds under himself, so the tank kites him steadily around the room [kite]. Slime Spray spawns one slime per player hit [adds].
- **Gluth** — Zombie Chow spawn every 10 s and walk to Gluth, who eats any that reach him to heal; assigned kiters hold them at the far end with slows [kite][job]. Decimate every 105 s sets everything in the room (players and zombies) to 5% health; the raid then has seconds to kill all zombies as they run to him [adds]. Mortal Wound on tanks [tank swap]; Frenzy needs Tranquilizing Shot; berserk after the third Decimate [enrage].
- **Thaddius** — P1: two mini-bosses, Stalagg and Feugen, on separate platforms with half the raid each; they must die within seconds of each other or revive, and their tanks are thrown across to the other platform every 20 s [split][council][tank swap]. P2: Polarity Shift every 30 s gives every player a positive or negative charge. Players with unlike charges standing near each other damage each other heavily; players with like charges standing together gain a stacking damage bonus. So the raid stands in two camps and everyone whose charge flipped runs through the boss to the other camp within about 5 s [resource][dance][stack/soak]. Enrage 5 minutes into P2 [enrage].

*Military Quarter*
- **Instructor Razuvious** — His melee kills any player tank. Priests mind-control his four Deathknight Understudies and tank him with their Taunt and Shield Wall, handing over as each control breaks [mind control][job][tank swap]. Disrupting Shout every 25 s burns mana and deals double that as damage, so casters stay out of sight or range [LoS].
- **Gothik the Harvester** — The room is split by a gate into a living side and a dead side, and the raid splits to match [split]. For 4.5 minutes waves of adds attack the living side; every add killed there rises as a spectral version on the dead side [adds]. Then Gothik comes down and teleports between sides; at 40% the gate opens [phase].
- **The Four Horsemen** — Four bosses fought at once in the four corners (Mograine, Thane Korth'azz, Lady Blaumeux, Sir Zeliek) [council]. Each puts a stacking Mark on everyone within range every 12 s; damage rises steeply per stack and is lethal around 7, and marks take 75 s to expire. So tanks hold a boss for about 3 marks and then hand over, and the damage and healing groups rotate between corners with them. The original needed eight tanks, two per Horseman, and a resisted taunt (16% chance) was a frequent cause of wipes; "generally regarded as one of the most difficult" fights of original WoW — [Warcraft Wiki: Four Horsemen (Classic)](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Four_Horsemen_(Classic)) [tank swap][dance][job][resource]. Each boss has its own area attack: Meteor (damage split among those hit, so stack), Void Zone (leave), Holy Wrath (chains, so spread) [stack/soak][spread][hazard]. A boss that dies early leaves a spirit that keeps marking. Enrage at 100 marks [enrage].

*Frostwyrm Lair*
- **Sapphiron** — Frost Aura hits the whole raid constantly: a healing and frost-resistance check [resource]. Ground phase: Life Drain curse to remove [dispel], a moving Blizzard to leave [hazard]. Air phase: he lifts off and Icebolts five players in turn, encasing each in an ice block, then breathes a Frost Breath that kills everyone not hiding behind a block; the raid must find and stand behind a frozen player [LoS][phase][dance].
- **Kel'Thuzad** — P1 (about 5 minutes): the boss is untouchable while three add types walk in from alcoves: skeletons that explode on contact (ranged kill them), abominations (tanked), banshees that knock back (killed at range) [adds]. P2: the raid spreads [spread]. Frostbolt on the tank must be interrupted every time [interrupt]. Frost Blast freezes a player and chains to anyone within 10 yd; frozen players die in 5 s unless healed hard. Shadow Fissure kills whoever stands in it after a delay [hazard]. Mana Detonation turns a mana user into a bomb. Chains of Kel'Thuzad mind-controls the tank and four others [mind control]. P3 at 40%: five Guardians of Icecrown enter; three are shackled by priests and two tanked, and each target switch makes a Guardian stronger [adds][job][phase].

#### THE BURNING CRUSADE (2007-2008)

Raid list and sizes: Karazhan 10 (11 encounters plus optional ones), Gruul's Lair 25 (2), Magtheridon's Lair 25 (1), Serpentshrine Cavern 25 (6), Tempest Keep: The Eye 25 (4), Battle for Mount Hyjal 25 (5), Black Temple 25 (9), Zul'Aman 10 (6), Sunwell Plateau 25 (6). TBC "reduced the standard raid size from the massive 40-player endeavors of WoW Classic"; "two new 10-player raids made their way into the game with Karazhan and Zul'Aman"; "No new difficulty modes were added to the game for raids in TBC." — [Warcraft Tavern, TBC raids overview](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/tbc/guides/raids/)

##### Karazhan (10) — sources: [Warcraft Tavern Karazhan guide](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/tbc/guides/karazhan/) (role notes for every boss); Warcraft Wiki pages linked per boss
- **Servants' Quarters (optional; once most of the trash is dead, one of three animal bosses spawns at random)** — *Hyakiss the Lurker* (spider): Acidic Fang stacks armour loss and poison on the tank (taunt at 2-3 stacks) and a 10 s web stun on the tank, so a second tank stays next on threat [tank swap]. *Rokad the Ravager* (hound): a bleed on the tank; tank and heal. *Shadikith the Glider* (bat): charges the farthest player, dives with a fear, and a Sonic Burst silence; an off-tank taunts the charge away [threat] — [Warcraft Wiki: Servants' Quarters](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Servant's_Quarters), [Hyakiss](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Hyakiss_the_Lurker), [Rokad](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Rokad_the_Ravager), [Shadikith](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Shadikith_the_Glider)
- **Attumen the Huntsman** — Starts as the horse Midnight; Attumen joins, and at low health mounts Midnight to become one boss with threat reset; damage stops while tanks rebuild threat [phase][threat]. He charges ranged players, and curses with Intangible Presence (hit chance debuff, decurse) [dispel].
- **Moroes** — The boss plus four dinner guests; two are crowd-controlled and the others killed in order [adds][council]. Garrote: a long bleed on random players that healers must keep up with; Blind and Gouge on tanks force a second tank to be next on threat [tank swap]. Enrages at 30% [enrage].
- **Maiden of Virtue** — Repentance incapacitates the whole raid for several seconds on a timer; the tank keeps taking hits, so healing must be pre-loaded and one healer placed so they are freed first [resource]. Holy Fire DoT to dispel [dispel]; her chained holy damage means the ranged stand spread around the room [spread].
- **Opera Event (one of three plays, random per week)** — *Big Bad Wolf*: turns a random player into Little Red Riding Hood and chases only them; that player runs laps round the room until it fades [kite][job]. *Romulo and Julianne*: fought one after the other, then both revive and must be killed at the same time; Julianne's heal (Eternal Affection) must be interrupted and Romulo's buff (Daring) removed [council][interrupt][dispel]. *Wizard of Oz*: four characters with different weaknesses in a kill order (Dorothee, her dog Tito, Roar, Strawman, Tinhead), then the Crone with moving cyclones [council][hazard].
- **The Curator** — Spawns Astral Flare adds continuously that must be killed as they come [adds]; Hateful Bolt hits a non-tank, so an off-tank is kept as its target [stack/soak]. Periodically he Evocates: he stands still and takes extra damage, the burn window all cooldowns are saved for [phase][dps race].
- **Terestian Illhoof** — Sacrifice: a random player is chained in the middle and drained; the raid must break the Demon Chains at once to free them [job][adds]. A steady stream of imps is AoE'd by a warlock; his imp companion Kil'rek respawns [adds].
- **Shade of Aran** — No tank: he casts at random players. Three special spells every 30-35 s, never the same twice running: Flame Wreath (rings round three players for 20 s; anyone crossing a ring blows up the raid for 3-4k each: nobody moves), Circular Blizzard (a storm that rotates round the room: walk ahead of it), Magnetic Pull plus Arcane Explosion (everyone is pulled to the centre and has 10 s to run to the wall or take 9-11k) [dance][hazard]. His Frostbolt, Fireball and Arcane Missiles are interruptible and interrupts are divided by spell school [interrupt]. At 40% four water elementals for 90 s [adds]; at 20% mana he polymorphs the raid, drinks, and Pyroblasts for 7k+, so his mana must not be drained [resource]. Berserk at 12 minutes — [Warcraft Wiki: Shade of Aran](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Shade_of_Aran)
- **Netherspite** — Portal phase (60 s): three coloured beams reach from portals to the boss and strengthen him unless a player stands in the beam. Red: the blocker becomes the tank (gains aggro and +31,000 health, healed fully each tick). Green: heals the boss 4,000 per stacking tick unless blocked; the blocker loses maximum mana per stack. Blue: raises his spell damage; the blocker gains +5% damage dealt per stack but takes +8% spell damage. Leaving a beam gives Nether Exhaustion (locked out of that colour for 90 s), so the raid needs two rotating shifts of blockers. Banish phase (30 s): he drops threat and breathes Netherbreath at random players (4.5k and knockback); the raid hides. Void Zones under players; enrage at 9 minutes [job][resource][dance][phase][enrage] — [Warcraft Wiki: Netherspite](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Netherspite)
- **Chess Event** — The raid plays a board game against Medivh: each player takes control of a piece by talking to it, moves or turns it (5 s shared cooldown) and uses its two abilities; uncontrolled pieces act alone. Medivh cheats at random (heals his king, sets fire under a player's piece, buffs a unit). Win by killing the enemy king; losing your own king resets the board [gimmick] — [Warcraft Wiki: Chess Event](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Chess_Event)
- **Prince Malchezaar** — Three phases. Infernals fall at random spots throughout and burn everything near them (Hellfire) for the rest of the fight, so safe floor shrinks and the boss must be moved [hazard]. Enfeeble cuts several players' health and healing briefly and is followed by Shadow Nova (30 yd knockback blast): enfeebled players run out, then come back [dance]. Later phases add stacking Sunder Armor and Thrash on the tank, then flying axes that hit random players and Amplify Damage [phase] — abilities: [Warcraft Wiki: Prince Malchezaar](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Prince_Malchezaar); the Enfeeble/Nova timing is unverified on the fetched pages.
- **Nightbane (optional, summoned)** — A dragon that alternates ground and air; he takes off at 75, 50 and 25%. Ground: fear every 45-60 s (one or two unhandled fears on the tank wipe the raid), Charred Earth fire zones under players, frontal breath and tail sweep [hazard][threat]. Air: Rain of Bones on one player spawns five skeletons that the gathered raid kills; Smoking Blast on the highest-threat player; anyone who strays too far is hit by Fireball Barrage [adds][phase][stack/soak]. He cannot be taunted, so each landing is a threat race — [Warcraft Wiki: Nightbane (tactics)](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Nightbane_(tactics))

##### Gruul's Lair (25) — source: [Warcraft Tavern Gruul's Lair guide](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/tbc/guides/gruuls-lair/)
- **High King Maulgar** — A five-boss council pulled all at once, where four need an unusual tank [council][job]: Krosh Firehand (mage) is tanked by a mage who Spellsteals his fire shield to survive his Greater Fireball; Olm the Summoner (warlock) summons Wild Fel Stalkers; a raid warlock enslaves one of them (and banishes or fears the rest), because the stalker is the only thing that removes Olm's stacking Dark Decay from the tank holding him (that the enslaved stalker itself tanks Olm is common practice, unverified on the fetched page); Kiggler the Crazed (shaman) is tanked at range by a moonkin or hunters because he polymorphs his target; Blindeye the Seer (priest) shields himself and then casts a full heal that can only be interrupted after the shield is burst [interrupt]. Maulgar Whirlwinds for 15 s (everyone out) and below 50% charges random players [phase].
- **Gruul the Dragonkiller** — Growth: +15% damage every 30 s, stacking; around 20 stacks nothing survives him: a soft enrage that makes the fight a race [enrage][dps race]. Ground Slam throws everyone in random directions and slows them more and more until they turn to stone; then Shatter makes every petrified player damage all others within 15 yd. So in the few seconds after each slam everyone must run apart [spread][dance]. Hurtful Strike hits the second-highest threat target in melee, which must be an off-tank [stack/soak]. Cave In patches [hazard].

##### Magtheridon's Lair (25) — source: [Warcraft Tavern Magtheridon guide](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/tbc/guides/magtheridons-lair/)
- **Magtheridon** — P1 (about 2 minutes): the boss is bound and five Hellfire Channelers are fought first, each with its own tank; their heal (Dark Mending) must be interrupted, each one killed makes the survivors stronger (+30% damage and cast speed, stacking), and each summons an Abyssal that a warlock banishes [adds][interrupt][job]. P2: Magtheridon breaks free on the timer whether or not the channelers are dead. Blast Nova every minute is a raid-killing channel that is interrupted only when all five Manticron Cubes around the room are clicked and channelled at the same moment; holding a cube costs the clicker 800 health a tick, and when the channel ends the clicker gets Mind Exhaustion (originally 90 s, cut to 30 s in patch 2.4), which is why raids ran two alternating teams. While all five cubes are held Magtheridon also takes 300% damage. An uninterrupted Blast Nova deals about 2,500 every 2 s for 10 s to everyone. The wiki plans one clicker and one backup per cube (10 players); the Tavern guide assigns "4 groups of 5 cube clickers plus a backup for each cube (20 players total)" [job][interrupt] — [Warcraft Wiki: Magtheridon (tactics)](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Magtheridon_(tactics)). Quake every 50 s knocks everyone about for 7 s, which is what makes the cube timing hard. P3 at 30%: the ceiling collapses (raid-wide damage and stun, so it must not overlap a Blast Nova) and falling debris marked on the floor kills on hit [phase][hazard]. Hard enrage at 22 minutes [enrage].

##### Serpentshrine Cavern (25) — sources: [Warcraft Tavern SSC guide](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/tbc/guides/serpentshrine-cavern/); Warcraft Wiki pages linked per boss
- **Hydross the Unstable** — Two forms set by where he stands: frost form on one side of a line, nature form on the other. Each form stacks a mark on the raid that multiplies that school's damage, so at about four marks the tanks drag him across the line to flip the form and reset the marks. Each crossing wipes threat and spawns four elementals; a frost-resistance tank and a nature-resistance tank take turns [tank swap][resource][threat][adds].
- **The Lurker Below** — Fought from platforms in a pool. Spout every 45 s: a beam he sweeps through a full circle (3.5k per tick and a 60 yd knockback); players dive under water to avoid it [dance][hazard]. If nobody is in melee he Water Bolts the raid for 9-11k [job]. Every 2 minutes he submerges for 60 s and nine nagas appear on the platforms (six ranged, three melee) [phase][adds] — [Warcraft Wiki: The Lurker Below](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/The_Lurker_Below)
- **Leotheras the Blind** — Alternates Humanoid form (45 s) and Demon form (60 s), wiping threat each time [phase][threat]. Humanoid: Whirlwind for 12 s, during which he chases random players and leaves a heavy bleed; everyone runs [kite]. Demon: Chaos Blast stacks a fire-vulnerability debuff on whoever tanks it, so a warlock in fire-resistance gear tanks this form from range [job]. Inner Demons: up to five players each get a private demon only they can damage; anyone who has not killed theirs when the form ends is mind-controlled for 10 minutes: a personal damage check on every role, healers included [mind control][adds]. At 15% the two forms split and are fought together. Berserk at 10 minutes — [Warcraft Wiki: Leotheras the Blind](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Leotheras_the_Blind)
- **Fathom-Lord Karathress** — Boss and three advisors (shaman Tidalvess, hunter Sharkkis, priest Caribdis). Each advisor that dies hands Karathress one of its abilities, so the kill order shapes the fight [council][adds]. Tidalvess's Spitfire Totem must be killed at once; Caribdis heals and is tanked far from the others [interrupt]. Cataclysmic Bolt hits a random mana user for half their maximum health with a stun. At 75%, any advisor still alive gives him +66% attack and cast speed. Enrage at 10 minutes [enrage] — [Warcraft Wiki: Fathom-Lord Karathress](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Fathom-Lord_Karathress)
- **Morogrim Tidewalker** — Earthquake every 45-60 s summons two packs of six murlocs from the two sides of the room; they are gathered by a paladin tank and AoE'd [adds]. Tidal Wave: frontal hit that slows the tank's attacks by 400%. Watery Grave: four players are teleported under the waterfalls, take about 6k and fall; healers are assigned to them [job]. Below 25%: Watery Globules chase players and burst for 4-6k [kite] — [Warcraft Wiki: Morogrim Tidewalker](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Morogrim_Tidewalker)
- **Lady Vashj** — P1 (100-70%): tank fight with Static Charge (the target damages everyone within 5 yd, so they step out), Shock Blast stun on the tank, Entangle roots near her [spread]. P2 (70-50%): she stands shielded in the centre while four add types arrive on separate timers and each needs its own team [adds][job][split]: Enchanted Elementals walk in from all sides and each one that reaches her gives her +5% damage (ranged kill them); Coilfang Elites every 45 s (tanked and killed by melee); Coilfang Striders every 60 s fear everything near them every few seconds, so they are kited by one assigned player and killed with damage-over-time spells [kite]; Tainted Elementals every 50 s despawn within 15-20 s and drop a Tainted Core. A core, used at one of the four shield generators, switches that generator off and costs her 5% health; all four must go. The player holding a core "may not move but throw it to someone who is closer to the generators", so the core travels down a line of players — [Warcraft Tavern Lady Vashj guide](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/tbc/guides/lady-vashj/). "A wipe is highly probable if more than two Tainted Elementals despawn." P3 (50%-0): threat resets, P1 abilities return, and Toxic Spore Bats drop poison pools that fill the room: a soft enrage after about four minutes [phase][hazard][enrage] — [Warcraft Wiki: Lady Vashj (tactics)](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Lady_Vashj_(tactics)). See Q5 for the core passing.

##### Tempest Keep: The Eye (25) — sources: [Warcraft Tavern TK guide](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/tbc/guides/tempest-keep-the-eye-raid-guide/); Warcraft Wiki pages linked per boss
- **Al'ar** — P1: the phoenix flies between four high platforms about every 30 s; a tank must be waiting on the next platform, because with nobody in melee range she Flame Buffets the raid with a stacking fire debuff [job][tank swap]. Each move spawns an Ember add that explodes for 7k on death, so it is pulled away to die [adds]. Flame Quills: she spins in mid-air and everyone on the upper ledges must jump down or take 6-8k a second [dance]. At 0% she is reborn at full health: P2 on the ground with Meteor (later Dive Bomb) on a random player, fire patches, and Melt Armor (-80% armour for 60 s), which forces a taunt swap [phase][tank swap][hazard]. Berserk at 10 minutes — [Warcraft Wiki: Al'ar](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Al'ar)
- **Void Reaver** — A gear check with three abilities: Pounding (18 yd AoE round him every 12 s, healed through), Arcane Orb (7k and a 6 s silence thrown at a random player at least 18 yd away; visible in flight, so ranged players spread at maximum range and step out of its landing spot), Knock Away (cuts the tank's threat; three or four tanks keep a threat ladder because he cannot be taunted). Wipes the raid at 10 minutes [hazard][spread][threat][enrage] — [Warcraft Wiki: Void Reaver](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Void_Reaver)
- **High Astromancer Solarian** — Wrath of the Astromancer: a marked player explodes after 6 s for about 6k within 10 yd and throws neighbours into the air; they run out of the raid; "the main cause of wipes" [spread][job]. Every 50 s she vanishes and 12 weak agents spawn from three points, then she returns with two priests whose heals must be interrupted [adds][interrupt]. At 20% she becomes a voidwalker with a fear [phase] — [Warcraft Wiki: High Astromancer Solarian](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/High_Astromancer_Solarian)
- **Kael'thas Sunstrider** — Five phases with no break [phase]. P1: his four advisors come one at a time (Thaladred fixates and chases a random player and is kited; Sanguinar fears; Capernian is a caster tanked at range by a warlock; Telonicus throws bombs and a toy debuff) [kite][job]. P2: seven legendary weapons come alive and attack; each needs a tank (the bow is tanked by a hunter). When killed, players loot and equip them: the weapons are the tools for the rest of the fight (the shield, Phaseshift Bulwark, lets a tank absorb a Pyroblast; the other weapons' effects, such as the staff's protection from fear and mind control, are unverified on the fetched page) [adds][job][resource]. P3: all four advisors revive together at full health, 2 minutes after P2 began, so P2 is on a clock; "if two or more advisors survive until Kael enters a wipe is highly probable" [council][dps race]. P4: Kael himself: Fireball to interrupt, Mind Control on players (polymorphed or dispelled), Flamestrike zones, a phoenix whose egg must be killed within 15 s or it is reborn, and every 60 s a Shock Barrier followed by three Pyroblasts (about 23k each); the barrier must be burnt through and the casts interrupted, or one absorbed with the legendary shield [interrupt][mind control][adds]. P5 at 50%: Gravity Lapse lifts the whole raid into the air for 30 s where everyone swims in three dimensions, spreads to avoid chaining Nether Beams, and avoids Nether Vapor clouds that cut maximum health by 10% per stack [dance][spread][hazard]. No hard enrage, but the weapons vanish after 15 minutes — [Warcraft Wiki: Kael'thas Sunstrider (tactics)](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Kael'thas_Sunstrider_(tactics))

##### Battle for Mount Hyjal (25) — sources: [Warcraft Tavern Hyjal guide](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/tbc/guides/mount-hyjal/); Warcraft Wiki pages linked per boss
- **Structure** — Each of the first four bosses arrives after eight timed waves of trash attacking an allied NPC base; the next wave comes on the clock whether or not the last is dead. A wipe means redoing the eight waves [adds][dps race].
- **Rage Winterchill** — Death and Decay zone to leave (a large part of the raid at a time) [hazard]; Icebolt freezes and damages a random player; Frost Nova roots [dispel].
- **Anetheron** — Carrion Swarm: a cone that cuts healing done by those hit, so healers stand spread in different directions [spread]. Sleep on random players. Inferno: an Infernal lands on a player and must be picked up by a fire-resistant tank [adds].
- **Kaz'rogal** — Mark of Kaz'rogal: drains 600 mana a second for 5 s from every mana user; anyone who hits zero mana explodes for about 10-11k shadow on themselves and neighbours. Marks come faster and faster (down to every 10 s), so the fight is a race measured in the raid's mana [resource][dps race][enrage]. War Stomp stuns melee — [Warcraft Wiki: Kaz'rogal](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Kaz'rogal)
- **Azgalor** — Doom every 45 s: the target dies when the timer ends, unavoidably, and a Lesser Doomguard rises from the body; the fight plans for losing players on a schedule [resource][adds]. Rain of Fire zones [hazard]; Howl of Azgalor silences everyone near him.
- **Archimonde** — One of the era's "every single player must execute" fights. Air Burst throws a player and those near them high into the air; each must use the consumable Tears of the Goddess at the right moment to slow-fall or die on landing ("late or unused Tears caused about 30% of new-raid wipes") [job][spread]. Doomfire: trails of fire that crawl after players and hurt more the longer you stand in them [hazard][kite]. Fear every 40 s can run players into the fire. Grip of the Legion: a 5-minute curse for 2,500 every 2 s; decursers are spread so everyone is in range [dispel]. Soul Charge: every player death gives him a charge that later hits the whole raid (about 4,500 plus a silence, damage debuff or mana burn depending on the dead player's class), so one death tends to cascade into a wipe [resource]. Finger of Death if nobody is in melee [job]. At 10% the fight ends in a scripted finish; Hand of Death kills the raid at 9 minutes [enrage] — [Warcraft Wiki: Archimonde (tactics)](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Archimonde_(tactics))

##### Black Temple (25) — sources: [Warcraft Tavern Black Temple guide](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/tbc/guides/black-temple/) (role notes); Warcraft Wiki pages linked per boss
- **High Warlord Naj'entus** — Impaling Spine every 20 s pins a random player (stun and heavy bleed) until another player clicks them to pull the spine out; the rescuer now carries the spine [job]. Tidal Shield every 60 s makes him immune and heals him; it breaks only when a carried spine is thrown at him, and breaking it hits the whole raid for 8,500 frost, so everyone must be above that health first [resource][gimmick]. Needle Spine splashes, so the raid stands about 6 yd apart [spread] — [Warcraft Wiki: High Warlord Naj'entus](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/High_Warlord_Naj'entus)
- **Supremus** — Alternates two 60 s phases. Tank phase: Hateful Strike hits the highest-health target in melee other than the tank for about 30k (off-tanks soak it) and Molten Flame trails [stack/soak][hazard]. Kite phase: no threat; he gazes at a random player and walks after them; whoever he reaches is hit hard, so the target runs. Volcanoes erupt under players [kite][phase] — [Warcraft Wiki: Supremus](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Supremus)
- **Shade of Akama** — An add-control encounter. The Shade is held in place by Ashtongue Channelers (six: unverified on the fetched page); adds stream in from two doors every 35 s while the raid kills the channelers. Once free, the Shade walks to the allied NPC Akama and fights him; the raid must kill the Shade (it takes +50% damage) before it kills Akama, about 60 s [adds][job][dps race] — [Warcraft Wiki: Shade of Akama (tactics)](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Shade_of_Akama_(tactics))
- **Teron Gorefiend** — Shadow of Death every 30 s: the chosen player will die 55 s later. They walk away from the raid first; on death they become a ghost for 60 s with a separate action bar (Spirit Volley, Spirit Chains, Spirit Lance) and must use it to slow and kill the four Shadowy Constructs that spawn from their corpse, which living players cannot damage. "Constructs reaching the raid usually cause a wipe." Every player has to learn this mini-game before the raid can win [job][gimmick][kite][adds]. Crushing Shadows (+60% shadow damage taken) and Incinerate (dispel) on the raid [dispel] — [Warcraft Wiki: Teron Gorefiend (tactics)](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Teron_Gorefiend_(tactics))
- **Gurtogg Bloodboil** — 90 s cycle. For 60 s: Bloodboil puts a 24 s bleed (about 1k a second) on the five players standing farthest from him; three groups of five rotate into the far position so nobody takes two in a row [stack/soak][job][dance]. Acidic Wound stacks on the tank (armour down, damage over time) and forces swaps [tank swap]. Then Fel Rage for 30 s: he fixates one random player, who is given +250% health, armour and healing received and must be healed through his attacks as an emergency tank; if that player dies he turns on the raid [job][phase] — [Warcraft Wiki: Gurtogg Bloodboil](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Gurtogg_Bloodboil)
- **Reliquary of Souls** — Three bosses in a row with rule-changing auras, with add intermissions that restore health and mana [phase][resource]. Essence of Suffering: all healing is reduced by 100%; it fixates whoever is closest, so tanks and then anyone else rotate in to be hit until it dies; its Soul Drain must be dispelled. Essence of Desire: every attacker takes damage equal to half the damage they deal, healing is doubled and maximum mana drains away; Spirit Shock must be interrupted every time and Deaden spell-reflected; a pure race against the mana drain [interrupt][dps race]. Essence of Anger: raid-wide shadow damage that ramps up; a tank change triggers Seethe (double attack speed), so there must be none [enrage] — [Warcraft Wiki: Reliquary of Souls](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Reliquary_of_Souls)
- **Mother Shahraz** — Fatal Attraction: about three players are teleported together and linked; each damages the others within 15 yd until they get 25 yd apart, so they scatter at once [spread][job]. A random beam every 30 s chains through players, so the raid clusters tightly under her to control it [stack/soak]. Saber Lash: about 80k split between up to three targets in front: three tanks stand together [stack/soak]. The constant shadow damage originally made full shadow-resistance gear for the whole raid a requirement [resource] — [Warcraft Wiki: Mother Shahraz](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Mother_Shahraz)
- **The Illidari Council** — Four bosses with one shared health pool [council]: Gathios (paladin; tanked, moved out of his Consecration, auras that make one school useless at a time), Veras (rogue; vanishes and poisons random players), Lady Malande (priest; untankable in practice, her Circle of Healing must be interrupted every time and she reflects spells) [interrupt], High Nethermancer Zerevor (mage; tanked at range by a mage who Spellsteals his Dampen Magic and dodges Flamestrike and Blizzard) [job] — [Warcraft Wiki: Illidari Council](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Illidari_Council)
- **Illidan Stormrage** — Five phases, three special tanks [phase][job]. P1: tank fight; Shear must be blocked or it cuts the tank's health by 60% (unverified number); Flame Crash leaves fire under the tank; Parasitic Shadowfiend infects a random player and, when it expires, spawns two fiends that infect whoever they reach, so they are frozen and killed [adds]. P2 at 65%: Illidan flies and throws his two glaives into the floor; each spawns a Flame of Azzinoth, tanked by two off-tanks in fire-resistance gear. A Flame that gets more than about 25 yd from its glaive enrages and wipes the raid, while Illidan's Eye Beam draws lines of lethal blue fire across the floor and Dark Barrage channels on a random player; the raid stands in fixed tight groups [adds][hazard][dance]. P3: P1 plus Agonizing Flames; players spread 5 yd. P4: Demon Form for 60 s, alternating with P3: his Shadow Blast kills normal tanks, so a warlock in shadow-resistance gear tanks him from range; Shadow Demons each paralyse a player and walk to them to kill them, and must be killed on the way [adds]. P5 at 30%: Maiev joins and lays Cage Traps; dragging Illidan into a trap when he enrages stops the enrage and doubles damage taken [gimmick][enrage] — [Warcraft Wiki: Illidan Stormrage (tactics)](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Illidan_Stormrage_(tactics)); [Warcraft Tavern Black Temple guide](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/tbc/guides/black-temple/)

##### Zul'Aman (10) — sources: [Warcraft Tavern Zul'Aman guide](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/tbc/guides/zulaman/); [Warcraft Wiki: Zul'Aman (BC Classic)](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Zul'Aman_(BC_Classic))
- **Timed event (the raid's optional hard mode)** — A 20-minute timer starts at the gong; killing Nalorakk adds 15 minutes and Akil'zon 10. Each of the first four bosses killed inside the timer frees a hostage who gives a better chest; the fourth chest holds the Amani War Bear mount. Speed, not a harder boss, is the challenge [dps race].
- **Akil'zon (eagle)** — Electrical Storm: the whole raid must run together and stack for its duration (the guide: "raid must stack together"; that the safe spot is directly beneath one player lifted into the air is unverified on the fetched page) [stack/soak][dance]. Between storms Static Disruption chains between players, so they stand spread [spread]. Eagles dive on the raid [adds].
- **Nalorakk (bear)** — Alternates troll form (Brutal Swipe: a frontal hit shared between two tanks standing together; Surge charges the farthest player) and bear form (bleeds and a Mangle that amplifies them): one tank per form [tank swap][stack/soak][phase].
- **Jan'alai (dragonhawk)** — Fire Bombs fill the platform and explode after a few seconds; players stand in the gaps (that he first teleports everyone to the centre is unverified on the fetched page) [dance][hazard]. ##### Sunwell Plateau (25) — sources: [Warcraft Tavern Sunwell guide](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/tbc/guides/sunwell-plateau/); Warcraft Wiki pages linked per boss
- **Kalecgos / Sathrovarr** — One fight in two realms at once [split]. In the normal realm the raid fights the dragon Kalecgos; Arcane Buffet stacks a magic-vulnerability debuff on everyone there. Every 15-25 s Spectral Blast throws a random player into the spectral realm and leaves a portal open for about 10 s that a group can follow through. Inside, the demon Sathrovarr is fought beside a friendly NPC (Kalec, who must not die). Players are sent back after 60 s with Spectral Exhaustion (no re-entry for 60 s), and going through clears their Arcane Buffet. So the raid is organised in three or four portal groups plus rotating tanks that cycle between the realms. Both bosses must be brought down close together (both enrage at 10%). Curse of Boundless Agony in the spectral realm doubles every 5 s and jumps to another player when removed [resource][job][tank swap][dispel] — [Warcraft Wiki: Kalecgos (tactics)](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Kalecgos_(tactics))
- **Brutallus** — The era's pure numbers check: about six minutes to kill him before berserk [dps race][enrage]. Burn: a 60 s ramping fire DoT on a random player (3,200 a second at the end) that spreads to anyone within 2 yd [spread]. Stomp hits the tank hard and forces a taunt.
- **Felmyst** — Ground phase: Gas Nova drains health and mana from everyone until mass-dispelled, so the raid stands in dispel-sized clumps (10 or fewer); Encapsulate lifts a random player and pulses damage around them [dispel][spread]. Air phase: two Demonic Vapor beams chase players and leave a trail that spawns skeletons, so the target runs it along the edge [kite][adds]; then three Fog of Corruption breaths each cover a third of the field; anyone caught is mind-controlled until killed, so the raid runs to the lane she is not flying over [dance][mind control]. - **The Eredar Twins (Lady Sacrolash, Grand Warlock Alythess)** — Two bosses, shadow and fire [council]. Every hit from one twin leaves a stacking debuff of her school (Dark Touched, Flame Touched); being hit by the other twin's school clears it. So players deliberately take the opposite element to clean themselves [resource]. Shadow Nova and Conflagration are aimed at marked players who must run out [spread]; Sacrolash's Confounding Blow needs two tanks first and second on threat [tank swap]. The guide kills Sacrolash first and then gathers on Alythess (that the survivor gains her sister's abilities is unverified on the fetched page).
- **M'uru / Entropius** — P1 (M'uru cannot be tanked and does not move): Negative Energy hits 4-5 random players every second; every 60 s three humanoids enter from each side; Void Sentinels spawn and split into eight Void Spawn on death; Darkness every 45 s creates a void zone under the boss (about 3k a second, no healing) that melee must leave, and releases eight Dark Fiends that explode on the raid unless dispelled or killed [adds][job][dispel][hazard]. P2 (Entropius): black holes (Singularity) chase players and Darkness zones accumulate; a burn before the shared 10-minute enrage [dps race][enrage][kite]. - **Kil'jaeden** — Five phases [phase]. P1: three Hands of the Deceiver, tanked apart, whose portals spawn exploding fiends [adds]. P2: Kil'jaeden rises out of the Sunwell (he never moves); Legion Lightning chains and burns mana, Fire Bloom makes five random players burn everyone near them (spread 10 yd), Shield Orbs circle above and shoot the raid until ranged kill them [spread][adds]. P3 at 85%: Sinister Reflections copy random players and use their class abilities [adds]; Shadow Spikes; and Darkness of a Thousand Souls: an 8 s cast for about 50k on everyone that is survived only inside Shield of the Blue [job]. P4 at 55%: Armageddon meteors with ground markers land all the time [hazard]. P5 at 25%: everything at once, faster. The dragon orbs: four orbs round the room, one activated per phase; a player who clicks one controls a blue dragon for two minutes with its own action bar (Shield of the Blue: -95% damage for those near it; haste and revitalise breaths; Blink). Shields are limited, so the raid must push 55% and 25% before the third Darkness of each phase or there is no shield left for it [gimmick][dps race] — [Warcraft Wiki: Kil'jaeden (tactics)](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Kil'jaeden_(tactics)); [Warcraft Tavern Sunwell guide](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/tbc/guides/sunwell-plateau/)

#### WRATH OF THE LICH KING (2008-2010)

Every raid exists in a 10-player and a 25-player version — [Warcraft Tavern, WotLK raids overview](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/wotlk/guides/raids/); how the difficulty system evolved is in Q4.

- **Noth, Heigan, Loatheb** — as Classic: decurse or wipe; the eruption dance; Loatheb's healing ban is now Necrotic Aura, which blocks all healing except in a short window when it fades, so healers time one big heal to land in each window [dance][resource].

- **Gothik the Harvester** — as Classic: raid split across a gate into living and dead sides [split].
- **The Four Horsemen** — Baron Rivendare replaces Mograine. - **Sapphiron, Kel'Thuzad** — as Classic: hide behind ice blocks from the Frost Breath; Kel'Thuzad's add phase, then spread 10 yd for Frost Blast chains, interrupts, Shadow Fissures, and Guardians of Icecrown held by an off-tank at the end [LoS][spread][interrupt].

##### The Obsidian Sanctum (10/25) — source: [Warcraft Wiki: Sartharion](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Sartharion)
- **Sartharion** — The base fight: walls of lava sweep the island from the left or the right after a warning emote and the raid stands in the gaps [dance][hazard]; fire elementals need an off-tank and enrage (+400% damage) if a lava wave touches them [adds]. The landmark is the built-in difficulty choice: three Twilight drakes guard the room, and each one left alive joins the fight on a timer, each with a raid-wide debuff and a portal mechanic (Tenebron: double shadow damage taken, eggs that hatch whelps inside the Twilight realm; Shadron: double fire damage taken, and an acolyte in the portal that makes Sartharion immune until killed; Vesperon: -25% maximum health, and an acolyte that applies Twilight Torment). Portal teams go into the Twilight realm to kill eggs and acolytes [split][job]. Zero to three drakes alive scales the loot: one extra item per drake, and a mount with all three. ##### The Eye of Eternity (10/25) — source: [Warcraft Wiki: Malygos (tactics)](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Malygos_(tactics))
- **Malygos** — P1: Power Sparks drift toward him; one that arrives gives him +50% damage, one that is killed leaves a spot that gives players standing there a stacking damage buff, so sparks are slowed, killed at a chosen place and the damage dealers stand in it [adds][stack/soak][job]. Vortex lifts and spins the whole raid for 10 s. P2 at 50%: he flies out of reach; Nexus Lords and Scions ride hover discs (melee can ride the discs of dead ones); the raid shelters from his Deep Breath inside shrinking anti-magic bubbles and moves from bubble to bubble [hazard][dance][adds]. P3: he shatters the platform and every player falls onto a red drake with its own action bar (combo-point damage, heals over time, a shield): the whole raid finishes the fight as a vehicle fight in three dimensions, spreading out of Static Field and shielding against Surge of Power [gimmick][phase]. A 10-minute enrage runs across all three phases [enrage].

##### Ulduar (10/25) — sources: Warcraft Wiki pages linked per boss; hard-mode triggers from [Engadget, "How to activate Ulduar's hard modes", 19 Apr 2009](https://www.engadget.com/2009-04-19-how-to-activate-ulduars-hard-modes.html); raid overview [Warcraft Wiki: Ulduar (instance)](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Ulduar_(instance))

He pursues one random Siege Engine or Demolisher for 30 s at a time and that vehicle kites him [kite][job]. Players thrown onto his back kill the turrets (2 in 10-player, 4 in 25-player); when all are dead he shuts down and takes +50% damage. Hard mode: before the fight, choose to leave one to four defence towers standing; each adds health and an ability (Thorim's Hammer, Mimiron's Inferno, Hodir's Fury, Freya's Ward) — [Warcraft Wiki: Flame Leviathan](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Flame_Leviathan)

Slag Pot: he drops a random player into his pot for 10 s of fire damage; Flame Jets interrupt and lock out anyone casting [interrupt]. - **Razorscale** — She is airborne. - **XT-002 Deconstructor** — Gravity Bomb and Searing Light are put on players who must run out of the raid (the bomb pulls neighbours in when it explodes) [spread][job]. Hard mode: destroy the Heart during an exposure; he heals to full with +15% damage and +50% health and gains Void Zones and Life Sparks — [Warcraft Wiki: XT-002 Deconstructor](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/XT-002_Deconstructor)
- **The Assembly of Iron (Iron Council)** — Three bosses: Steelbreaker (melee; Fusion Punch on the tank), Runemaster Molgeim (Rune of Power: a floor rune that buffs whoever stands on it, boss or player; later Rune of Death, which must be left), Stormcaller Brundir (Chain Lightning to interrupt; Overload to run from; later flies with Lightning Tendrils and chases players). - **Kologarn** — A stationary giant made of three targets: body, left arm, right arm. The right arm's Stone Grip picks up players and squeezes them until the arm takes enough damage [job]. Destroying an arm damages the body and spawns Rubble adds; arms regrow after 60 s [adds]. Focused Eyebeam follows one player, who runs it away from the others [kite]. Crunch Armor on the tank (25-player rotates tanks; in 10-player it expires fast enough for one tank) [tank swap]. - **Auriaya** — Pulled with her four Sanctum Sentries by breaking line of sight [LoS][adds]. Terrifying Screech fears everyone and is followed by Sentinel Blast, which must be interrupted by someone who escaped the fear [interrupt]. Feral Defender: an add with nine lives that leaves a void zone at each death [adds][hazard]. 10-minute enrage — [Warcraft Wiki: Auriaya](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Auriaya)

- **Algalon the Observer** — Exists only as a hard-mode boss ("he is the hard mode"), and originally gave the raid one hour per week in total to attempt him. ##### Trial of the Crusader (10/25, each normal and heroic) — sources: Warcraft Wiki pages linked per boss; heroic rules from [Warcraft Wiki: Trial of the Grand Crusader](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Trial_of_the_Grand_Crusader)
- **Structure** — Five encounters in one arena room with no trash. Heroic ("Trial of the Grand Crusader") gave the raid 50 attempts for the week; each wipe cost one, and the reward chest after the last boss improved with the attempts left (tiers at 25, 45 and 50 remaining).
- **Northrend Beasts** — Three fights in a row with no rest [phase]. *Gormok*: Impale stacks a bleed on the tank (swap at 2-3 stacks); Snobolds jump onto players' backs, stunning and interrupting them until killed [tank swap][adds]. *Acidmaw and Dreadscale*: two worms, one mobile and one rooted, swapping each time they burrow; Acidmaw's Paralytic Toxin slows a player to a stop and is cured only by standing next to a player who carries Dreadscale's Burning Bile, so the burning players run to the poisoned ones [resource][job]. When one worm dies the other enrages. *Icehowl*: Massive Crash throws everyone to the walls and stuns them; he then stares at one player and charges that spot. If the charge hits nobody he slams into the wall, is stunned and takes extra damage; if it hits anyone that player dies and he enrages [dance]. On heroic the next beast enters on a timer whether or not the last is dead [dps race] — [Warcraft Wiki: Northrend Beasts](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Northrend_Beasts)
- **Lord Jaraxxus** — Incinerate Flesh: a healing-absorb shield on a player that healers must "heal off" within 12 s or it explodes on the raid [resource][job]. Legion Flame: the target drops fire wherever they walk, so they run a line along the wall [kite][hazard]. Nether Power: a stacking buff on the boss that must be purged or spell-stolen [dispel]. Fel Fireball to interrupt [interrupt]. ### Inferences
- Across the three eras the same small set of verbs carries almost every fight: stand apart, stand together, move out, move as one, swap the tank, kill the adds, interrupt the cast, remove the debuff, click the thing. What changes from boss to boss is which two or three of these collide at the same moment (Defile wants the raid spread while the Val'kyr want it stacked; Festergut wants ranged spread for Vile Gas but gathered for spores).
- The memorable bosses add one rule that exists nowhere else in the game and make it the whole fight: polarity (Thaddius), beams you block with your body (Netherspite), a ghost with its own action bar (Teron Gorefiend), a sanity meter (Yogg-Saron), a boss resource the raid starves (Saurfang), healing a boss up (Valithria).
- Classic bosses lean on preparation and class tools (resistance gear, Tranquilizing Shot, decurse, banish, the Onyxia Scale Cloak); TBC leans on assigned specialists and unforgiving single points of failure; Wrath leans on movement patterns everyone performs, with the hardest version made optional.
- The guides' recurring "failure" is rarely the boss's raw damage: it is one player's error spreading to others (a bomb carried into the raid, a chain, a curse left up, a death that feeds the boss).

### Gaps
- The catalogue is built from one summary pass per page; short connecting details inside a boss line (exact stack counts at which to swap, which class does a job) follow the fetched guides where stated and otherwise common practice. Numbers given are from the fetched pages; where a page gave none, none is given.
- Not confirmed on a fetched page and marked "(unverified)" in place: Zul'jin's fifth form and eagle-phase storm, the Enfeeble and Shadow Nova timing on Prince Malchezaar, the Brutallus soak-group rotation, the Val'kyr count per raid size on the Lich King (the Val'kyr Shadowguard page gives no count), the lifted player on Akil'zon, Jan'alai's teleport, Malacrass's add pool, the effects of Kael'thas's weapons other than the shield, the Eredar survivor gaining abilities, whether Olm is tanked by the enslaved stalker, Thorim's empty-arena wipe, and the counts of Garr's Firesworn and Akama's channelers.
- Thorim's hard-mode time limit is given three ways (2:45 and 90 s on the wiki page, 3 minutes by Engadget in 2009); Mimiron's hard-mode clock is 10 minutes on the wiki and 8 minutes in Engadget's 2009 list. Launch values probably differ from later ones.
- The Ruby Sanctum's three mini-bosses and Karazhan's three Servants' Quarters bosses rest on one short wiki summary each.
- The Warcraft Tavern TBC and Wrath raid guides are role checklists that name abilities without always describing them; where no Warcraft Wiki page was fetched for a boss (most of Karazhan's early bosses, Hyjal's first, second and fourth bosses, Zul'Aman, Brutallus and the Eredar Twins) the line rests on those checklists.
- Original launch-state numbers (before nerfs) were mostly not on the fetched pages. Noted changes: Gruul and Magtheridon were tuned too hard at launch and corrected in patch 2.1; M'uru's health was cut in 2.4.3; all TBC raid bosses lost 30% health and damage in patch 3.0.2.
- Icy Veins returned HTTP 403 and Wowhead guide pages render no text without scripts, so neither could be used as a source.

## 2. Which mechanic type does each belong to?

### Takeaway
Every mechanic in the catalogue above carries a type tag in square brackets, so the catalogue doubles as the classification. Nearly every boss combines two to four types; pure single-type bosses are the exception and are usually deliberate gear checks (Patchwerk, Brutallus, Void Reaver, Archavon).

### Cited Findings
- The type tags per boss are inline in section 1; the sources are the same as for each raid's section.
- Gear-check bosses are named as such by their sources: Patchwerk "Win the DPS race" with a 7-minute berserk — [Warcraft Tavern Classic Naxxramas guide](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/wow-classic/guides/naxxramas/); Void Reaver "about 8k total raid DPS is needed" before the 10-minute enrage — [Warcraft Wiki: Void Reaver](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Void_Reaver); Archavon needs over 7,670 DPS (10-player) or 33,236 DPS (25-player) inside a 5-minute enrage — [Warcraft Wiki: Archavon](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Archavon_the_Stone_Watcher); Festergut "mainly a DPS race with a 5-minute enrage" — [Warcraft Wiki: Festergut](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Festergut)
- Enrages come in three shapes in these eras: a hard timer (Twin Emperors 15 minutes, Magtheridon 22 minutes, Brutallus about 6 minutes, Blood-Queen 5:30); a growing buff or shrinking interval (Gruul's Growth +15% per 30 s, Loatheb's Inevitable Doom, Koralon's Burning Fury +5% per 20 s, Kaz'rogal's marks down to every 10 s); and a room that fills up (Vashj's spore pools, Rotface's oozes, Valithria's add waves, Mimiron's hard-mode fire) — sources in the respective raid sections.
- The mechanic-type table is at the end of this document (section 5).

### Inferences
- Type frequencies that stand out when the tags in section 1 are counted: [adds] is the most common tag in all three eras; [tank swap] driven by a stacking debuff runs from Classic's Mortal Wound bosses (Kurinnaxx, Fankriss, Gluth) to almost every Icecrown boss; [dance] patterns that the whole raid performs are rare in Classic (Heigan, C'Thun's Dark Glare, Onyxia's Deep Breath) and common in Wrath.
- Types work as layers a designer can turn on per raid size: a 2-player fight can carry [dance], [kite], [interrupt], [phase] and [resource]; [tank swap], [split] and [job] rotations need more bodies; [council] with special tanks and soak rotations (Maulgar, Bloodboil) need many.
- A soft enrage is the gentler tool: it lets a weaker group see more of the fight before it fails, where a hard timer only says no.

### Gaps
- No source classifies these bosses by type; the tags are this researcher's reading of the described mechanics.

## 3. Which encounters were firsts or landmark designs?

### Takeaway
Each era has a handful of fights built on a rule not seen in earlier raids (judged by release order, not by any source's claim of a "first"): Classic's Razorgore, Vaelastrasz, C'Thun, Thaddius and the Four Horsemen; TBC's Netherspite, Magtheridon, Vashj, Kael'thas, Archimonde, Teron Gorefiend, Illidan, Kalecgos, M'uru and Kil'jaeden; Wrath's Sartharion, Malygos, Flame Leviathan, Mimiron, Yogg-Saron, Algalon, Faction Champions, the Gunship, Saurfang, Valithria, Sindragosa and the Lich King.

### Cited Findings
Classic
- **Razorgore**: a boss fight whose first phase is an objective (destroy the eggs) played by mind-controlling the boss himself through an orb — [Warcraft Tavern BWL guide](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/wow-classic/guides/bwl/)
- **Vaelastrasz**: the fight gives unlimited resources and takes them away after 3 minutes, and kills its tanks on a schedule (Burning Adrenaline): "Expect 3-4 tank deaths, so bring five warriors" — [Warcraft Tavern BWL guide](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/wow-classic/guides/bwl/)
- **Chromaggus and Nefarian**: content that differs from week to week (two of five breaths; two of five drakonid colours) and Nefarian's class calls, which punish each class in its own way — [Warcraft Tavern BWL guide](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/wow-classic/guides/bwl/)
- **Hakkar and the Bug Trio**: early player-chosen difficulty. Hakkar "gains extra powers if the five aspects are not killed first"; the Bug Trio's kill order "determines loot" — [Warcraft Tavern ZG guide](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/wow-classic/guides/zg/); [Warcraft Tavern AQ40 guide](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/wow-classic/guides/temple-of-ahnqiraj-aq40/)

- **C'Thun**: fixed spread positions for 40 players (eight groups of five, 10 yd apart) because Eye Beam doubles with each jump; a sweeping death beam; and a second fight inside the boss's stomach — [Warcraft Tavern AQ40 guide](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/wow-classic/guides/temple-of-ahnqiraj-aq40/)
- **Heigan**: a pure floor-pattern fight, the "Safety Dance" (phase two is named so on the page) — [Warcraft Tavern Classic Naxxramas guide](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/wow-classic/guides/naxxramas/)

- **The Four Horsemen (original)**: "Eight tanks are needed, two per Horseman"; marks every 12 s lasting 75 s force a rotation; taunt had a 16% chance to be resisted and "taunt resists are described as a frequent cause of wipes"; "generally regarded as one of the most difficult" fights of original WoW — [Warcraft Wiki: Four Horsemen (Classic)](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Four_Horsemen_(Classic))
- **Razuvious and Faerlina**: mind control as the intended tool: priests tank the boss with his own students, or sacrifice the boss's own worshipper to calm her — [Warcraft Tavern Classic Naxxramas guide](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/wow-classic/guides/naxxramas/)

- **Netherspite**: players are the mechanic: standing in a beam makes you tank, healer-sink or glass cannon, with a lockout that forces two shifts — [Warcraft Wiki: Netherspite](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Netherspite)

## 4. How did raid size matter, and which mechanics changed with it?

### Takeaway
Classic tuned for 40 (with two 20-player raids), TBC cut to 25 and added two 10-player raids, and Wrath shipped every raid in both 10 and 25, then added a second difficulty axis: first inside the fight (Sartharion, Ulduar), then as a menu choice (Trial of the Crusader, Icecrown). Between 10 and 25 players the designers scaled counts (targets hit, adds, portals, soakers), sometimes swapped a class-bound job for an object anyone can click, and sometimes dropped a mechanic entirely.

### Cited Findings
The eras, in Blizzard's words (Ion Hazzikostas's 2014 blog as reported by [Engadget, 28 Apr 2014](https://www.engadget.com/2014-04-28-blizzard-on-raid-design-evolution.html))
- Classic raids were tuned for 40, where "bringing more players generally made things easier"; the main challenge was assembling and keeping a geared roster. Zul'Gurub and the Ruins of Ahn'Qiraj added 20-player raids, which put more responsibility on each member.
- TBC cut the largest raids from 40 to 25: "bigger raid-group sizes didn't necessarily make players feel more heroic", and smaller groups were easier to assemble. True 10-player raiding (Karazhan, Zul'Aman) was for friends-and-family groups. Launch tuning of Gruul and Magtheridon was "a brick wall to the rest of the raiding population" and was corrected in patch 2.1.
- Wrath: from patch 3.0 every raid came in parallel 10- and 25-player versions, the 25-player tuned harder with its own loot. Ulduar: nine of 13 encounters had optional hard modes with better loot (the Warcraft Wiki counts "ten of the fourteen", plus Algalon as hard-mode-only — [Warcraft Wiki: Ulduar](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Ulduar_(instance))). Trial of the Crusader: because in-fight hard modes "were inconsistent and sometimes unclear", four versions (10 and 25, each Normal and Heroic); the team "never hesitated to err on the side of making the 10-player version easier". Icecrown: one lockout per size, with the difficulty switchable per boss, to end the burnout of clearing Trial of the Crusader up to four times a week.
- "There are no difficulty levels or variable raid sizes in Vanilla WoW" — [Warcraft Tavern Classic raids overview](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/wow-classic/guides/raids/). "No new difficulty modes were added to the game for raids in TBC" — [Warcraft Tavern TBC raids overview](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/tbc/guides/raids/)
- Daelo (lead encounter designer) before Ulduar: Naxxramas was "the introduction to raiding 101 class for players who have never raided before", Ulduar "the second year class", with more hard modes "analogous to Sartharion" — [Engadget, 3 Jan 2009](https://www.engadget.com/2009-01-03-more-on-ulduar-and-hard-modes.html)
- Trial of the Grand Crusader (heroic): "50 attempts to complete the instance"; the tribute chest needed at least 25 attempts left and improved at 45 and 50 — [Warcraft Wiki: Trial of the Grand Crusader](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Trial_of_the_Grand_Crusader)
- Icecrown Citadel: difficulty "can be changed per boss, even mid-raid"; heroic requires a normal Lich King kill first; limited attempts on the wing-end bosses; a zone-wide buff to health, damage and healing started at 5% and was raised in 5% steps to make the raid easier over the months — [Warcraft Wiki: Icecrown Citadel (instance)](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Icecrown_Citadel_(instance))

How fixed-size raids used their numbers (share of the raid tied up by one mechanic)
- 40 players: C'Thun's eight groups of five in fixed positions; Huhuran's 15 nearest players as poison soakers in resistance gear; the original Four Horsemen's eight tanks; Razuvious's priest rotation; Nefarian's two door teams and class calls on whole classes; Gothik's two half-raids — sources in section 1 (Classic).
- 25 players: Magtheridon's five cube clickers every minute, with five backups or a second team (10 to 20 of the 25 players have a clicking duty, depending on the source); Bloodboil's three soak groups of five; Mother Shahraz's three stacked tanks; Vashj's "2 tanks, 1 kiter, 7-8 healers, 8-11 ranged, 4-7 melee"; Kalecgos's three or four portal groups; Illidan's "8-9 healers, 12-13 damage dealers, including at least 2 mages" beside four special tanks — sources in section 1 (TBC).
- 10 players (Karazhan, Zul'Aman): the same ideas at small scale: Netherspite needs three beam-blockers in two shifts (six of ten players have a named job); Nalorakk and Halazzi need two tanks sharing a hit; Akil'zon needs all ten under one player — sources in section 1.

What changed between Wrath's 10- and 25-player versions (concrete examples)
- **Number of players targeted**: Sindragosa's Frost Beacons 2 / 5 / 6 (10 / 25 / 25 heroic) — [Sindragosa](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Sindragosa_(tactics)); Blood-Queen's Pact links 2 or 3 players and the vampire timer is 75 s or 60 s — [Blood-Queen](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Blood-Queen_Lana'thel_(tactics)); Anub'arak's Penetrating Cold 2 or 5 targets — [Anub'arak](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Anub'arak_(Trial_of_the_Crusader_tactics)); Marrowgar's Bone Slice is shared with 1 or 2 extra players — [Marrowgar](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Lord_Marrowgar); Freya's Storm Lasher lightning chains to 3 or 5 — [Freya](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Freya_(tactics))
- **Number of adds or objects**: Saurfang's Blood Beasts 2 or 5 — [Saurfang](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Deathbringer_Saurfang); Festergut's Gas Spores 2 or 3 per set — [Festergut](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Festergut); Toravon's Frozen Orbs 1 or 3 — [Toravon](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Toravon_the_Ice_Watcher); Deathwhisper's waves 3 or 7 cultists — [Deathwhisper](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Lady_Deathwhisper); Valithria's add doors 2 or 4 — [Valithria](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Valithria_Dreamwalker); Flame Leviathan's turrets 2 or 4 — [Flame Leviathan](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Flame_Leviathan); Razorscale's harpoons 2 or 4 — [Razorscale](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Razorscale); Hodir's frozen helpers 4 or 8 — [Hodir](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Hodir_(tactics)); Faction Champions 6 or 10 enemies — [Faction Champions](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Faction_Champions)

### Inferences
- The difficulty dials of the era sort into six kinds (grouping by this researcher; each item is sourced in sections 1 and 3). Leave something alive: Hakkar's priests, Sartharion's drakes (0-3), Flame Leviathan's towers (1-4), Freya's Elders (1-3). Kill order: Bug Trio, Iron Council (Steelbreaker last). Beat a clock: Zul'Aman's chests, Hodir's cache, Thorim's Sif. Do the forbidden thing: destroy XT-002's heart, press Mimiron's Big Red Button, never use Vezax's vapors. Refuse help: Yogg-Saron's Keepers (4 down to 0). Menu toggle: Trial of the Crusader and Icecrown heroic, where heroic mostly adds one mechanic per boss (Putricide's Unbound Plague, the Blood Princes' Shadow Prison, the Lich King's Shadow Traps, Festergut's Malleable Goo) and removes safety (only six Frost Spheres on Anub'arak; no Tear Gas pause on Putricide).
- Health and required damage grew faster than head-count from 10 to 25 (about four times for 2.5 times the players), matching Blizzard's statement that 25-player was tuned harder.
- The scaling rule that recurs: the share of the raid touched by a mechanic stays roughly constant (2 of 10 against 5 of 25 beacons; 4 of 10 against 10 of 25 portals), while "one special job" mechanics keep an absolute count of one (one kiter, one abomination driver, one ranged tank) and therefore weigh far more on a small group.
- Mechanics that depend on a class being present (priest mind control, hunter Tranquilizing Shot, mage Spellsteal, warlock tank) were tolerable at 40 and 25, and were replaced by clickable objects or dropped at 10. For a game with 2 to 40 players that is the pattern to copy: bind special jobs to objects, not classes, and scale counts by share.
- Below ten players the sources offer no direct evidence; the 10-player versions suggest which mechanics survive shrinking (movement patterns, interrupts, kiting, a shared soak between two tanks) and which do not (multi-group rotations, councils with special tanks).

### Gaps
- Blizzard's original blog text was not fetched; the quotes are as reported by Engadget. Jeff Kaplan's "25 is the new 40" (a Ten Ton Hammer interview) appeared only in a search summary and was not verified on the page.
- The Trial of the Crusader 10-player guide and exact vehicle counts for Flame Leviathan by raid size were not found.
- No source gave the reasoning for individual scaling choices (for example why Dominate Mind was left out of 10-player).

## 5. Which mechanics required assigning specific players to jobs?

### Takeaway
From Classic onward the hardest coordination was not "everyone does X" but "these named players do X while the rest fight": clickers, kiters, special tanks, mind-controllers, portal teams, carriers and soak groups. TBC is the peak of such assignments; Wrath keeps them but more often lets any player fill the job.

### Inferences
- Jobs fall into four kinds: (a) pre-assigned specialists the fight cannot be won without (cube clickers, mage tank, kiter); (b) rotations, where a lockout or stacking debuff forces shifts (Netherspite's beams, Horsemen marks, Bloodboil groups, Kalecgos portals); (c) "the mechanic picks you" jobs, where a random player must suddenly do something exactly right (Teron's ghost, Living Bomb, Frost Beacon, Mutated Infection, Icehowl's stare); (d) vehicle or tool operators (Razorgore's orb, Kil'jaeden's dragons, the abomination, cannons).
- Kind (c) scales to any party size, because it needs no roster planning, and it is the kind Wrath used most. Kinds (a) and (b) need spare bodies and are the ones that broke down or were redesigned at 10 players (Razuvious's crystals).
- A job is only as reliable as its backup: the guides assign backups wherever one missed click wipes the raid (Magtheridon's cubes, the Horsemen's taunts, interrupt rotations).

### Gaps
- The fetched pages say that Vashj's core must be thrown from player to player and that Magtheridon's clickers get a lockout debuff, but not how raids laid out the chain or the rotation in detail; the two Magtheridon sources disagree on the number of clickers planned (10 or 20).
- Which of these jobs players enjoyed and which they resented is not covered by any fetched source.
