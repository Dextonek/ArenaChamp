How they fit the blocks

The blocks write a spell's rules short: the shape, the timing, the hit, the danger zone and the tooltip from the same numbers. The look (about seven eighths of each file) moves as it is.

The fight prints

Each hero fights twice, 60 s each, in the Harbour, the same inputs every time: against Isolde, who keeps her distance, and against Brannoc, who closes in. Green: the same print in every run (the two codes are the two fights). The Isolde fights were run six times each, the Brannoc fights twice: always the same.

Found broken (not fixed)

  • Madame Cleaver's table heals the enemy 50 too; the tooltip says the 50 is for her.
  • Gus's Flood bonus does nothing: the mop's flood damage equals its normal damage.
  • Late Fee, Foreclosure, Lightning Risk only hit who stood in the circle at the cast, not who walks in.
  • Six kit flags do not match their spell (Ember Flight is a dash without the flag ...). They may be meant, and the CPU reads them: yours to decide; the move leaves them as they are.
  • Some rules run on the computer's clock instead of the fight's (recasts of Crosswind, Barbed Stinger, Collapse ...).
  • Online: a late Uproot and a quick second Tag Out are dropped by the server; old hits make the HP drift.
  • A dead CPU rule (Isolde's ice block, Oblivion's maw) never fires.
  • About 80 spell comments give old numbers; the tooltips are right (but for 3).
  • 126 spell files the game never uses (showcases, retired hero spells, tests) ship in its start.
