One spell, before and after: Ember Quill

A spell's rules were spread through its file, and its danger zone was written a second time by hand. Now they are one short block, and the danger zone, the hit and the tooltip come from the same numbers. The look (the fan, the feather, the landing) is the same drawing code, moved as it was. The look is most of each spell's lines, so the files are only a fifth shorter.

Her four spells use four blocks:

  • Q, a bolt: 22 m/s, 10 m, 0.3 s wind-up; where it stops it lays an ember.
  • W, circles after a fuse: one at each ember, 2.2 m, 0.8 s, one hit of 54; then the embers burn out.
  • E, a move and a circle: she flies to the chosen ember in 0.7 s, then a 1.8 m burst where she lands, if the ember still burns.
  • R, a lane swept by a front: 1 s wind-up, 12 by 3 m, crossed in 0.5 s; her own hook says the front is her beak.

Every check

    Not changed on purpose

    • Her numbers, look and behaviour. The list of things found broken (on the framework page) is as it was, for you to decide.
    • Ember Flight still has no "dash" flag: adding it would change what the CPU does with it.

    • The four shared engine files (the duel, the arena, hero mode, the frame loop): not touched.
    • Her spells still load with the page. Loading a hero's spells only for the fight it is in is the design's next step.
