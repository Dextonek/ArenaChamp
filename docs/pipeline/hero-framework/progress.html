The hero framework · Anka, the sample

Heroes into folders

Each hero now lives in one folder (src/heroes/<name>/): its card, its passive, its four spells, its look parts and its icons. The game finds the folders by itself. Moved in batches; after every batch all 80 fight prints must be the same. The moves change no spell: each spell file moved as it was, so a hero's lines stay about the same (its card adds about 40). The next step puts the spells onto the shared blocks, a spell at a time; that is where they get shorter.
